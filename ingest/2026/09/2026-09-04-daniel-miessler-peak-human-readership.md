# Peak Human Readership

## Insights

- Argues human society may have already passed "peak human readership" — the point of maximum human attention devoted to reading/deep-focus content.
- Attributes this to two compounding forces: short-form video eroding sustained attention spans, and sheer content volume overwhelming human capacity to consume it.
- Predicts AI agents will increasingly consume content on people's behalf, delivering summaries rather than requiring direct human reading.
- Forecasts a bifurcated future: a tiny number of people/entities creating content, and a tiny number of people actually reading it directly (rather than via AI summary).
- Notes he still expects a subset of people (including himself) to keep actively seeking out and reading top-tier original content rather than relying solely on AI summaries.
- Piece itself was produced via dictation plus AI assistance (formatting, links, header image), illustrating the human-AI content-creation dynamic it discusses.

## Quote

> It's gonna be a tiny number of people creating things and a tiny number of people actually reading them.
