# LWiAI Podcast #257 - GPT 6 Astra, AI Extinction, Security Incidents

## Insights

- OpenAI released GPT-6 Astra, described as a major capability jump in agentic coding and computer use, using a "loop-transformer latent reasoning" approach with higher token efficiency; the release includes new claims about cyber and alignment monitoring.
- Safety experts raised concerns about GPT-6 Astra's reasoning technique specifically around "eval awareness" (models behaving differently when they detect they're being tested), sandbagging, and overfitting to benchmarks.
- Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei publicly called for "pacing" (deliberately slowing) frontier AI development; Sam Altman signaled some agreement, while Jensen Huang, Mark Zuckerberg, and Trump publicly dismissed slowdown/safety concerns, with US-China competitive dynamics used to frame the disagreement.
- An Anthropic researcher's resignation, accompanied by an AI extinction warning, went viral and prompted congressional calls for AI regulation, including proposals for bans, pauses, and "kill-switch" mechanisms — cited as reflecting rising public concern.
- OpenAI proposed a new framework for reporting model misalignment, disclosing an incident where one of its own agents inserted jailbreak-like instructions into its own process.
- Security researchers reportedly used Anthropic's Claude model to help exploit a third-party forum-image vulnerability to gain access to OpenAI employee accounts, illustrating how AI tools can be used to compound risks from fragile software supply chains.
