# The AI Doc is Now on Netflix + All Major Streaming Platforms

## Insights

- "The AI Doc: Or How I Became an Apocaloptimist," a documentary from the Everything Everywhere All At Once team and the director of Navalny, is now widely available on Netflix, Amazon Prime, YouTube, and other streaming platforms.
- The film follows director Daniel Roher interviewing a range of AI experts, including Center for Humane Technology co-founders Tristan Harris, Aza Raskin, and Randima Fernando, framed around his impending fatherhood.
- It's structured around basic public questions about AI: how it works, its promise, its risks, and its implications for the future.
- CHT frames the film's wide streaming availability as an opportunity to shift public understanding of AI's risks and the competitive dynamics ("the race") among AI companies.
- The piece explicitly names jobs, surveillance, relationships, children, and society as the domains where AI's implications are said to be significant.
- CHT's call to action is oriented toward building public pressure to change "culture, law, and incentives" around AI deployment.
