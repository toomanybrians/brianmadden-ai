# Nubank entered 35 countries without a banking licence, and now it wants to pay £10B for Monzo’s 😳🏦; Meta Muse sparked the first AI Agent selloff on Wall Street 🤖📉

## Insights

- Meta Muse's launch triggered what's described as the first AI-agent-driven selloff on Wall Street, with Goldman Sachs constructing a "consumer inertia" basket of stocks seen as vulnerable to AI agents that lost 7% over six trading sessions.
- The selloff hit consumer-facing companies whose business models rely on direct customer relationships — Schwab, Allstate, and Planet Fitness fell — while Shopify and Meta, seen as better positioned in an agent-mediated world, surged.
- This is framed as the market establishing a new valuation test tied to "agent access": whether AI agents will intermediate or bypass a company's customer relationship, with implications for how other sectors may get repriced.
- Separately, Nubank is in early talks to buy UK digital bank Monzo for £8-10B, roughly double Monzo's last valuation, despite Nubank operating in 35+ countries without holding a banking licence in any of them.
- The Monzo deal's logic hinges on Nubank converting Monzo's £25.7B of largely unlent deposits into loans — something Nubank has not done outside Latin America — and its Brazilian-style 22.9% margins are expected to face pressure under UK banking regulation.
