# How to job again..

## Insights

- The author frames her career work explicitly as sitting "at the intersection of AI/technology and people," suggesting a recurring professional category distinct from pure technical AI roles.
- She describes launching an "AI Accelerator" business to help teams "redesign their workflows and set up their ways of working for AI," implying a market for structured AI-adoption consulting aimed at organizational workflow redesign rather than tool rollout.
- She ran a workshop with 25+ HR leaders on "the future of work," indicating active practitioner-level interest among HR leadership in AI-driven workplace change.
- Her return to corporate employment is into a role titled "Organisation Effectiveness in AI," suggesting some organizations are formalizing a distinct function around AI's effect on org design/effectiveness rather than treating it purely as an IT or HR initiative.
- She characterizes the current moment as one where "the technology has progressed enough that it's deeply impacting not just what we do at work, but HOW we do it" — a claim that AI's effect has shifted from task-level to process/structural-level change.
- She frames her ongoing purpose as helping create shared language for "AI in organisations as a question of how we design work, and who we design it for," positioning AI adoption as a work-design and workforce-design problem rather than a technology procurement one.
