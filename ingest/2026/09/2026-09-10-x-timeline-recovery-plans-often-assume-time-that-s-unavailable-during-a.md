- A new "dual boot" feature installs a second isolated UniconOS partition alongside Windows; if Windows fails, users reboot into UniconOS and resume work via Citrix DaaS within minutes, without spare hardware or reimaging.
- The product's stated value proposition centers on business continuity/resiliency — positioning recovery plans that assume unavailable time during outages as a gap it addresses.
- Claimed benefits include IT cost reduction (over 35%) via hardware repurposing, extended device lifecycles, and eliminating need for a third-party OS license on endpoints.

- Security features include a write-protected OS, full disk encryption, TPM 2.0 support, secure boot, and silent/automated updates for compliance.
