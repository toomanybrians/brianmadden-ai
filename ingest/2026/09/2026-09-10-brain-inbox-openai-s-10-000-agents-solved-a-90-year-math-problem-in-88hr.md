# 🧮 OpenAI's 10,000 agents solved a 90-year math problem in 88hrs

## Insights

- OpenAI deployed 10,000 AI agents on an unreleased model to resolve a 90-year-old open question about the Navier-Stokes equations, finding conditions under which the equations mathematically break down, in 88 hours.
- The result was formally verified using the Lean proof-checking system, which the piece cites as grounds for mathematicians to trust the finding, though credit-attribution controversy surrounds it.
- The agents operated as a coordinated multi-agent system: reading from a cached internet snapshot, executing code, and communicating within subgroups rather than working as a single model.
- The newsletter frames this as a category shift — AI moving from assisting scientific research to autonomously conducting it at scale — and pairs it with Sony AI's tool predicting real gene-aging discoveries from 1.5M generated hypotheses as a second example of the same pattern.
- Separately, OpenAI released ChatGPT Images 2.5 (faster, cheaper image generation with precise local editing and sketch input) and Mistral raised a €3B round at a €21B valuation, positioning itself as a sovereign/self-hosted alternative to cloud-locked frontier AI providers.
