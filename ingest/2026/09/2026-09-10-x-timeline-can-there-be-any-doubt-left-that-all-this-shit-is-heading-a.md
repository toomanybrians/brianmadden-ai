# Can there be any doubt left that all this shit is heading a bad direction?

http

## Insights

- Terence Tao's public stance on AI has shifted sharply from cautious optimism (2024) toward alarm, arguing solving puzzles is not the same as generating genuine new mathematical insight.
- Tao raises a transparency/IP concern tied to the OpenAI–NYU–Anthropic Navier-Stokes dispute, where OpenAI reportedly spent $22.5M to scoop independent researchers and would not rule out using de-identified data from their work.
- OpenAI's Chief Research Officer confirmed the company uses user feedback and de-identified data to improve models broadly, while denying any human/agent directly viewed the specific researchers' data — a distinction Marcus finds unreassuring.
- Tao warns of broader fallout: trust erosion from AI labs' IP practices could undermine willingness to collaborate with AI in other fields (e.g., cancer research), not just math.
- A named departing Anthropic researcher (Jacob Coxon) claims AI lab insiders privately believe frontier AI could cause human extinction by decade's end, distinct from public messaging; Anthropic's Alignment Science lead corroborated this sentiment, citing no clear plan for superintelligence alignment.
- Marcus is skeptical of near-term extinction risk but agrees catastrophic harm is plausible and that neither leading lab has a credible plan; he suggests the underlying problem may require abandoning LLMs as the technical foundation, an option neither company is pursuing.
