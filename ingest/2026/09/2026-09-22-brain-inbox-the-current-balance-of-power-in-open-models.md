# The current balance of power in open models

## Insights

- Chinese open-weight models (GLM-5.3, Kimi K3, DeepSeek) now clearly lead US open-weight models (Llama, Nemotron, Thinking Machines' Inkling) on benchmarks and have led Hugging Face downloads since July 2025, with a 1.6B-download gap and China's total download share double America's.
- Chinese open-weight models trail closed American frontier models (OpenAI, Anthropic) by roughly 2-5 months; US open-weight models trail the closed frontier by 6-9 months — meaning China's open models are now closer to the true frontier than America's own open models.
- Distillation of American model outputs is a real but limited factor in Chinese success — fully blocking it would only widen the gap by 1-2 months; author attributes much of the gap to faster release cadence, narrower task focus, and increasing Chinese investment in purchasing high-quality training/RL data.
- Real-world adoption is shifting decisively toward Chinese models: OpenRouter usage of Chinese open models grew from ~70% to over 80% share as total open-model inference volume rocketed from ~1T to ~80T tokens/week; coding platform OpenCode shows ~95%+ Chinese-model inference share.
- Major US companies and startups (Cursor, Harvey, DoorDash, Airbnb, Perplexity) are building products on Chinese open-weight models, and US startups are increasingly signing enterprise agreements directly with Chinese labs for model usage rights — described as a new form of cross-border AI collaboration.
- In academic research, Chinese open models (led by Alibaba's Qwen) now appear in ~40% of arXiv AI/ML papers versus ~30% for US models, with China's share still growing faster, signaling an erosion of US leadership as the hub of open AI research.
- Restricting access to risky open-weight models is structurally difficult since bad actors can still obtain them while legitimate US businesses would be cut off — illustrated by HuggingFace using a Chinese open model to investigate a cyberattack because closed US models refused the request.

## Quote

> Chinese AI companies are the clear leaders in open weight models.
