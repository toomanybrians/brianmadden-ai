# 💍 Oura Targets a $15.6B Fully Diluted IPO Valuation. 21 Silicon Valley Startups Raised $1B+ Last Week

## Insights

- Oura's IPO roadshow targets a $15.6B fully diluted valuation on Nasdaq (ticker OURA), with $1.21B revenue over nine months (up 74% YoY) and ~5M paid members — notable as a hardware/wearables company reaching this scale.
- Oura's HSA/FSA payment strategy is flagged as a key distribution advantage, tapping into ~62M Americans with HSAs and ~32M with FSAs as a benefits-funded purchase channel competitors can't quickly replicate.
- Snorkel AI raised a $350M Series E at $3.5B, reaching ~$375M annualized revenue (9.3x multiple), competing directly with Scale AI, Mercor, and Surge AI in the AI training-data/evaluation market — its valuation trails competitors despite claimed 18x growth in a year.
- An open question raised: whether frontier AI labs will keep paying for third-party training/evaluation data long-term, which underpins the entire data-labeling sector's business model.
- Weekly funding snapshot: 21 Silicon Valley-area startups raised $1B+ total, with "Enterprise" (largely AI infrastructure/agent companies) dominating at $664.5M across 9 deals — far ahead of FinTech, BioTech, Defense Tech, and other categories.
- Enterprise AI funding targets specific infra niches: AI training data/expert recruitment (Micro1), agentic AI employees (Ema), web data infrastructure for AI agents (Firecrawl), optical connectivity for AI infrastructure (PicoJool), and agent-native messaging (Ando) — signaling continued build-out of the AI agent tooling stack.
