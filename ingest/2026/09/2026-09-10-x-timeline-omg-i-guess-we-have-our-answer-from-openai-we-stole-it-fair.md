# OMG. I guess we have our answer from OpenAI

“we stole it fair and square”

Not

## Insights

- OpenAI claims to have produced a solution to the Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize Problem, generated by "a group of agents" using an unreleased, next-generation OpenAI model described as significantly more capable than GPT-6 Astra.
- OpenAI's announcement makes no mention of human mathematicians the company was reportedly in communication with during the work.
- Gary Marcus alleges this omission suggests OpenAI may have used ideas or contributions from those human mathematicians without credit — characterizing it as OpenAI effectively saying "we stole it fair and square."
- Marcus frames this as evidence of a broader pattern of AI labs claiming full credit for research breakthroughs while sidelining or erasing the human expert input that contributed to them.
