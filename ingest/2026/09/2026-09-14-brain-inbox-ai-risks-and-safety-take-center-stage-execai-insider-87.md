# AI Risks and Safety Take Center Stage (ExecAI Insider #87)

## Insights

- A former OpenAI and Anthropic pretraining researcher (Jacob Coxon) publicly resigned from Anthropic, saying an X post explaining why has reached 167M+ views and drawn mainstream media attention.
- Coxon's core claim: both labs are "racing straight to self-improving superintelligence" — OpenAI hasn't fully internalized the civilizational stakes, while Anthropic understands them but feels locked into a race, believing no other actor will act responsibly.
- Coxon frames near-term systems as potentially superhuman at hacking, revolutionizing fields overnight, and acquiring real power/resources, with no sign of progress slowing.
- Newsletter author (Paul Roetzer) reads this moment, combined with a referenced "OpenAI/Hugging Face incident," as a possible tipping point pushing AI and government leaders toward more serious safety/alignment commitment.
- Roetzer's own position: opposes banning/ignoring AI, but argues for slowing development and directing top talent/resources to safety and alignment; predicts increased likelihood of collaboration between competing AI labs and nations.
- Separately noted industry developments: Jensen Huang claiming "AGI has arrived" tied to GPT-6 Astra training; OpenAI claiming a solved Millennium Prize math problem (Navier–Stokes) via ~10,000 concurrent agents; Anthropic released an interactive economic-futures model on AI's effect on jobs/growth/unemployment through 2030; Google reportedly completed a $1.5B deal absorbing Mechanize talent into DeepMind's "midtraining" efforts.
