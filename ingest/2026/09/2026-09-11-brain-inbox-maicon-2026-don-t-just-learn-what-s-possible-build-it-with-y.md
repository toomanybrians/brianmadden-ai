# MAICON 2026: Don't just learn what's possible. Build it with your team.

## Insights

- The piece frames a distinction between organizations "consuming" AI (learning about it, watching demos, trying tools) versus "putting it to work" (building applications, redesigning workflows, changing analytics practices).
- It proposes a diagnostic question for teams: not how much they know about AI, but what they can now do that they couldn't do six months prior.
- Argues that individual knowledge gains ("everyone will know more about AI in five weeks") are becoming a weak differentiator — organizational capability to apply AI is positioned as the actual gap that matters between teams.
- Suggests a team-based approach to AI upskilling: rather than one person learning broadly, a team can split up, each build a different workflow/tool in parallel, then compare and integrate results back at the org level.
- Implies a low bar for ROI on AI-workflow experimentation — claims a single new workflow saving one person a few hours weekly could justify itself at team scale.

## Quote

> The question is whether your organization will be better at using it.
