# Vent Mauvais

## Insights

- Mistral's founder promised an open-weight "frontier" model for summer 2026 with July early access; by the season's end (Sept 23) nothing shipped, and days before, Mistral's own product account made a Chinese-trained model (Z.ai's GLM-5.3) the default in its coding/chat app.
- Across every measurable "free choice" signal — a German enterprise survey (0/343 firms cited Mistral), Hugging Face downloads (Mistral's flagship trails a comparable open model by roughly 500x), and OpenRouter's usage rankings (Mistral 8th, 2.6% share) — organic adoption of Mistral's models is effectively invisible next to US and Chinese competitors.
- Government contracts to Mistral (Luxembourg, Hesse) were awarded without competitive tender and published to the euro (~€52M total), while private/commercial contract values are almost never disclosed, and Mistral has never filed audited financial accounts in France despite a >€21B valuation.
- The CEO testified under oath that public-sector orders equal 20% of software revenue (10% French state), but no audited data supports what the remaining ~80% (private buyers) actually contributes.
- Mistral's own CLI coding agent, running the company's three-month-old flagship model with live web-search tools available, denied that Mistral had a speech-to-text product (Voxtral, live since mid-2025) until directly corrected — despite having called its search tool four other times in the same session.
- Mistral's 2023 founding memo bet on training-efficiency gains (10-100x) to compete without matching compute budgets; by mid-2026 the founder publicly stated that compute "flops budget" now governs the competition — a reversal of the original strategic bet, occurring within the memo's own 4-year deadline window.
