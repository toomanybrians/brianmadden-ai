# Nvidia’s Backstop Universe – Heads I Win, Tails Who Loses?

## Insights

- Nvidia's disclosed off-balance sheet guarantees jumped from $184B to $530B in one quarter, driven by supply/capacity commitments (mostly memory, up to $279B), a $108.5B guarantee on a 4.25GW OpenAI-leased Ohio campus, and two new line items: $36B in AI cloud take-or-pay floors and $20B in datacenter leases Nvidia plans to reassign.
- Nvidia's strategy is to act as an alternative credit anchor to the "Gigascalers" (Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Meta, Oracle), who have historically gatekept investment-grade capital for the AI buildout while profiting from the spread between offtake and spot pricing; Nvidia's backstops let non-investment-grade Neoclouds/Neolabs borrow at IG-like rates.
- The structure is asymmetric in Nvidia's favor: it earns full margin on GPU sales upfront plus revenue share above guaranteed floors if demand holds ("heads"); if demand falls, floors are set high enough that Neoclouds stay solvent and lenders are protected ("tails") — Nvidia only loses if backstopped Neoclouds fail and Nvidia's own cash generation simultaneously weakens.
- Nvidia currently backstops ~6.5GW of capacity (mostly unbuilt), which is described as smaller than the Gigascalers' aggregate implicit backstop of leased capacity (~15GW in 2026, projected 35GW+ by 2028), though Nvidia's newer program draws more investor scrutiny due to comparisons with Cisco's dot-com-era vendor financing collapse.
- A new capital-partnership program (targeting $500B+, potentially up to $2.5T by F1/31) uses a smaller residual value guarantee (up to 25% per deal) modeled loosely on the Google-Broadcom-Anthropic-Apollo TPU structure, and is far more capital-efficient per GW ($9.4B/GW) than the AICP program ($59B/GW) or the PORTS-Pike lease structure ($25B/GW).
- Nvidia's cash balance and investment/equity securities book have grown sharply (investment book from $45B to $128B YoY), with consensus estimating ~$441B EBITDA in F1/28, against an estimated $11T of cumulative AI capex industry-wide from CY24–CY29.

## Quote

> Heads, demand holds and Nvidia wins twice... Tails, demand falls and the Neocloud makes no money but remains solvent.
