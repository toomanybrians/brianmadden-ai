# sounds like the government testing standard fail to adequately factor in monitor

## Insights

- Gary Marcus criticizes government AI testing standards for failing to adequately account for "monitorability" — the ability to monitor/oversee AI systems after deployment.
- The critique is prompted by OpenAI's announcement of a new model ("Astra") and comments from OpenAI's @gdb about the company's compliance process.
- At a briefing, OpenAI's @gdb stated the company followed the "standard testing process together with the government" under the new White House (WH) AI framework.
- @gdb reported that the government testing process raised no requirements or objections ("nothing that they came back saying that you need to...") — implying a clean pass.
- Marcus frames this as concerning, suggesting existing government evaluation frameworks may have a structural gap around ongoing oversight/monitorability rather than just pre-release testing.

## Quote

> "sounds like the government testing standard fail to adequately factor in monitorability. that's bad." — @GaryMarcus
