# Automating Catastrophic Risk Forecasts: Introducing AIRO

## Insights

- The Automated AI Risk Outlook (AIRO) is a dashboard that uses an ensemble of four frontier LLMs (one per model family, selected via the Epoch Capabilities Index) to forecast catastrophic AI risk probabilities in near real time.
- As of launch, the model ensemble puts the odds of an AI-caused catastrophe (10%+ of global population killed, or equivalent economic/morbidity damage) at 0.47% by 2030 and 6% by 2050 — higher than human expert/superforecaster estimates of 2% by 2050 from a prior survey.
- Models are also asked to forecast risk conditional on future AI capability levels; assuming a 75th-percentile capability outcome raised catastrophe forecasts by a factor of ~1.42 versus the unconditional baseline.
- The project's rationale rests on a claim that top AI models have reached "parity" with human superforecasters on a forecasting benchmark (ForecastBench), and that more capable models (per capability index scores) are more accurate on rare-event and conditional forecasts in simulated testing.
- The methodology has AI models autonomously conduct their own research (web search, page-reading) before submitting forecasts with rationale and sources, after a minimum number of tool calls.
- The piece names a specific risk to this approach: "sandbagging," where misaligned models or companies motivated by regulatory/profit concerns could deliberately underreport risk estimates.

## Quote

> a highly capable but misaligned model trying to evade human suspicion may lower its estimates to avoid causing alarm
