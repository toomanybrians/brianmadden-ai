# It’s entirely plausible that the AI industry can deal with safety and security t

## Insights

- Argues the AI industry may be able to handle safety and security for now through voluntary shared standards and practices, without external mandates.
- Predicts that as AI capability advances, this self-regulation phase will eventually give way to more formal oversight, testing regimes, and layers of legal liability.
- Frames this as a two-stage trajectory: informal industry self-governance now, formal regulatory/liability structures later, tied to capability progress rather than current politics.
- References a claim (via quoted post) that leaders of major American AI labs have committed to internal controls, audits, and reviews, positioned as a step toward building public confidence.

## Quote

> At some point in capability progress there will inevitably be greater oversight, testing, and layers of liability
