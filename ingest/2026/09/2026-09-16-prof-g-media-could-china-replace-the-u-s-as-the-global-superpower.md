# Could China Replace the U.S. as the Global Superpower?

## Insights

- China spent roughly 23x less than the U.S. on private AI investment in 2025 but shipped 10x more humanoid robots and deployed industrial AI robots at nearly 9x the U.S. rate — reframing the AI race around physical-economy embedding rather than model quality alone.
- The episode frames the real competitive question as who integrates AI into manufacturing, robotics, and industrial processes fastest, not just who builds the most capable model.
- A Pew poll cited shows more countries now view China more favorably than the U.S., alongside the Lowy Institute's 2025 Asia Power Index recording the U.S.'s largest-ever single-year decline in regional power.
- Discussion touches on shifting balance of AI talent and chip capability between the U.S. and China, though specifics beyond the robotics stats aren't detailed in the excerpt.
- China's global-south strategy and cultural exports are raised as additional dimensions of its broader positioning versus the U.S.

## Quote

> the real AI race isn't just about who has the best model — it's about who embeds AI into the physical economy fastest
