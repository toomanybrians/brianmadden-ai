# At Box, we've been testing Sonnet 5.5 in early access on our complex work eval w

## Insights

- Box has been early-access testing Claude Sonnet 5.5 against its internal "complex work eval," which is built around the Box Agent and enterprise content tasks.
- Box reports Sonnet 5.5 shows another meaningful jump over Sonnet 5 specifically on knowledge-work tasks involving enterprise content.
- The reported improvement is a 4-point overall gain on Box's hardest test cases, per early testing.
- Sonnet 5.5 is described (via Anthropic's announcement) as the second model in the Claude 5.5 family, running over 30% faster and costing up to 30% less than its predecessor for most workloads.
- The combination of capability gains and lower cost/latency is being framed as significant for enterprise deployment of agents handling complex work.

## Quote

> Sonnet 5.5 is another strong jump from Sonnet 5 on knowledge work with enterprise content.
