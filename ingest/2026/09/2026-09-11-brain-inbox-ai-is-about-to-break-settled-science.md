# AI Is About to Break Settled Science

## Insights

- The piece frames a recurring historical pattern: new measurement instruments surface data that contradicts consensus science well before belief actually changes — the resistance, not the discovery, is the slow part (cites heliocentrism, geologic time, germ theory, evolution, relativity, quantum mechanics, cosmic expansion, plate tectonics, DNA-as-code, exoplanets as ten cases).
- Diamandis argues AI plus a new generation of instruments (protein-folding models, all-sky imaging telescopes, single-cell sequencing, continuous molecular monitoring, whole-brain imaging) is now generating data faster than humans can interpret it, with AI doing the reading — positioned as the mechanism for the next wave of "settled science" being overturned.
- Explicit example given: AlphaFold predicted structures for ~200 million known proteins, versus roughly 200,000 solved by human crystallographers over the prior 50 years — used as evidence of a step-change in measurement/interpretation capacity.
- His personal predictions for beliefs likely to fall within 20 years: that aging is inevitable rather than treatable, that intelligence requires a biological brain, and that Earth is the only place in the galaxy with life.
- Prescriptive framing by audience: entrepreneurs should look for fields where new instruments just arrived and ask what data no longer supports current belief; executives should audit "settled" industry assumptions and predefine what evidence would change their mind; investors should fund teams making previously unmeasurable things measurable.
- Underlying claim about human cognition: people mistake the durability of an old idea for its truth, and the actual constraint on scientific progress is psychological/institutional willingness to revise belief, not the availability of evidence.

## Quote

> You can spend the next decade defending the map, or trusting the instrument.
