# NVIDIA compute is fungible, durable and highly rentable. It is a productive, rev

## Insights

- Jensen Huang characterizes NVIDIA compute as fungible, durable, and highly rentable — framing it as a productive, revenue-generating asset rather than a depreciating cost.
- Cited data point: the H100, a three-year-old training chip, saw its rental price rise 22% month-over-month to $3.28/hour.
- This is presented as evidence against standard depreciation assumptions, which treat aging chips as strictly losing value over time.
- The implicit argument: market pricing (rising rental rates for older hardware) contradicts accounting models that assume compute value only declines with age.

## Quote

> Every depreciation schedule assumes a chip this old only loses value. The market is paying up for it instead.
