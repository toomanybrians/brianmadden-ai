# Embedded evaluation requires both company-specific and assessment-specific exper

## Insights

- Effective "embedded evaluation" (assessors working inside AI companies) requires two distinct types of expertise: deep familiarity with a specific company's systems/culture, and specialized assessment methodology that transfers across companies.
- Company-specific evaluators need a wide-ranging mandate that spans the entire model development lifecycle, rather than being confined to a single stage or checkpoint.
- Assessment specialists should be portable across organizations, but this requires strict information barriers to prevent leakage of proprietary details between the companies they evaluate.
- Evaluators need insight into the training process itself, not just the finished model, because alignment interventions can mask problems rather than genuinely resolve them.
- An intervention that suppresses a problem's symptoms can be indistinguishable from one that fixes its root cause if the evaluator only examines the final output.

## Quote

> An intervention can hide problems without fixing the cause. An evaluator who sees only the final model cannot tell the difference.
