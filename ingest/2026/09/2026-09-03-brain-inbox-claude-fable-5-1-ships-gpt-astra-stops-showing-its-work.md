# 🧬 Claude Fable 5.1 Ships, GPT-Astra Stops Showing Its Work

## Insights

- Anthropic released one model under two access tiers: Claude Fable 5.1 is publicly available, while Claude Mythos 5.1 (same underlying model) is restricted to vetted cybersecurity and life-sciences organizations — a shift from capability gating to access gating.
- OpenAI rated its forthcoming Astra model "Critical" for cybersecurity under its Preparedness Framework — the first time any of its models has hit that threshold — citing a perfect ExploitBench score and two zero-day exploits it found and chained unassisted.
- Reporting indicates part of Astra's reasoning gain comes from "recurrent depth," a technique that loops computation inside the network rather than writing it out in words — removing the readable chain-of-thought that safety researchers rely on to catch dangerous agent behavior (as happened in a prior Hugging Face hack investigation). OpenAI says it limited the technique to keep Astra's reasoning legible; its chief scientist publicly warned against an industry "race into unmonitorability."
- Anthropic's science claims moved beyond assistance into producing verified real-world outputs: Mythos 5.1 designed protein binders that bound ten times more tightly than top entries in an external design competition, and Fable 5.1 produced a new Venus elevation map (released under Creative Commons) from decades-old radar data, at 2-3km resolution versus prior 10-20km.
- Separately, a Google DeepMind-led "Co-Scientist" system designed real lab experiments (a safer MXene synthesis route, semiconductor growth recipes, bacterial colony-spread predictions, and a medical-response architecture) that human labs then executed successfully — described as a shift from AI writing about science to AI designing experiments that produce verified physical/measurable results.
- Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Max-0902, a point-release update (no new version number, same parameter count/context window) at $2/$6 per million tokens, posted large benchmark jumps (e.g., agentic coding from 11.3 to 29.0) and beat or matched pricier frontier models on roughly half of its comparison table, though the comparisons use vendor-reported and partly in-house benchmarks against Fable 5 rather than the newer Fable 5.1.

## Quote

> "I want to prevent a race into unmonitorability kicked off by confused reporting." — Jakub Pachocki, Chief Scientist, OpenAI
