# Hot take on a weak White House Accord on “Super Intelligence”

## Insights

- Marcus characterizes the White House "Superintelligence Accord" as functionally an agreement among AI companies to avoid regulation and exclude public input, dressed up as voluntary "self-policing."
- He flags skepticism about the term "independent" in the Accord, suggesting oversight may come from subcontractors chosen by and already doing business with the signing companies — undermining claims of independence.
- He notes that "pacing" commitments (apparently discussed as a safety/slowdown mechanism in prior weeks) are absent from the final Accord, implying the concept was quietly dropped.
- He names Dario (Amodei), Sam (Altman), and Elon (Musk) as having "chickened out" — i.e., not pushing for stronger commitments they'd previously signaled support for.

> Surprise, surprise: Dario, Sam, and Elon all chickened out.
