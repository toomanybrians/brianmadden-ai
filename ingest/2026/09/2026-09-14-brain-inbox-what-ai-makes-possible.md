# What AI Makes Possible

## Insights

- OpenAI frames its core narrative as democratizing "expertise" — positioning ChatGPT/Codex as substitutes for expensive or hard-to-reach professionals (doctors, financial advisors, consultants) for ordinary people.
- Cites 40M+ daily ChatGPT healthcare-related queries and anecdotal cases of people using it to decode medical jargon, prep for appointments, and advocate within time-constrained healthcare systems.
- Small business framing: Fed 2025 survey finds 31% of small employers using AI reported increased sales; examples include competitive price-checking and a two-person startup (freddy.coach) breaking even at 5,000+ users via Codex-built product.
- Education angle: OpenAI's own report claims ~70M weekly ChatGPT conversations devoted to knowledge-testing/practice, positioned as an always-available tutor.
- Accessibility use cases highlighted: visually impaired researcher using ChatGPT/Codex to summarize papers and hold spoken conversations with texts; a low-vision specialist building browser accessibility shortcuts via ChatGPT.
- Announces $60M OpenAI Foundation investment for AI-driven weather/crop-disease forecasting reaching 100M smallholder farmers across South/Southeast Asia and East Africa over three years, enabled by falling compute costs for forecasting models.
- OpenAI Academy expanded to 14 free courses (added 11) targeting workers, leaders, builders, educators, and students to build "AI know-how" alongside access.

## Quote

> AI is a tool of liberation and equity for marginal and underserved communities... AI helps level the playing field.
