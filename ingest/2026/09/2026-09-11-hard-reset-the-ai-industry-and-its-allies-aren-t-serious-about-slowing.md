# The AI Industry and Its Allies Aren’t Serious About Slowing Down

## Insights

- Recent AI industry news cluster: Nvidia's $12.9B acquisition of Hugging Face, Meta's poorly-functioning Muse agent with privacy issues, and Jensen Huang again declaring "AGI has arrived" in reference to GPT-6 Astra.
- Reporting from The Intercept indicates frontier labs (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI) are more deeply integrated into Pentagon/DOD AI development than previously disclosed, raising doubts about labs' claimed contractual limits on military use.
- The American Prospect reported Anthropic is building a monitoring system to track activists opposed to rapid AI development.
- A viral X thread from ex-OpenAI/Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon warned both companies are "racing straight to self-improving superintelligence" irresponsibly; an Anthropic researcher publicly agreed, citing >10% odds of AI-caused extinction within a decade without a solved alignment plan.
- Author argues political response is fragmented and toothless: Sanders' superintelligence ban bill has no chance of passage; most Democratic responses are vague; Van Hollen and Khanna are cited as exceptions with more substantive proposals. Altman and Amodei stayed silent on the controversy.
- Sen. Ted Cruz publicly argued the US can't slow AI development because China won't, framing competition in terms of "American killer robots" vs. Chinese ones — used as an example of "China is bad" being used to justify no regulation or bilateral safety dialogue.
- Author's own take: AI industry leaders and doomsayers alike describe stakes through convenient abstractions (odds of extinction, GDP scenarios) that let them treat consequences as theoretical rather than acting responsibly; skeptical that whistleblowers like Coxon have enough insider tenure/evidence to be fully trusted.

## Quote

> They are racing straight to self-improving superintelligence and gambling with our lives.
