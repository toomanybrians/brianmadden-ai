# The secret behind Meta’s Muse

## Insights

- Meta previously attempted a personal-assistant agent, Facebook M, launched in 2015; it was never scaled beyond ~10,000 users due to high operating costs.
- Facebook M reportedly relied on hidden human operators behind the scenes, echoing the "Mechanical Turk" pattern of concealed human labor behind an apparent AI system.
- Despite internal assurances of AI capability, the project was quietly canceled in January 2018, with no more than 30% of requests ever handled by AI.
- Meta's new agent, Muse, is described as attempting essentially the same tasks as the earlier failed effort — restaurant reservations, theater tickets, etc.
- The author frames this as a recurring pattern of Zuckerberg overestimating AI capabilities and repeating past mistakes, including apparent tone-deafness on privacy.
