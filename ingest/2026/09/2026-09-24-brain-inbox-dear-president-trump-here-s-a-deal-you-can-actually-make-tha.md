# Dear President Trump, here’s a deal you can actually make that will make you look great

## Insights

- Marcus frames the Trump-Xi meeting as an opportunity for a collaboration-focused AI deal rather than a slowdown/arms-control deal, arguing collaboration is more politically palatable to Trump.
- He draws an analogy to Cold War US-Soviet cooperation on space and smallpox eradication, proposing cancer research and cybersecurity as the modern equivalents for US-China AI collaboration.
- Marcus argues a coordinated AI slowdown, if pursued now while the US is ahead, could actually lock in America's competitive lead — and warns that skipping a slowdown risks serious negative consequences that would be politically attributed to Trump.
- He cites the People's Daily (CCP's official newspaper) publicly calling for the US and China to make AI "a new frontier for cooperation" via an intergovernmental AI dialogue mechanism, as evidence China is open to collaboration despite assumptions otherwise.
- Marcus suggests such a deal could position Trump for a Nobel Prize, noting Sam Altman reportedly shares this view.

## Quote

> China is practically begging to collaborate.
