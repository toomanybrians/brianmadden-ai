# We believe foundational biology tools should be accessible to everyone.

AlphaGe

## Insights

- Google DeepMind is releasing AlphaGenome Atlas resources publicly, framing foundational biology tools as something that should be broadly accessible rather than gated.
- Access is being distributed across multiple channels: a dedicated Atlas website, an API, a skill integration within Google Antigravity, and (soon) Google Cloud.
- The Atlas includes an "AlphaGenome Variant Impact (AVI) score" that combines AlphaGenome, AlphaMissense, and other features into a single ranking system.
- The AVI score serves two functions: ranking genetic mutations by severity (low to high impact) and explaining the mechanism of damage, such as disrupted gene switches or RNA splicing instructions.
- This represents an example of AI-driven tooling being packaged for distribution through developer/cloud infrastructure and agentic platforms, rather than kept as an internal research artifact.

## Quote

> We believe foundational biology tools should be accessible to everyone.
