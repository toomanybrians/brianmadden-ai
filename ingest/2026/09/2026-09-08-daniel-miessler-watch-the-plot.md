# Watch the Plot

## Insights

- Proposes that AI's value should be judged by measurable effects on human/societal wellbeing, not by the novelty or coolness of the technology itself.
- Lists specific societal health metrics to track AI's actual impact: housing, education, poverty, disease, oppression, and freedom of expression.
- Raises concern that impressive AI capabilities are meaningless as a societal win if benefits accrue only to a small group while most people continue to struggle.
- Frames the core open question as: is AI actually helping yet, and if not, why not and on what timeline would that change — with a call to define concrete metrics for answering this.

## Quote

> But not if only a few people get them while everyone else struggles.
