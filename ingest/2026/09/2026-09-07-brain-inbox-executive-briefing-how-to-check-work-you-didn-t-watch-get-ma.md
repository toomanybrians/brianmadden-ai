# Executive Briefing: How to Check Work You Didn't Watch Get Made

## Insights

- Frames GPT-6 Astra's release as a threshold moment: computing shifts from waiting for instructions to being "self-directed" — a brief, imprecise goal can now run for days unsupervised and return finished work embedding hundreds of unseen decisions.
- Argues this hollows out the middle of a knowledge worker's job: only the upfront specification and the final judgment call remain as human-owned work; everything in between is delegated to the agent.
- Extends the same "hollowing of the middle" to organizations — the middle management layer that existed to catch and resolve loose ends becomes unnecessary, which the piece claims lets small firms operate with capabilities previously requiring larger staffs.
- Raises a legal/liability claim: agency law (governing people who act on someone's behalf) now applies to AI agents, making principals liable for commitments their agents make autonomously.
- Frames reliability as the core economic variable going forward — the gap between 98% and 100% trustworthy agent output is described as where enterprise value concentrates, since reliability determines what tasks/authority people are willing to hand over.

## Quote

> Which leaves you answerable for decisions you never made.
