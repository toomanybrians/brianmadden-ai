# Amazon blocked Meta’s Muse AI Agent to protect $76B in ad revenue ✋🤖; Revolut confirms dual IPO on Nasdaq and LSE, and it’s London’s best available deal 🇺🇸🇬🇧

## Insights

- Amazon blocked Meta's Muse AI shopping agent from its retail site two weeks after launch, citing lack of authorization, failure to self-identify, and risk of credential mishandling — but the underlying driver is Amazon's $76.1B trailing annual ad revenue (~10% of total revenue, high-margin), which depends on shoppers browsing search results and sponsored listings that an AI agent bypasses entirely.
- Shopify took the opposite approach the same week, opening its catalog and Shop Pay checkout to Meta's agent via a connector program launched Sept 18, positioning itself as the default checkout layer regardless of which AI agent originates the purchase.
- Shopify's AI-driven traffic grew 8x YoY and AI-powered search orders grew ~13x in Q1 2026, with Shop Pay processing $35B that quarter — evidence of a fast-growing agentic commerce channel.
- The split illustrates two strategic bets: Amazon optimizing for control of demand (protecting the search/ad funnel), Shopify optimizing for ubiquity of supply (becoming the checkout layer agents default to) — with the risk that Amazon's block pushes users toward Shopify/Walmart instead of protecting its ad business.
- Key signals to watch: whether Amazon builds a controlled API for agents with identity/ad requirements attached, whether OpenAI or Google ship competing personal shopping agents (making blanket blocking impractical), and how fast agentic checkout volume scales through Shopify's payment rails.

## Quote

> Amazon is optimizing for control of demand, while Shopify is optimizing for ubiquity of supply.
