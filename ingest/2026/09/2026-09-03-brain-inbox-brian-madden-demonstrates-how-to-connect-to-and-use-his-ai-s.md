# Brian Madden demonstrates how to connect to and use his AI second brain

## Insights

- Canon isn't designed upfront but "measured into existence" — recurring gaps surfaced through logging and classifying every question an AI system gets asked (fully answered, partially answered, retrieval miss, genuine gap), with promotion to canon requiring deliberate human decision, never automatic AI writing.
- Proposed governance structure treats a canonical knowledge layer like source code: role-based access (input owners, output owners, domain SMEs, reviewers), with a strict rule that raw inputs and outputs never touch directly and nobody hand-edits canon — errors get fixed at the ingestion process, not patched in the file.
- AI agent identity should use restricted service accounts (existing IdP technology), not a worker's own credentials; the stated bottleneck is IT's inability to provision these at scale, not missing technology. The real governance unit is argued to be shared files/channels multiple agents touch, not the agent's identity alone.
- Shadow AI is characterized as skewing top-heavy (90%+ of execs, ~80% of middle managers vs. ~40% of ICs using AI outside sanctioned policy), reframing shadow-AI governance as an executive problem rather than a worker-discipline one.
- Human-in-the-loop oversight is challenged with cited data: humans blocked a dangerous agent command only 13.6% of the time vs. 89% for automated policy checks, and a three-day agent stoppage went unnoticed on a monitored dashboard — suggesting confirmation prompts function as a gap, not a control.
- Local/on-device AI (Wave 3) is framed as re-raising identical governance questions (identity, trust, patching, access) at the endpoint rather than requiring new governance philosophy, with an open concern about single-vendor dependency if a model gets banned or degraded overnight.
- For non-technical workers, the proposed minimum baseline for AI benefit is not technical skill but noticing recurring task-annoyance as a signal, chat-level comfort, and willingness to be a knowledge source on one's own terms (voice memo, email) — with onboarding design treated as an organizational responsibility, not a worker skill gap.

## Quote

> The confirmation prompt in front of a human isn't the control, it's the gap.
