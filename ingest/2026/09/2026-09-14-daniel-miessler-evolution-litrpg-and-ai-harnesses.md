# Evolution, LitRPG, and AI Harnesses

## Insights

- Miessler frames evolution, LitRPG game systems, and personal AI "harnesses" as structurally identical: all are multi-axis upgrade infrastructures that inject goals, reward progress ("dings"), and drive continuous escalation.
- He argues AI harnesses function as an externalized goal-pursuit system: paired with a "TELOS" (a personal articulation of goals, obstacles, and direction), the harness acts as a 24/7 agent advocating for and advancing an individual's defined objectives.
- Describes a working example — "Socrates," built inside his open-source "LifeOS/Pulse" harness — as a running checklist of personal-optimization questions (finances, health, relationships, news) that agents continuously monitor and act on.
- Traces his own intellectual lineage on this idea: 2006 writing on "semi-intelligent" digital assistants, a 2016 book (*The Real Internet of Things*) predicting AI agents that know and advocate for users, and TELOS work starting in 2022, culminating in harness-building in 2025.
- Introduces "Human 3.0" as a successor to an education/work system (Human 2.0) built to produce compliant workers rather than self-directed individuals; argues AI's core promise is giving more people (not just the privileged) the freedom/time to identify and pursue their own authentic goals instead of inherited ones.
- Cautions against over-reliance on the harness — stresses that periodic disconnection (offline retreats, physical reading/writing) matters, and that humans, not systems, remain the central focus, even while operating within evolutionary/biological constraints.

> We are the mech suits for evolution... Evolution is on the inside, pressing pedals and pulling levers.
