# Single Digit Thousand Dollar AI SDR

## Insights

- Vercel reduced its inbound sales development team from 10 people to 1.25, with AI automating 90% of inbound sales development work.
- A separately built support agent at Vercel handles 93% of all support cases.
- Vercel's COO stated the total annual infrastructure cost for both the sales and support AI agents combined is in the single-digit thousands of dollars.
- The sales development agent reportedly delivers a 32x ROI, framed by Vercel's COO as making cost largely irrelevant given the return.
- The piece frames this as evidence that early AI sales-automation promises (from "early days" hype) have now actually materialized, drawing a comparison to self-driving cars taking longer than promised to deliver but eventually doing so.
- An analogy is drawn to Waymo's reported 92% reduction in pedestrian injury crashes as a coincidentally similar performance figure to the AI automation rates cited.
