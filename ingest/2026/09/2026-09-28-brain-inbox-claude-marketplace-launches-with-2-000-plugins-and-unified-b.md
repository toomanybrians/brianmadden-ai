# Claude Marketplace launches with 2,000+ plugins and unified billing

## Insights

- Anthropic launched Claude Marketplace, an app-store-like platform for connecting Claude to enterprise tools, with over 2,000 connectors/plugins available at launch (including Google Drive, Slack, Notion, Salesforce, Microsoft 365).
- The marketplace lets organizations buy Claude-powered agents from third parties (e.g., Cursor, CrowdStrike, Snowflake) using existing Anthropic budget, unifying billing rather than requiring separate vendor contracts.
- It also enables hiring consulting partners (Accenture, Deloitte) directly through the platform for enterprise-scale Claude rollouts, and lets third-party developers list their own tools/agents to reach existing Claude users.
- Connectors run on MCP (Model Context Protocol), described as an open standard letting Claude interface with external tools and data sources.
- The newsletter frames this alongside other releases (LLM "personality fingerprinting" research, NVIDIA's speaker-diarization model, OpenAI's mental health benchmark) as evidence of a broader shift from generic AI models toward specialized, identifiable, ecosystem-locked AI products.
- OpenAI's new MentalHealthBench (built with 80 clinicians across 22 countries) found top models scoring only ~52-57% on realistic mental-health conversation handling, indicating current AI is still far from reliable in this domain.
