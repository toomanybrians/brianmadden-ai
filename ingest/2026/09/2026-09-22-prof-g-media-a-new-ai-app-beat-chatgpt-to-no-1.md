# A New AI App Beat ChatGPT to No. 1

## Insights

- Meta released "Muse," an AI agent app designed to handle everyday tasks (dinner reservations, doctor appointments, plane tickets, pet food), which hit No. 1 in the Apple App Store, ahead of ChatGPT (No. 2), Gemini (No. 8), and Claude (No. 13).
- Meta's monetization plan combines a small transaction-cut model with deeper user data collection intended to improve ad targeting/pricing, unlike subscription-first competitors.
- Meta's scale advantage is structural: roughly 9 of 10 internet users outside China touch a Meta product monthly, giving it unusual training data and a built-in distribution base for new AI products.
- Since Muse's September 8 unveiling, Meta added nearly $170B in market cap and outperformed the S&P 500 (+9% vs. -2%).
- A head-to-head "dinner test" (booking a same-day restaurant reservation) showed Muse completing the task end-to-end in under a minute, while ChatGPT and Claude failed to complete bookings and Gemini succeeded only after repeated confirmation loops.
- Meta's public AI positioning diverges from OpenAI/Anthropic: Zuckerberg avoids existential-risk framing and has pushed cheaper, open-source models, coinciding with broader price competition already reducing enterprise AI spend among top adopters (per Ramp data).
