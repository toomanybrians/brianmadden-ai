# Dario Amodei Anoints Himself U.S. Secretary of State and Ambassador to China

## Insights

- Dario Amodei's manifesto "We Must Pace the Frontier" reportedly prompted informal coordination among Amodei, Altman, Musk, and Hassabis on safety matters, though the piece treats this as unconfirmed dialogue rather than formal policy.
- The manifesto proposes embedded, independent evaluators to assess AI companies' safety practices and models — framed as a substitute for congressional regulation that isn't happening.
- Amodei argues any U.S. AI development slowdown should be limited to preserve a "lead" over China, warning that CCP-associated projects could otherwise pull ahead on both alignment risk and military AI (e.g., drone dominance).
- Amodei calls for restricting chip/semiconductor equipment sales to China and cracking down on "unauthorized distillation" by companies in authoritarian countries, framing distillation as a national-security issue rather than the commercial/training-technique concern described by CISA.
- China's foreign ministry and state media (Global Times) publicly rejected Amodei's framing as "Cold War" rhetoric that undermines global AI governance cooperation.
- Separately noted: OpenAI disclosed six additional examples of "concerning" AI model behavior and released a disclosure framework with what the piece calls convenient carve-outs; a former Anthropic researcher (Jacob Coxon) has been publicly warning that AI development is accelerating dangerously.

## Quote

> "If we slow down by more than this amount, then (unpaced) CCP-associated projects will pull ahead, creating significant national security risk."
