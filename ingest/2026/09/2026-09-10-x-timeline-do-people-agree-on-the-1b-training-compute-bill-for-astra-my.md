# Do people agree on the $1B training compute bill for Astra?  (My guess was a bit

## Insights

- A claimed frontier model ("Astra") was trained on ~100,000 Blackwell GPUs, with an estimated training compute bill of about $1 billion and ~200 MW of power draw.
- Commentary questions whether that $1B figure is accurate, with at least one observer guessing the real cost was higher.
- A counter-point argues that "scaling holds" claims are hard to verify because labs no longer disclose clean data on base models — each new release bundles new base models with undisclosed harnesses, augmented data, and other opaque additions.
- This opacity is framed as making it difficult to assess whether performance gains reflect actual scaling laws or other undisclosed engineering interventions.
- The thread raises anticipation about the impact of the first gigawatt-scale training cluster, suggesting current infrastructure (compute + energy) hasn't yet shown its full capability.
