# RT @theojaffee: One of the worst takes you can have is "AGI is already here". No

## Insights

- Argues that claiming "AGI is already here" is factually wrong, defining AGI specifically as models capable of replacing humans across all economically valuable tasks — a threshold current systems don't meet.
- Warns that prematurely declaring AGI already achieved has a rhetorical side effect: it makes people wrongly believe that genuine post-AGI predictions have already failed to materialize, undermining future claims when real capability jumps occur.
- Frames "AGI is here" as one of the most damaging takes in AI discourse, not just inaccurate but actively confusing calibration around what AGI would actually look like.

## Quote

> One of the worst takes you can have is "AGI is already here". — @theojaffee
