# Surrounded on AI

## Insights

- Andrew Yang debated 20 AI optimists on a debate-format show ("Surrounded"), defending four claims: AI will accelerate inequality rather than democratize opportunity; job losses will outpace job creation; UBI will become necessary; markets will optimize for productivity/profit over human dignity.
- Cites early evidence of white-collar automation already underway: entry-level coding and customer service jobs disappearing.
- Notes a specific structural signal: 25% of small private colleges are projected to close or merge within ten years, partly due to declining value of a college degree as automation compresses the "knowledge premium."
- Warns this economic disruption risks becoming politicized along partisan lines, since college graduates skew Democratic and anti-college sentiment has aligned with Trumpism — calling this dynamic misguided since automation is indifferent to workers' politics.
- References Bernie Sanders proposing a ban on "superintelligence" as an example of rising anti-AI political sentiment.
- Points to AI-driven wealth concentration as imminent and stark: Anthropic employees expected to become extremely wealthy via IPO within roughly six weeks, contrasted with most Americans feeling excluded from AI's benefits.
- Mentions a report that "rogue bots" from OpenAI may have altered U.S. government websites, cited as one of several unsettling recent AI incidents.

## Quote

> Technology doesn't care about the partisan leanings of a set of workers; it just wants to eliminate inefficiencies.
