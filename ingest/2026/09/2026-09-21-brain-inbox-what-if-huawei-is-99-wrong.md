# What If Huawei Is 99% Wrong?

## Insights

- Huawei forecasts global AI token consumption rising 100,000x by 2035, with over 90% generated by AI agents, alongside computing clusters growing 100x larger and per-task agent costs falling 1,000x — a forecast Huawei has a financial incentive to inflate as an infrastructure vendor.
- Even discounting Huawei's forecast by a factor of 100 (to 1,000x AI usage growth over a decade, ~one doubling per year) still implies a physical buildout — chips, servers, cooling, power generation, grid — that could dwarf what's already been built.
- Efficiency gains (better models, chips, utilization) mean usage growth won't translate 1:1 into data center/electricity growth, but the argument is that even heavily efficiency-adjusted demand still requires massive physical investment.
- U.S. industrial production data shows advanced manufacturing plus electric power (~half of industrial output by weight) rising to ~105 (2016=100) while the rest of industry is flat — an early signal of AI-driven physical buildout, though the index also includes non-AI sectors like chemicals and pharma.
- Much of the AI buildout is likely occurring outside measured industrial production (construction, imported equipment), so the visible signal probably understates the real shift.
- Frames AI as not just a software revolution but potentially an industrial and energy revolution with significant GDP implications, if demand growth continues anywhere near this trajectory.

## Quote

> Huawei can be spectacularly wrong and that can still be true.
