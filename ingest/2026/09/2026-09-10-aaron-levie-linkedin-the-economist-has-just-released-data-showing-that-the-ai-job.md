# The Economist has just released data showing that the AI jobs prediction so far

## Insights

- New Economist data reportedly shows AI's effect on jobs running counter to common predictions.
- Levie attributes this to AI automating into areas of "non-finite demand" — meaning increased capability expands total work available rather than simply replacing fixed-quantity tasks.
- Agents still require human operators and oversight to produce value, which limits pure displacement effects.
- Jobs seen as most exposed to AI risk — engineers, lawyers — have not been broadly replaced so far.
- AI is credited with creating or expanding entirely new job categories: cybersecurity, forward-deployed engineers (FDEs), agent operators, and engineering roles in non-software domains.
- Levie frames this as an open-ended trend, suggesting new areas of AI-driven demand keep emerging with no clear sign of reversal.

## Quote

> AI is also creating or growing entire categories of jobs across the economy.
