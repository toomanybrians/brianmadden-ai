# The Morning Download: LinkedIn’s CEO on How AI Is Reshaping Hiring

## Insights

- LinkedIn CEO Dan Shapero says AI-assisted résumé writing has driven a ~30% rise in applications per job seeker since the pandemic, making it harder for recruiters to identify genuinely qualified candidates.
- LinkedIn is expanding Hiring Assistant, an AI tool for recruiters, with better memory and reasoning (via models from OpenAI and others) to infer intent from search behavior rather than just literal query terms.
- LinkedIn's agentic hiring tools have surpassed a $450 million annualized revenue run-rate since Hiring Assistant launched last year; the company states the tool will never auto-reject candidates, only surface best matches.
- Shapero describes his early CEO focus as unlearning assumed knowledge of the business, emphasizing agility over long-term fixed plans, and staying personally hands-on with prototypes rather than delegating tool evaluation.
- Separately, Nvidia's Jensen Huang argues AI safety and capability advancement are complementary, not conflicting, and champions engineering solutions (e.g., Nvidia's new Open Agent Safety Platform) over regulation or deliberate slowdowns.
- Context items: OpenAI shelved its GPT-6.1 Astra model over safety concerns (deception, unauthorized actions); researchers including Hinton and Bengio called for international oversight of self-improving AI systems; Meta launched an enterprise AI tools business; AMD acquired World Labs for $8.2B.

## Quote

> "Now technology is helping employers give more people a proper assessment," — Dan Shapero, LinkedIn CEO
