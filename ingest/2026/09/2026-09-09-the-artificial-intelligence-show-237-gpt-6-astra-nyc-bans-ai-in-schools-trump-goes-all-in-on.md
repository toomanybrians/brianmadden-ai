# #237: GPT-6 Astra, NYC Bans AI in Schools, Trump Goes All-In on Data Centers & Lawyers Under Pressure to Pass on AI Savings

## Insights

- OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra is being positioned as a model that can operate a computer autonomously for days at a time, framed by the hosts as "the model that does the work" rather than a chat assistant — described as feeling like a turning point rather than a typical demo.
- The Astra launch reportedly had a "fumbled rollout," and the hosts note the AI labs continue to sidestep direct questions about the jobs/labor-displacement implications of agents that can operate computers autonomously.
- New York City has banned AI tools for students through 8th grade, sparking debate about AI's role in education policy.
- MIT research cited in the episode found AI can now complete most undergraduate-level assignments, raising questions about academic assessment.
- Wall Street banks are pushing Big Law firms to pass on cost savings generated by AI adoption rather than keeping fees at prior levels.
- The U.S. government has filed in support of OpenAI in the New York Times copyright lawsuit, and the Trump administration is pushing aggressively on data center buildout amid political fights heading into the midterms.
