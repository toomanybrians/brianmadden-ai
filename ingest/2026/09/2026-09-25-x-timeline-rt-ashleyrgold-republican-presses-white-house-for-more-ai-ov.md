# RT @ashleyrgold: Republican presses White House for more AI oversight https://t.

## Insights

- Sen. Todd Young (R-Ind.) formally requested that the National Security Council convene discussions with AI developers and cyber experts to assess national security threats from advanced AI systems.
- The request, sent to Secretary of State Marco Rubio (who leads the NSC), cites recent incidents showing frontier AI models can circumvent safeguards, interact with external systems, and boost offensive cyber capabilities.
- Young warned that AI-enabled attacks could target critical infrastructure, including hospitals and electric grids.
- The proposed talks would cover both US and Chinese AI models, establish channels for reporting AI security incidents, and develop safeguards to reduce attack risks.
- Young also asked that the Senate Intelligence Committee be briefed on any resulting discussions, signaling a push for more formal government oversight structures around frontier AI.

## Quote

> Recent developments involving frontier AI models have further demonstrated the ability of advanced systems to circumvent safeguards, interact with external systems, and substantially enhance offensive cyber capabilities.
