# GPT-6 Astra, OpenAI's latest breakthrough model, is out. And it's a big deal. We

## Insights

- Box tested GPT-6 Astra in early preview against its enterprise "complex work" eval, which uses real-world, multi-hour, domain-expert-level tasks across industries — and it scored highest of any model tested so far (77% vs. 74% for GPT-5.6 Sol).
- Gains were uneven across domains, with the largest jumps in media/entertainment (+52 points) and technology (+28 points), suggesting the improvement isn't a flat capability bump but concentrated in specific reasoning/data-handling patterns.
- A recurring theme in the examples: Astra was better at flagging ambiguity, gaps, or proxy data rather than confidently reporting wrong or incomplete answers as fact (e.g., labeling a proxy metric as a proxy, catching a mismatched growth claim).
- In legal review, both models reached the same correct verdict, but only Astra could justify it by citing the specific policy provision — Levie frames unsupported reasoning as a "critical error" in legal contexts, implying correctness alone isn't sufficient for enterprise trust.
- In healthcare, Astra caught an error a prior model missed twice and was described as more reliable at severity grading, not just error detection — a distinction between spotting problems and correctly weighting their importance.
- Levie positions this as materially upgrading what's possible in agent-orchestrated enterprise workflows, with Astra being rolled into Box AI Studio for customer-built agents.
