# The Morning Download: Trust in Leadership Proves Latest Challenge for Corporate AI Ambitions

## Insights

- Andus Labs (founded by Chris Perry) surveyed ~600 organizations and found 7 of the top 10 reasons AI transformations fail are leadership-related; the primary barrier shifted from "trust in models" last quarter to "trust in leadership" this quarter — described as an abrupt change.
- A gap exists between top-down AI messaging (town halls, "AI-first" mandates) and what employees actually experience in terms of training, investment, and momentum at the point of work.
- Corporate messaging tends to push generic "AI proficiency"/efficiency narratives rather than helping employees reimagine specific workflows, which fuels job-loss fears and causes workers to ignore or reject AI tools.
- Efficiency/productivity messaging aimed at boosting stock price is identified as directly creating a trust deficit with employees.
- Proposed fix: leaders need a "translation layer" that answers individual-level questions — what AI means for my role, what I need to do, what I have access to, how I'm held accountable, and whether I have a place in the AI-transformed future.
- Executives at a WSJ Leadership Institute summit echoed this: leaders often underweight the human/change-management side in favor of tech/ROI focus, and technology leaders are increasingly expected to act as "AI educators" partnering with business units on workflow redesign.

## Quote

> "Last quarter, the biggest barrier was trust in models. This quarter it's trust in leadership." — Chris Perry, Andus Labs
