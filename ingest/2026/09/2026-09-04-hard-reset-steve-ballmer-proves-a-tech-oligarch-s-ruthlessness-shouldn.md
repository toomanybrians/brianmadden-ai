# Steve Ballmer Proves a Tech Oligarch’s Ruthlessness Shouldn’t Be Mistaken for Smarts

## Insights

- Uber cut roughly 10% of its workforce (about 3,300 people) citing a need to "remove layers" of management, echoing similar restructuring language recently used by Xbox in its own layoffs.
- Uber simultaneously shut down operations entirely in Nigeria and Uganda, offering drivers a vague "goodwill payment" with no clear terms disclosed.
- Sam Altman told Time he wants "a personal robot" to handle tasks he doesn't want to do, framing it as a near-universal future consumer good ("I think everyone should have a personal robot").
- Altman separately criticized AI-camera glasses (implicitly Meta's) as socially uncomfortable to interact with, distancing OpenAI's framing from that form factor.
- A federal judge ruled Google's ad-tech business is an illegal monopoly but declined to force a breakup, requiring only unspecified changes — mirroring an earlier ruling on Google's search monopoly that also stopped short of structural remedies.
- Anthropic co-founder Tom Brown publicly praised the Trump administration's framing of data centers as "an enormous source of prosperity," reflecting AI firms' political positioning around infrastructure buildout.

## Quote

> "I think everyone should have a personal robot ... I would love to have a personal robot that could do the tasks I don't want to do." — Sam Altman
