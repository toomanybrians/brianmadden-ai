# What’s Your Moonshot?

## Insights

- Frames AGI/ASI as "amplifiers" — technologies that multiply whatever intent is pointed at them, giving individuals capabilities (research, design, build, deploy at scale) that previously required organizations of thousands.
- Cites Sam Altman's reaction to OpenAI's 10,000-agent swarm producing a result on a two-century-old Millennium Prize math problem as evidence the AGI-to-ASI transition is moving faster than expected.
- Argues that without a clear personal or organizational goal, powerful AI tools just "multiply noise," while a well-defined target produces outsized leverage.
- Claims that having an intrinsically motivating goal for AI use functions as an antidote to AI-related anxiety — those with clear building intentions report less dread than those without.
- Draws on 30 years of running XPRIZE competitions to assert that teams far more often regret aiming too low than too high when设定 ambitious goals.
- Describes two concrete XPRIZE structures as examples of goal-directed AI/creative work: a filmmaking prize for hopeful future-vision content, and a 90-day hackathon for building profitable AI businesses judged by investors/entrepreneurs.

## Quote

> AGI multiplies whatever you point it at. Aim it at nothing and you get multiplied noise. Aim it at your Moonshot and you change the world!
