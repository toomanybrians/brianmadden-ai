# always the CEOs, very rarely independent scientists.

and that, my friends, is h

## Insights

- Marcus observes that attendees at high-profile AI policy meetings (citing a Trump/Johnson AI meeting) are consistently company CEOs rather than independent scientists or researchers.
- The named attendees are all leaders of major AI companies: Dario Amodei (Anthropic), Mark Zuckerberg (Meta), Sundar Pichai (Google), Jensen Huang (Nvidia), and Greg Brockman (OpenAI), with Sam Altman absent due to a competing OpenAI event and Elon Musk making a separate appearance.
- Marcus frames this pattern — industry executives, not independent scientific voices, shaping government AI policy discussions — as an example of regulatory capture.
