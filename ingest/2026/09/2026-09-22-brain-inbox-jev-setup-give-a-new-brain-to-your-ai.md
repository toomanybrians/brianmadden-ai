# Jev Setup: Give a New Brain to Your AI

## Insights

- A new model called Jev, from a ChatGPT co-founder, is positioned not as a content-generating LLM but as a lightweight decision-making layer that sits alongside models like Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini.
- The framing splits AI workflow labor into three tiers: large models handle open-ended creation (writing, coding, research), Jev handles small binary/multi-choice decisions (urgency, tool selection, source relevance, task completion), and traditional code executes actions.
- The argument is that many AI workflows currently waste expensive frontier-model calls on decisions that ultimately resolve to simple yes/no/A/B/C outputs — a mismatch Jev is meant to fix.
- Jev is described as extremely fast and cheap (cited at $0.042 per million tokens) compared to frontier models, making it suited to high-volume micro-decision tasks like sorting emails, leads, or research inputs.
- The piece mentions Jev has three "decision modes" and supports confidence routing, model selection, and context filtering within agent workflows, though implementation specifics sit behind a paywalled guide.
- A caveat is flagged but not detailed: there's "one limitation behind the 'no hallucinations' hype" that matters before automating with Jev.

## Quote

> Claude writes. Jev decides. Code does.
