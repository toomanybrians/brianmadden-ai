# ⚙️ Granola for face-to-face conversations

## Insights

- Granola launched an Apple Watch app that lets users record in-person conversations (whiteboard sessions, walking 1:1s, coffee chats, customer/investor meetings) via a tap on the wrist, without opening a laptop.
- The interface is deliberately transparent: the watch screen turns green and plays a sound when recording, so all participants can see a note is active — positioned as a control/consent mechanism rather than covert capture.
- Internal team testing reportedly led some employees to stop bringing laptops into meetings altogether, with Granola handling note-taking in the background instead.
- Notes sync automatically across Apple Watch, iPhone, and Mac/Windows, and can feed into other AI tools via "Granola MCP" or flow into CRM/other business tools through integrations.
- The framing treats the smartwatch (a device people already own) as the delivery vector for ambient AI assistance, rather than requiring a new dedicated wearable.

## Quote

> Tap your wrist to start taking notes. The screen turns bright Granola green and plays a sound, so you know it's working.
