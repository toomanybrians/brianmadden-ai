# Anthropic has just released Fable 5.1, it's latest state of the art AI model for

## Insights

- Box tested Anthropic's new Fable 5.1 model against its internal "complex enterprise work eval," reporting a 7 percentage point overall improvement over Fable 5 on unstructured data tasks.
- Testing used the Box Agent paired with Fable 5.1 across real-world enterprise document scenarios in financial services, life sciences, and public sector domains, emphasizing analytical, mathematical, logical, and domain-specific reasoning.
- Cited improvements by sector: +17% in financial services (correctly sequencing capital allowances before tax calculations), +37% in technology (recognizing metric ambiguity and computing/presenting the correct normalization), +16% in public sector (correctly executing weighted-mean methodology for rankings).
- Failure patterns attributed to the prior model (Fable 5) include mis-ordering calculations, committing to a single wrong interpretation under ambiguity, and miscategorization errors that cascade through subsequent analysis.
- Framed as a meaningful capability jump specifically for "long running agentic workflows" in enterprise settings.
- Fable 5.1 is slated for availability in Box AI Studio for building custom enterprise AI agents.
