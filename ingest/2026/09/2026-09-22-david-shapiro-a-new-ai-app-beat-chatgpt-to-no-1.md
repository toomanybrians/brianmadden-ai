# A New AI App Beat ChatGPT to No. 1

## Insights

- Meta's new AI agent app, Muse, hit No. 1 in Apple's App Store, surpassing ChatGPT (No. 2), Gemini (No. 8), and Claude (No. 13), and Meta gained ~$170B in market cap (up 9% vs. S&P's 2% decline) since its Sept 8 unveiling.
- Muse is designed for real-world transactional tasks (dinner reservations, doctor appointments, flights, pet food); Zuckerberg plans to eventually take a cut of transactions completed through the app.
- Meta's competitive edge: 9 of 10 non-China internet users touch a Meta product monthly, giving it outsized training data and an existing user base advantage over frontier labs at launch.
- Meta diverges strategically from OpenAI/Anthropic by avoiding AI-risk "doomerism" rhetoric and instead pursuing cheaper, open-source models.
- A hands-on "dinner test" comparing four agents on an actual reservation task found Claude and ChatGPT both failed to complete the booking (stopped short, citing inability to finalize), Gemini passed but required repeated confirmations, and Muse completed the task in under a minute — described as the clear winner.
- Broader signal: price competition among AI agents is already reportedly reducing enterprise AI spending among top adopters, per cited Ramp data.
