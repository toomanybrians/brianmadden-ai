# Claude just got cheaper

## Insights

- Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5, claiming performance comparable to Claude Fable 5.1 on most work while costing 40% less to run than Opus 5 at default settings; pricing is $4/million input tokens and $20/million output tokens, with output generation over 30% faster than Opus 5.
- OpenAI released GPT-6 Sol and Luna models priced at half the API cost of comparable 5.6 models, indicating broader downward pressure on frontier model pricing across the industry.
- Snorkel AI raised a $350M Series E at a $3.5B valuation (nearly triple its prior valuation), with annualized revenue run rate reaching $375M — evidence that demand for curated training data, simulated environments, and domain expertise persists even as models improve at generating their own data.
- Apple is pitching upgraded Mac Mini and Mac Studio hardware (some configs near $20,000) as cheaper than continuous cloud rental for certain AI workloads, demonstrating four Mac Studios running a trillion-parameter model together — framing local AI as an economic choice for predictable enterprise workloads, not just a privacy play.
- AI infrastructure company Nscale disclosed over $103 billion in contracts ahead of its IPO, with about 85% of that value concentrated in agreements with just two partners (Microsoft and Anthropic), highlighting how tightly interlinked AI labs, cloud providers, chip makers, and data centers have become.
- Baselayer raised $35M to help financial institutions verify businesses and assess fraud risk, reflecting growing difficulty in establishing trust as automated/AI systems proliferate.
