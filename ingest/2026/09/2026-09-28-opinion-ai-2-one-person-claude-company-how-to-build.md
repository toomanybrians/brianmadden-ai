# One-Person Claude Company: How to build

## Insights

- Claude has moved from a single-turn chat tool to a system that connects directly to email, calendar, Drive, CRM, accounting tools, Shopify, Stripe, and Slack, enabling it to act across a business's actual operational surface.
- Anthropic's "Small Business" package bundles 43 pre-built workflows and 27 integrations spanning sales, marketing, finance, and operations.
- Claude can now run scheduled, recurring tasks (daily, weekly, monthly) remotely on Anthropic's servers, without requiring the user's laptop to stay open.
- This shifts the operative question for solo operators from "what can I ask Claude to do" to "what should already be done by the time I sit down to work" — i.e., pre-emptive automation rather than reactive prompting.
- The proposed model is a "one-person company" where a human still makes key decisions but offloads the recurring, low-judgment operational load (email triage, follow-ups, reporting, content prep) to Claude.
- The piece frames this as distinct from multi-agent "AI employee" gimmicks — it's oriented around a single configured Project with connectors, reusable Skills, and scheduled automations rather than simulated staff.

## Quote

> Do not build agents yet. Give Claude a business first.
