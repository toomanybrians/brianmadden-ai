# so it was the harness not the model.

more sneaky games with unacknowledged doma

## Insights

- OpenAI reported a 99.9% score on a benchmark, but the benchmark's own software (ARC Prize) returned 62.7% for the same model and test — the discrepancy traced to differences in the surrounding "harness"/scaffolding, not the underlying model itself.
- The claim is that unacknowledged domain-specific, neurosymbolic components built into the harness are doing much of the actual problem-solving work, while the benchmark result gets attributed to the model alone.
- ARC Prize published both figures and explicitly stated it is not claiming this constitutes AGI, treating the gap as evidence of the harness's influence rather than of the model's raw capability.
- The framing casts this as a repeated pattern ("more sneaky games") of benchmark results being inflated or obscured by hidden engineering around the model rather than genuine gains in general model capability.
