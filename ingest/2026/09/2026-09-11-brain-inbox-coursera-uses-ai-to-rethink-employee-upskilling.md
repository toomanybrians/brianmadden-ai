# ⚙️ Coursera uses AI to rethink employee upskilling

## Insights

- Coursera launched Project Helix, an AI-native corporate learning platform announced at its FWD 2026 event, aiming to replace generic training content with adaptive, personalized learning paths and "portable proof of skills."
- Both organizational leaders and individual learners can use natural-language prompts to generate customized learning paths drawing on content from universities, institutions, and practitioners.
- The platform emphasizes continuous assessment, practical observation, and recognized credentials over traditional pass/fail completion badges, aiming to more accurately track what employees actually learn.
- Personalization is role- and goal-based: the system suggests tailored learning experiences based on a learner's stated goal, current role, and demonstrated capability.
- Helix is explicitly framed by Coursera's CEO as closing "the gap between AI infrastructure and talent readiness" — positioning upskilling as the bottleneck to enterprise AI adoption rather than technology itself.
- The product is enabled by Coursera's 2026 merger with Udemy, which combined a consumer-heavy audience (Coursera) with an enterprise-heavy one (Udemy) into what Coursera calls the "most comprehensive skills platform in the world"; broad enterprise availability is expected H1 2027.

## Quote

> "It uses AI not just to deliver more content, but to create deeply personalized, adaptive learning paths and portable proof of skills." — Greg Hart, Coursera CEO
