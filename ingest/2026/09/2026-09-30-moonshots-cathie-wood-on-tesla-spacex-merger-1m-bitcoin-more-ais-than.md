# Cathie Wood on Tesla-SpaceX Merger, $1M Bitcoin, More AIs Than Humans | EP #296 | Moonshots Live

## Insights

- Cathie Wood (ARK Invest) predicts a Tesla-SpaceX merger this year, framing Musk's endgame as Mars, with connectivity (satellites), AI/Neocloud compute, and orbital data centers as intermediate steps; she claims SpaceX/Tesla are her largest holdings.
- ARK's official real GDP growth forecast is 7-8% over the next five years — notably more conservative than Musk's reported claims of 20-30% or even "triple-digit" growth this decade, which Wood says she hasn't personally heard him say.
- Circle's Nikhil (building the ARC blockchain) predicts more AI agents than humans on the internet by next year, with agents becoming "full economic actors" that transact with each other, requiring new infrastructure for agent identity, liability, provenance, and incentives.
- Panelists frame proof-of-humanity/personhood verification (orb-based ID, work-history logs, public blockchain audit trails) as a coming necessity once agent populations scale into billions, and argue human experience/relationships will become comparatively more valuable as machine interaction proliferates.
- Wood argues AI's most undervalued application is healthcare/multiomics (citing Moderna's record single-day stock gain and a cited "vaccine against cancer"), attributing market underweighting to a historical mismatch between cautious healthcare analysts and impatient tech analysts.
- On humanoid robots, ARK's research reportedly finds them "200,000 times more complex" than robo-taxis (hands cited as hardest part) and expects Musk's late-2028/2029 timeline to slip by a couple of years; Wood also argues nuclear deregulation could have made today's US electricity 50% cheaper and pushes back on "AI takes jobs" narratives, arguing tech is a "net job creator" and labor shortages are more likely than mass job loss.

## Quote

> "Technology is a net job creator always." — Cathie Wood
