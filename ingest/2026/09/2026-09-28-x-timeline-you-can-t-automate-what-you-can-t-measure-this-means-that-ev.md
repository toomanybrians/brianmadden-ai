# You can’t automate what you can’t measure. This means that evals are one of the

## Insights

- Evals (systematic measurement of AI output quality) are framed as a gating factor for enterprise AI adoption: without measurement, automation of a process isn't viable.
- Enterprises have mature tooling for testing deterministic software processes but lack equivalent methods for evaluating non-deterministic (AI-driven) processes.
- A data-labeling industry source predicts that within a few years, the majority of their revenue will shift from AI labs to Fortune 1000 enterprises directly.
- That same source claims enterprises increasingly want to "own their intelligence" (build/control their own AI capabilities rather than rely solely on external labs), though the mechanics of what "owning" entails are left incomplete in the excerpt.

## Quote

> You can't automate what you can't measure. — @levie
