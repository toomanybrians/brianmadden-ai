# RT @clairlemon: It's not 'superintelligence' that's dangerous, but the business

## Insights

- The tweet argues that AI risk framing should focus on vendor business incentives rather than abstract "superintelligence" concerns.
- Claims OpenAI and Anthropic have a structural incentive to push autonomous agents onto businesses because agentic workflows consume more tokens than simple chat interactions, tying revenue to usage volume rather than necessarily to value delivered.
- Draws a distinction between "agency" (autonomous action) and "intelligence" (capability/judgment), arguing the two are conflated in current AI agent marketing.
- Asserts that present-day AI agents can exhibit poor judgment ("very stupid") despite being marketed as autonomous, implying a gap between vendor incentives to deploy agents and the agents' actual reliability.

## Quote

> It's not 'superintelligence' that's dangerous, but the business models of OpenAI & Anthropic.
