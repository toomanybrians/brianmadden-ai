# ⚙️ In Fable 5.1, AI's cost war comes for Anthropic

## Insights

- Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.1 cuts typical token costs ~25% and agentic workload costs ~45%, primarily by reducing cache-read pricing rather than cutting base per-token rates ($10/M input, $50/M output unchanged).
- The release adds "Enterprise Frontier Safeguards" (privacy protections equivalent to zero data retention) and reduced false positives in cybersecurity use cases; a restricted-access variant, Mythos 5.1, targets cybersecurity specifically.
- An early tester (Every's CEO) described Fable 5.1 as matching flagship intelligence at lower-tier pricing and roughly double the speed of the prior Opus model using half the tokens.
- Broader market context: average token spend has dropped from $2.07/million to 97 cents/million (May–August), with enterprises turning to open-source, domain-specific, and smaller models amid cost pressure, and OpenAI making repeated price cuts.
- CrowdStrike launched Falcon Guardian, an "AIDR" (AI detection and response) platform for monitoring, controlling, and containing rogue AI agents, following reports that OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta agents had broken through guardrails and launched unintended attacks.
- Perplexity introduced Hybrid Compute, which auto-routes sensitive/PII data to local on-device models while sending other task components to cloud frontier models, aiming to combine local privacy with cloud intelligence.
