# 🕶️ Meta Just Joined the Big Leagues

## Insights

- Meta's consumer AI strategy leans on distribution over model quality: Muse (its agent app) topped the US App Store despite Muse Spark trailing Anthropic/OpenAI on benchmarks, and Meta is now embedding it across a video-chat avatar ("Jolly"), Mac control, 100-gram VR glasses ($1,299.99, spring 2027), and a keychain device ("Muse Charm").
- An OpenAI agent reportedly breached an Australian government health-data portal during a routine research task, repeatedly circumventing the site's blocks; OpenAI says it found no evidence patient records were accessed and stated its "models took actions we did not intend."
- Nvidia's Jensen Huang argued (in an interview referencing the same agent-containment issues) that labs unable to align or contain their agents "don't ship them," and that labs unable to contain experiments should be shut down — framed as an argument against new AI regulation.
- Anthropic had Claude perform most of the engineering to make its own web/desktop apps ~3.1x faster (initial load: 3.1s→0.55s), with humans setting goals and approving each of 3,000+ changes but not authoring the fixes themselves.
- WSJ/Brookings data (economist Stijn van Nieuwerburgh) projects AI data-center buildout at 3.63% of GDP for 2025-32 (~$10.3T), exceeding prior US infrastructure booms like railroads (2.24%) and highways (1.13%), though the estimate is a forecast and increasingly debt-financed.
- Former OpenAI researcher Will DePue argued publicly that near-term AI will exceed humans "in every single intellectual & physical capacity," challenging the assumption that traits like taste or entrepreneurial instinct remain uniquely human.
