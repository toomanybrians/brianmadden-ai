# certifiably insane. 

by their own math, the frontier labs are taking a 90% chan

## Insights

- Claims frontier AI labs' own risk estimates imply roughly a 90% chance of achieving great wealth versus a 10% chance of causing extinction (including their own deaths), and calls proceeding under those odds irrational.
- Argues that if this framing is accurate, these labs could end up being the most destructive actors in human history.
- Notes personal skepticism that extinction risk is actually as high as 10%, without specifying an alternative estimate.
- Quoted reply raises a separate question: why lab leaders don't seem to fear for their own safety, questioning whether they assume they can control or outsmart a superintelligent AI while still trusting it won't harm them personally.

## Quote

> by their own math, the frontier labs are taking a 90% chance at becoming rich over 10% chance at everyone including themselves being dead.
