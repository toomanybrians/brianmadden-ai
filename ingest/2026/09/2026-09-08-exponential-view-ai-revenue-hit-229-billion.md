# 📈 AI revenue hit $229 billion

## Insights

- The AI economy's annualized revenue reached $229 billion by end of August, a 3.5x increase over one year, per Exponential View's proprietary tracking.
- Trailing twelve-month AI revenue hit $140 billion, up 3.2x from $44 billion a year earlier (August 2025), suggesting sustained rather than one-off growth.
- Snowflake cut its full-year product gross-margin guidance from 75% to 74%, attributing this to AI workloads carrying lower contribution margins — even though an investor estimate puts AI at only ~4-5% of Snowflake's revenue.
- AI-attributed US job cuts declined in August, ending a five-month streak as the top-cited layoff reason, but AI still accounts for 22% of the year's total job cuts.

## Quote

> AI workloads "carry a lower contribution margin today"
