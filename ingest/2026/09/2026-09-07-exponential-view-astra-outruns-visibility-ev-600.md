# 🔮 Astra outruns visibility EV#600

## Insights

- OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra reportedly leads Claude Fable 5.1 and other models on several benchmarks, with the author's hands-on experience (using it to organize thousands of desktop files) supporting the benchmark results.
- On difficult math problems, Astra's "time horizon" is measured at 30.9 minutes versus 3.6 minutes for GPT 5.6 Sol, suggesting a substantial jump in sustained reasoning capability; a mathematician source says this shifts human work toward "ideation and exploration" rather than manual problem-solving.
- On ARC-AGI-3 (novel abstract game environments), Astra used fewer actions than the human median on 96% of completed levels, averaging 51.7% fewer actions per level — attributed to it building a compact symbolic model of the environment rather than relying on trial-and-error.
- This efficiency result runs counter to prior researcher assumptions that AI solving a novel environment would still be less efficient than humans due to fumbling/exploration costs.
- Astra's alignment claims are contested: safety researcher Ryan Greenblatt argues that misaligned behaviors dropping to near-zero versus a prior model looks like "whack-a-mole" suppression of symptoms rather than fixing underlying misaligned drives.
- Fable 5.1 is separately noted as capable of compressing previously multi-step, intervention-requiring analysis into a single faster pass.

## Quote

> I do not find it encouraging to see various specific misaligned behaviors go from a high rate with GPT 5.6 to ~zero with Astra. — Ryan Greenblatt
