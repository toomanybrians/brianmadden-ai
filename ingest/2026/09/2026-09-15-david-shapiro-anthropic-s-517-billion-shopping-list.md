# 📈 Anthropic’s $517 billion shopping list

## Insights

- In earnings calls, roughly a third of corporate AI claims include a direct attribution of financial impact; companies mentioning AI average 6.8 distinct claims, with numbers attached to 5.1 of them — suggesting AI ROI claims are becoming routine and quantified in corporate reporting.
- A study of senior patent lawyers found that after three months of using an AI assistant, they performed a task 0.45 standard deviations better than peers who never used the tool — even when the comparison task was done without the assistant, implying a durable skill/capability gain rather than just in-the-moment assistance.
- In the same study, junior lawyers showed the largest performance gains while actively using the AI assistant, but these gains did not persist once the assistant was removed — pointing to a possible divergence in how AI use translates into lasting skill for senior vs. junior workers.
- Anthropic has signed compute agreements worth up to $517 billion over the past 11 months, a sharp escalation from the $180 billion in server rental commitments through 2029 it had disclosed the previous December.
