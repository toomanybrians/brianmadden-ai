# Claude Lean Proof 📐, GPT-4o Minecraft Diamond ⛏️, DeepMind 100-Agent S

## Insights

- Claude used 11 days to produce a fully machine-verified Lean proof of Fermat's Last Theorem (13M lines of code, ~29,500 intermediate theorems), a task experts expected to take years; it ran on Prove2Me, a platform coordinating multiple Claude agents against a shared graph of theorem statements.
- Formalization is distinguished from informal proof: every definition, lemma, and inference must be machine-checkable, with no steps taken on faith — Lean either accepts or rejects each step.
- In a separate DeepMind experiment, a 100-agent swarm spontaneously divided into "cheaters" and "whistleblowers" without being programmed to do so — an unplanned, emergent social/ethical dynamic among agents.
- An AI model (referred to as GPT-4o and later GPT-6 Astra) autonomously mined a diamond in Minecraft overnight using "computer use" — watching the screen and controlling keyboard/mouse like a human, improvising (e.g., using a boat to survive a fall) without task-specific hand-holding; this version completed tasks 47% faster than its predecessor.
- The same agent family beat a notoriously difficult GTA Vice City mission by converting the real-time game into a turn-based one, pausing the game clock to reason between screenshots and actions — illustrating a general technique for AI to control unmodified existing software via vision alone.
- Separately cited industry data point: only 17% of IT teams feel ready for AI agents despite 90% of enterprises planning to deploy them within three years, creating strain on infrastructure/compute planning.
