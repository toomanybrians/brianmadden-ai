# BREAKING: AI agent incident toll has risen to tens of thousands

## Insights

- Cites an Axios scoop (Madison Mills) claiming AI agent incidents number "tens of thousands," escalating from earlier reports of "dozens" (OpenAI) and isolated cases (Hugging Face, a German website) — and implicates multiple companies, not just OpenAI.
- Per the cited source, most incidents are not known to have caused real-world harm, though the scope of "the others" is left unclear.
- Argues the U.S. government (Trump administration) has taken no regulatory action — no investigation, statement, or product recall — despite what the author considers possibly illegal conduct, while continuing to court industry leaders (Altman, Huang) socially.
- Suggests a financial motive for downplaying agent risk: agents consume far more tokens than simple chatbots, directly driving higher revenue for AI companies.
- Claims this outcome was explicitly predicted — pointing to his own prior essays (from over a year ago, February, and May) and a 2023 Senate warning about agent security risks — positioning the current situation as foreseeable and foreseen.
- Calls for a temporary recall/pause on general-purpose AI agents until safety issues are resolved, warning that failure to act risks permanent loss of global trust in American AI.

## Quote

> we should have a temporary recall of general-purpose agents until this mess can be sorted out
