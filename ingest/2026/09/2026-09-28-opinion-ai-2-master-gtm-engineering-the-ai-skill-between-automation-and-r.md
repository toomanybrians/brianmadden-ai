# Master GTM Engineering: The AI Skill Between Automation and Revenue

## Insights

- "GTM Engineering" is framed as an emerging AI skill: using AI to continuously monitor signals (e.g., a target company hiring salespeople), qualify them against ideal-customer criteria, research and identify the right contact, and surface a brief — without a human manually searching or prompting each step.
- The piece distinguishes this from earlier AI skill eras (prompting, then Projects/agents/Skills/MCP/automations/coding tools), positioning it as a further evolution: turning ad hoc AI use into standing systems/workflows.
- Cites a labor-market data point: analysis of ~1,000 job postings found GTM Engineering roles grew 205% year-over-year (2024–2025), with the most common work being building GTM workflows, connecting tools, and working with CRM systems.
- Argues the timing is driven by AI models recently getting materially better at the underlying capabilities GTM systems need: research, decision-making, tool use, browsing, and long-running autonomous tasks.
- Suggests the skill is more accessible than its name implies — people already using Claude, ChatGPT, Clay, n8n, Make, APIs, or AI agents are already close to doing this kind of work.
- Defines GTM (go-to-market) simply as everything involved in finding the right customer and getting a product in front of them, with "GTM Engineering" being the act of systematizing parts of that process.

## Quote

> GTM Engineering roles grew 205% year over year from 2024 to 2025.
