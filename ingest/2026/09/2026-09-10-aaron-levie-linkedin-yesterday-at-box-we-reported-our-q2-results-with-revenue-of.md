# Yesterday, at Box we reported our Q2 results with revenue of $321.1 million for

## Insights

- Box reported Q2 revenue of $321.1M, up 9% (11% constant currency, its highest in 14 quarters), and raised full-year guidance to $1.290B, with growth attributed to enterprise demand for AI-era content transformation.
- Enterprise leaders' top concern is getting the right context to AI agents and unlocking value from unstructured data securely and with governance controls in place.
- Levie frames unstructured content (contracts, research, financial docs, marketing assets, roadmaps) as the bulk of corporate knowledge that AI agents need but that companies have historically underused.
- Enterprises are shifting from querying/automating structured data toward using AI agents to mine, question, and automate workflows against unstructured files at scale.
- As model capabilities advance across multiple AI labs, enterprises reportedly want model-neutral applied AI platforms so they can swap models/agents for cost-performance tradeoffs without migrating data.
- Citing the "recent OpenAI Hugging Face incident," Levie argues enterprises will increasingly need guardrails, audit logs, and real-time security alerts to prevent unauthorized human or agent access to data.

## Quote

> The world's most advanced superintelligence is only as useful as the underlying enterprise knowledge it has access to.
