# the problem with claiming AGI is that it means GENERAL intelligence. 

which mea

## Insights

- Argues that "AGI" is frequently misused because true general intelligence requires competence across virtually all cognitive tasks, not just impressive narrow ones.
- Traces the term's origin to distinguishing general systems from historical AI specialists (e.g., chess-playing programs) that excelled at one task but couldn't generalize.
- Uses the inability of current AI agents to reliably complete a mundane task (ordering pizza) as evidence that claims of AGI are premature or overstated.
- Implies a gap between marketing/business incentives to declare AGI progress and the actual technical bar the term was meant to represent.

## Quote

> the problem with claiming AGI is that it means GENERAL intelligence.
