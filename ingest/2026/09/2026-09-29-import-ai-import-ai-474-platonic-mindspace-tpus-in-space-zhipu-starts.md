# Import AI 474: Platonic mindspace; TPUs in space; Zhipu starts an outer RSI loop

## Insights

- Zhipu describes using its own GLM-5.3 model as an "Infra Agent" to autonomously analyze, hypothesize, and code changes to its own inference infrastructure, compressing a model launch (GLM-5.3-Flash) from initial adaptation to production in under two weeks while tripling throughput — an early example of a lab automating its own internal engineering loop.
- Zhipu's own language captures a shift in internal sentiment: earlier versions of GLM were used out of "obligation" as the team's own creation, but the latest version has become an "indispensable daily coding partner" that is "moving steadily toward replacing" engineers on coding tasks.
- Zhipu frames the remaining human comparative advantage as higher-level design decisions and system-boundary-setting, explicitly stating this boundary "will not slow down simply because we want it to" — suggesting the team views even this as a temporary line.
- Robotics researchers (Stanford/Physical Intelligence) argue robotics is at an "LLM-moment" but lacks the standardized post-training recipe (defined success criteria, reset procedures, feedback loops) that let language model post-training scale; without this, robot fine-tuning remains ad hoc and unreliable.
- Periodic Labs trained a specialized model (Periodic Neon) via reinforcement learning against real physical lab equipment, achieving a 20x jump in a scientific analysis task (X-ray diffraction interpretation) over a general frontier model, using comparatively little compute — pointing toward AI systems that learn directly from lab experimentation rather than static data.
- A separate philosophical paper (Michael Levin) proposes minds — human or AI — may be "patterns" from a shared abstract space that different physical substrates (brains, datacenters) merely interface with, raising speculative questions about how AI cognition relates to human cognition at a foundational level.

## Quote

> "Today, GLM-5.3 has become an indispensable daily coding partner for everyone on the team, and it is moving steadily toward replacing us." — Zhipu AI
