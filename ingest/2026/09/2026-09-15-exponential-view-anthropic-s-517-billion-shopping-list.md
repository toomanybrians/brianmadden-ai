# 📈 Anthropic’s $517 billion shopping list

## Insights

- In earnings calls, companies mentioning AI make an average of 6.8 distinct claims about it, with numbers attached to 5.1 of them — suggesting AI ROI claims are becoming detailed and quantified rather than vague marketing language, though roughly a third directly attribute financial impact to AI implementation.
- A study of patent lawyers using an AI assistant for three months found senior lawyers retained a 0.45 standard deviation performance edge over non-users even after the study period — a lasting skill/productivity gain.
- Junior lawyers in the same study showed the largest performance boost while actively using the AI assistant, but that gain did not persist once measured without the tool — raising a question about who actually "keeps" AI-driven skill gains long-term (experienced vs. novice workers).
- Anthropic has signed compute agreements totaling up to $517 billion over the past 11 months, a sharp escalation from the $180 billion in server rental commitments through 2029 that had been announced the previous December.
