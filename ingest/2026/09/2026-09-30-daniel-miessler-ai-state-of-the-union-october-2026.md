# AI State of the Union (October 2026)

## Insights

- AI competitive rankings between frontier labs are swinging on a timescale of weeks, not months — sentiment flipped from Anthropic being "in the dirt" to Opus 5.5 "blowing everyone away" within roughly two months.
- Usage/pricing anxiety has become a real driver of tool choice: Codex users reportedly feel high anxiety watching credits deplete quickly, while Claude Code's Opus+Sonnet 5.5 combo now lasts far longer on a fixed ($200) subscription than it did weeks earlier.
- A cited data point (via "Theo" on T3 Code) claims Claude went from trailing Codex to double its popularity in about two weeks — offered as evidence of how fast developer adoption can flip.
- Developer/builder loyalty to specific AI tools is described as essentially nonexistent — a better harness or model released tomorrow could shift half of builders over within a day or two.
- OpenAI's DevDay released GPT-6.1 ("Sol"); the author is testing it but currently favors Claude models mainly due to harness quality and lower usage anxiety, despite running a router across both providers' top models.
- The author frames current volatility as likely to intensify, expecting 2027 to be especially disruptive as AI-driven change compounds across many interconnected areas simultaneously.

## Quote

> The tech is moving so fast that our collective reactions are emotionally exaggerated.
