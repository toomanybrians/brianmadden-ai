# Latest open artifacts (#24): Motif-3, GLM-5.3, Hy4-preview and open model licenses

## Insights

- Open-weight model licensing is diverging by geography: Western labs (Google, Meta) are moving toward permissive Apache 2.0 licenses, while frontier Chinese labs are adding more restrictive commercial terms.
- Kimi K3 and MiniMax M3 now require commercial agreements for inference/fine-tuning services above certain thresholds, and MiniMax additionally prohibits specific use cases.
- Zhipu's GLM-5.3 dropped its prior MIT license in favor of a custom one requiring a Z.AI security review for companies (and "affiliates," an undefined term) with over $10B in trailing 12-month revenue running Model-as-a-Service businesses.
- The GLM-5.3 license's English/Chinese bilingual text uses "关联方" for "affiliates," a term with an actual legal definition in Chinese law, adding interpretive uncertainty for global adopters.
- A recurring "playbook" among Chinese model releases: launch quietly as an anonymous "stealth model" on platforms like OpenRouter to build organic hype and speculation about its creator/size before revealing it, which also blunts accusations of benchmark-gaming.
- Several new open models were released this cycle (Motif-3, dots3, Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, GLM-5.3-Flash, Hy4-preview), spanning architecture experiments (hybrid attention, sparse attention, n-gram embeddings) and continued rapid iteration from both established and newer labs.
