# RT @yoavgo: there is a difference between "i am ok with models being trained on

## Insights

- The tweet draws a distinction between two forms of consent around AI training data: being okay with a model trained on one's data being released publicly later, versus being okay with an internal model trained on one's recent work being used competitively against them.
- The second scenario described: an internal model trained on someone's very recent work is used to "race" to a solution ahead of that person, specifically after rumors circulate that they are close to achieving it.
- The core claim is that these two consent scenarios are fundamentally different and shouldn't be conflated, even though both involve "being okay with training on my data."

## Quote

> i am ok with internal models trained on my very recent efforts, being used to race the solution before me... really not the same thing.
