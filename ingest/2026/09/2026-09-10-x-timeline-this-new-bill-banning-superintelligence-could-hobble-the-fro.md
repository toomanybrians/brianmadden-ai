# This new bill banning superintelligence could hobble the frontier AI industry (w

## Insights

- A bill titled the "Artificial Superintelligence Security Bill" is being introduced in the UK Parliament by MP Alex Sobel, with backing from the advocacy group ControlAI, marking what's claimed as the first legislative attempt anywhere to ban the development of superintelligent AI.
- The bill reportedly has cross-party support in the UK Parliament.
- Gary Marcus argues such a bill could significantly constrain ("hobble") the frontier AI industry, since that industry is oriented toward building superintelligence.
- Marcus asserts the frontier AI industry doesn't actually know how to build superintelligence, despite aspiring to.
- Marcus places blame on frontier AI industry leaders themselves for prompting this kind of regulatory response, though the specific reasoning is cut off in the source.
