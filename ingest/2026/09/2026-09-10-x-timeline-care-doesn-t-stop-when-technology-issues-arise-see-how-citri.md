# Care doesn't stop when technology issues arise.

See how #Citrix helps #healthca

## Insights

- Citrix positions its platform as decoupling clinical workflows from device/infrastructure failures, aiming for continuity of access to systems like EMRs during outages, cyber events, and site disruptions.
- The company frames a specific AI-adoption use case for healthcare IT: providing a "seamless clinician workspace" for surfacing AI insights, zero-trust controls to prevent "Shadow AI," and automation to free IT resources for scaling AI.
- Healthcare CIOs are described as facing pressure from cyber risk, aging infrastructure, proving AI ROI, and vendor/tool sprawl — Citrix pitches consolidation of point solutions as the answer to these combined pressures.
- A cited case study claims Citrix's platform lets clinicians access patient records 88% faster (under 5 seconds), and that automated provisioning/centralized management can cut manual IT labor hours by 25%.
- Zero trust security and PHI protection are framed as needing to coexist with reduced workflow friction — access control is only valuable to the vendor's pitch if it doesn't slow clinical work.
- The material also touches M&A: standardized workspaces are pitched as enabling "Day-1 clinical readiness" across newly merged organizations with different devices, networks, and identity systems.
