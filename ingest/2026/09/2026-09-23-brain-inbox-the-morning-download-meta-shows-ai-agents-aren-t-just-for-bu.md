# The Morning Download: Meta Shows AI Agents Aren’t Just For Businesses Anymore

## Insights

- Meta's consumer-facing AI agent, Muse, hit No. 1 in Apple's App Store within two weeks of launch, and Meta's stock jumped 11% (its biggest one-day gain since April 2025) on the news, suggesting AI agents may be gaining traction with everyday consumers, not just businesses.
- Meta and Shopify partnered to let Muse complete purchases on users' behalf, extending agent capability into transactional commerce.
- Muse-style agents can book appointments, manage finances, send emails, and shop for users — but require deep access to personal data (email, calendar, e-commerce, banking), raising adoption and trust hurdles.
- Enterprise AI agent adoption is already substantial and accelerating: Gartner projects the average global Fortune 500 enterprise will run over 150,000 AI agents within two years; FedEx is building an "AI agent workforce" to work alongside human staff.
- Unlike enterprises, which have governance teams to manage AI agent errors, consumers lack equivalent safeguards — positioning "rogue agent" risk (mistaken purchases, bookings, appointments) as a key unresolved issue for consumer-grade agents.
- Separately, OpenAI has proposed that the U.S. government lead an international effort to establish shared safety standards for advanced, self-improving AI systems.
