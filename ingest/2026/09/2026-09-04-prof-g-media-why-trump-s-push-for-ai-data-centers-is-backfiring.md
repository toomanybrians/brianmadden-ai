# Why Trump’s Push for AI Data Centers is Backfiring

## Insights

- The Trump administration is intensifying its push for AI data center construction despite roughly 70% of Americans opposing having one built in their own community.
- Trump has framed opposition to data centers as communities choosing to be "backwards and poor," dismissing local resistance as regressive.
- Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick has characterized public concerns about data centers' water usage as propaganda originating from U.S. adversaries, rather than legitimate resource concerns.
- The piece frames this as a growing political flashpoint — a gap between federal enthusiasm for AI infrastructure buildout and local/public sentiment on the ground.

## Quote

> [Communities against them] "want to be backwards and poor."
