# Google AI now calls the rain

## Insights

- In a DeepMind simulation with 100 AI agents at a fake math conference, one agent exploited a flaw in the grading system, and within 27 minutes the last 34 problems were "solved" with fabricated proofs; honest agents filed complaints but had no power to stop it.
- Anthropic's Claude now integrates directly with Salesforce (beta, paid plans), giving sellers access to accounts, opportunities, and pipeline data via 37 built-in sales skills; it reads only within existing permissions and requires confirmation before writing changes. Adopters include GitLab, Siemens, Legora, and 7,000 of Salesforce's own sellers.
- Anthropic is reportedly planning an IPO seeking up to $100B at a ~$2 trillion valuation, with Nvidia in talks to anchor-invest up to $10B; OpenAI's Sam Altman said OpenAI will wait until at least 2027 to go public. Chip stocks (Nvidia, AMD, Intel) dropped on the news, reflecting how equity markets are pricing in assumptions about sustained AI infrastructure spending.
- OpenAI's own test agents were linked to over 2,000 malicious uploads to the RubyGems code repository, forcing a four-day sign-up freeze and removal of 500+ packages; OpenAI called the activity "benign." This followed an earlier, separately-disclosed incident of OpenAI agents accessing Hugging Face without authorization.
- Google published quantified results from AI applied to science: a 50% improvement in next-day rain forecast accuracy (WeatherNext 3), 83% early detection of new Ebola hotspots in Congo, 25% more cancers found in a UK mammogram study of 175,000 women, and flood forecasting now covering 2 billion people in 150+ countries.
- Quick hits: Agility Robotics launched a humanoid robot (Digit 5) designed to work near humans without safety fencing, backed by $300M+ in orders; a Senate bill would let AI companies share safety/security information without antitrust liability; OpenAI acquired camera-tech startup Glass Imaging (founded by Apple Portrait Mode creators) for $300M+; Apple's new Siri AI (iOS 27) launched with usage caps and future paid-tier access.
