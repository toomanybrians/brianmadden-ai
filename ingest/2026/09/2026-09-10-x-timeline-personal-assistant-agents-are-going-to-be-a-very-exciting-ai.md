# Personal assistant agents are going to be a very exciting AI category. It’s the

## Insights

- Personal assistant agents are framed as a new AI category notable for being the first agentic use-case with high token volume that makes economic/practical sense for consumers (as opposed to enterprise-only agentic workloads).
- The space is described as having many different competing approaches emerging simultaneously, and is expected to become highly competitive.
- A concrete example cited is "Muse," a personal AI assistant launched by Scale AI's founder, positioned as always-on, fast, able to use a browser, connect to apps, and designed with security in mind.
