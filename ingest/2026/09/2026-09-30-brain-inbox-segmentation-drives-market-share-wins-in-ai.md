# Segmentation Drives Market Share Wins in AI

## Insights

- The AI foundation model competition is shifting from technical differentiation (bigger/thinking models, RL environments, agents) to business-model innovation, specifically customer segmentation and price discrimination on the same underlying models.
- Anthropic introduced enterprise metered billing in March 2026, which doubled its revenue in a single quarter — evidence that pricing/packaging changes can move revenue faster than model improvements.
- OpenAI countered roughly three months later by cutting the price of its cheapest model ("Luna") by 80%, closing competitive ground with Anthropic.
- OpenAI's run rate is approaching $70B, and both companies are projected to near $100B in annual revenue by year-end, though margins remain unclear — gross profit per token is proposed as a better comparative metric than revenue alone.
- Anthropic's leaked S-1 shows Amazon and Google together made up almost a quarter of its revenue last year, and neither has a long-term contract — suggesting even the largest AI buyers are still uncommitted and the market remains unsettled.

## Quote

> Technical innovation, customer segmentation, price discrimination : we are watching a business school case play out on the water.
