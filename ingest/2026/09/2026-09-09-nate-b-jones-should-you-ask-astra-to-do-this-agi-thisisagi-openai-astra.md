# Should you ask Astra to do this? #AGI #thisisAGI #openai #astra

## Insights

- The claim being made is narrower than "full autonomy": not handing Astra unsupervised control (e.g., a credit card) to fully execute a task, but delegating large, multi-step chunks of grunt work to it.
- The example given is household moves — the surrounding admin (address changes, cancellations, scheduling, forms) rather than the move itself — framed as the kind of sprawling, tedious task previously requiring sustained human effort.
- The asserted shift is in scale of delegation: AI's value isn't answering one discrete request but absorbing an entire messy pile of interrelated small tasks at once.
- The outcome claimed is time recovered (evenings), not perfect execution — described as making "a real dent," explicitly distinguished from a superficial demo.
- Author references a companion resource: 23 "recipe cards" cataloging specific categories of work suitable for delegating to Astra.
