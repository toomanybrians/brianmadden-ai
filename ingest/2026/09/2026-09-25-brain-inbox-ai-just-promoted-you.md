# AI just promoted you

## Insights

- Cites J.C.R. Licklider's 1957 self-tracked time-study: he found 85% of his working hours went to searching, reformatting, and prep, with only 15% spent on actual thinking.
- Licklider predicted machines would eventually absorb that 85%, leaving humans to "set the goals, formulate the hypotheses, determine the criteria."
- The piece frames current AI tools as fulfilling that prediction now — automating the execution/prep work and pushing everyone toward the strategic 15% by default.
- Argues this removes the traditional career path of years of execution work before earning input on strategy; people now start closer to strategic-level work.
- Frames this shift as similar to how founders have always had to operate — jumping straight into goal-setting and judgment rather than earning it through tenure.

## Quote

> No more years of execution before you earn a say in strategy. You start in the 15%.
