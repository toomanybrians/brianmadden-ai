# Dario Amodei Anoints Himself U.S. Secretary of State and Ambassador to China

## Insights

- Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's manifesto "We Must Pace the Frontier" argues AI development pacing/slowdowns should be limited by the US's competitive "lead" over China, framing China as the primary safety risk rather than misalignment itself.
- The manifesto reportedly prompted informal coordination among Amodei, Sam Altman, Elon Musk, and Demis Hassabis on safety matters, plus a proposal for embedded independent evaluators to assess frontier model safety practices.
- Amodei's claim that US AI companies are "carefully preventing" misalignment is disputed by the piece, citing OpenAI's admission of six new "concerning" model behaviors and a disclosure framework criticized for containing carve-outs.
- The manifesto calls for restricting chip/semiconductor exports to China and cracking down on "unauthorized distillation," though distillation is characterized elsewhere (including by CISA) as a legitimate, if commercially unwelcome, training technique also used by xAI.
- Chinese officials (foreign ministry spokesperson and state-run Global Times) publicly rejected Amodei's framing as a "hypocritical" attempt to contain China under the guise of AI safety cooperation.
- Separately, the newsletter notes Hamilton Nolan's argument that AI researchers could unionize to collectively demand safety-driven slowdowns, positioned as a more grounded alternative to lab-CEO self-governance.
