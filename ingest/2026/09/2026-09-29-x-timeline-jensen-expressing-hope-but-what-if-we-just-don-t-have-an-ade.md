# Jensen expressing hope, but what if we just don’t have an adequate engineering s

## Insights

- Jensen Huang reportedly frames rogue AI agent risk as contingent on treating it as "an engineering problem" — implying that if it isn't, it may be unsolvable.
- Gary Marcus questions this framing, suggesting Huang's optimism assumes an engineering solution exists at all, which may not be true.
- Marcus raises a specific doubt: that LLMs themselves may not be a "stable enough foundation" to build reliable/safe agentic systems on.
- The exchange highlights a fork in industry framing: AI safety/reliability as a solvable engineering challenge vs. a possibly fundamental limitation of the current LLM paradigm.

## Quote

> "We all need to hope it's an engineering problem. If it's not an engineering problem, it's not solvable." — Jensen Huang (via @MarioNawfal)
