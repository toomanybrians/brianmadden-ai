# OpenRouter: Top AI Tools Course 3/5

## Insights

- OpenRouter functions as a unified API/routing layer sitting between a workflow and hundreds of underlying AI models (Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Qwen, Llama, and others), letting developers swap models by changing a name rather than rebuilding integrations.
- The tool addresses a specific pain point in the current AI landscape: rapid model churn (new models becoming cheaper, better at coding, or better with large context/agents every few days) makes maintaining separate accounts, API keys, and payment setups for each provider impractical.
- OpenRouter can either let the user pick a specific model or automatically choose one on their behalf, effectively abstracting model selection from the application layer.
- Scale claims cited: over 10 trillion tokens processed daily, 400+ models available, more than 10 million developers/companies using it.
- Stripe agreed to acquire OpenRouter in August 2026, signaling this routing/aggregation layer is seen as strategically significant infrastructure in the AI stack.
- The piece frames OpenRouter's value as most apparent in high-volume, multi-call use cases: agents, coding tools, automations, research systems, customer support, and document processing.
