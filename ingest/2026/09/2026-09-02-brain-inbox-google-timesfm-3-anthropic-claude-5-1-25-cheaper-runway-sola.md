# Google TimesFM-3 📊, Anthropic Claude 5.1 25% cheaper 💸, Runway Solaris

- Google's TimesFM-3 forecasting model now processes multiple correlated data streams simultaneously (e.g., sales, weather, promotions together) rather than single time series, requires no fine-tuning, and tops three major forecasting benchmarks (GIFT-Eval, FEV-Bench, TIME); it's released under a non-commercial license, limiting production use.
