# 🚨 The first existential IPO

## Insights

- Anthropic's leaked S-1 shows a company investing heavily for growth: an $8bn operating loss for 2025 and a $42bn net loss (inflated by an accounting charge), but revenue growth is outpacing cost growth.
- That revenue/cost trend is expected to continue into 2026, with Anthropic projected to turn profitable that year.
- Anthropic has committed to $518bn in compute spending over 7-10 years, with roughly 80% (~$410bn) non-cancellable — implying it needs on the order of $1 trillion in sales over that period to sustain margins.
- Annualized revenue is projected to exceed $100bn by the end of 2026.
- Anthropic is rumored to go public in November, positioning this as a landmark "existential" IPO for the AI platform cycle.

## Quote

> the S-1 shows a business investing for growth, with the first signs of green shoots
