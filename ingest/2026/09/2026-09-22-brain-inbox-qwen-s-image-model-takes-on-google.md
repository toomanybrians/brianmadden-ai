# 🎨 Qwen’s Image Model Takes On Google

## Insights

- Qwen released Qwen-Image-2.1 (Sept 20) as an open-weight, downloadable 7B visual generator paired with a separate Qwen3-VL-8B text encoder, scoring 60.28 on Qwen's own benchmark — ahead of Google's Nano Banana 2.0 (59.82) and larger models like FLUX 2 Max (32B) and Hunyuan Image 3.0 (80B), though six closed models still score higher.
- The model accepts up to ten reference images, supports targeted edits, and generates transparent backgrounds — useful for design workflows needing consistency across a person/product/outfit combination.
- Weights are open for noncommercial research/evaluation under Qwen's Research License; commercial use requires a separate license. Diffusers and ComfyUI supported it at launch.
- Anthropic is reportedly considering releasing a new model ahead of its IPO (now targeted for November) to counter OpenAI's Astra, while its CEO simultaneously calls for stronger AI safety guardrails; reported annualized revenue run rate exceeds $100B (up from $65B in July), supporting a ~$2 trillion valuation discussion.
- Vercel's AI Gateway data shows OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra outpaced Anthropic's Fable 5.1 in early spend (surpassing it by day 4, reaching 2x by day 12) at equal list pricing — cited as context for competitive pressure on Anthropic.
- OpenAI is in trillion-dollar valuation talks ($1.2T investor proposal vs. OpenAI's $1.5T target) while projecting $278B in cumulative negative free cash flow from 2026–2030, with revenue forecast to grow from $36B (2026) to $350B (2030).
