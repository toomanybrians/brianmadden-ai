# BREAKING: Secret US AI evaluation framework has been partly revealed

## Insights

- The U.S. government maintains a secret evaluation framework for determining which "frontier" AI models can be released, despite public rhetoric opposing AI regulation.
- Protect Democracy filed a FOIA request and lawsuit to obtain this framework; the government responded with 132 pages of records, but nearly all substantive content was redacted.
- Officials involved in the framework's development reportedly include the Director of OSTP and the U.S. Chief Technology Officer, though little else about their roles or the framework's actual criteria was disclosed.
- The redacted release leaves the specific standards, criteria, and justifications for regulating frontier AI models entirely opaque to the public.
- Protect Democracy's lead counsel argues that secretive regulatory processes create risk of corruption, autocratic abuse, and avoidable mistakes, and states the organization intends to keep pursuing transparency through litigation, expecting a production schedule agreement soon.

## Quote

> Across the spectrum of views on AI risk and how to mitigate it, there's one clearly wrong way: in secrecy. — Deana El-Mallawany, Protect Democracy
