# Great questions from @DKThomp! https://t.co/lltNAwujsh

## Insights

- Raises a challenge to the "China will build it anyway" justification frontier AI labs use to defend building systems they themselves consider dangerous.
- Argues that this justification requires an extremely high bar of certainty about China's actual intentions and capabilities, not just an assumption.
- Frames the core question as whether labs and policymakers have actually verified this premise ("Are we? Are we") rather than treating it as self-evidently true.
- Positions this as an open, unresolved question rather than asserting a definitive answer either way.

## Quote

> If the frontier labs feel obligated to build something they think is dangerous because China is going to build it anyway, we'd better be really sure
