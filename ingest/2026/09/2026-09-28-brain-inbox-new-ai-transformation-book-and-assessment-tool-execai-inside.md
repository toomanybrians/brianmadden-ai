# New AI Transformation Book and Assessment Tool (ExecAI Insider #89)

## Insights

- Paul Roetzer's new book, "The AI Transformation Blueprint: A Human-Centered Guide to Organizational Change," frames AI adoption as a change-management problem, not a technology rollout — introducing the "AI Transformation System" (AITS) framework for moving from experimentation to sustained organizational innovation.
- The companion tool, AI Score by SmarterX, splits assessment into a Business AI Score (8 pillars, 67 dimensions) and a Personal AI Score (5 pillars, 37 dimensions), scored on a strong/moderate/weak scale (80-100%/60-79%/0-59%).
- Business pillars are Vision, Strategy, Data, Technology, Governance, Literacy, People, and Performance; Personal pillars are Knowledge, Application, Judgment, Impact, and Mindset — each dimension maps to a specific action step in the book, turning a weak score into a to-do list.
- Underlying thesis: simply handing employees tools like Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini without context and understanding is what's currently happening in many enterprises, and it doesn't constitute real transformation.
- Both the assessment and the framework are positioned as diagnostic/repeatable — meant to establish a baseline and be retaken over time to track transformation progress.
- Separately noted in the same issue: an Anthropic safety researcher publicly resigned this week warning that the AI industry is moving too fast, a story that reportedly spread beyond typical AI-industry coverage.

## Quote

> It cannot simply be technology... handed to employees who lack the context and understanding of how to use the tools.
