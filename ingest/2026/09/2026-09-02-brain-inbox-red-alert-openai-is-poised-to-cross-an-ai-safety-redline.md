# Red Alert: OpenAI is poised to cross an AI safety redline.

## Insights

- OpenAI is reportedly developing a new technique that causes models to reveal less of their internal "thinking" (chain-of-thought), which would make their reasoning harder to monitor externally.
- The author and Zack Korman previously argued that better CoT monitoring might have prevented an unspecified "Hugging Face incident," a failure OpenAI itself is said to have acknowledged.
- References a prior paper, "Chain of Thought Monitorability: A New and Fragile Opportunity for AI Safety," which argues that CoT monitoring — while imperfect (per Subbarao Kambhampati's critiques) — is one of the few tools available for inspecting the internal workings of large language models.
- The concern raised is that trading away this monitoring capability for marginal performance gains represents a risky tradeoff given how few safety mechanisms currently exist for these systems.
- A former OpenAI safety researcher (Steven Adler, now at Guidelight.ai) publicly agreed with these concerns, as did another commentator (Nathan Calvin), signaling shared unease among people who worked on OpenAI's safety teams.

## Quote

> CoT monitoring is imperfect, but it is one of the best threads we have for monitoring the giant black boxes that we call LLM.
