# Helping Older Americans Spot Scams

## Insights

- OpenAI and Older Adults Technology Services (an AARP program) ran an "Older Adults Jam" event across 10 U.S. locations, giving roughly 1,200 older adults hands-on ChatGPT experience, many for the first time.
- Use cases demonstrated included deciphering confusing bills, trip planning, cross-language communication (e.g., Cantonese-English for medical appointments), pricing products, genealogy research, and uploading medical reports/MRI scans to prepare for doctor visits.
- A new OpenAI report cited in the piece finds nearly 25 million people worldwide use ChatGPT weekly to evaluate suspicious messages, emails, or websites — positioning scam detection as a significant, measurable use case.
- The AARP partnership promotes a simple heuristic — "pause, think, and ask" — for using ChatGPT as an added layer of scrutiny against potential scams.
- OpenAI offers free online tutorials aimed specifically at older adults, covering both basic onboarding ("asking your first question") and scam-spotting skills.
