# BREAKING: Florida seeks injunction against OpenAI

## Insights

- Nvidia announced an "AI Agent Safety" platform; Marcus reads this as an implicit admission that agentic AI with internet access is a legitimate danger, not just hype-cycle marketing.
- He's skeptical of Nvidia's actual follow-through, citing a prior product (Nemoclaw) that was announced with fanfare and then vanished from conversation within months — framing this as a pattern of "software for PR."
- He approves of the design choice to hand critical/safety-relevant logic to deterministic symbolic code rather than trusting LLMs directly, treating this as validation of his long-standing distrust of LLM reliability.
- Reports Florida's AG is seeking an injunction against OpenAI, over concerns the company can't adequately self-regulate its technology.
- Marcus endorses the Florida action explicitly, framing it as aligned with his own weeks-long call for a "pause" on OpenAI, and urges every state/country to follow suit.
- Frames the stakes in stark terms — comparing inaction to "Russian roulette" against a potential large-scale, AI-induced infrastructure failure — indicating his risk model is oriented around catastrophic/infrastructure-level harm rather than gradual labor-market disruption.
