# How could we take this body seriously if someone as deeply anti-regulatory as Sr

## Insights

- Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic are reportedly collaborating to form a new AI standards body called the "Standards Authority for Frontier AI."
- Candidates floated for the body's CEO role include Sriram Krishnan and Arati Prabhakar; candidates for chair include Condoleezza Rice and David Friedberg.
- Gary Marcus questions the credibility of the effort, specifically objecting to Sriram Krishnan as a potential CEO due to his strongly anti-regulatory stance.
- Marcus also raises concern that this new body could be sidelining or displacing established standards organizations like IEEE.

## Quote

> How could we take this body seriously if someone as deeply anti-regulatory as Sriram Krishnan was the CEO?
