# When you hear that data is the new oil, this is ultimately what that looks like.

## Insights

- Frames the "data is the new oil" analogy as now materializing concretely: AI's demand for data is so extensive that data has value "almost in any form," regardless of quality or original purpose.
- Argues that in an AI-driven economy, information should be treated as a formal balance-sheet asset for companies, not just an operational byproduct.
- References an unspecified "data sale" as a concrete example of this shift toward monetizing organizational information as an asset.
- Predicts that how well companies manage and extract value ("mine") from their internal data/intelligence will become a key determinant of future competitiveness and value creation.

## Quote

> Information actually belongs as an asset on the balance sheet.
