# Sam Altman 2024: “What you really want… is a super-competent colleague that know

## Insights

- Contrasts Sam Altman's 2024 vision of AI as a "super-competent colleague" with total access to a person's emails, conversations, and life history — a stated ambition for deep personal data integration.
- Juxtaposes that stated ambition against a more recent Altman claim of ignorance about what data OpenAI's models were actually trained on, tied to a controversy referred to as "Mathgate."
- The tweet functions as a gotcha, implying inconsistency between OpenAI's ambition to know "everything" about users and its stated lack of transparency/knowledge about its own training data sourcing.
- Raises the underlying tension between AI companies' appetite for comprehensive personal data access and their accountability for what data was actually used to build their models.
