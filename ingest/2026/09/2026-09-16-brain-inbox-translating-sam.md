# Translating Sam

## Insights

- Marcus interprets Sam Altman's use of the word "pacing" (in an X post ahead of Altman's Salesforce appearance) as a euphemism — reframing "moving as fast as possible while avoiding legal/regulatory consequences" as deliberate restraint.
