# 📈 AI revenue hit $229 billion

## Insights

- Exponential View's proprietary AI-economy revenue estimate reached $229 billion annualized by end of August, up 3.5x year-over-year.
- Trailing twelve-month AI revenue hit $140 billion, 3.2x the $44 billion figure from August of the prior year.
- Snowflake cut full-year product gross-margin guidance from 75% to 74%, citing lower contribution margins on fast-growing AI workloads.
- An investor estimate pegs AI at only ~4-5% of Snowflake's current revenue, despite its outsized margin impact.
- AI-attributed US job cuts declined in August, ending a five-month streak as the top-cited layoff reason, but still represent 22% of total 2026 job cuts.

## Quote

> AI workloads "carry a lower contribution margin today."
