# Humans Aren't Aligned Either

## Insights

- The AI alignment debate implicitly assumes humans are well-aligned (consistent, predictable, ethical), but historical outcomes — poverty, war — suggest humans fail at this too.
- Determinism/variance is often cited as an AI shortcoming (different models produce different outputs, same model produces different outputs on repeated runs), but human behavior shows the same or greater variance, especially in organizations and teams.
- The argument isn't that AI shouldn't be held to alignment/consistency standards — it's that the standard should be applied honestly, without assuming humans already meet it.
- Frames a common critique of AI (unreliability, inconsistency) as a double standard rather than a unique AI deficiency.

## Quote

> Anyone who's ever worked in large organizations or led a team knows that this is the case with humans as well.
