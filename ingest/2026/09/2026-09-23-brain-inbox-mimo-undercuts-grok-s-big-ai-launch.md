# 💸 MiMo Undercuts Grok’s Big AI Launch

## Insights

- Xiaomi released MiMo-V2.6 Pro (MIT-licensed, open weights) within hours of xAI's Grok 4.7 launch, matching Grok's rounded Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index score of 46 while pricing output tokens at roughly one-seventh of Grok's rate ($0.87 vs $6 per million tokens).
- xAI's launch materials leaned on a selective benchmark menu (e.g., EEBench, Harvey Legal Agent Benchmark) where Grok leads, while omitting competing models like Opus 5 and Astra from its main comparison table — a framing the piece flags as a "thin basis for claiming leadership."
- On broader/independent evaluations, MiMo Pro scored close to leading closed models on computer-use (OSWorld-Verified) and software engineering (DeepSWE) tests, though the widely-cited Intelligence Index chart circulating actually compared MiMo against Grok 4.6, not Grok 4.7 — an important caveat for accurate comparison.
- MiMo Pro has 1.02 trillion total parameters; the widely-referenced "runs on two GPUs" setup applies only to the smaller MiMo Flash variant, not to Pro, complicating claims about accessible self-hosting.
- Separately, OpenAI is reportedly using its Astra model to run and self-correct AI research experiments (changing training methods, monitoring results) with humans still directing the work — raising internal concern about safety research keeping pace with automated experimentation.
- SOMA Robotics launched a two-arm mobile research robot with a data-recording package (VR/desktop teleoperation, LeRobot export) aimed at reducing the setup cost for labs collecting human-demonstration training data; Xiaomi separately showed MiMo controlling a simulated robot arm, though no integration between the two exists yet.
