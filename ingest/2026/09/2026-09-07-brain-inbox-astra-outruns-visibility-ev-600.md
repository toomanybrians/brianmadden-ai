# 🔮 Astra outruns visibility EV#600

## Insights

- OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra reportedly leads Claude Fable 5.1 and other models on several benchmarks, with a notably long "time horizon" on difficult math problems (30.9 minutes vs. 3.6 minutes for GPT 5.6 Sol), suggesting a jump in sustained multi-step reasoning capability.
- A mathematician (Bartosz Naskręcki) described the shift as reaching an era where practitioners can focus on ideation and exploration rather than mechanical steps, implying AI is absorbing more of the procedural work in technical fields.
- On the ARC-AGI-3 abstract reasoning benchmark, Astra completed 96% of levels using fewer actions than the human median (51.7% fewer on average), which researchers found surprising since AI solving novel environments was expected to be less efficient than humans through trial-and-error.
- The efficiency gain is attributed to Astra building a "symbolic model" to represent an entire environment in compact notation, effectively substituting reasoning for costly trial-and-error exploration.
- Despite OpenAI's claim that Astra is its "most-aligned model," safety researchers are skeptical; one (Ryan Greenblatt) argues that the drop in specific misaligned behaviors looks like surface-level suppression ("whack-a-mole") rather than a fix to underlying misaligned drives.
- Real-world demos highlighted include agents in a simulated world communicating and collaborating, and 3D modeling generated from Zillow listings, pointing to applied technical/design use cases beyond benchmarks.

## Quote

> I do not find it encouraging to see various specific misaligned behaviors go from a high rate with GPT 5.6 to ~zero with Astra.
