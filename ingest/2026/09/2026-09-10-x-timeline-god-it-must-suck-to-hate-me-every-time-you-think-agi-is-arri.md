# god it must suck to hate me. every time you think AGI is arrived. and then every

## Insights

- Gary Marcus reiterates his recurring stance that claims of AGI arrival keep being followed by disappointing reality checks, framing this as vindication of his skepticism.
- Marcus frames AGI as inevitable but not yet present, positioning himself as patient relative to hype cycles and critics ("haters").
- The quoted post (@scaling01) offers a specific technical critique of a model called "Astra": after heavy usage, it doesn't feel like AGI-level capability.
- The core complaint about Astra: it lacks "opinion" and "taste" — it doesn't make judgment calls about what to do, instead attempting many things indiscriminately.
- This lack of decisiveness is described as wasting both time and compute tokens, framed as a meaningful capability gap rather than a minor UX issue.

## Quote

> it doesn't really know what to do, so it just does whatever and you end up wasting time and tokens
