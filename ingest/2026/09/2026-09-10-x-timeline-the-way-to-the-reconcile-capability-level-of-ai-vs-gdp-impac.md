# The way to the reconcile capability level of AI vs. GDP impact is that the diffu

## Insights

- Argues the apparent contradiction between AI's raw capability level (e.g., models solving Millennium Problems) and low measured GDP growth (~1.5%) is reconciled by diffusion lag — AI capability existing does not mean it has been adopted or integrated into the economy yet.
- Claims real-world diffusion of AI into work and organizations will take "much longer than people think," implying current capability benchmarks are poor predictors of near-term economic impact.
- Suggests that even where AI is being adopted, its effects will show up in ways that are hard to capture in standard GDP measurement, at least in the near term.
- Frames the capability-vs-GDP gap as expected and structural rather than as evidence AI is overhyped or non-transformative.

## Quote

> The way to reconcile capability level of AI vs. GDP impact is that the diffusion of AI will take much longer than people think.
