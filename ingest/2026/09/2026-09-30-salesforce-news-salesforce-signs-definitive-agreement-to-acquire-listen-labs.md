# Salesforce Signs Definitive Agreement to Acquire Listen Labs

## Insights

- Salesforce is acquiring Listen Labs, an AI-agent platform that automates customer research: designing studies, recruiting participants, conducting interviews, and synthesizing findings on one platform.
- The platform claims to run interviews continuously across 120+ languages, drawing from a pool of 50M+ participants, compressing research timelines from months to days.
- Beyond gathering feedback, Listen Labs builds "digital twins" — AI simulations grounded in real customer behavior — that let teams test how customers might react to a product or message before it ships.
- Salesforce frames the acquisition as extending customer/user understanding into Marketing Cloud, Service Cloud, and its broader AI portfolio, positioning research insights as context feeding AI agents at scale, not just human decision-makers.
- Deal is structured as a signed definitive agreement, not yet closed; expected close is Q4 of Salesforce's fiscal 2027, pending regulatory clearance — so integration details and terms are not yet public.
- Messaging leans heavily on speed/scale superlatives ("thousands of real conversations at once," "days instead of months") that reflect vendor framing rather than independently verified performance claims.

> Companies have always wanted to hear from every customer, but until now it was only possible to talk to a handful. — Alfred Wahlforss, CEO, Listen Labs
