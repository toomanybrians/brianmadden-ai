# The engine was built for analysts who could wait. Agents can't: Starburst CEO Justin Borgman on what AI agents need from enterprise data

## Insights

- Starburst CEO Justin Borgman argues enterprise data infrastructure was designed for patient human analysts, but AI agents run thousands of parallel queries and will silently lock onto a wrong definition (e.g., gross billings vs. recognized revenue) and keep going — producing confident-looking but incorrect answers.
- His fix: governed, shared business definitions and permissions must be enforced at query time (not after), with full audit trails back to source data and SQL, so agent answers are traceable and verifiable.
- User-level access rights are necessary but not sufficient for agent safety — agents can combine and iterate over data at a scale and speed that wasn't anticipated when those permissions were originally designed, so governance/masking rules need consistent enforcement regardless of query volume.
- At Starburst itself, ~1/3 of code now originates as AI-drafted (via tools like Claude Code), targeting 80%, with the human engineer remaining "author of record"; measured effect so far is up to 60% faster idea-to-production time (quality/security/review-time impact not measured/disclosed).
- Company plan: grow revenue 2.5x while growing headcount only 1.5x, which Borgman says makes junior hiring "a harder door, not a closed one" — the entry-level job shifts from producing first drafts to judging whether AI-produced drafts are correct.
- Build-vs-buy framework ("Ferrari or bus") for AI agents rests on three questions: how specialized is the use case, does the org have engineering capacity to maintain it, and is there enough scale to justify the cost.
- Borgman predicts the common assumption that "the smartest model wins" will prove wrong — he expects durable advantage to come from data foundations (governed context, access, meaning) rather than model quality alone.

## Quote

> The engine was built for analysts who could wait. Agents can't. — Justin Borgman
