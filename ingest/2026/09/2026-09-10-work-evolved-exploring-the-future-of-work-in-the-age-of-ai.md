# Exploring the Future of Work in the Age of AI

## Insights

- The white paper (co-authored with Brooks Miller, informed by a March 2026 HoRizon Collective event with 25+ senior HR leaders) reportedly reframes HR's role as managing a broader "ecosystem of capability" spanning human, service, and digital resources — not just managing employees.
- A core argument (per reader reaction) is that knowledge is losing its primacy as a source of value in work, since AI can increasingly supply it.
- What remains distinctly human and valuable, per this framing, is interpretation and meaning-making — the sense-making work AI cannot replicate.
- The paper positions the future of work as something organizations can actively design and shape, rather than a fixed outcome AI imposes on them.
- Forthcoming pieces from the author will cover change management in the age of AI and a workflow-design method used in a paid AI Accelerator workshop, suggesting practical/operational (not just conceptual) material is in the pipeline.

## Quote

> The shift from managing employees to managing the whole ecosystem of capability, so human, service and digital.
