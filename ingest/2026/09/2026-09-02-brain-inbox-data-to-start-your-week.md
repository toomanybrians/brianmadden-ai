# 📈 Data to start your week

## Insights

- Frontier AI models are described as rapidly depreciating assets: even top-tier performers (highest GPQA Diamond scores) lose pricing power quickly after release, per Exponential View's ongoing tracking since their June State of the AI Economy report.
- Nvidia's revenue more than doubled year-on-year to $96.2 billion in the most recent quarter, signaling continued strong demand for AI compute infrastructure.
- Frontier model reasoning capability has been accelerating since April 2024, according to the newsletter's tracked data.
- US hyperscale data centers have only a marginal average effect on nearby households' electricity bills, per cited research — pushing back on narratives of heavy local cost burden from AI infrastructure buildout.
