# Why Tech Titans Want to Leave Humanity Behind

## Insights

- The episode frames an "ideology" lens (paralleling the incentive-based Munger quote) for explaining the behavior of AI industry leaders, arguing their worldview — not just incentives — drives outcomes.
- The ideology described holds that humans as currently constituted are a "defective early draft," that human traits are bugs to fix, that Earth is expendable, and that present human suffering is a rounding error against a cosmic future.
- This cluster of beliefs is characterized as falling under transhumanism, also referenced as techno-optimism or accelerationism, with roots as a fringe Silicon Valley subculture roughly twenty years ago.
- The piece claims this ideology has moved from message-board fringe to being held by many people currently building and governing AI systems, making it, per guest Alex Thomas, "arguably the most powerful ideological force on the planet."
- The stated goal of the conversation is to help listeners recognize this ideology when it appears in press statements, manifestos, or social media posts, and to counter it with an alternative "pro-human" vision.

## Quote

> Human beings, as they are, are a defective early draft; the things that make us humans are bugs to be fixed.
