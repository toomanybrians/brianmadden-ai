# Dusting off this tweet from April because this gap in shared understanding of LL

## Insights

- Karpathy argues there's a growing gap in public understanding of current LLM capability, which he describes as shifting from "two groups talking past each other" to a sharper, funnel-shaped divide.
- He estimates roughly 6 billion people (~75% of the global population) have had only minimal exposure to modern LLMs.
- He attributes part of the gap to recency and tier of use: many people's opinions of AI were formed by trying the free tier of ChatGPT roughly a year earlier, and those early impressions have stuck.
- The implication is that public perception of AI capability lags significantly behind the actual frontier, with outdated or limited-tier experiences anchoring most people's mental models.
