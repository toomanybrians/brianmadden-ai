# “a bursting AI bubble would be a much bigger shock to the economy than the burst

## Insights

- Argues a potential AI market bubble bursting would be a bigger economic shock than the dot-com bust of the late 1990s.
- Notes the dot-com bust pushed the economy "to the edge of a liquidity trap," implying the AI bubble's aftermath could be comparably or more disruptive.
- Points out the Fed has far less room to respond this time: the current Fed funds rate is only 4%, compared to the much higher starting point available for rate cuts after the dot-com crash.
- Implies monetary policy tools for cushioning an AI-driven downturn are more constrained now than in the earlier tech bust.

## Quote

> "a bursting AI bubble would be a much bigger shock to the economy than the bursting IT bubble"
