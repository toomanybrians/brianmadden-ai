# i don’t know whether this is a joke or serious but the whole challenge with gene

## Insights

- Argues that generative AI cannot be reliably trusted to follow "simple, generalizable instructions," framing this as the core challenge with the technology.
- Responds to a quoted post sarcastically proposing that AI could simply be given rules like "don't harm people" and "always obey what people tell you to do."
- Claims that broad instruction-following of this kind is not something current generative AI systems can be counted on to do consistently.
- Warns that widespread public failure to understand this limitation will have negative societal consequences ("bite society in the ass").

## Quote

> the whole challenge with generative AI is that it literally cannot be trusted to follow "simple, generalizable instructions"
