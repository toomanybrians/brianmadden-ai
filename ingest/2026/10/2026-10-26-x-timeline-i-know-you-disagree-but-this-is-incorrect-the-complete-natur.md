# I know you disagree, but this is incorrect. 

The complete, natural language art

## Insights

- Argues that a complete, natural-language articulation of intent should be the primary artifact in software development, not the code or a formal spec.
- Frames traditional specs as "lossy abstractions" of that natural-language intent — compressed and therefore imperfect translations of what someone actually wants.
- Extends the same critique to code itself, characterizing it as a "flawed implementation of the idea" rather than the idea in its ideal form.
- Positions this as a direct rebuttal to the view (quoted from @mattpocockuk) that code, not the spec, should be the center of the development process.

## Quote

> The complete, natural language articulation of what you want should be primary.
