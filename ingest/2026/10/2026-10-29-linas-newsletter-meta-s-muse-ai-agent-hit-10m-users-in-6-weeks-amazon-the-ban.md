# Meta’s Muse AI Agent hit 10M users in 6 weeks. Amazon & the banks now decide what it earns 🤖🔒; Revolut finally starts using its banking licences to lend 🏦🇬🇧

## Insights

- Meta's Muse reached over 10 million users across the U.S. and Canada in roughly six weeks, primarily used for shopping — framed as the first AI agent to achieve mass-market adoption.
- Amazon has blocked Muse from its platform, and JPMorgan along with other large U.S. banks have barred similar AI agents from accessing customer accounts.
- On its Q3 2026 earnings call, Meta acknowledged that the next phase of agentic commerce will operate on terms set by merchants and banks rather than by Meta itself.
- Meta reportedly plans to charge merchants (not shoppers) for Muse transactions, a monetization approach the piece says is being accelerated by the company's negative free cash flow.
- The piece positions banks that have blocked password-based agent logins as effectively shaping the "official" access terms agents like Muse will need to operate under.

## Quote

> Meta just conceded that the next phase of agentic commerce will run on terms that merchants and banks set.
