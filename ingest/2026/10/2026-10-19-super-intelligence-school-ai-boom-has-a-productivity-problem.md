# AI Boom Has a Productivity Problem

## Insights

- AI dramatically compresses task-level production time (e.g., a market research report dropping from three days to a two-hour draft), but this doesn't translate into proportionally faster business outcomes.
- The bottleneck in AI-driven work shifts from production to everything downstream: approvals, legal review, revisions, meetings, and handoffs — processes AI hasn't touched.
- The piece distinguishes output (a polished report, a faster email, more ideas) from business results (a decision made, a problem solved, value delivered) — the two are being conflated in productivity narratives.
- Current AI adoption discourse overweights measures like speed of output and usage rates, while underweighting coordination overhead, duplicated effort, and outdated processes that absorb the time saved.
- The core argument: the real challenge isn't deploying better AI tools, it's redesigning the surrounding workflows and organizational processes to actually capture the time savings.
- Raises the risk that the "AI boom" could produce more work volume without genuine productivity gains, absent this workflow redesign.

## Quote

> AI can remove hours from a task while leaving the real bottleneck untouched.
