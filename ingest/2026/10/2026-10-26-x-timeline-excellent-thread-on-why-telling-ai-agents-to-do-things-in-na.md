# Excellent thread on why telling AI agents to do things in natural language is no

## Insights

- Natural language instructions given to AI agents do not guarantee the system will act as the human intends — there's a gap between "what you intend" and "what a system processes."
- A command like "do X" does not implicitly rule out unintended side effects or violations ("don't do Y") — the instruction-following problem includes an underspecification issue, not just comprehension.
- Simple natural-language commands are not a reliable mechanism for direct, precise control over agent behavior.
- The underlying difficulty of translating human intent into reliable machine behavior was already identified in "Rebooting AI" (2019) and has not meaningfully improved since then, according to the poster.

## Quote

> What *you intend* is different from *what a system processes*. — @mmitchell_ai
