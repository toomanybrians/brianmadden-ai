# It remains “very difficult [to find] actual, materially measurable financial impacts” from spending on AI, per new study

## Insights

- A new study (reported via The Information, not independently linked) reportedly finds no measurable financial/productivity impact from enterprise AI spending at scale, contradicting early 2023-era predictions of 10x productivity gains and GDP-level boosts from ChatGPT-class tools.
- Marcus frames the gap between massive AI capital investment (trillions of dollars) and the near-absence of documented ROI as a central, unresolved anomaly in the current AI investment cycle.
- Cited as corroborating evidence: Character.AI, an early generative AI consumer product, has filed for bankruptcy — offered as a concrete case of hype not converting to sustainable business value.
- Also cited: CoreWeave bond yields reaching 15%, interpreted by Marcus as a signal that credit markets are losing confidence in AI infrastructure financing.
- No methodological detail on the underlying study is provided (direct link unavailable), so the "zero evidence" claim rests on secondhand reporting rather than primary data — worth treating as a directional signal rather than a settled finding.

## Quote

> The juxtaposition between the minimal documentable ROI and the trillions of dollars invested in this technology remains perverse and often baffling.
