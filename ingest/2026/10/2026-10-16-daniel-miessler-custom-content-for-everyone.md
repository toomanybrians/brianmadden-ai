# Custom Content for Everyone

## Insights

- Current discourse about small teams using AI to build AAA-rivaling games misses a bigger shift: AI could interview an individual for hours and then generate games custom-built entirely for that one person.
- Personalization could extend to continuously varied content matched to an individual's specific storyline preferences and fetishes, not just genre preferences.
- The same logic applies to TV: instead of indie studios producing scaled-down content, AI could generate full custom series (the example given: a personalized Game-of-Thrones-like series) for a single viewer.
- The argument frames this as a leapfrog scenario — bypassing the indie-creator/small-studio middle layer entirely and going straight from large-studio content to AI-generated, fully individualized content.
- The author flags this as a radical, underappreciated possibility that isn't currently part of mainstream planning or discourse around AI and content creation.
- Gaming is referenced as an early/leading indicator of broader "metaverse"-style personalization trends dating back to a 2022 post.

## Quote

> Soon we'll have games customized to your favorite storylines and fetishes that are continuously varied by AI.
