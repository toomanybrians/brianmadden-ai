# SpaceX Earnings: Heading Towards the First $100 Trillion Company

## Insights

- SpaceX's fastest-growing and now-largest segment is "Super Intelligence" (renting compute, selling Grok/Cursor access), which hit $7.6B in quarterly revenue (+830% YoY) — surpassing both Starlink ($5.25B) and launch ($1.8B).
- The company frames AI's infrastructure bottleneck as having shifted from chips to energy, cooling, and physical deployment speed; SpaceX claims compute buildout paybacks in under a year, with a path from 1.7 to 13+ gigawatts of capacity by end of 2027.
- Starlink's strategic value is being reframed beyond human connectivity: the author argues future dominant network traffic will be AI agents communicating with other agents at planetary scale, and Starlink is positioned as the only network with full global coverage for that.
- The business case rests on vertical integration — SpaceX controls launch, satellite network, data centers, power generation, and (via Terafab) chip design — which is described as a structural moat competitors can't replicate; a SpaceX-Tesla merger is floated to add robotics/physical-AI to the stack.
- Valuation narrative moves from current ~$2T to a $10T case (via ~$1T revenue by 2028-29) to a speculative $100T+ case premised on Elon Musk's claim that AI and robotics will grow the global economy 10x within a decade.
- Named risks: massive capex outpacing revenue (Q4 capex guidance of $35-40B vs. quarterly revenue), a possible AI spending/credit cycle correction flagged by another commentator, and heavy dependence on a single individual (Musk) and shifting political support.

## Quote

> SpaceX has stopped being a rocket company. It's a superintelligence company, a global communications company and the dominant infrastructure company of the Intelligence Revolution.
