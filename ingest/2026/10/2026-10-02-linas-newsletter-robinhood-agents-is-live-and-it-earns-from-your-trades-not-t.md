# Robinhood Agents is live, and it earns from your trades, not the AI 🤖📈; Meta’s Muse now goes head to head with Claude for Small Business 🛍️🤖

## Insights

- Robinhood Agents, launched at the HOOD Summit, resells OpenAI and Anthropic model tokens to users at the models' public price rather than marking up AI usage — the company isn't trying to profit from the AI layer itself.
- OpenAI is reportedly subsidizing GPT-6 Luna usage within Robinhood Agents through the end of the year, suggesting a deliberate land-grab/distribution play by OpenAI rather than a revenue deal for Robinhood.
- More than 150,000 agentic trading accounts have already been opened, indicating fast uptake of agent-based trading interfaces.
- Robinhood's actual monetization appears to come from the trades the agent executes, not from the AI service itself — the agent functions as a funnel into trading activity.
- A "marketplace rule" reportedly steers users away from using their own Claude or ChatGPT accounts and toward Robinhood's bundled agent access, raising questions about lock-in design.
- House Democrats are reportedly raising questions about liability — who is responsible when an AI trading agent loses a customer money — signaling emerging regulatory scrutiny of agentic financial products.

## Quote

> Robinhood Agents, the in-app AI trading agent launched at HOOD Summit this week, isn't built to earn money from AI.
