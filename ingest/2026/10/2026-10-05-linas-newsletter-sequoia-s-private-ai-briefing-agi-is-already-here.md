# Sequoia's Private AI Briefing: AGI Is Already Here 🤖

## Insights

- Sequoia Capital (via partner Pat Grady, in a briefing to Boston College's investment committee) dates the arrival of AGI to November 2025, tied to Claude Code and Opus 4.5 demonstrating that AI agents could execute long, complex tasks autonomously.
- Grady frames the AI market opportunity not against the ~$650B software market but against a $10-20 trillion services market, reframing the addressable opportunity by an order of magnitude.
- Across seven recent Sequoia deals, startup valuations rose an average of ~31x within a single month, which the piece frames as reshaping how founders and LPs should think about round pricing and "two-step" deal structures.
- The briefing reportedly covers a "diffusion gap" (a chart Grady says defines the top startup opportunity) and four startup categories Sequoia is most excited about, including one company that went from $0 to $100M in revenue in 7 days.
- Fast-growing AI companies are described as "owning their intelligence" and reinventing their business model roughly every 4 months, suggesting unusually short strategic cycles in this market.
- The briefing also reportedly addresses frontier-lab dynamics: recursive self-improvement, Dario Amodei's call to "pace the frontier," and "Deploy Co" consulting arms of labs competing directly with application-layer startups.
