# 🔮 The transition is hiding in plain sight #604

## Insights

- A new Google DeepMind paper (co-authored by EV readers Shamil Chandaria and Anil Seth) proposes a five-level framework for evaluating whether an AI system could be conscious: behavior, algorithms, hardware wiring, living/stake-based criteria, and world-connectedness.
- Current AI systems score reasonably on the behavioral level of that framework but perform poorly on the deeper levels (hardware, stakes, world-connection) — the authors' own test scores ranged from 0.003 to 0.083 out of 1.
- Research from Tempo (enterprise portfolio management) finds that most organizations have only partially adopted AI into existing, human-designed workflows, while the small number of teams using AI end-to-end are outperforming peers by a notable margin — suggesting legacy platforms built for human-only workforces are becoming a bottleneck.
- Economist Torsten Sløk raises the idea of an "agentic bank run": as personal AI agents begin automatically moving household cash into higher-yielding accounts, banks could see funding costs rise because their lending model depends on large pools of idle, low-cost deposits.
- OpenAI's release of "dots" is noted alongside Grok Bot and other personal/agentic AI assistants as part of a wave of agents aimed at automating everyday financial and life-management decisions.
