# Multiplayer AI needs to move past the group chat phase. Chats are chaotic, every

## Insights

- Group-chat-style interfaces are framed as a transitional, flawed phase for multiplayer AI tools rather than an end state.
- Shared visibility in group chats is identified as a source of social and management friction — everyone seeing everything creates problems, not just convenience.
- Group chats are described as poorly suited to long-running projects, implying a need for structure/continuity that chat threads don't provide.
- A key critique: current multiplayer AI designs confine AI to working within existing, pre-defined teams.
- The argued real opportunity for AI is facilitating work and collaboration *across* existing team boundaries, not just within them.

## Quote

> Multiplayer AI needs to move past the group chat phase.
