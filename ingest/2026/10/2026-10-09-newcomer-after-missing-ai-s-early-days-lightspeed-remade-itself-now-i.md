# After Missing AI’s Early Days, Lightspeed Remade Itself. Now It’s Poised to Score with Anthropic.

## Insights

- In April 2023, shortly after ChatGPT's release, Lightspeed's co-leaders Ravi Mhatre and Bejul Somaia called an emergency all-firm meeting, telling partners to clear calendars and skip board meetings.
- The trigger was self-assessed competitive failure: Lightspeed had missed early stakes in OpenAI (which rival Khosla Ventures had captured) and had also missed ElevenLabs and Hugging Face.
- The meeting ran seven hours, with partners splitting into subgroups and reconvening; European and Israeli team members stayed on past midnight local time.
- The central question driving the session was framed as: if AI is truly going to change everything, what would it actually mean for the firm to act as though that were true.
