# Latest open artifacts (#25): DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017, American-made models, and new regulatory controls in the US & China

## Insights

- China's MOFCOM proposed requiring frontier AI models and related technologies to get government pre-approval before international release; reactions are mixed, with some viewing it as a serious regulatory precedent and others arguing it's more targeted at preventing deals like the (attempted) sale of startup Manus to Western buyers rather than at frontier models broadly.
- The U.S. DHS issued an order barring DeepSeek (company and models) from federal civilian procurement; it's unclear whether this extends to finetunes or models trained on DeepSeek outputs (e.g., Nvidia's Nemotron models reportedly rely heavily on DeepSeek in training).
- DeepSeek publicly released its internal inference stack, DeepInfer, reportedly far more efficient than existing open-source tools (vLLM/SGLang) for its models, with estimates of up to 90% gross margin when DeepSeek sells tokens via this stack — notable as the first time a leading lab has shared such internal tooling.
- Xiaomi's MiMo-V2.6-Pro-RL, trained with heavy reinforcement learning investment, is now top-ranked among open models on Artificial Analysis's leaderboard; Xiaomi also released an in-depth tech report and training environments, unusual transparency at this scale.
- DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017, a new 552B-A16B model supporting image inputs, is drawing attention for strong performance at low price and continues DeepSeek's practice of using the permissive MIT license, contrasting with a broader industry trend toward more restrictive, bespoke licenses.
- Reflection's Beam-1.0-501B-A23B is described as the strongest open American model under a permissive license (Apache 2.0), notable for token efficiency even though not the most capable overall, suggesting a well-developed post-training process.

## Quote

> We found the reaction to this to be overblown – as the document states that open-source technologies will be set up for automatic approvals.
