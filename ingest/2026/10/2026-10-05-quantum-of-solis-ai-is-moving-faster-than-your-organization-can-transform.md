# AI Is Moving Faster Than Your Organization Can Transform

## Insights

- ServiceNow AI Index data cited: overall enterprise AI maturity scored 35/100, down nine points year-over-year, even as AI tool usage expands — only 30% of organizations have integrated workflows across business functions.
- Distinguishes "iterative AI" (optimizing existing workflows, asking if they're even the right workflows to keep) from "innovative AI" (starting from a desired outcome and inventing new products/services/business models) — argues organizations need both but are structurally biased toward the former.
- In services businesses, AI shifts the core question from "how do we make services more efficient" to "what does the service become when AI can deliver the outcome itself" — automating existing services only lowers cost, while rethinking outcomes creates new value categories.
- "AI-forward" is framed as distinct from "AI-enabled": prioritizing possibilities unachievable without AI, while emphasizing human judgment, creativity, and oversight working alongside AI rather than autonomous replacement.
- Describes a maturity progression (Evaluator → Experimenter → Expander → Advancer → Transformer) where only the final stage involves genuine reimagining and enterprise-wide agentic AI orchestration rather than scaled automation.
- Outlines three competitive postures — AI Native (AI embedded in DNA/workflows), AI First (AI as default work method), AI Forward (deliberate human-AI collaboration with governance) — and argues incumbents should ask what an entrepreneur with no legacy assumptions would build in their industry today.

## Quote

> You can't cut your work to growth. You can't automate your way to innovation.
