# Tech leaders really need better media training on the most obvious AI questions:

## Insights

- Tech industry leaders are criticized for being poorly prepared in media interviews when asked about AI regulation, risks, and job impacts — responses are characterized as either absent or weak.
- The critique suggests a binary pattern: there's little middle ground between no answer and a bad answer on these three recurring topics.
- Bezos's offered analogy on AI regulation: rather than regulating the technology itself (the "knife"), regulation should target harmful outcomes/misuse (the "murder").

## Quote

> So the simple example would be, you don't want to regulate knives. You want to regulate murder.
