# [Legal Notice] Updates to Google Cloud Terms of Service and AI guidelines

## Insights

- Google Cloud is introducing specific legal terms for "long-running agents" and other agentic AI offerings, updating service-specific terms to address IP ownership and liability questions tied to different types of AI agents.
- New "Agentic AI Responsible Use Guidelines" are being added as a supplement to existing acceptable-use and generative AI policies, specifically targeting emerging risks from autonomous agent deployment and user privacy protection.
- The updated Terms of Service extend Google's use restrictions to cover customers' AI agents directly, not just human use of the platform — and require customers to indemnify Google if their AI agents violate the Acceptable Use Policy.
- This shift places legal responsibility/liability on enterprises for the actions of autonomous agents they deploy on Google Cloud, treating agent behavior as equivalent to direct customer conduct under the contract.
- Separately (and seemingly unrelated to AI), Google also tightened payment-risk suspension rights and termination-for-incurable-breach clauses, suggesting broader contract hardening alongside the AI-specific changes.
- The notice frames this as anticipating "emerging agentic risks" as agent technology "continues to evolve," indicating Google sees current agent capabilities as an unsettled, fast-moving risk category requiring proactive contractual guardrails.

## Quote

> These changes clarify your and our responsibilities regarding the deployment and management of AI agents by and within your organization.
