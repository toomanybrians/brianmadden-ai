# AMD Acquired World Labs for $8.2B. 30 Silicon Valley Startups Raised $2.66B Last Week

## Insights

- AMD acquired World Labs (a "world model" AI startup founded Jan 2024 by Fei-Fei Li and collaborators from Stanford, Facebook AI Research, Google Research, and Meta Reality Labs) for $8.2B in an all-stock deal — AMD's second-largest acquisition ever, after Xilinx. World Labs had raised $1.23B since founding.
- The rationale given: world-model research gives AMD visibility into where AI workloads are heading (reasoning, robotics, simulation, physical AI), letting it shape chip/software/systems roadmaps years ahead and bolstering its open-ecosystem competition with Nvidia. Li joins as AMD EVP and chief scientist, reporting to CEO Lisa Su.
- Instinct, a "personal agent for everyday life" startup with only 14 people, raised a $1B Series C at $10B valuation — just 33 days after a $250M Series B at $2.5B, a 4x valuation jump in about a month. It claims approaching $1B in annualized transaction volume while still invite-only.
- Meta's consumer AI agent product "Muse" launched the same period and topped app stores, suggesting a competitive consumer AI-agent race is heating up alongside Instinct.
- Weekly Silicon Valley-area funding snapshot: 30 startups raised $2.66B total, heavily skewed to enterprise ($1.11B across 11 startups) and consumer tech ($1B, effectively Instinct alone), with smaller amounts in semiconductors, HR, fintech, hardware, construction, healthcare, hospitality, AI research, and telecom.
- Other notable M&A in the same week: Dynatrace acquired AI-observability startup Arize for $915M; Salesforce acquired Listen Labs (AI customer research); Pearson acquired Workera (AI skills assessment); Inworld acquired Ultravox (real-time voice agents).

## Quote

> World-model expertise will tell AMD where AI workloads are heading (reasoning, robotics, simulation, physical AI).
