# Does your work cost less on Opus 5.5 now that its tokens are 20% cheaper? My 3-prompt guide shows which jobs do before you move them.

## Insights

- Introduces "model ergonomics" as a concept: the fit between an AI system and its user, measured by the effort needed to direct it, understand its output, correct it, and decide next steps.
- Argues the amount of usable intelligence from a model is gated not by raw capability but by how well human and model can communicate requirements and preserve prior decisions across corrections.
- Claims Opus 5.5 is notably easier to steer for writing tasks and produces "extraordinary" results on visual work, with efficiency gains that change how much work feels feasible.
- Suggests that when correction and steering get easier, users become willing to delegate more ambitious assignments, not just more of the same small tasks.
- Frames repeat/continued usage (rather than one-off capability) as the key commercial metric for AI labs, tied explicitly to Anthropic's reported IPO trajectory.
- Notes Sonnet 5.5 was released alongside this piece and is described as reinforcing the same ergonomics argument, suggesting a pattern across Anthropic's model lineup rather than a one-off.

## Quote

> By ergonomics, I mean the fit between the system and the person using it.
