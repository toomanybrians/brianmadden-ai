# The Morning Download: Oracle Creates AI Workspace on ChatGPT

## Insights

- Oracle launched an "AI Workspace" inside ChatGPT that acts as a single entry point to all Oracle applications — users ask a question and ChatGPT returns answers synthesized across connected Oracle tools/data, with plugins assembling the right tools based on described business goals rather than specific commands.
- Oracle framed the move as making its software stack "agent-ready," reflecting a shift toward natural-language/agent interfaces as the primary way users interact with enterprise software, rather than navigating applications directly.
- OpenAI's CEO described the Oracle partnership as OpenAI's "coming out party" to prove enterprise readiness, a positioning need sharpened by competitive pressure (Anthropic's S-1 filing) to demonstrate real enterprise traction.
- A core enterprise AI problem named directly: businesses have struggled to link disparate corporate data and applications together and make that unified data accessible to AI — this integration gap is what the Oracle/OpenAI workspace is meant to solve.
- Oracle and OpenAI are co-building "Oracle AI Security Mesh," a managed cybersecurity service to find/fix vulnerabilities, with Altman noting AI is surfacing vulnerabilities faster than organizations can patch them across the software supply chain.
- Separately, Anthropic's S-1 disclosed Claude wrote over 90% of code contributions to its own codebase in September, and Amazon's internal memo to engineers admits it's hard to define lasting standards for "good AI-powered engineering practices," urging rapid adoption focused on customer outcomes instead.
- Oracle is diversifying its cloud chip offerings (Google TPUs, Cerebras, OpenAI's Jalapeño chip) explicitly to give customers flexibility amid continued AI compute constraints; SK Hynix separately projects the memory-chip shortage will persist through 2030.
