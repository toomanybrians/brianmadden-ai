# You Need a New CFO

## Insights

- The CFO archetype has historically shifted with the scarcest skill of the era: Big 4 accountants for controls, bankers for capital-raising, FP&A/PE types for planning — and now the scarce skill is designing the "operating system" (data, workflows, agents, controls) the company runs on.
- AI has removed the historical bottleneck of fragmented, hard-to-wrangle financial data, which previously consumed most of a finance team's energy; AI-native point solutions now exist across the entire finance stack (ERP, tax, procurement, AR, audit) and increasingly perform the work itself rather than just helping manage it.
- A new role, the "finance engineer," is emerging — a finance person (often with some engineering fluency) who builds internal tools/agents using products like Codex or Claude Code, connecting systems and fixing workflow bottlenecks that used to require dedicated engineering support.
- Finance headcount norms are compressing: the old ~5%-of-headcount heuristic is shifting toward leaner teams targeting ~2%, prioritizing talent density and AI fluency over headcount; senior domain experts (not juniors) are described as getting the most leverage from AI tools.
- Planning is moving from periodic (monthly/annual) cycles to continuous forecasting and live scenario modeling, partly driven by harder-to-forecast usage-based revenue models (tokens, API calls) and agents acting directly in the P&L.
- As software takes on more financial work, auditable control chains (tracing numbers to source, logging which agents were involved, enforcing approval rules before actions like posting journal entries) become a critical product feature, enabling speed rather than restricting it.
- Finance's mandate is expanding into strategic territory: compute/capital allocation, pricing, build-vs-buy decisions, and how work is divided between people and software are increasingly treated as finance decisions enabled by richer, real-time data.

## Quote

> The job is still finance, but more of the job now is designing how finance gets done.
