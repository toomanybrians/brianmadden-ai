# 🦦 Otter.ai Raises $320M in Non-Equity Financing. 32 Silicon Valley Startups Raised $4.41B Last Week

## Insights

- Otter.ai secured $320M in non-dilutive growth financing from General Catalyst, having evolved from a transcription tool into an enterprise AI meeting assistant now surpassing $100M ARR and 40M users worldwide.
- Enterprise AI tools dominated the week's funding: Town (AI assistant for teams, $108M Series B), Blacksmith (code validation infrastructure, $66M), Greptile (AI code review/testing agents, $50M at $750M valuation), and AuxoAI (enterprise AI deployment, $20.7M).
- Enterprise was the single largest funding category for the week at $1.62B across 9 startups, out of $4.41B total raised by 32 startups.
- Tenstorrent raised $1.5B at a $6.2B valuation to build an alternative AI compute stack (chips, servers, licensable IP, open-source software) aimed at reducing customer lock-in to Nvidia and AMD.
- Notable AI-related M&A: OpenAI acquired Orchestra Bio (AI agents for drug development), Meta acquired Sundial (AI data analytics platform), and Salesforce acquired Braze (customer engagement platform) for $5.66B.
- An AI model research lab ("Flapping Airplanes") raised $1B Series A at a $6B valuation, among the largest single rounds of the week.
