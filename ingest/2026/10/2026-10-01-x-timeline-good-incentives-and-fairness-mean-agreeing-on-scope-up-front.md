# Good incentives and fairness mean agreeing on scope up front, keeping the evalua

## Insights

- Apollo Research outlines principles for "embedded evaluations" of AI systems: scope should be agreed upon upfront, the evaluator must remain independent, and both the developer's and evaluator's confidential IP should be protected.
- The group argues voluntary commitments to allow such evaluations are likely insufficient, and advocates that embedded evaluations should eventually be mandated by law.
- Their transparency standard calls for publishing evaluation reports by default, with explicit disclosure of what access the evaluator was granted.
- Developers may redact sensitive details from reports, but the redacted report must state what type of information was removed and whether that omission affected the findings.
- One component is explicitly non-negotiable: the evaluator's overall conclusion can never be redacted, regardless of what other content is withheld.

## Quote

> The evaluator's conclusion can never be redacted.
