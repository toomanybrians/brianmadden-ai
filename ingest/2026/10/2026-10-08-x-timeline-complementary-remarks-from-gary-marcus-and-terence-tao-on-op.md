# Complementary remarks from Gary Marcus and Terence Tao on OpenAI’s giant math dr

## Insights

- OpenAI released a large batch of new mathematical results claimed to come from an internal frontier model, consulted on with an outside advisory group (IAS's Advisory Group on Mathematics and AI).
- Gary Marcus argues the announcement lacks basic scientific disclosure: no details on architecture, whether proofs were one-shot or iterative, whether Lean was used to verify, failure rates, or training/data augmentation methods.
- Marcus contends this opacity makes it impossible to judge whether the result generalizes beyond math — it could be a genuine AGI step or just clever exploitation of Lean plus synthetic data in a narrow, verifiable domain.
- He criticizes social media reaction as an "ignorant cheering section" applauding without understanding what's being claimed or asking scientific questions.
- A cited critic (Bryna Kra) objects to publishing results via GitHub dump and press release rather than normal peer-reviewed scientific channels, calling it unsustainable for the research ecosystem.

> The real news here isn't the result; it's not what we were told.
