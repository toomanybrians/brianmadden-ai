# Meta Muse vs OpenAI Dots vs Grok Bot: Which AI Agent Should You Use?

## Insights

- Within a 49-day span, three major AI companies released autonomous agent products: xAI's Grok Bot, Meta's Muse, and OpenAI's Dots.
- Pricing models differ sharply: one option is free to start, another offers multiple AI "workers" for $20/month, and a third (built on GPT-6 Astra) costs around $100 and connects to thousands of apps.
- These agents are positioned as able to research, manage projects, and work across applications autonomously, continuing tasks even after the user closes their laptop.
- The piece explicitly challenges a "more expensive = better" assumption, noting the $100 agent isn't necessarily superior for work tasks compared to cheaper or free alternatives.
- Framed as enabling an "entire AI team" for as little as $20/month, suggesting a shift toward assembling multiple cheap AI agents rather than relying on one premium tool.

## Quote

> The $100 agent isn't necessarily better for your work than the free one.
