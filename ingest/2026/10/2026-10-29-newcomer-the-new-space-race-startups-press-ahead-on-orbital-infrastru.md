# The New Space Race: Startups Press Ahead on ‘Orbital Infrastructure,’ Test Feasibility of Data Centers

## Insights

- Google tested an experimental satellite running Gemini queries against four onboard TPUs, part of early research into whether AI chips can function reliably in orbit.
- Cowboy Space, founded by Robinhood cofounder Baiju Bhatt and backed by Index Ventures' Jan Hammer, launched the Reason-1 satellite, which experiments with beaming solar power to Earth via laser.
- Cowboy Space unveiled a purpose-built rocket designed to carry five racks of Nvidia Vera Rubin GPUs into orbit, and plans to sell launch services to competitors under the brand "Big Iron Express," directly challenging SpaceX.
- Bhatt frames the company's goal as making space-based compute cost-competitive with terrestrial data centers, pursuing extreme vertical integration (rockets, satellites, power transmission) to get there.
- Starcloud, another orbital data center startup backed by Benchmark, is already valued at $2.3 billion, signaling investor appetite for this category despite its unproven feasibility.
