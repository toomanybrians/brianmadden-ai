# Anthropic's IPO Can't Wait Forever

## Insights

- Anthropic's leaked draft prospectus (via Reuters) reportedly shows an ~$8B operating loss on ~$4.6B revenue last year, plus a committed $518B in future cloud/compute/infrastructure spending — figures that predate its Opus 4.6 model launch (Feb 2026), which reportedly drove a surge in "tokenmaxxing"-style agentic coding revenue.
- Anthropic has pushed its IPO timeline from October to targeting a pre-Thanksgiving November debut, amid questions about whether revenue growth can support a $2 trillion valuation.
- Broader IPO market conditions look shaky: SB Energy (SoftBank's AI data center subsidiary) delayed its IPO in September, and Oura postponed its IPO indefinitely citing market conditions.
- OpenAI is reportedly in early talks for a private raise of at least $30B at a $1.4T valuation to delay a public offering until 2027, with annualized revenue reported near $70B; Sam Altman has attributed delay to model-safety concerns rather than business weakness.
- AMD acquired Fei-Fei Li's World Labs (a "world models" / simulated interactive environment startup) for $8.2B in all-stock, partly to better understand evolving AI compute workload demands; AMD's Instinct chip line reportedly has 14 gigawatts of demand from OpenAI, Meta, and Anthropic combined, against McKinsey's projected 62 gigawatts of total 2026 global AI workload capacity.
- Data from Odin shows the largest VC megafunds (2010–2020 capital raised) also backed the most unicorns, with Accel as an outlier — fewer funds raised but comparable unicorn count to a16z and Sequoia.
