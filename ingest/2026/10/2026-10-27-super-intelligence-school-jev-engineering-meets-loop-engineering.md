# Jev Engineering Meets Loop Engineering

## Insights

- The piece distinguishes two failure modes in intelligent systems: knowing the right answer vs. knowing what to do with it — a model can solve a problem correctly yet still choose poor actions, repeat wasted steps, or keep working past the point it should stop.
- "Jev Engineering" is framed as the discipline of structuring individual decisions — defined operations for choosing, scoring, and evaluating information.
- "Loop Engineering" is framed as the discipline of structuring what happens after a decision — connecting actions, observations, and feedback into an ongoing process, including knowing when to continue or stop.
- The two are presented as complementary but insufficient alone: a well-made decision can still be mismanaged in execution, and a well-managed loop can simply repeat a bad decision indefinitely.
- The argument is that improving system performance may depend less on scaling model capability and more on how decision-making and process/action management are organized around the model.

## Quote

> A system can give you the right answer, make the wrong move, repeat the same mistake and still convince you that everything is working as expected.
