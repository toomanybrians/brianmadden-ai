# Starling Bank’s Sovereign AI still depends on Google and NVIDIA 🤖🇬🇧; Ramp is building the operating system for AI-native companies 📲🤖

## Insights

- Starling Bank claims to be building the "first banking AI model" trained on UK sovereign cloud, but the claim reportedly holds only with three unspecified qualifiers.
- At the time of announcement, the model had not yet been trained on real customer data, despite being slated to run Starling's fraud detection starting early 2027 and later power an AI assistant.
- At least three other lenders already run similar transaction models, with one of them already running on a non-US cloud — undercutting the "first" framing.
- Starling's "sovereign" cloud setup routes customer data through Google Cloud before it reaches Civo, the UK-owned provider, raising questions about how sovereign the architecture actually is.
- The piece frames this as relevant to Engine, a Starling software business, and to the fraud-detection performance benchmarks Starling will need to meet in 2027.
