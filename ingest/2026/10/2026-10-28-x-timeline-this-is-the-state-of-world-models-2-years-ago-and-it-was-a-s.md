# This is the state of world models 2 years ago, and it was a specialized model tr

## Insights

- Two years prior to this post, world model technology was represented by a specialized model trained specifically on DOOM gameplay—not a generalist world model.
- That early DOOM model used diffusion techniques (the same class of model used in AI image generation) to predict the next frame of gameplay, producing a playable game at 20 fps with no underlying traditional game engine.
- The comparison point is Genie 4, described as generating game-like, explorable environments in real time from text prompts (e.g., "Neogothic brutalist city inhabited by robots and horses"), with the ability to iteratively add elements like dragons or NPCs on the fly.
- The implied trajectory is rapid progress from narrow, single-game diffusion models to more flexible, generalist, real-time interactive world generation within a two-year span.
