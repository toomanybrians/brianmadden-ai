# TG-AI-F: AI finds critical security flaws, banks block AI agents, and Wall Street gets nervous, oh my!

## Insights

- Two PHP vulnerabilities (dubbed "PHP2Shell") were discovered using AI, allowing remote code execution on vulnerable sites; PHP powers an estimated 70% of websites, making the exposure widespread.
- A Palo Alto Networks threat intelligence VP (Sherrod DiGrippo) characterizes AI-discovered vulnerabilities as a "new normal" rather than a one-off event, predicting sustained high "vulnerability velocity" going forward.
- AI is described as unusually effective and fast at finding high-severity and critical security vulnerabilities — a capability framed as both the story's core threat and its systemic implication.
- Social media reaction amplified the event with dramatic framing ("most serious 48 hours in cybersecurity history," "Black October"), but sources closer to the issue reportedly view it as serious rather than catastrophic — suggesting a gap between public alarm and practitioner assessment.
- The piece frames this alongside other unspecified items (banks restricting AI agents, Wall Street unease) as part of a broader week of growing anxiety about AI pushing boundaries in regulated and security-sensitive domains, though those specific stories aren't detailed in the excerpt.
