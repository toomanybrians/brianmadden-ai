# Learn to Build a Multi-Model Team of AI Agents With Frontier Models

## Insights

- Current multi-model AI use involves significant manual overhead: users act as the connective tissue between Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, etc., manually carrying context and outputs between them.
- A Cohere study (published Oct 14) found that well-organized teams of AI agents outperform single agents on difficult coding tasks.
- In one Cohere experiment, pairing a powerful model with cheaper models matched the performance of a much more expensive all-premium team, at roughly 70% lower cost.
- Poorly organized agent teams aren't just inefficient — the study found careless agent addition can increase costs 3-7x, indicating team structure/orchestration matters more than raw model count or capability.
- Recent frontier model releases (Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Sonnet 5.5, GPT-6 Astra, Gemini 4 Argon as of Oct 13) each have distinct specialized strengths (planning, research, coding, software interaction, review).
- These different models can now be combined into a single workspace with assigned roles, starting simply (connected agents, basic instructions, visual workflow) and scaling up (adding memory, tools, automations) incrementally.

## Quote

> How you organize the team matters enormously.
