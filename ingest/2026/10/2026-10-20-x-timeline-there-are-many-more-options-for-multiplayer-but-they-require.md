# There are many more options for multiplayer, but they require serious experiment

## Insights

- Current "multiplayer AI" implementations are largely limited to group-chat formats, which the author argues is an immature stage of the concept.
- Group chats as a multiplayer AI structure create problems: they're chaotic, expose all information to all participants (raising social and management friction), and don't hold up well over long-running projects.
- A deeper limitation is structural: chat-based multiplayer AI keeps AI usage confined within existing team boundaries, rather than enabling it to work and coordinate across teams — which is framed as AI's larger potential.
- Moving beyond chat requires genuine experimentation with organizational form/structure, not just bolting AI onto existing communication tools.
- This added effort is framed as worthwhile because novel organizational forms could become a durable strategic advantage, whereas a generic AI-chat capability would not differentiate a company.

## Quote

> Multiplayer AI needs to move past the group chat phase.
