# Improving security and productivity doesn’t require a multi-year overhaul.

See

## Insights

- The piece frames security and productivity in financial services/insurance (FSI) not as competing priorities but as both suffering from the same root cause: fragmented point solutions accumulated over years, creating tool sprawl and inconsistent policy enforcement.
- It argues governance is shifting to the browser as the primary workspace boundary, since SaaS, collaboration, and AI-enabled tools now route through it rather than traditional endpoint controls.
- Proposes consolidating "digital employee experience" (DEX) measurement by correlating existing telemetry (app launch times, authentication friction, workflow completion times, service desk tickets) rather than buying new monitoring tools.
- Recommends converting branch/contact-center/contractor endpoints into lightweight centrally-managed thin clients to shrink attack surface and reduce device management overhead, rather than maintaining full local operating systems.
- Flags that specialized technical roles (engineers, quants, data scientists) often fall outside standard security models, requiring dedicated secure cloud development environments rather than blanket policies.
- Advocates for "intelligent" audit/monitoring triggers (e.g., recording only when PII is accessed) as a way to get compliance-grade visibility without blanket employee surveillance.
