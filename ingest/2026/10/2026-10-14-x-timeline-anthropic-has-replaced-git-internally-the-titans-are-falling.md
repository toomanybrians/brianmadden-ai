# Anthropic has replaced Git internally. 

The titans are falling. https://t.co/YX

## Insights

- Anthropic built and deployed an in-house source control system to replace Git for internal engineering use.
- The company cites a roughly 12x increase in daily code merges over four months (~2,500/day in May to 30,000+/day in September) as the driver.
- Anthropic frames the shift as necessitated by infrastructure bottlenecks: existing tooling was designed for human-scale development, not agent-scale.
- The scale of code changes implies a large share of commits are being generated or driven by AI agents rather than human developers alone.
- The post's framing ("the titans are falling") positions this as a signal that even foundational, long-standing developer tools (like Git) are being displaced as AI changes the pace and nature of software development.

> In September, we merged more than 30,000 [code changes] per day.
