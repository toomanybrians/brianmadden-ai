# Ari Emanuel on his New Memoir, ‘Ragent’ Reputation, and Changing Hollywood

## Insights

- Emanuel argues that if AI drives GDP growth to 3–3.5%, it will free up people's leisure time (citing anecdotal evidence of "Baby Friday" — people starting weekends on Thursday) and fuel a boom in live entertainment, sports, and music consumption.
- He expects AI to make content production cheaper and more abundant, citing recent AI-assisted box office films, and predicts growth in new formats like micro-dramas and social content.
- He acknowledges AI will cause job losses and "dislocation" in some sectors, calling it a predictable pattern of technological change, not inherently good, but frames the net effect for entertainment and creative communities as positive.
- Uses a Steve Jobs analogy (humans as the slowest movers across land until the bicycle) to frame AI as a tool that amplifies human capability rather than threatens it.
- Expresses conditional optimism about AI, contingent on effective regulatory "guardrails" being established, while voicing unease about the current political environment's ability to deliver that oversight.
- Separately notes that AI developers (OpenAI, Google, Amazon, Meta, xAI, Perplexity, Nvidia, Suno, others) face roughly 130 lawsuits, cited as context for the pressure AI puts on traditional media/entertainment business models.

## Quote

> "If AI works... we're going to be out of a deficit and... you're going to have a lot more free time." — Ari Emanuel
