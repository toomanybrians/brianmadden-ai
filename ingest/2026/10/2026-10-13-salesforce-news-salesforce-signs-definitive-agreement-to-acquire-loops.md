# Salesforce Signs Definitive Agreement to Acquire Loops

## Insights

- Loops is an AI analytics platform that monitors business performance metrics, explains causes of change, and surfaces improvement opportunities using machine learning models — positioned as going beyond traditional dashboards to explanatory/prescriptive analytics.
- Salesforce frames this as advancing an "agentic analytics" strategy: moving from passive data visibility (Tableau's traditional strength) to systems that identify drivers of performance and prioritize actions.
- Loops has "headless" agentic capabilities, meaning it can operate outside a dedicated UI — triggered directly from Slack and email to continuously monitor KPIs and proactively surface anomalies/trends.
- Post-acquisition, Loops's technology and team will be folded into Tableau specifically, rather than operating as a standalone product, suggesting it's meant to extend Tableau's core offering rather than create a new product line.
- Deal framed explicitly as a step from "insight to action in the flow of work" — emphasis on closing the loop between analytics and automated/agent-driven action, not just reporting.
- Close expected early in Salesforce's FY2027, subject to standard conditions — still a pending, not completed, acquisition.

## Quote

> "Analytics should help businesses understand what drives their results and decide what to do next." — Tom Laufer, CEO and Co-Founder of Loops
