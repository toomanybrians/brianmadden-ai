# Meta’s Personal Agent Protocol just opened the fight over who controls Agentic Payments 🤖💳; Musk is putting Claude inside Grok Bot, and the AI race is moving from models to agents 🤖🧠

## Insights

- Meta and Sierra launched the Personal Agent Protocol (PAP) on October 6, with backing from Stripe, Shopify, and Walmart; it designates the business (merchant) as the party that vouches for a consumer's AI agent, rather than card networks (Visa, Mastercard) or an AI platform, which is how rival designs handle it.
- The entity that vouches for an agent gains control over customer data and liability in agentic commerce, and is positioned to eventually collect fees on agentic payments.
- The piece frames the central unresolved issue as a "payment clause" not yet written into these protocols — whoever controls that clause determines whether Stripe/Shopify or the card networks end up owning the credential that every AI agent carries.
- Amazon's decision to cut off Meta's Muse agent is cited as a triggering event behind the broader dispute over agent credentialing and control.
- The newsletter also teases coverage of Anthropic's Claude Commerce Agents (a "merchant-side bet" on agentic commerce) and rival designs like Visa's Trusted Agent Protocol and Google's Universal Commerce Protocol, positioning PAP as one of several competing standards for agentic payments.
