# The big bitter lesson question in the applied AI layer is that as models get bet

## Insights

- Frames a core open question for applied AI: as foundation models get more capable, what is the best mechanism for diffusing that intelligence into enterprise workflows and products.
- Notes an apparent irony: improving model capability (longer-running tasks, better performance) changes what "diffusion into the enterprise" even requires — the post is cut off before explaining how.
- Quoted counterpoint (@signulll): as underlying models grow more intelligent, founders need to engineer less intelligence into their own products — in the limit, AI products could become "just intelligence without any fluff."
- @signulll further notes that today, building AI software is still much more about adding [something beyond raw model intelligence] — implying current products still require significant non-model engineering/scaffolding.
