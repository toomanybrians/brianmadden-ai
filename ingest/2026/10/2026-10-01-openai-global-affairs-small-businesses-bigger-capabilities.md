# Small Businesses, Bigger Capabilities

## Insights

- OpenAI reports ~4 million small-business employees worldwide used its tools in a single week in September, with nearly 20% working at firms of fewer than 10 employees.
- Agentic AI usage among small businesses is accelerating fast: it accounted for two-thirds of small-business output tokens in August, double its share in April.
- Case examples show AI compressing time-to-revenue: a real-estate software company built and tested a new feature with Codex in about a day (~$40 in compute costs) and landed a $10K/month contract six days later; an entrepreneur generated 48 personalized sales contacts in 10 minutes and booked a paid training session within 10 days.
- Framed as solving a classic small-business bootstrapping problem: needing revenue to afford specialized expertise, but needing that expertise to generate revenue — citing Fed survey data that 57% of small firms struggle to reach customers/grow sales and 54% struggle to cover operating expenses.
- At broader scale, ~100 million ChatGPT messages per week begin with "How do I...?"/"How can I...?" across 16 languages, out of ~1.2 billion weekly users (avg. 36 messages/week, up from 28 earlier in the year).
- OpenAI Academy (2 years old) has reached 4M+ people via online courses and 250+ live trainings across small-business owners, teachers, nonprofit leaders, veterans, older adults, and journalists — positioned as necessary skill-building alongside access.
- Adoption is described as increasingly global: at least 1 in 10 adults use ChatGPT weekly in 169 countries/territories, and at least 1 in 3 in 77 of them.

> The measure of progress isn't simply how capable AI becomes; it's how much more capable people become with it.
