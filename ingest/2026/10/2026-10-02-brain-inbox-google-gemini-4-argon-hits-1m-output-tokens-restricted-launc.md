# 🚀 Google Gemini 4 Argon hits 1M output tokens, restricted launch

## Insights

- Google's Gemini 4 Argon and OpenAI's new releases both landed the same day at identical pricing ($2/1M input, $10/1M output tokens), suggesting pricing convergence in the frontier model market.
- Argon extends output generation from a prior 64,000-token ceiling to 1 million tokens, aimed at letting models work through long, multi-step tasks (full codebase migrations, legal document review, security audits) without stopping.
- Argon's initial release is restricted to vetted cybersecurity defenders via Google's "Fairwind Program" (governments and trusted partners only), with general availability still pending.
- OpenAI countered with a speed-focused approach: an "Ultrafast" tier hitting 300 tokens/sec (8x faster in Codex, 6x via API) plus a new cheaper model, GPT-6.1 Sol, priced at one-fifth of its flagship Astra model.
- A separate research note reports a method where LLMs manage their own context/memory, outperforming human-designed memory strategies by 47.6% — framed as a sign that manual scaffolding approaches may be getting displaced by models self-managing these tasks.
- Both companies frame these releases around sustaining longer, uninterrupted "agentic" work sessions rather than single-turn responses, whether via longer output windows or higher throughput.
