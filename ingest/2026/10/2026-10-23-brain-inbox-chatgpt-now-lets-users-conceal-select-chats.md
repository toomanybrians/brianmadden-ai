# ⚙️ ChatGPT now lets users conceal select chats

## Insights

- OpenAI used GPT-5.6 Sol to discover two critical PHP vulnerabilities (affecting ~70% of websites with identifiable server-side language) before attackers did, giving maintainers a "Defender's Window" to patch ahead of disclosure — illustrating AI's dual-use potential in offensive/defensive security.
- Security experts quoted note that AI isn't creating new weaknesses but dramatically accelerating the discovery and exploitation of long-existing ones; CVE disclosures rose 81% quarter-over-quarter, and one expert expects accelerating exploitation for 2-3+ more years.
- The piece frames this as evidence that "security-by-obscurity" is obsolete and argues AI labs bear responsibility for helping the world prepare for consequences of tools that can be weaponized at scale.
- Anthropic launched Claude Duet, a proactive always-on agent (built on Opus 5.5) for professionals/small business owners — it sends morning briefs, works autonomously while users are away, flags items needing attention, and integrates with 6,000+ marketplace plug-ins (Gmail, Slack, QuickBooks, Stripe, etc.).
- Duet positions Anthropic against OpenAI's "Dots" and Meta's "Muse" in a emerging category of persistent personal/professional AI agents; the article suggests multiple agents will coexist in workflows rather than one winning outright, given low switching costs between models.
- OpenAI introduced "Locked Chats" in ChatGPT, letting users PIN/biometric-protect sensitive conversations (health, personal matters) in a separate space excluded from memory — addressing device/password-sharing privacy gaps (75% of partnered Americans share phone passcodes per Pew) that prior "Temporary Chat" didn't solve.
