# Tottenham Hotspur Launches Ask Spurs on Agentforce

## Insights

- Tottenham Hotspur's deployment is built on a two-year data unification effort: ~30+ disparate ticketing/retail/hospitality/app/web systems were replaced or consolidated, with 10+ systems now connected into a single ecosystem.
- Salesforce Data 360, integrated with Snowflake via "Zero Copy," unifies 4.6 million fan accounts into single "SpursID" profiles — underscoring that the AI agent's utility depends on a prior, non-trivial data integration layer, not just the agent itself.
- The flagship agent, Ask Spurs, is multilingual (11 languages) and handles ticketing/membership/retail/event queries for both logged-in and guest users; it reports an 86% engagement rate but only 20% full resolution by the agent alone, with the rest escalated to human support — a concrete, modest autonomous-resolution figure amid higher-level marketing framing ("agentic enterprise").
- The platform extends beyond fan-facing chat into commercial operations: hospitality/events contracting and finance tracking were moved from manual/spreadsheet processes to automated quoting and contracting, with real-time payment visibility via integrations with Stripe and Workday.
- Alongside the technical rollout, Salesforce is funding/supporting a STEM and AI-workplace-readiness program ("Spurs in the Community") for local schools, framed as preparing young people for an AI-shaped job market — a CSR/workforce-development angle attached to the commercial deal.
- Vendor language ("agentic enterprise," "world's #1 AI CRM") should be read as marketing positioning; the only hard performance metric offered is the 20% full-resolution rate, with "86% engagement" left undefined in terms of what counts as an engagement.
