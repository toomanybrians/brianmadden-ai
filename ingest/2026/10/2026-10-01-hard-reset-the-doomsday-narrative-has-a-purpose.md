# The Doomsday Narrative Has A Purpose

## Insights

- TESCREAL (Transhumanism, Extropianism, Singularitarianism, Cosmism, Rationalism, Effective Altruism, Longtermism) is a term coined by Émile Torres and Timnit Gebru to describe a shared ideology among tech elite driving AGI development, which they characterize as "eugenics on steroids" aimed at merging technology and biology into a post-human species.
- Both AGI "accelerationists" and "doomers" are argued to share this same underlying worldview — they disagree only on whether the resulting future is desirable, not on the premise itself.
- Gebru argues that framing AI risk as an abstract existential threat is a deliberate distraction that benefits the same billionaires funding both AI labs and the "AI safety" institutions warning about them, making concrete harms (data centers, pollution, copyright, labor) look trivial by comparison.
- A White House meeting produced "voluntary safety standards" from AI CEOs and Trump, who called the deal "morally binding"; author frames this as PR management ahead of midterms amid polling showing majority public support for pausing AI development.
- Gebru defends her 2021 "stochastic parrots" paper against criticism (e.g., from Anthropic's Jack Clark calling it a "cognitive virus") by pointing to ongoing real-world LLM failures like AI misdiagnosing cancer, arguing the problem today is over-trust in systems, not overestimating risk.
- Author's throughline: public can't fully verify AI executives' claims of losing control over models, but can scrutinize whose financial and ideological interests are served by which risk narratives get amplified.

## Quote

> "If I stand to get lots of money from a particular thing, why do I seem to be the person saying this particular company might build something that kills us all?" — Timnit Gebru
