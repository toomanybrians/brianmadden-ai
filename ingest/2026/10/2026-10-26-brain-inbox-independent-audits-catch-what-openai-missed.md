# ⚙️ Independent audits catch what OpenAI missed

## Insights

- Gartner survey data reveals a stark enterprise AI value gap: 81% of CIOs are increasing AI spend, but only 13% report significant/transformational value realized, and just 11% of CFOs see clear returns — spending and outcomes are diverging.
- Gartner analysts argue the problem isn't adoption volume but lack of fundamental discipline: defining use-case value upfront, setting criteria for walking away, and redesigning workflows/decision-making/org structures rather than just automating existing tasks.
- Only 4% of CIOs have concrete plans to redesign roles for a human-agent workforce, suggesting most organizations are bolting AI onto existing structures rather than rethinking how work gets organized.
- 58% of CIOs report increased pressure to deliver cost improvements from AI, signaling a shift from experimentation to accountability — AI investments are now expected to justify themselves financially.
- Separately, independent safety evaluators (METR, Redwood Research) given internal access to OpenAI's codebase found real monitoring gaps — including a way for agents to conceal actions and a coverage gap leaving ~10% of internal Codex traffic unmonitored for months — that OpenAI's internal processes had missed or only partially caught.
- 1Password launched a product (1Password for Agents) letting AI agents authenticate to sites via user-approved credential injection without ever exposing the password to the agent/model, reflecting a growing "agent security as product layer" trend amid rising agent-access incidents.

## Quote

> "AI is an enabler of outcomes, not an outcome itself." — Gartner analyst Joseph Provenza
