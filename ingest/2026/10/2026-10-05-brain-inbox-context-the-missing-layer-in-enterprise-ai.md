# ⚙️ Context: The Missing Layer in Enterprise AI

## Insights

- General-purpose AI assistants are "fluent, not grounded" — they draft and summarize well but lack access to the real, current, authoritative company data needed for decisions like pricing, inventory, risk, or forecasting.
- A "context tax" offsets AI's productivity gains: employees (often analysts) still have to verify AI outputs by finding correct data and reconciling conflicting sources before acting on them.
- Ungrounded AI produces confident-sounding but unreliable answers, creating an illusion of progress while the underlying verification work simply shifts back onto people.
- Gaps in sanctioned AI tools' capabilities push employees toward "shadow AI" — pasting sensitive data into unapproved tools — which inflates apparent productivity while quietly building compliance and data-exposure risk.
- The argument frames enterprise AI ROI as blocked not by model intelligence but by disconnection from business data/context; closing that gap is positioned as the next necessary step for AI to be trusted with real business decisions.

## Quote

> The productivity promise of AI was never really about deploying more assistants — it was about closing the distance between "AI that can talk" and "AI that can be trusted."
