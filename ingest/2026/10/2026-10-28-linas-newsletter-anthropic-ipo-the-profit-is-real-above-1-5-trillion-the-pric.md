# Anthropic IPO: The Profit Is Real. Above $1.5 Trillion, the Price Isn't 🤖

## Insights

- Anthropic's S-1 shows revenue growth from $41M (2023) to $4.6B (2025) to $16.3B in H1 2026, with compute/infrastructure costs dropping from 1,056% of revenue in 2023 to 48% in Q2, producing its first operating profit ($974M) in six quarters of disclosed data.
- Despite profitability, the company carries $267.7B in non-cancelable commitments (13.6x trailing revenue) plus $321.4B in additional estimated/conditional leases, with cloud providers (Google, Amazon, Microsoft) owed payment "regardless of actual usage."
- Revenue concentration risk is notable: 80% of revenue is consumption-based and many top customers lack long-term contracts; a prior 18-day government shutdown of its top two models didn't stop billing, illustrating volatility exposure.
- Governance is tightly controlled — a single Class F share held by seven co-founders carries a fixed 50.1% voting stake, and a trust elects 4 of 7 board directors, meaning public (Class A) shareholders "generally will not be able to affect" company decisions.
- Other disclosed details: a SpaceX compute deal worth up to $120.5B cancelable on 90 days' notice, 5-7% of nine-month revenue tied to customers violating usage policy, an $80.7B first-half net loss described as "almost entirely accounting," 5% equity given to a Public Benefit Fund, and 92 pages of risk factors including a warning about AI's potential "catastrophic or existential risks."
- Valuation estimates from investors have compressed from a prior $965B private round (5 months earlier) to a range of $1.4T–$2T for the IPO, implying 71x–101x trailing revenue or 30x–43x Q2 run-rate.

## Quote

> Class A holders "generally will not be able to affect" outcomes.
