# 🔮 The end of screen time

## Insights

- Author conducted a personal experiment: after accidentally leaving his laptop at a hotel, he did a week of work using only voice interaction with ChatGPT on his phone.
- He frames office work as historically built on a "fiction" — the assumption that work means sitting at a desk typing into Slack, Word, Excel — and argues AI agents are starting to dissolve that assumption.
- The shift is enabled by OpenAI's GPT-Live (released July), a feature allowing users to direct AI agents by voice conversation rather than text/typing.
- He compares the experience to talking to Jarvis (Iron Man's AI assistant), describing it as voice-directed AI that can act on a "workshop" — currently limited to digital tasks.
- The piece previews a deeper account of his setup and findings from a week of voice-only work, suggesting screen-based/typing-centric knowledge work may not be a permanent given.

## Quote

> It feels a little like talking to Jarvis, Iron Man's AI assistant.
