# JPMorgan shut AI Agents out of Chase. Mercury let them in, and AI Assistants now recommend it 5 to 1 😳🏦; Stripe brings Privy wallets to the XRP ledger, where 14.3M agent payments moved $22K 🥶🤖

## Insights

- JPMorgan Chase is blocking outside AI agents (e.g., Meta's Muse) from logging into customer accounts or initiating money movement, per CEO Jamie Dimon.
- The rationale tied to protecting $2.7 trillion in average deposits that cost JPMorgan very little (Chase Savings pays just 0.01% interest), which is cheap funding for the bank.
- The block doesn't prevent agents from persuading customers to move money to other institutions, leaving a gap in the strategy even if logins are blocked.
- Mercury, a fintech competitor, takes the opposite approach by letting agents connect via a hosted MCP server, and AI assistants reportedly already recommend Mercury over Chase by more than 5-to-1.
- The piece references an earlier prediction that large banks would first block unidentified agents, then build proprietary ones, and eventually sell access — framing JPMorgan's move as fulfilling the first two stages.
- Separately, Stripe is bringing its Privy wallets to the XRP Ledger, raising questions about who ends up owning the "wallet layer" of agentic finance, and how this squares with Stripe's claimed "blockchain agnostic" stance alongside its own Tempo chain and OUSD stablecoin.
