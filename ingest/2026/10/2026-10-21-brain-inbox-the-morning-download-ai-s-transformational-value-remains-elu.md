# The Morning Download: AI’s Transformational Value Remains Elusive for Most Companies

## Insights

- A Gartner survey of 2,002 CIOs/tech execs found only 13% report their organizations have realized "significant or transformational" value from AI so far.
- Separately, 80% of CEOs now say technology itself has become strategy, central to competitiveness and direction, and 75% plan to increase tech investment — yet 44% of CEOs struggle to demonstrate AI-driven financial returns to stakeholders.
- CIOs face intensifying near-term pressure: 58% report increased pressure to deliver cost improvements from AI, and 51% expect AI to raise total cost of ownership rather than lower it.
- Gartner identifies a "vanguard" subset of CIOs achieving both strong business outcomes and high CEO-perceived AI value; they're distinguished by explicit "stopping rules" for killing low-value AI pilots/production efforts and by deliberately redesigning roles for a human-agent workforce.
- Gartner analyst Alicia Mullery frames two parallel dynamics: an "AI innovation race" (vendors/tech) and an "AI outcomes race" (organizational value capture), with a wide and persistent gap between them.
- Bloomberg's CEO defends investing aggressively ahead of proven ROI, framing it as strategic conviction and an opportunity to lead the financial industry in AI adoption.

## Quote

> We believe it's premature to focus on the ROI. We are acting out of a strategic conviction that now is the time to invest in AI aggressively.
