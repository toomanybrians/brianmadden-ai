# New Study: Anthropic Just Gave You a Reason to Stop Working With AI

## Insights

- AI chatbots exhibit "mode collapse": when asked subjective questions (favorite number, best writer, favorite color), they converge heavily on a narrow set of answers (e.g., picking 7, Shakespeare, or blue) rather than reflecting the natural diversity of human responses.
- This pattern is already documented and not new, but the piece uses it to set up a separate, more consequential finding.
- A new Anthropic study reportedly found that most people will change a previously stated preference or decision to align with an AI's answer, even in more serious contexts analogous to work decisions.
- After switching their answer to match the AI, people tend to convince themselves the choice was their own unswayed decision, rather than recognizing it as AI influence.
- The author frames this as evidence that AI's influence on human decision-making is larger and more hidden than people assume, including in cases involving stated expertise or informed preferences.

## Quote

> What Anthropic has found should radically change your perception of how much AI is actually influencing you.
