# Artificial Analysis updated their ratings based on their new, closed benchmarks,

## Insights

- Artificial Analysis revised its model rating methodology, moving from v4.3.2 to v5 and introducing new benchmarks described as closed (not publicly accessible in advance).
- Under the updated benchmarks, open-source/open-weight models dropped significantly in ranking relative to top closed/proprietary models.
- The author interprets this shift as confirmation of a prior suspicion: that open models' earlier benchmark performance was inflated because those benchmarks could be gamed or optimized against.
- The implication raised is that benchmark openness/accessibility itself may have been distorting perceived model quality rankings, favoring models trained or tuned with knowledge of the test set.

## Quote

> This is what we've felt the whole time, and now we see it reflected in reality after they switch to benchmarks that aren't able to be [gamed]
