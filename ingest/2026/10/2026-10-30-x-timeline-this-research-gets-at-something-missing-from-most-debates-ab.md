# This research gets at something missing from most debates about AI’s economic im

## Insights

- The claim is that most AI economic-impact debates focus on individual agent capability, missing the role of coordination between agents.
- Organizations outperform not simply because of their best individual performers — organizational value comes from how people coordinate, not just individual skill.
- By analogy, smarter individual AI agents alone won't transform the economy; the argument is that agents capable of coordinating with each other are what could drive economic transformation.
- This framing is presented as emerging from a new DeepMind research effort ("The Economics of Intelligence") studying the economics of AGI, co-developed by Alex Imas, Andrew Koh, and Shane Legg.

## Quote

> Organizations are more than the sum of their best performers, so smarter individual agents won't transform the economy on their own.
