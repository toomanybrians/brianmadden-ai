# Anthropic’s new IPO prospectus shows why profits didn’t save its $2 trillion valuation 😳📈; Revolut stayed down for hours after Google Cloud recovered 🤔☁️

## Insights

- Anthropic's updated S-1 prospectus (via WSJ, obtained Oct 20) shows two consecutive quarters of adjusted operating profit, yet investor valuation talk has dropped from $2 trillion to $1.5 trillion — suggesting profitability alone isn't what underpins valuation expectations.
- The prospectus discloses an $80.7 billion net loss for the first half, alongside compute commitments already above $600 billion that could rise to $750 billion.
- Some of Anthropic's cloud partners have reportedly begun refusing to provide additional compute capacity, raising questions about supply constraints even as commitments grow.
- The piece frames the valuation cut as being driven more by concerns over slowing growth than by the scale of the reported loss itself.
- The analysis ties Anthropic's situation to broader market conditions, noting two other AI-related listings stumbled this month, which is raised as a factor relevant to Anthropic's upcoming November roadshow.
- A December revenue figure is flagged as the number the revised valuation now hinges on.
