# I think the big add is that even if they lose on a particular sub, it's because

## Insights

- Argues that competitive losses on individual AI subscription products aren't necessarily bad signs — if a competitor is "crushing it" on a sub, it indicates a thriving market generating more ideas, projects, and apps overall.
- Frames AI subscription competition as non-zero-sum: growth from one player doesn't just redistribute existing demand but expands total demand for AI products and services.
- Cites a claim (from @atmoio) that Anthropic's losses on its $200/month subscription tier are smaller than expected, suggesting subsidized high-end plans may be financially sustainable long-term.
- Asserts that AI inference is "quite profitable," and that this profitability is calculable from first principles rather than just anecdotal.
- Implies unit economics of premium AI subscriptions are healthier than commonly assumed, challenging a narrative of AI providers burning cash to subsidize heavy users.

## Quote

> This is all a "pie bigger" move. It's not zero sum.
