# At 740M parameters, it’s competitive across benchmarks – even outperforming some

## Insights

- Google DeepMind released EmbeddingGemma 2, described as their first natively multimodal open model built for on-device embeddings.
- The model unifies text, code, images, audio, and video into a shared embedding space, rather than handling modalities separately.
- At 740M parameters, it reportedly performs competitively on benchmarks, in some cases outperforming specialist models more than twice its size.
- Suggested developer use cases include multimodal search (e.g., locating a moment in a video via a voice memo).
- It can reportedly be paired with Gemma 4 to enable private, on-device functionality (detail cut off in source text).

## Quote

> At 740M parameters, it's competitive across benchmarks – even outperforming some specialist models more than twice its size.
