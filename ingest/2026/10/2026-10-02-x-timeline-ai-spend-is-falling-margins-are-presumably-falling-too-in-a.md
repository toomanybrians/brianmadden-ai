# AI spend is falling. Margins are presumably falling, too.

In a rational world t

## Insights

- Cites the Ramp AI Index showing a decline in business AI spending, attributed to price cuts at frontier labs plus cheaper/more efficient models at standard and lite tiers.
- Notes open-source models remain under 5% of business AI spend, implying frontier/proprietary models still dominate enterprise usage despite falling prices.
- Argues falling spend implies falling margins for AI providers, and frames this as a signal that current startup/company valuations in AI are irrational and unsustainable.

## Quote

> AI spend is falling. Margins are presumably falling, too. In a rational world that would be game over for the crazy valuations.
