# A thing I see happening at large organizations as a result of AI is the expansio

## Insights

- AI is enabling senior managers at large organizations to directly monitor the entire firm, expanding both their role and their span of control.
- This shift represents a meaningful change to how companies operate day-to-day, not just a marginal efficiency gain.
- Expanding centralized monitoring via AI is framed as a low-effort alternative to full organizational redesign — it's "straightforward to implement" by comparison.
- The implication is that some organizations may choose this centralization path over more structurally ambitious changes to how the firm itself is organized.

## Quote

> A thing I see happening at large organizations as a result of AI is the expansion of the role and span of control of senior management.
