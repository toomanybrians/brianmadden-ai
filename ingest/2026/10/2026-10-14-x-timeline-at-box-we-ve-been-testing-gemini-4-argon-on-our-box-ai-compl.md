# At Box, we've been testing Gemini 4 Argon on our Box AI Complex Work eval for th

## Insights

- Box tested Google's Gemini 4 Argon model against its internal "Complex Work" evaluation benchmark for AI agents over roughly a week.
- Box reports a 14 percentage point improvement over the prior model (Gemini 3.1 Pro) across all its tests, which it characterizes as a "huge upgrade."
- Box frames the new model as now "firmly at the frontier" of model capability, based on its own enterprise-task evaluation rather than public benchmarks.
- Google's own announcement positions Gemini 4 Argon as broadly available at launch: paid-tier Gemini API/platform access for developers and enterprises, Ultra subscribers in Google Antigravity, and Ultra/Pro subscribers in the Gemini app and Search.
- The release reflects a pattern of enterprise software vendors (Box) rapidly benchmarking and integrating new frontier models into existing AI agent products as they ship.

## Quote

> It's a huge upgrade over Gemini 3.1 Pro, jumping by 14 percentage points across all our tests, and firmly at the frontier.
