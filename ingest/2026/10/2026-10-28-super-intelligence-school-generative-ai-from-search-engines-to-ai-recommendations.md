# Generative AI. From Search Engines to AI Recommendations

## Insights

- Frames the shift in online discovery as moving from search (returning lists of links) to AI systems that synthesize, compare, and narrow down options before a user ever visits a website.
- Describes a spectrum of AI-mediated discovery: AI-powered search (explains and compares results), and recommendation systems (go further by suggesting options tailored to stated needs or past behavior).
- Notes these systems' usefulness is bounded by the quality of accessible information and how well they interpret the user's query — they can miss good alternatives, use outdated data, or misjudge fit.
- Identifies a business visibility problem emerging from this shift: companies must figure out how to get recommended by AI systems rather than just ranked in search results.
- Flags a parallel technical question for developers/learners: understanding the mechanics of how a system ingests a query, retrieves relevant information, compares possibilities, and produces a recommendation.
- Positions the overall trend as discovery becoming "more conversational, more personalized, and increasingly focused on helping people evaluate their options" across shopping, travel, education, software, and business research.

## Quote

> Online discovery is becoming more conversational, more personalized, and increasingly focused on helping people evaluate their options.
