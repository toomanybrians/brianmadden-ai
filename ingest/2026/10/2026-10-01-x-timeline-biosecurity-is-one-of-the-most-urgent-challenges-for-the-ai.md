# Biosecurity is one of the most urgent challenges for the AI era. Bringing SynthI

## Insights

- Google DeepMind positions biosecurity as one of the most urgent challenges of the AI era, tying it directly to risks from AI-generated biological content.
- The team extended SynthID (previously used for watermarking AI-generated media) to biology, creating "SynthID Bio" to watermark AI-generated proteins.
- DeepMind reports a proof-of-concept synthesis of AI-designed proteins that are simultaneously functional and watermarked — demonstrating the watermark doesn't compromise the protein's biological activity.
- The work was published in Nature, and the SynthID Bio tools are being open-sourced so the broader research community can build on and adopt the watermarking approach.
- Framing suggests watermarking is intended as a traceability/provenance mechanism for AI-generated biological building blocks, analogous to content provenance efforts in other AI domains.
