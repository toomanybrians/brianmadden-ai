# 🔮 Anthropic’s S-1 in charts

## Insights

- Anthropic's S-1 is framed as settling whether AI is "real": the filing shows a company with actual customers, cash flow, and a large cash reserve, not just a research lab.
- The filing reportedly does not resolve whether Anthropic can sustain the revenue growth needed to fund its committed compute spending.
- Growth is still very high but decelerating, and over 80% of current revenue comes from models that are six months old or newer — implying heavy dependence on continual new model releases to sustain growth.
- The company may post a marginal loss in Q3, attributed to payments made to its foundation.
- The analysis states customer concentration is not a significant risk factor in the filing.
- A separate, more detailed breakdown (six key questions, 39 charts) was published via "AI Investment Brief," with this piece offering a condensed take focused on demand signals, shifting economics, and growth constraints.
