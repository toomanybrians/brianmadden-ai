# Karpathy estimates only about 5,000 people have seen the most advanced internal

## Insights

- Karpathy estimates roughly 5,000 people worldwide have direct exposure to the most advanced internal/frontier AI capabilities — a vanishingly small group relative to global population.
- The gap between those with access to cutting-edge AI capability and everyone else is described as widening, not stable, shifting from "two groups talking past each other" to a sharper, funnel-shaped divide.
- Napkin math cited suggests around 6 billion people (~75% of the global population) have barely engaged with AI at all.
- The framing implies a steep capability-awareness curve: a tiny elite at the frontier, a large uninformed base, and shrinking middle ground between them.
