# Centralized Data to Intelligence | ERP Systems

## Insights

- Argues ERP's core value isn't software installation but "engineering how a business operates" — defining processes, data models, system responsibilities, and controls.
- Warns that digitizing a broken process just makes existing inefficiencies faster to reproduce rather than fixing them.
- Claims the usefulness of advanced analytics, LLMs, and AI-driven automation is bottlenecked by the quality and consistency of the underlying operational data and systems feeding them.
- States that adding AI/intelligence on top of conflicting records or poorly defined business rules does not resolve the underlying data weakness.
- Frames a well-built ERP as providing accountability, bottleneck visibility, and reduced manual reconciliation — positioned as the foundation required before AI-driven analysis can be trusted.

## Quote

> Building a modern ERP is ultimately an exercise in engineering how a business operates not merely choosing the software it uses.
