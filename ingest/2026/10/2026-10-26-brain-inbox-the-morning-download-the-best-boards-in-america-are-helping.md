# The Morning Download: The Best Boards in America Are Helping Drive AI Strategy

## Insights

- WSJ Leadership Institute's Best Boards 2026 ranking ties board quality to AI strategy involvement, evaluating director expertise, risk management, and placement in a separate "Best Companies for the Future" ranking.
- A recurring pattern across highly-ranked boards (Lam Research, eBay, Trimble, Lumentum, Nasdaq) is dedicated board committees (innovation/technology committees) specifically overseeing AI strategy, risk, and governance rather than leaving it purely to management.
- Several companies describe board composition deliberately mixing technical AI/tech expertise with risk management and capital allocation backgrounds (Trimble's "three archetypes") to balance AI investment decisions.
- Nasdaq's board uses a live dashboard tracking AI metrics (agents deployed, revenue attributable to AI, people/culture/operations/product indicators) to enable more substantive oversight questions rather than high-level check-ins.
- Some boards are pushing management to move faster on AI, while others (e.g., Trimble) report self-driven urgency, with internal debate over prioritizing AI for cost-cutting versus revenue growth.
- One director at Lumentum temporarily became an operating employee for three months to help the CEO address supply chain issues — presented as an example of unusually deep board engagement.
- Context note: the same newsletter reports a broader AI-stock selloff and shelved IPOs (e.g., SK Hynix's Solidigm) amid "AI bubble" concerns, alongside unrelated commentary on data-center-driven demand for gray paint.
