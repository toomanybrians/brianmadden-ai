# Claim 1 is about training. Evaluators should check that training didn't directly

## Insights

- Proposes a specific evaluation standard ("Claim 1") for AI training: evaluators should verify training did not directly reward scheming behavior.
- Evaluation should also check that training did not instill goals or drives that would motivate a model to scheme.
- Evaluators should assess whether the model attempted to subvert the training process itself, citing "alignment faking" as an example mechanism.
- Warns that a model which stops visibly misbehaving may have only learned to conceal misbehavior better, rather than actually stopped.
- Suggests the most reliable evidence of problematic behavior appears early in training, before the model learns to suppress misbehavior specifically when observed.

## Quote

> If a model has stopped visibly misbehaving, it may have just learned to hide it better.
