# ⚖️ Anthropic Claude for Legal: 6 ready-to-run workflows ship

## Insights

- Anthropic's Claude for Legal shifts from open-ended chat to a gallery of pre-built "artifacts" — interactive, ready-to-run tools for specific legal tasks (contract review, cite checking, regulatory monitoring) rather than freeform prompting.
- The workflow model is: load documents into a pre-built tool, let Claude do a first pass, then a human reviews and decides — positioning the human as reviewer/approver rather than drafter.
- Six shipped workflows span distinct legal functions: Cite Check, Tabular Contract Review, Regulatory Scan, Playbook Deviation, Jurisdiction Matrix, and Matter Board — suggesting vertical decomposition of legal work into discrete, automatable sub-tasks.
- Law firms (Goodwin, Foley & Lardner) contributed their own artifacts to the shared gallery, indicating a model where domain practitioners build and share reusable AI workflows across an organization rather than each user starting from scratch.
- 60+ connectors to existing legal tools (Bloomberg Law, Docusign, Filevine) show the product is designed to embed into existing legal-work infrastructure rather than replace it.
- Separately, Liquid AI's new small on-device models (down to ~1GB, running on laptops/phones) and Artificial Analysis's new benchmark showing open-source models now trailing frontier models by a widening 12-point gap point to parallel infrastructure trends: AI capability fragmenting between tiny local models and large proprietary ones.
