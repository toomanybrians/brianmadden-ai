## Insights

- Google's Gemini is being positioned as an AI agent capable of planning and executing multi-step tasks, not just responding to prompts.
- The agent can operate across business apps and systems, suggesting integration into enterprise workflows beyond a single app context.
- Gemini can delegate subtasks to "subagents," indicating a multi-agent architecture rather than a single monolithic model handling all work.
- The system auto-selects the best underlying model for a given task, but users retain manual override to pick a specific model.
- Third-party models are being incorporated into the picker, starting with Anthropic's Claude, with plans to expand to open-source and other private models.
- The agent is being given its own workplace identity, including an email address — treating it as an addressable entity within organizational systems rather than just a tool.
