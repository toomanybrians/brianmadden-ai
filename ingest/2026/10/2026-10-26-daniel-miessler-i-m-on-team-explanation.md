# I'm on Team Explanation

## Insights

- Draws on David Deutsch's argument (via Bertrand Russell's turkey/chicken example) that explanation is superior to prediction as a measure of understanding: prediction can be accurate for a long time while resting on a false model, and fails catastrophically when the hidden context changes.
- Frames the relationship as one-directional: good explanations typically generate predictive power, but accurate predictions don't guarantee any real explanatory understanding.
- Values frontier AI specifically for its ability to generate high-quality explanations at arbitrary levels of abstraction (technical detail down to extreme compression), treating this as a marker of genuine intelligence.
- Describes a personal AI tool/prompt pattern ("5 Levels," built in Fabric as `create_5_sentence_summary`, invoked via `/ws` in his LifeOS setup) that compresses an idea into progressively shorter summaries (5 words down to 1 word) as a demonstration of this explanatory capability.
- Concludes that understanding is the actual goal of inquiry, and explanation — not prediction or demonstration — is the primary mechanism for transferring understanding between people.

## Quote

> With Explanation you can usually make predictions, but with predictions alone you often lack an explanation for why they're accurate.
