# Anthropic Haiku 5.5 💸, OpenAI GPT-6 Intelligent UI 🖥️, Google Playgrou

## Insights

- Anthropic's Claude Haiku 5.5 cuts per-token cost ~90% vs. Haiku 4.5, landing at $0.10/M input and $0.50/M output tokens — matching OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna pricing, which the piece frames as a deliberate race to make inference cost a non-factor for developers.
- Haiku 5.5's cheap pricing only applies to prompts under 100,000 tokens (above that, cost jumps 5x); roughly 90% of real-world requests reportedly fall under that threshold. It ships with a 1M token context window and 128K max output.
- Suggested use cases for cheap small models include high-volume summarization/classification, acting as a low-cost subagent alongside larger models (Sonnet/Opus) in coding pipelines, and powering real-time customer support or browser automation.
- OpenAI's GPT-6 "Intelligent UI" update moves ChatGPT away from plain text responses toward auto-generated interactive formats — clickable diagrams, live calculators, tappable buttons/forms — described as building "mini-apps" on demand without coding, currently limited to the Chat tab (not Work or Codex).
- Two GPT-6 variants split by tier: Sol for paid users, Luna for Free/Go.
- Separately, Google Labs launched "Playground," a no-code tool where users describe a game in natural language and AI generates it, framed as a test of how far natural-language interfaces can replace traditional creation tools (e.g., game engines).
