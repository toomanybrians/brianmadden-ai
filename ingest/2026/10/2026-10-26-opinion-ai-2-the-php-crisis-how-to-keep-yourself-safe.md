# The PHP Crisis: How to Keep Yourself Safe

## Insights

- Two critical vulnerabilities (severity 9.2/10) were discovered in PHP, the software underlying millions of websites including WordPress sites, allowing attackers to potentially run malicious code on servers without logging in.
- The flaws went undetected for over a decade until AI models, including OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol, were used by researchers to find them — suggesting AI can surface vulnerabilities humans missed for years.
- The same AI capability cuts both ways: attackers can use similar AI tools to scan for and exploit weaknesses faster, raising the stakes and urgency of the response.
- Cloudflare reported millions of attack attempts, and some governments took websites offline as precautionary measures, though the piece distinguishes attempted attacks from confirmed breaches.
- A website can appear fully functional and secure (strong passwords, clean code) while still running vulnerable software underneath — surface normalcy doesn't indicate safety.
- The author frames AI security tools (from OpenAI and Cloudflare) as part of the practical defense, alongside safeguards for AI agents and connected accounts.

## Quote

> You could have a perfectly normal-looking website, with everything working as usual, and still have no idea that the software behind it needs an urgent security update.
