# Anthropic Subscriptions Offer 5x+ More Value Than OpenAI

## Insights

- AI subscription plans are heavily subsidized relative to API pricing, and despite being a small share of revenue (~10% for Anthropic), they consume a disproportionate share of inference compute (~40%), dragging down blended revenue per MW by tens of millions of dollars.
- Subscription "value" isn't a fixed number — it depends on the specific (plan, model, workload) combination, since credit-consumption ratios for different models/token types can diverge sharply from their API price ratios.
- At current limits, Anthropic's mid-tier Claude plans (Opus-class models) offer roughly 5x the API-equivalent value of OpenAI's comparable plans, making Anthropic subscriptions a markedly better deal for daily-driver usage.
- OpenAI recently halved the value of its $200/month plan and introduced a new $500 tier, while largely avoiding public backlash — attributed partly to favorable DevDay PR timing and a grandfather period for existing subscribers.
- The two labs take different approaches to curbing subsidy losses: Anthropic quietly reduces API-equivalent value for premium models as it releases them, while OpenAI opted for an abrupt, uniform cut to "Fable-level" limits across its whole plan lineup.
- Chinese AI labs' subscription plans remain subsidized and offer good value, though less so than OpenAI's historical generosity — averaging somewhat under the ~12x per-dollar value ratio seen from OpenAI.
- Labs can and do silently alter subscription limits (e.g., via undisclosed A/B tests on usage caps) without announcing changes, which the article's measurement methodology was sensitive enough to detect.

## Quote

> Anthropic is an overwhelmingly better deal, offering ~5x the API-equivalent value across the board.
