# Did AmEx and Perplexity just launch an AI CFO for small businesses? 🤖📊; Stripe bought its way into lending on payments it doesn’t process 💳💸

## Insights

- American Express and Perplexity launched roughly 10 "Skills" inside Perplexity Computer that use Amex Small Business Card data to generate cash-flow forecasts, reconciliation, and tax-prep support for small businesses — described as an "AI CFO" tool.
- The tool is deliberately read-only: it can forecast finances but cannot execute payments, which is framed as a strategic choice rather than a technical limitation.
- The reasoning given: whoever controls payment authority captures most of the value in AI CFO products for SMEs, so AmEx is retaining that authority rather than ceding it to Perplexity.
- Perplexity's gain from the deal is distribution — access to AmEx's small-business cardholder base — while AmEx keeps the payment/lending layer in-house.
- The piece positions this launch against competing small-business AI offerings from Anthropic (Claude for Small Business) and Meta (Muse), and flags a future signal to watch: whether AmEx converts these AI forecasts into an actual lending channel.

## Quote

> The AI CFO (if we can really call it like that) can now forecast a company's cash but cannot pay a single bill.
