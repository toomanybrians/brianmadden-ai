# 🔮 AI & Math 2.0, Pakistan’s solar hedge & who controls computing++

## Insights

- OpenAI's release of math manuscripts/proofs (framed by Tao as "Math 2.0") suggests AI is shifting mathematics away from prioritizing first-to-solve credit toward exposition, community-building, and opening new research directions, since raw problem-solving has become "unsustainable" as a differentiator.
- A tension is emerging between AI producing proofs/answers and humans retaining understanding — if AI systems aren't required to explain their reasoning, knowledge could outpace comprehension, breaking a ~300-year historical link (traced to Condorcet) between discovery and shared understanding.
- The new "Association for Human Mathematics" is calling on mathematicians to boycott working with OpenAI, reflecting real institutional/professional anxiety; a Fields medallist reports PhD students and postdocs in "total panic" over AI solving problems that previously organized years of academic careers.
- A WEF-linked "Next-Generation Computing Framework" argues compute is now strategic infrastructure, and recommends: (1) stress-testing supply-chain sovereignty for crisis scenarios rather than assuming peacetime access, (2) planning for energy demand since quantum computing still relies on conventional compute for control/error-correction, and (3) identifying binding constraints (electricity, data, skills) before investing further in raw compute capacity.
- Separately, an independent researcher used AI coding tools to investigate a possible new planet, illustrating a pattern where AI-assisted amateurs may take on more frontier research work while experts shift to larger, currently unaddressed problems.

## Quote

> If we don't, we'll have more answers and less understanding.
