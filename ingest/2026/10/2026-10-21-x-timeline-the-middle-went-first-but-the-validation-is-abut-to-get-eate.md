# The middle went first, but the validation is abut to get eaten as well by both t

## Insights

- Proposes a three-part breakdown of software work: deciding what to build, building it, and checking that it works (validation/testing).
- Argues the "building" (middle) part was automated/disrupted first by AI tooling.
- Claims validation is now being absorbed next, driven by both AI models themselves and the surrounding tooling/harnesses built around them.
- Predicts "knowing what to build" — the deciding/judgment layer — will be the last part of software work to resist AI automation.
- Implicitly frames this as a sequential erosion: each layer of knowledge work falls to automation in order of how structured/checkable it is.

## Quote

> In this three-tier model it's the "knowing what to build" that will be our last hold out.
