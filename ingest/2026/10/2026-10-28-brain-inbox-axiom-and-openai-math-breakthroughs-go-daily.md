# 🧮 Axiom and OpenAI: Math Breakthroughs Go Daily

## Insights

- Axiom's AI agent swarms reformalized the ~15,000-page "Classification of Finite Simple Groups" proof into ~43.1 million lines of machine-checkable Lean code in 24 days, with two human advisors who worked on the original proof guiding the hardest gaps; the process surfaced 34 deviations from the published proof and generated one new theorem.
- OpenAI and Anthropic each independently produced a Lean-verified refutation of the Exponential Time Hypothesis on the same day (October 16) using internal models, one of which (Anthropic's) found the core algorithm with no human input.
- OpenAI also claims proofs of the local smoothing conjecture (all dimensions) and rigorous existence proofs for 4D quantum field theories including QCD, but is routing all three claims to outside expert "shepherds" (including a Nobel laureate and the original ETH proposers) for verification rather than claiming credit itself.
- The piece frames the new bottleneck in mathematical discovery as not generation but verification/comprehension: machine-checked logic settles whether a proof is valid, but explaining what a result *means* still requires human experts.
- Axiom's founding mathematician Ken Ono is quoted (via a linked interview) arguing that "knowledge has become cheap" while "knowing how to use and verify it has become expensive" — a reframing of what intelligence/expertise now means once AI outpaces human problem-solving on crafted test questions.
- Contextual business signals in the same issue: Anthropic filed for a Nasdaq IPO (reported ~$1.5T valuation, 1,368% YoY revenue growth, swing to adjusted profit, but >$600B in compute commitments); OpenAI shipped a cheaper/faster "computer use" model (GPT-6.1 Terra) aimed at routine screen-based tasks at a fraction of flagship pricing.
