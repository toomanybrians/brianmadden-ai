# Evernorth (Ripple Treasury) IPO on Nasdaq. 27 Silicon Valley Startups Raised $853.4M Last Week

## Insights

- Anthropic acquired Opaque Systems, a UC Berkeley RISELab spinout that lets enterprises run AI on sensitive data using hardware-enforced privacy and cryptographic verification — folding confidential-computing capability directly into a frontier AI lab.
- Enterprise AI security and governance drew significant capital in a single week: Harmonic Security ($50M), Reach Security ($50M), and Opsin ($21M) all raised rounds for AI security/governance platforms, suggesting investor focus on securing and governing AI deployments inside organizations.
- Agentic infrastructure is a recurring funding theme — startups raised for agent-building platforms (Swamp Club), agent-to-agent enterprise finance (Causa Prima), AI agents for enterprise workflows (Parallel Loop), and payments infrastructure for enterprise AI agents (AIsa).
- M&A activity shows large enterprise software incumbents acquiring narrow AI/agentic capabilities: Salesforce bought Loops (agentic analytics), Workday bought C1 (agentic identity platform), and Freshworks bought Torii (AI spend governance) — pointing to platform players absorbing point solutions for agent-era enterprise needs.
- Clinical and healthcare AI continues to attract large rounds, led by Suki ($90M for clinical AI agents) and Abby Care ($82M for an AI platform for family-led home care), indicating AI adoption pressure in regulated, labor-intensive service sectors.
- Overall weekly totals ($853.4M across 27 startups, 15 exits) show enterprise and healthcare categories dominating deal volume and dollars, with AI/agentic framing present across nearly every enterprise-tagged deal.
