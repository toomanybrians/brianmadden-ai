# 9 Videos from Machine Earning: Town’s Jean-Denis Greze, Lead Bank’s Jackie Reses, Chime’s Ryan King & More

## Insights

- Several speakers framed agent trust as a near-term operational problem rather than existential risk: Town's Greze expects humans to approve agent purchases initially, with errors absorbed like credit card fraud is today.
- Liability for AI agents in finance is unresolved — Lead Bank's Reses has drafted a 30-page proposal on agent accountability and is raising hallucination-liability questions directly with Washington regulators.
- Multiple speakers see agentic commerce infrastructure as the near-term opportunity: Browserbase lets agents navigate websites, Sapiom builds payment rails for them, and one agent already executed a $300,000 purchase.
- Predictions diverge on consumer readiness: Chime's King says agents will make bank-switching trivial (threatening incumbent lock-in), but believes people aren't yet ready to let AI control their money directly.
- a16z's Acharya predicts the next unicorn wave in vertical/industry-specific agents, small-business coding agents, and AI entertainment, with horizontal agents acting as orchestrators that "hire" vertical agents.
- AI is already reshaping finance leadership roles in practice: Gusto's CFO says Claude has changed his job so he now reviews AI-generated summaries rather than raw spreadsheets.

## Quote

> The horizontal agents are going to hire the vertical agents to do things. Not enough people are building there.
