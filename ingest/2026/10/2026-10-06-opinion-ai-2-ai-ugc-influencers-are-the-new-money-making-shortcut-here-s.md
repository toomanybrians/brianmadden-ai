# AI UGC Influencers Are the New Money-Making Shortcut — Here’s How

## Insights

- Two AI-generated Instagram "characters" (not real people) grew to a combined 1.24M followers and ~370M views in about a week, with estimated revenue between $369,000 and $1.8 million.
- The underlying workflow is described as simple and repeatable: create a consistent AI character, place it in repeated video content, then monetize once an audience forms via products or affiliate links.
- Memorability is framed as more important than visual polish — one character works via absurdist contrast (poor-looking man living a billionaire fantasy), the other via a highly distinctive, recognizable look (haircut, moustache, outfit).
- Current AI tools reportedly allow consistent character/face persistence across many generated videos, including speech, enabling an ongoing "series" built around one fictional persona.
- The piece frames this as a full productized system — character setup, video generation workflow, a "realistic UGC stack," hook formulas, affiliate monetization, and automation — rather than a one-off trick.

## Quote

> Create someone people can remember.
