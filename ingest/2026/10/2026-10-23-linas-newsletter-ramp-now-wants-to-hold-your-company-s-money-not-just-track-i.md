# Ramp now wants to hold your company’s money, not just track it 🏦🤖; Claude Duet arrived last to the AI Agent race, and OpenAI has the most to worry about 🤖📲

## Insights

- Ramp filed with the OCC for a national trust bank charter one day after raising $1.85B at a $60B valuation, and four days before acquiring Vesto, a treasury software company with visibility into balances across 12,000+ banks.
- The charter would let Ramp move beyond tracking company spending toward holding, investing, and moving customer money directly — for both human employees and AI agents.
- Ramp currently holds over $5B in customer account balances but depends on third-party partners for nearly all the regulated financial services behind those accounts, including a dependency on Stripe.
- The Vesto acquisition could let Ramp pull corporate cash balances away from incumbent banks like JPMorgan and Bank of America.
- A pending lawsuit is flagged as a factor that could narrow the scope of Ramp's charter plans.
- Anthropic's new "Claude Duet" agent arrived later than competitors but is framed as a major competitive threat to OpenAI; it undercuts rival "Dots" at one-fifth the price while leveraging a user's existing Claude work, and its core pitch to small businesses is that it refuses to move money even when given approval.

## Quote

> Customers keep more than $5B in Ramp accounts, yet Ramp relies on partners for almost every regulated service behind that money.
