# 🚨 Anthropic: The agony and the ecstasy

## Insights

- Anthropic's Q3 revenue hit $16.8bn (~4x all of 2025), implying a $76bn annualized exit run-rate — yet markets still read it as a "miss" because a July leak had set expectations at $65bn annualized based on an anomalous Q2.
- Net revenue retention reportedly runs around 900%, meaning existing customers are spending roughly 9x what they did a year earlier — read as a sign of deepening, not just broadening, demand.
- Unit economics are improving with scale: $1.70 of revenue per $1 of compute spend, an 85% adjusted gross margin (despite a price war with OpenAI and Chinese low-cost competition), and an 11% adjusted operating margin even while plowing 44% of revenue into R&D compute.
- Growth is decelerating sequentially (147% Q1→Q2 vs. 45% Q2→Q3), but the piece frames this as ambiguous: unclear whether Q2 pulled forward demand, whether Q3 is the new baseline, or whether this is the start of a structural slowdown from competition and price cuts.
- Hitting a $60bn full-year revenue target would require 60% quarter-on-quarter growth in Q4 — a pace achieved earlier in the year but not sustained in Q3; current trajectory implies ~$57.5bn if Q4 mirrors Q3.
- The piece notes Anthropic reached (or is approaching) a comparable revenue milestone far faster than Google, Meta, Apple, or Microsoft did historically, and ties the broader market reaction (e.g., KOSPI down sharply) to doubts about whether the "AI trade" can keep paying off.
