# Personal Agents Will Drive More Contact Center Traffic Than They Offset

## Insights

- Personal AI agents performing errands (subscription cancellations, reservations, order tracking) will increasingly need to reach businesses by phone when tasks can't be resolved via website/API, since they lack full account context, hit bot-blocking security checks, or need human-only answers.
- Unlike human callers, personal agents don't mind hold times or repeated contact — they can call back every few hours, contact multiple vendors simultaneously, and operate outside business hours (e.g., 2 a.m.), which could push call volumes toward "astronomical" levels.
- The piece argues this could convert contact centers' seasonal peak-volume spikes into a permanent baseline, straining voice channels specifically since voice is already the most expensive engagement channel.
- Salesforce's proposed response: deploy a business-side AI agent (its "Casey" product, part of Agentforce Contact Center/Service Cloud) to meet personal agents directly, armed with defined processes, workflows, and real customer/case data that personal agents lack.
- Product specifics mentioned: Casey operates across voice, chat, SMS, WhatsApp, Apple Messages; "Agent Script" constrains agent behavior; "Testing Center" runs simulated calls pre-deployment; escalations to humans include AI-generated summaries/transcripts; "Service Rep Assistant" gives human reps step-by-step action plans.
- Underlying strategic framing: contact centers that already blend human reps with AI agents are positioned as better prepared for a surge in agent-to-agent (bot-to-bot) customer service interactions.

## Quote

> Personal agents could turn peak volume into the new status quo.
