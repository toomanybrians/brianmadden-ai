# Good assessment of the on the ground reality for security in most large enterpri

## Insights

- The security risk surface at large enterprises is described as increasingly fragmented, making it harder to manage holistically rather than easier.
- Enterprises face a "never ending set of libraries" to track and patch, pointing to dependency sprawl as a structural source of ongoing risk.
- The past week saw multiple vulnerabilities surface across internet-facing software and coding repositories, treated as a representative snapshot of current conditions rather than an anomaly.
- AI agent behavior is flagged as a new and concerning category of security risk, alongside more traditional software vulnerabilities.
- A crowded field of security vendors is emerging, each offering point solutions that address only part of the overall problem, leaving the core challenge of fragmentation unresolved.

## Quote

> The fragmented landscape of the risk surface is only going to get more gnarly.
