# #AI adoption is accelerating, but you don't have to rebuild your IT infrastructu

## Insights

- Citrix frames enterprise AI adoption as progressing in stages: from employee productivity tools and AI-powered applications toward autonomous agents that interact directly with enterprise systems.
- The piece identifies three IT leadership priorities for the AI era: AI economics/governance (cost control, portability, compliance), security and trust (protecting data while enabling human-AI collaboration), and business resilience (adapting without disrupting operations).
- It argues organizations don't need to rebuild IT infrastructure to adopt AI — instead, existing access and identity control frameworks should be extended to cover both human and AI-driven work.
- Introduces the concept of "persona alignment" — treating AI-assisted workers as a distinct persona type (alongside roles like bank tellers or developers) with different security requirements and resource usage, allowing right-sized access/cost optimization.
- Positions the "adaptive enterprise" as the strategic goal: an organization structured to absorb technology shifts (AI included) without sacrificing security, user experience, or cost predictability.

## Quote

> AI is already part of everyday work... advancing from employee productivity tools... to autonomous agents that interact directly with enterprise systems.
