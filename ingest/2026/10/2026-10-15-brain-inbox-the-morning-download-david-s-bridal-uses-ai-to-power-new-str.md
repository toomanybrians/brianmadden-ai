# The Morning Download: David’s Bridal Uses AI to Power New Strategy

## Insights

- David's Bridal (post-bankruptcy, acquired by CION Investment Corp) pivoted from a dress retailer to an "aisle to algorithm" model: an asset-light business connecting brides directly to vendors via AI, rather than just selling product.
- The company built two AI products: Pearl Planner (consumer-facing wedding planning agent) and Pearl Connect (vendor-facing platform), with proprietary bride data — appointment bookings, site activity, commerce behavior — positioned as the real competitive differentiator, not the AI itself.
- Pearl is an agentic system built on a knowledge graph, combining first-party customer data, proprietary research, and anecdotes from in-store stylists; it can autonomously rebook vendors/appointments and adjust planning timelines when a wedding date changes.
- Engineering org stayed under 10 full-time developers plus outside help; leadership credits agentic coding tools with letting a small team do what "would have taken one hundred-plus people before."
- Reported outcomes: 3x increase in customer engagement, 26x increase in user count, and claimed compression of ~4 years of roadmap work into 18 months.
- Funding model notably didn't involve incremental budget — investment was reallocated from existing spend rather than treated as new cost, per CEO Kelly Cook.

## Quote

> What would have taken one hundred-plus people before, we were able to do with just tens.
