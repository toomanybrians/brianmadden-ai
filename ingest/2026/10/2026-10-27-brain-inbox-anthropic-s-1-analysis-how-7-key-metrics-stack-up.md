# Anthropic S-1 Analysis: How 7 Key Metrics Stack Up

## Insights

- Anthropic's revenue hit $16.3B in H1 2026, a 14x jump over H1 2025, with three consecutive years of roughly 10x annual growth (9.4x, 11.9x, 14.0x); OpenAI grew 253% in comparison for the same period.
- The company turned cash-flow positive in 2026, reporting $974M of operating income in Q2 (8.4% GAAP margin, 19% adjusted), after an $8.1B operating loss in 2025 that included a $1.5B copyright settlement.
- Price cuts tied to model releases (e.g., Claude Opus 4.5 lowering cost per task) preceded revenue growth accelerating from 48% to 132% quarter-over-quarter — cited as a real-world Jevons paradox example, where cheaper capability drove more consumption, not less revenue.
- Compute commitments total an estimated $637B across seven providers (Broadcom, SpaceXAI, Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Nscale, Lambda), with two-thirds non-cancelable; this is weighed against just $4.6B of 2025 revenue, with Anthropic carrying almost no drawn debt despite the scale of guarantees.
- Customer concentration has fallen sharply: no single customer exceeded 10% of revenue by late 2026 (top five ~25%), down from customers over $100K/year representing 76% of 2025 revenue — alongside a reported Net Revenue Retention of ~900%.
- Nearly half of revenue now flows through marketplaces (e.g., cloud platform marketplaces), described as a typical maturation pattern for enterprise software companies.
