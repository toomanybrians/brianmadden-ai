# ⚙️ Gartner's AI predictions address the control gap

## Insights

- Gartner's 2027 predictions frame a widening "control gap": AI expands what can be automated faster than organizations can govern cost, access, and accountability for it.
- Gartner forecasts specific risk/efficiency thresholds: by 2028, 25% of enterprise security breaches will trace to AI agents with mismanaged credentials; by 2029, 80% of new applications will be intentionally "disposable" (used under a year); by 2030, 80% of companies with public-facing AI will face "cost exhaustion" attacks designed to inflate AI bills.
- Gartner ties competitive advantage to data readiness, not just model choice: companies that make enterprise data AI-ready could see up to 80% better agentic accuracy and 60% lower costs by 2027.
- Gartner analyst Daryl Plummer explicitly pushes back on cost-cutting-first AI strategy and on a "replace workers" framing, advocating "augment and extend" instead.
- Current ROI reality cited as context: only 11% of CFOs and 13% of CIOs report clear/transformational returns from AI, per a separate Gartner executive survey — suggesting governance and accountability gaps are already visible in outcomes, not just risk.
- Separately, a Creatio survey of 600+ IT/business leaders finds 70% of organizations past the "experimentation" stage with AI agents, but only 17% qualify as true "AI Adoption Leaders" — distinguished by broader workflow automation, cross-team agent scaling, and strong governance (4x more confidence in scaling). Only 9% of respondents anticipate significant headcount cuts from AI; 89% expect capacity expansion, augmentation, or new roles.

## Quote

> "Don't think 'replace workers.' Think 'augment and extend.' That's the better way to go." — Daryl Plummer, Gartner
