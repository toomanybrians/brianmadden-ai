# The verdicts are ranked to reward openness. Insufficient access gives the worst

## Insights

- Describes a five-tier verdict system for evaluating AI safety claims: Verified, Unrefuted, Self-reported, Evaluator-found, and Blocked.
- The ranking is explicitly designed to incentivize openness — a developer who discloses a problem themselves scores better than one where an external evaluator discovers it.
- Insufficient access to information by evaluators results in the worst possible verdict (Blocked), penalizing opacity rather than rewarding it.
- The stated design goal is to align incentives so that the easiest way for a developer to achieve a better verdict is to genuinely improve safety, not merely to manage disclosure optics.

## Quote

> Insufficient access gives the worst verdict. A problem the developer reports itself scores better than one the evaluator finds.
