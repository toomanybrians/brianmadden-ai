# Powerful testimony from ex Google DeepMind researcher @Turn_Trout at NYC Hearing

## Insights

- A former Google DeepMind researcher (Turn_Trout) testified at an NYC hearing on AI that he left the company in June, citing a reversal of its earlier AI safety commitments.
- The testimony claims Google — including CEO Demis Hassabis — walked back prior public commitments to AI safety.
- The researcher states that many researchers inside frontier AI labs believe they are building systems that could pose existential/catastrophic risk ("could kill everyone on the planet").
- The researcher describes this risk assessment as having been part of his actual job responsibilities, not a speculative outside concern.
- The framing invokes Google's former "don't be evil" ethos as a pointed contrast to its current safety posture.
