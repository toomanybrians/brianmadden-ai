# There's a huge opportunity right now in being the deployment layer for AI into t

## Insights

- Frames "deployment" — actually installing AI into enterprise workflows — as a distinct, currently underexploited business opportunity separate from building AI models themselves.
- Argues the labor required to replace or rework existing enterprise workflows is consistently underestimated, both by outside observers and by the organizations themselves.
- Predicts this deployment/implementation work will define the "applied layer" of the AI industry — the layer that actually translates AI capability into enterprise use.
- A quoted companion post extends the claim: companies of any size, from 50-person firms to organizations with hundreds of thousands of employees, will need large teams of people dedicated to managing this transformation.

## Quote

> Every single one of those companies... is gonna need an army of people to go in and help them with that transformation.
