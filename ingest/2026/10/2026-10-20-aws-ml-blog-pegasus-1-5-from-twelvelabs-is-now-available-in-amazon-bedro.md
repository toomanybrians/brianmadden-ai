# Pegasus 1.5 from TwelveLabs is now available in Amazon Bedrock

## Insights

- Pegasus 1.5, now on Amazon Bedrock, shifts video AI from single-clip Q&A to full-video analysis: it scans an entire long-form video and returns every matching segment as structured, timestamped JSON rather than a one-off text answer.
- New "time-based metadata generation" lets users define arbitrary criteria (editorial segments, sports plays, speaker changes, brand appearances) once and apply them consistently across an entire video library, producing standardized metadata at scale.
- Multimodal prompting now accepts reference images alongside text, enabling use cases like finding a specific product, logo, or person on screen — described as enabling visual brand monitoring and product placement analysis.
- No pre-indexing or ingestion pipeline is required — video is analyzed directly from S3 or base64 input, data stays within the AWS boundary, and processing capacity doubled to 2-hour videos (up from 1 hour in Pegasus 1.2).
- The vendor frames the labor impact explicitly: "people remain part of the workflow," with work shifting "from finding and tagging every moment by hand to reviewing and refining the metadata generated by the model" — a reskilling/role-shift claim rather than a replacement claim.
- Billing is routed through AWS Marketplace under TwelveLabs rather than standard Bedrock charges, and usage is trackable via CloudWatch metrics and application inference profiles — relevant for enterprise cost-attribution practices.
