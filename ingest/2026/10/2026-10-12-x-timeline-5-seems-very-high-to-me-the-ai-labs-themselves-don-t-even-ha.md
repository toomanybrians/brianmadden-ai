# 5% seems very high to me. The AI labs themselves don’t even have the agent contr

## Insights

- BCG's 2026 Applied AI Index found 42% of companies expect their AI agents to operate autonomously by 2030.
- Only 5% of companies report having the relevant agent controls in place today, per the same BCG index.
- Paul Roetzer argues even the 5% figure is likely inflated, noting that even AI labs themselves lack robust agent control infrastructure.
- He suggests IT departments and company leadership may be overconfident about their readiness to manage autonomous agentic systems.
- The quoted BCG material frames scaling agentic AI workflows as carrying inherent risk, tied to this governance/control gap.

## Quote

> 5% seems very high to me. The AI labs themselves don't even have the agent controls in place.
