# ⚙️ AI is turning specialists into generalists

## Insights

- Rokt's COO describes AI as dissolving boundaries between functions (legal, finance, security, marketing), letting both technical and nontechnical employees work across disciplines that were previously siloed.
- As a direct result, Rokt consolidated its internal career "job ladders" from 15 down to 4, citing AI's ability to let employees "go beyond those old boundaries."
- The company reframes employee roles: rather than doing hands-on execution, workers increasingly act as orchestrators/supervisors of multiple autonomous AI agents working in parallel ("conductors of the symphony orchestra of autonomous AI agents").
- This shift is credited with compressing timelines — work once taking weeks now reportedly completed in hours.
- Rokt has invested deliberately in company-wide AI fluency, including hackathons and competitions aimed at nontechnical staff, not just engineers.
- The COO argues technical skill is becoming less differentiating than durable personal attributes — relentlessness, resilience, rigor, curiosity, optimism, humility — as the key traits for thriving amid AI-driven change.

## Quote

> AI is beginning to really blur the lines between different disciplines.
