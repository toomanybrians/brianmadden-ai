# Seeing a lot of strong Personal AI Scores of 90+ among early beta users. I expec

## Insights

- Early beta users of SmarterX's Personal AI Score are clustering at 90+ (the "strong" tier), but the author expects this average to fall sharply once the assessment reaches a broader enterprise population — suggesting early adopters are unrepresentative AI enthusiasts, not a baseline for typical knowledge workers.
- The tool pairs two assessments: a Business AI Score (org-level, 8 pillars — Vision, Strategy, Data, Technology, Governance, Literacy, People, Performance; 67 statements) and a Personal AI Score (individual, 5 pillars — Knowledge, Application, Judgment, Impact, Mindset; 37 statements).
- Both scores use a shared three-tier grading scale (Weak 0–59%, Moderate 60–79%, Strong 80–100%), explicitly designed so leadership and individuals share a common language for AI maturity.
- A "Team Report" feature aggregates individual Personal AI Scores into one organizational score and can surface perception gaps — e.g., where leadership rates the organization's AI readiness differently than staff.
- Each assessment statement maps to a three-rung "action ladder" (Starting, Building, Operating) tied to a companion book, "The AI Transformation Blueprint," turning a weak score directly into prescribed next steps.
- Underlying philosophy stated on the page: "every organizational transformation is a collection of individual transformations," positioning personal AI maturity measurement as foundational to enterprise-wide transformation.
