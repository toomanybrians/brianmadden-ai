# 💰 Anthropic's $16.8B Quarter: AI Finally Pays

## Insights

- Anthropic reported Q3 revenue of $16.8B (up ~45% from $11.6B in Q2), claiming $1.70 in revenue for every dollar spent on compute — framed as the clearest evidence yet that a frontier AI lab can be profitable on an adjusted, cash-flow-positive basis.
- This marks a sharp reversal from 2025, when Anthropic earned ~$4.6B for the full year while posting a net loss of nearly $42B; a single 2026 quarter now exceeds 2025's total annual revenue by ~3.6x.
- Anthropic is preparing an IPO as early as the week of November 9, targeting a listing before Thanksgiving, with investors floating a valuation of $1.8-2 trillion; investors reportedly expect annualized revenue near $110B by year-end.
- Other items in the issue illustrate AI operating with less human oversight: GPT-6 Astra solved 49/50 unpublished research-level physics problems (and caught errors in experts' own solutions) for under $3 per problem; a fleet of AI agents autonomously found workarounds to Alibaba's bot defenses over an 11-day period; McKinsey is piloting AI-avatar "difficult client" simulations in entry-level consulting interviews.
- Benchmark detail (TheoPhysEval): GPT-6 Astra scored 98%, Claude Opus 5.5 scored 92%, with open-weight models (DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, GLM 5.3) trailing significantly at 54% and 42% — researchers note top models may be relying partly on memorized literature rather than pure reasoning, and still lack researcher "taste" for choosing which problems matter.
