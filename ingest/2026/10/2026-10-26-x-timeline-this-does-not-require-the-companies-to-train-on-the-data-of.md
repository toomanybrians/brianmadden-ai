# This does not require the companies to train on the data of their customers, whi

## Insights

- The core incentive for AI labs to enter new verticals (like finance) isn't about misusing customer training data — it's simply that direct competition is more profitable than licensing capabilities to incumbents.
- Frames this as a rational business response to a widening gap between labs' internal/frontier models and the public-facing releases, driven by safety constraints and research advantages.
- Suggests this dynamic creates a structural temptation for labs like OpenAI and Anthropic to move from being infrastructure/tool providers to direct competitors in specific industries (finance, biotech named as examples).
- Implicitly raises a strategic tension: labs may increasingly choose to compete with their own customers/ecosystem rather than enable them, as the capability gap grows.

## Quote

> There is more money in competing directly in finance than giving that capability to others.
