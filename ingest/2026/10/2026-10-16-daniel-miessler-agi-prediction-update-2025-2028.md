# AGI Prediction Update (2025-2028)

## Insights

- Miessler redefines AGI as the point where an AI product can replace an average knowledge worker, and claims this threshold was crossed in 2026 with a product he calls "Claude Tag," revising his earlier 2023 prediction of 2027.
- The capability he points to: persistent, organization-wide memory that can read all emails, meetings, Slack messages, and customer interactions, then autonomously generate reports, documentation, code, and analysis on an employee's behalf.
- He describes a scenario where the AI can audit an individual employee's output (e.g., report volume, meetings, code shipped) and then offer to take over that employee's entire workload — and scale that offer across an entire team.
- He acknowledges limits: average/weaker performers are most replaceable, especially where management itself is mediocre; top performers remain harder to replace because much of their value is tacit and hard to articulate or copy.
- He frames the business impact as layoffs and/or reduced rehiring after attrition, rather than instant mass firing, and argues uneven adoption doesn't negate that AGI has arrived — it's just unevenly distributed.
- A key interface point: the tool works inside existing chat tools (Slack/Teams) via a simple @mention rather than as a separate "AI app," removing the friction of context-switching to a dedicated AI product.

## Quote

> I hate to tell the people who want to quibble about definitions, but that's fucking AGI.
