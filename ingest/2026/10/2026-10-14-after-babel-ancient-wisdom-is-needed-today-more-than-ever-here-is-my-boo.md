# Ancient Wisdom Is Needed Today More Than Ever. Here Is My Book About It.

## Insights

- Haidt argues modern digital technology (smartphones, social media, and now AI) is breaking the "vertical" transmission of accumulated wisdom from one generation to the next, replacing it with "horizontal" transmission of low-quality, fast-forgotten content.
- He predicts that as AI begins creating "much—and eventually most—" of the content people consume, this disconnection from historical/cultural knowledge will accelerate further.
- His framing: people are shifting from reading books (vertical, hand-me-down culture) to watching short-form video (horizontal, ephemeral culture), and AI-generated content is poised to deepen this shift rather than reverse it.
- He positions ancient wisdom/psychology as a corrective — durable insight into human nature that doesn't depend on technological conditions — as a counterweight to this acceleration.
- Broader argument: without deliberate resistance, "digitalization" of life domains (childhood, education, dating, work) will undermine human flourishing; he calls for consciously preserving cross-generational knowledge rather than passively accepting the drift.

## Quote

> As AI begins to create much—and eventually most—of the content we all consume, the loss of connection to the past will accelerate.
