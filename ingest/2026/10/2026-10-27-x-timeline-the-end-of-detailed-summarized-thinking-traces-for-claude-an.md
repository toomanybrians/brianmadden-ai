# The end of detailed summarized thinking traces for Claude and ChatGPT (likely bo

## Insights

- Claude and ChatGPT have reportedly stopped showing detailed/summarized thinking traces (the step-by-step reasoning previously visible to users).
- The change is attributed to two likely drivers: preventing distillation (competitors training on exposed reasoning) and UX simplification.
- Losing visibility into in-process reasoning makes it harder for a human to meaningfully intervene or weigh in while the AI is working.
- This shift is framed as structurally favoring full automation over human-AI augmentation, since augmentation depends on being able to inspect and correct work mid-process.

## Quote

> It pushes us towards automation, rather than augmentation, as it has become much harder to weigh in on in-process AI work
