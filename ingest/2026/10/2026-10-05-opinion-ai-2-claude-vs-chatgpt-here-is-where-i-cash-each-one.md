# Claude vs ChatGPT: Here Is Where I Cash Each One

## Insights

- The author argues that as AI models (both Claude and ChatGPT) become more capable, the right approach shifts from using one tool for everything toward deliberately splitting tasks between tools based on their distinct strengths.
- Proposed heuristic: Claude is suited to "deep" single-track work — long documents, complex writing briefs, messy problems, large codebases, research piles, or tasks requiring many instructions to stay coherent — because it's good at staying inside one job.
- ChatGPT is framed as suited to "wide" work that moves across different modes/tools — web search, file reading, table creation, image generation, presentations, app use, browser work, moving into Codex, then returning to synthesize results.
- The piece references a wave of new model releases in a single month (GPT-6 Astra, Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Sonnet 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol), citing this as evidence of rapid capability growth (search, tool use, large context, code, file handling, longer task persistence).
- The author frames tool choice as a practical first decision before starting any task, rather than a loyalty question to one platform.
- Piece teases further guidance on "not starting with the biggest model" as a setup tip, suggesting model-size selection itself is a distinct consideration beyond Claude-vs-ChatGPT choice.

## Quote

> Claude goes deeper. ChatGPT goes wider.
