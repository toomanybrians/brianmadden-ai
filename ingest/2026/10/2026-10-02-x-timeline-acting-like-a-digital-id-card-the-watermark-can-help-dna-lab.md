# Acting like a digital ID card, the watermark can help DNA labs verify synthetic

## Insights

- Google DeepMind's SynthID Bio embeds an imperceptible watermark directly into AI-generated protein sequences without altering their biological function.
- The watermark functions like a digital ID card, letting DNA synthesis labs verify whether a given sequence design originated from AI.
- Verification is framed as a tool to keep sequence databases accurate as synthetic biology sequence volumes scale.
- The stated goal is to let labs automate verification and strengthen biosecurity as AI-driven design requests increase.

## Quote

> Acting like a digital ID card, the watermark can help DNA labs verify synthetic proteins and keep sequence databases accurate.
