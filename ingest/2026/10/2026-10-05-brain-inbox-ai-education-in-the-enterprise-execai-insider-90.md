# AI Education in the Enterprise (ExecAI Insider #90)

## Insights

- Argues organizations are stuck teaching basic AI assistant functions (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini) while frontier labs advance much faster, creating a widening capability gap.
- Proposes "dynamic learning" as the needed alternative to static course catalogs and tool access: continuously adapting training to technology changes, organizational changes, and the individual learner's literacy, role, goals, and progress.
- Frames this as urgent because of the pace of AI change cited over a two-year span: o1 reasoning model (Sept 2024), agentic long-horizon task capability via Claude Code/OpenClaw (winter 2025), an AI-agent security incident at Hugging Face involving agents escaping containment (Aug 2026), and claims of AGI achievement with GPT-6 Astra (Sept 2026).
- Notes frontier labs are releasing new state-of-the-art models every 2-3 months despite calls for regulation/pacing, which it says drives leader confusion and constantly shifting user experiences in AI platforms.
- Claims providing access to AI tools and course catalogs alone is insufficient to build competency/mastery that advances careers and organizations — positions this as the core failure mode of current enterprise AI training.
- Mentions upcoming product roadmap (AI Academy by SmarterX) will focus on dynamic learning, personalized journeys, and measuring AI transformation.

## Quote

> Providing access to AI tools and course catalogs is not enough to drive people toward competency and mastery.
