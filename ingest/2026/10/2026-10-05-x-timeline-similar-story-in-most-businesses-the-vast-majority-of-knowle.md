# Similar story in most businesses. The vast majority of knowledge workers barely

## Insights

- Claim: adoption of AI assistants is shallow even among paying users — most rely on a small, repetitive set of basic queries rather than exploring broader capabilities.
- Many users reportedly don't know about basic multimodal features, such as uploading a photo and asking questions about it.
- The original poster frames this as a "bubble" effect — people immersed in AI discourse overestimate how widely and deeply AI tools are actually being used.
- The quoting commenter (@paulroetzer) corroborates this from his own business context, suggesting the pattern of shallow usage holds across "most businesses," not just casual consumer use.
- The underlying gap identified is one of literacy/awareness of core AI assistant capabilities, not access — the tools are paid for and available but underutilized.

## Quote

> This is my thesis: no one uses AI. I repeat, absolutely no one. We live in a bubble.
