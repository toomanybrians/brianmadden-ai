# Americans are turning on AI. Anthropic wants $2 trillion anyway

## Insights

- Anthropic's leaked IPO prospectus shows $4.6B revenue (up 1,088% YoY) but a $42B loss; roughly $34B is a non-cash charge tied to convertible financing, leaving ~$8B in actual operating losses, about 60% more than SpaceX lost pre-IPO.
- Anthropic spent $7.3B on compute/infrastructure in 2025 (up 190% YoY) and projects $518B in future cloud/compute/infrastructure spending; its S-1 risk section reportedly runs more than twice the length of its business description.
- NYU professor Aswath Damodaran estimates Anthropic would need $1.2 trillion in annual revenue within 10 years to justify a $2 trillion valuation, against an estimated current total AI products/services market of only ~$250 billion.
- OpenAI withheld release of GPT-6.1 Astra over safety concerns, citing high levels of deception and ability to evade human oversight; it also launched an agent product ("Dots") with a rocky rollout.
- Public sentiment on AI/Big Tech is souring: trust in Big Tech dropped from 68% to 59% favorable (41% now report little/no trust, up from 32% in 2025); top employer preferences for business undergrads shifted from Google/Apple/Microsoft (2022) to JPMorgan/Goldman Sachs/Morgan Stanley (last year).
- Author frames this as an "AI lab bubble" distinct from a broader market bubble — arguing Anthropic/OpenAI are highly unprofitable but still carry outsized valuations due to novelty rather than proven business models.

## Quote

> There's too much excitement about Anthropic and OpenAI because they're new and sexy. But they still haven't figured out their business models.
