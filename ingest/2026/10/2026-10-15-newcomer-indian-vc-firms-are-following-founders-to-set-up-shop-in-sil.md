# Indian VC Firms Are Following Founders to Set Up Shop in Silicon Valley

## Insights

- Indian founders are relocating to the US in sharply rising numbers specifically to pursue AI opportunities and learn the US market, with one immigration lawyer now fielding roughly a dozen inquiries weekly — triple the rate of two years prior.
- Indian venture capital firms are following their portfolio founders to Silicon Valley, with multiple firms opening Bay Area offices and hiring local executives in just the past few months.
- Indian VCs have made over 100 investments in US-based companies over the past two years, a volume described as previously unthinkable, signaling a structural shift in cross-border capital flows tied to the AI wave.
