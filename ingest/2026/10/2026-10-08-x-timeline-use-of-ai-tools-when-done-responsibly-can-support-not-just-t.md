# “Use of AI tools, when done responsibly, can support not just the initial genera

## Insights

- Tao argues AI tools, used responsibly, could support the full lifecycle of problem-solving — not just generating an initial solution but also the valuable follow-up work (verification, refinement, extension) that normally follows.
- He claims that in current practice, the opposite pattern is common: AI use is concentrated on generating the initial solution while the downstream follow-up activities are neglected or undermined.

## Quote

> Use of AI tools, when done responsibly, can support not just the initial generation of a solution, but all of the valuable followup activities. — Terence Tao
