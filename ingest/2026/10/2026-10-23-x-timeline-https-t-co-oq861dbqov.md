- The strongest claims about autonomous AI-run attack campaigns remain contested, while the best-documented AI security use cases so far are defensive (e.g., AI systems like Big Sleep and DARPA's AIxCC finalists finding real vulnerabilities).
- Overall interpretive stance: existing outcome evidence cannot yet determine whether AI has net increased or decreased real-world security harm — vulnerability types tracked haven't fundamentally changed either.
