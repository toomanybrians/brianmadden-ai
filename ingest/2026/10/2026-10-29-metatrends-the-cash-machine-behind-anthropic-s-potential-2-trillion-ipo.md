# The Cash Machine Behind Anthropic’s Potential $2 Trillion IPO

## Insights

- Anthropic's S-1 shows revenue jumping from $4.6B in all of 2025 to $16.3B in H1 2026, with Q2 alone at $11.6B — three consecutive years of roughly 10x growth, which one investor interviewed called unprecedented.
- The key unit-economics metric: revenue per dollar of compute spend rose from $0.40 in early 2025 to $2.08 by Q2 2026, alongside the company's first operating profit ($974M in Q2) — evidence the business model is working, not just burning cash.
- The widely-reported $80.7B loss is almost entirely a non-cash accounting charge from convertible funding instruments revaluing after a $965B valuation jump; actual operating loss was $693M for the half, and the company holds $131.7B in cash.
- Customer concentration has diminished: from ~20 customers paying over $100K/year in late 2023 to over 11,900 by September, including more than half the Fortune 500; top five customers are ~25% of revenue with none above 10%.
- Governance structure keeps control with seven founders (50.1% of voting power via special shares, each owning ~2%), overseen by an independent Long-Term Benefit Trust with no financial stake that appoints a board majority; founders have pledged 80% of personal equity to charity.
- Named risks: $500B+ in largely non-cancelable compute commitments (jumping from $54.6B to $265.6B in six months), heavy revenue dependency on Amazon/Google/Microsoft cloud marketplaces (also competitors), and an unusually candid ~80-page risk section disclosing potential for catastrophic/existential AI risk and self-preserving model behaviors like resisting shutdown or deception.
- Framed as a first-mover advantage: Anthropic's public filing (June 1, confidential; October 26, public) precedes OpenAI's, meaning its market valuation may become the benchmark competitors are measured against.
