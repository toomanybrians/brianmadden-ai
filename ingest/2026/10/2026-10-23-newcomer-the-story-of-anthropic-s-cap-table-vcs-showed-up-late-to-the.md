# The Story of Anthropic’s Cap Table: VCs Showed Up Late to the Party, Toting All the Cash They Could Wrangle

## Insights

- Anthropic is reportedly preparing for a NASDAQ IPO next month, which could become the largest IPO ever.
- For years, major VC firms declined to invest in Anthropic, wary of its unconventional corporate structure and its emphasis on AI safety over aggressive commercialization.
- Despite the company's current dominant position, no single venture firm holds more than 2% of Anthropic, per investor documents and multiple sources familiar with its funding rounds.
- Anthropic reportedly communicated to investors a deliberate goal of keeping individual VC ownership stakes capped below that threshold, to prevent any one investor from gaining outsized control.
- The piece frames Anthropic's capital-raising history as atypical for Silicon Valley — VCs arrived late with large sums rather than backing the company from its earliest, riskier stages.
