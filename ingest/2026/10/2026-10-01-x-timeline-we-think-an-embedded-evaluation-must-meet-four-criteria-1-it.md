# We think an embedded evaluation must meet four criteria:
1. It reduces risk from

## Insights

- Apollo Research proposes four criteria for an "embedded evaluation" of frontier AI systems to be meaningful: it must reduce risk from frontier AI, inform the public, align incentives for both evaluator and developer toward safety, and be fair to both parties.
- The framing implies embedded evaluations are a distinct model of AI oversight — evaluators working in close proximity to or within a developer's process, rather than fully external auditors.
- A companion point warns that embedded evaluations can fail in practice if evaluators lack sufficient access or resources, or if their findings don't meaningfully influence real decisions.
- The worst-case failure mode described is not neutral but actively harmful: a weak embedded evaluation can create a "false sense of security" rather than simply providing no value.

## Quote

> In the worst case, they give a false sense of security.
