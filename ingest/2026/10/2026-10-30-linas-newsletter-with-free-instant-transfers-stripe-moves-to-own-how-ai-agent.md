# With free instant transfers, Stripe moves to own how AI Agents pay businesses 🤖💳; Ramp took AmEx’s corporate card customers and now wants their cash too 😳💸

## Insights

- Stripe launched "Stripe Pay" on October 27, a free, instant B2B transfer service built around "handles" — verified business addresses that let an AI agent pay a counterparty without needing a routing number.
- Businesses already transact on Stripe 4.8 million times a day; Stripe Pay extends this by letting businesses (and AI agents acting for them) find each other by name and settle directly on Stripe's own ledger.
- When both parties are Stripe businesses, Stripe controls which payment rail is used — positioning Stripe as the payment network for AI agent-to-business transactions rather than just a processor.
- The piece argues the real long-term value isn't the free transfers themselves but the underlying business directory/identity layer that the handle system creates.
- Shipped minimally (a CLI command and a dashboard button), suggesting the launch is framed as infrastructure-first rather than a consumer-facing product push.

## Quote

> The most valuable part of Stripe Pay is the handle, a verified business address that an AI agent can pay without guessing a routing number.
