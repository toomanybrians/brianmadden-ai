# Not finding a counterexample is not enough to establish a claim as true. We also

## Insights

- Absence of a counterexample to a safety/capability claim doesn't prove the claim true — it only proves the search method didn't find one, which is a weaker standard.
- Evaluating a claim requires separately assessing whether the developer's testing methodology is actually capable of surfacing a counterexample if one existed.
- Proposed example: for AI monitoring system claims, evaluators would plant realistic evasion attempts and measure how many the monitor successfully flags.
- Describes a three-step evaluation process: (1) review evidence the developer already has, (2) independently search for new counterexamples, (3) assess whether the developer's own methods would have caught a counterexample had it existed.

## Quote

> Not finding a counterexample is not enough to establish a claim as true.
