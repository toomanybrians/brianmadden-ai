# Artificial Analysis finally moves to closed benchmarks (goodbye GDPval-AA), and

## Insights

- Artificial Analysis has switched its benchmark methodology to closed benchmarks, retiring its GDPval-AA metric.
- Under the new v5 methodology, open-weights models show a larger gap behind closed/proprietary leaders than previously measured.
- The top scorer, Kimi K3.1 Pro, scores 46 — a 12-point lead over the next model — but its weights have not been released.
- The gap between leading and open models widened compared to the prior version (3-point gap in v4.3 vs. 12-point gap in v5).
- Among models with actually released weights, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (max) and GLM-5.3 (max) tie for highest score at 32.
- The poster notes this should be taken with caution but aligns with a broader sense that no open model has yet matched "Fable-class" performance.

## Quote

> We haven't seen a Fable-class open model.
