# We’re already starting to see what kind of new jobs AI is creating. 

AI require

## Insights

- New AI-related job categories are emerging around deploying AI into the economy rather than just building core models: applied AI engineers who build products sold to or within enterprises, and forward-deployed engineers (FDEs) who deploy agents.
- LinkedIn data cited shows AI-linked roles have produced over 750,000 new US jobs since 2023.
- The largest single category of new AI-linked jobs is data annotators, with +282,000 positions created.
- Data center jobs are cited as another major contributor to AI-driven job growth, though the specific figure is cut off.
- The framing suggests AI's labor-market impact so far is less about model research and more about the "surrounding services" needed to operationalize AI — deployment, annotation, infrastructure.

## Quote

> AI requires significant technical work and surrounding services to deploy into the economy.
