# This is good advice for now to think about working with models like Opus 5.5.

B

## Insights

- The suggested working pattern for advanced models (e.g., Opus 5.5) favors iterative checkpoints over single-shot task completion.
- Rather than giving a model a goal and letting it run to completion, the model should pause at interim stages to report ideas or prototypes back to the user.
- This checkpoint approach allows the human to guide and give feedback mid-process, rather than only evaluating a finished output.
- The framing implies a tradeoff between speed/autonomy (one-shotting) and control/quality (checkpointed collaboration) when directing AI models on complex tasks.

## Quote

> It is often best to build in checkpoints where the model reports back to you with interim ideas or prototypes.
