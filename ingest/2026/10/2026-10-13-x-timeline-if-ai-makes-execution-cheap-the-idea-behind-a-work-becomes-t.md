# If AI makes execution cheap, the idea behind a work becomes the valuable part. C

## Insights

- As AI drives execution costs toward zero, the scarce and valuable element shifts from the finished output to the underlying idea or concept.
- This inverts a historical dynamic: "idea guys" were once dismissed as low-value because execution was the hard, skilled part; now execution is cheap and ideas remain scarce since audience attention is a limited resource.
- Possible future creative model: creators release concepts, scripts, or drafts rather than finished works, and audiences (or AI) generate their own personalized final versions.
- Cultural consequence: short-form, conceptual, or novel ideas could be valued over polished, high-quality execution — "conjectures" over "proofs," draft over finished product.
- Analogy drawn to photography's effect on painting: when a technology makes realism/execution trivially easy, the creative field shifts toward what the easy technology can't replicate — in this case, conceptual novelty.
- Current AI is seen as strong at remixing/execution but comparatively weak at genuine novelty, which is why idea origination retains disproportionate value.

## Quote

> If AI makes execution cheap, the idea behind a work becomes the valuable part.
