# RT @EliLanger: Wells Fargo is warning its customers about using AI and personal

## Insights

- Wells Fargo has issued a warning to its customers specifically about using AI and personal AI agents in connection with its financial products.
- The bank frames the issue as a liability question: customers, not the institution or the AI vendor, may bear responsibility for mistakes made by an AI tool.
- This signals a financial institution formally and publicly addressing AI-agent risk at the point of customer interaction, rather than treating it as an internal policy matter only.
- The warning implicitly acknowledges AI agents are already being used by customers in ways that touch core financial transactions, prompting a need for explicit risk disclosure.

## Quote

> "...you may be liable for any mistakes made by the AI tool."
