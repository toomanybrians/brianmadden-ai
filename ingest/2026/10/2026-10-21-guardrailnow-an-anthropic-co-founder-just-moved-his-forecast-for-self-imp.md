# An Anthropic Co-Founder Just Moved His Forecast for Self-Improving AI to 2027

## Insights

- Anthropic co-founder Jack Clark revised his timeline for recursive self-improvement (RSI) in AI, now considering 2027 more likely than 2028 as the year it becomes possible.
- His earlier (May) estimate was a 30% chance of AI training its own successors by 2027, rising to ~60% by end of 2028.
- RSI is defined as an AI conducting the research needed to build a more capable AI, which then repeats the process on itself.
- The revision was published in Clark's own newsletter, Import AI, on October 12.

## Quote

> I now think it's more likely we'll see full RSI become possible in 2027, whereas before I thought it was more likely in 2028.
