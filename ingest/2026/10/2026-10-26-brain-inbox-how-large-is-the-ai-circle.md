# How Large is the AI Circle?

## Insights

- Using Carlota Perez's framework (infrastructure build-out, then applications that pay for it), the piece compares the telecom/internet buildout (1996-2005, $1.5t capex inflation-adjusted) against $180b in application revenue, to the current AI era, where application revenue is reaching comparable milestones roughly twice as fast.
- Infrastructure payback periods appear much shorter this cycle: SpaceX's S-1 reportedly cites under a year for new compute deployments to pay back, versus the decade-long payback horizon for dark fiber in the telecom era.
- US venture investment hit $516b in the first nine months of 2026, already 44% above the full-year 2021 record, and AI companies captured 82.7% of 2026 deal value — the highest share on record.
- Cumulative venture investment in the AI era's first four years (2023-2026) is roughly 6.2x the inflation-adjusted investment in the web era's first four years, raising the question of whether this is a larger real economic shift or a self-referential bubble.
- A circularity concern is raised: ~80% of the largest AI spenders are venture-backed, and 80% of spend on OpenAI/Anthropic reportedly comes from just 1% of firms — but venture-backed firms now represent about 13% of US employment (up from ~5% in the web era), suggesting the "circle" of venture-funded economic activity is now much larger and arguably more structurally embedded than before.
- Caveats noted: the spend data measures dollars not tokens, excludes ~40% of AI spend from free tools/personal accounts, and venture backing of a firm isn't proof that venture dollars are directly funding the AI spend.

## Quote

> The circularity is real, but the circle is a sixth of the economy.
