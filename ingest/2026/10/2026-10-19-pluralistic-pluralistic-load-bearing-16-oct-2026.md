# Pluralistic: Load-bearing (16 Oct 2026)

## Insights

- Doctorow argues "digital sovereignty" rhetoric is typically used to justify building AI data centers while skipping environmental review, but AI itself is not actually load-bearing infrastructure for nations or firms.
- His test: imagine removing all chatbots from a country — public administration, firms, and households would continue functioning largely unaffected (aside from students losing a homework shortcut).
- The actual load-bearing digital infrastructure is mundane enterprise software — specifically Microsoft 365 — whose removal would collapse government ministries, shutter firms, and disrupt most households.
- He documents specific entanglements between Microsoft and the Trump administration: $1M personal payment by CEO Satya Nadella to attend the inauguration, a $10M corporate donation to a Trump-linked ballroom, and M365 serving as back-end infrastructure for ICE operations.
- Cites a case where Microsoft terminated M365/Outlook access for the ICC's Chief Prosecutor after he issued a genocide warrant for Netanyahu, and a Microsoft France executive's admission the company would secretly comply with a Trump order to surveil French government/citizen data.
- Conclusion: real digital sovereignty risk lies in dependency on US-based cloud monopolists (Microsoft, Oracle, Google, Apple, Amazon) now politically fused with the Trump administration — not in AI capability itself.

## Quote

> AI isn't load-bearing. Boring-ass tech monopolist cloud software is … and we can not trust it.
