# RT @alessandrorisk: You can't fix an AI that makes things up by telling it to st

## Insights

- Apple attempted to prevent AI hallucination through direct system-level instruction: a 2024 macOS beta reportedly contained the literal directive "Do not hallucinate."
- That instruction-based fix did not work in practice — Apple's iPhone news-summary feature later produced a false headline falsely attributed to the BBC.
- Apple's own response to the failure was to disable the AI news alerts feature entirely (reported as happening in January) rather than rely on prompt-level correction.
- The incident is cited as evidence that hallucination in AI systems is not something that can be solved simply by telling the model not to do it.

## Quote

> You can't fix an AI that makes things up by telling it to stop.
