# TypeSafe AI Raises $870M Series A. 31 Silicon Valley Startups Raised $2.2B Last Week

## Insights

- TypeSafe AI raised an $870M Series A at a $7.5B valuation (led by a16z, with Sequoia and DCVC), a ~37.5x jump from its ~$200M seed valuation reached just 24 days after emerging from stealth — an unusually fast and steep markup even by current AI funding standards.
- TypeSafe AI's product, Jev, is pitched as a "System One" foundation model: a deterministic, structured decision/classification engine rather than a general text-generating LLM, betting that narrow, specialized decision models can beat general-purpose LLMs on speed, cost, and reliability.
- Sabi raised a $50M seed at a $600M valuation (Khosla Ventures lead) for a baseball-cap wearable that converts imagined speech into AI commands via a custom EEG chip and a "Brain Foundation Model" trained on 100K+ hours of neural recordings — positioned as a surgery-free alternative to implant-based brain-computer interfaces (e.g., Neuralink).
- Across the broader weekly snapshot, 31 Silicon Valley-area startups raised $2.19B total, with Enterprise AI/infrastructure startups capturing the largest share ($1.3B across 14 deals), far outpacing hardware, healthcare, energy, and other categories.
- M&A activity in the period skewed heavily toward AI tooling and infrastructure consolidation: acquisitions spanning AI coding (Harness/Augment Code), AI audio (Apple/Huxe), database infra for AI agents (Supabase/Turso), AI marketing (Hightouch/Strata), and agentic marketing (Bolt.new/Dokai).

## Quote

> Jev is... the first "System One" foundation model... a decision engine built purely for high-speed, deterministic, and structured software classification.
