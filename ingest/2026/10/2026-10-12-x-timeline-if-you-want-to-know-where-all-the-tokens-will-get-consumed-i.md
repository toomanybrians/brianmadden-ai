# If you want to know where all the tokens will get consumed in the future, here i

## Insights

- Claim: future token/compute consumption will be dominated by "agent swarms" operating across enterprise domains — software development, cybersecurity, life sciences research, AI research, and finance are named as areas where this will have major implications.
- Cited example: Prime Agent (from @PrimeIntellect) orchestrated a swarm of over 2,000 agents over two weeks to rewrite its own codebase in Rust.
- Scale of that example: the task used 10,000+ sandboxes, consumed 200B+ GLM-5.3 tokens, and involved 16,000+ agent-to-agent messages.
- This is described as the largest test yet of Prime Agent's multi-agent capabilities and supporting infrastructure.
- Underlying implication: self-directed, large-scale multi-agent coordination (agents messaging and coordinating with each other, not just single-agent task execution) is presented as the emerging consumption pattern for AI compute.
