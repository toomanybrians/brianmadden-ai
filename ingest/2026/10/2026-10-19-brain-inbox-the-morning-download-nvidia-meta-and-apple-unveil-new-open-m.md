# The Morning Download: Nvidia, Meta and Apple Unveil New Open Models

## Insights

- Nvidia released three new open models under its Nemotron family aimed at helping companies build AI agents, part of a stated strategy of regular, predictable open-model releases so developers and enterprises can "depend on" the company.
- Meta released a new open-weight model and partnered with Apple to run it locally on high-end Mac computers, adding to the pool of American-made open-model alternatives.
- Nvidia's VP of generative AI software says enterprise interest in Nemotron is driven by two factors: U.S. origin and the ability to inspect underlying training data — public-sector organizations and financial institutions are specifically wary of Chinese-made models.
- The new Nemotron 3.5 Super VL model targets "always-on" agentic tasks, processes images/video plus text, and has already been fine-tuned by companies including Atlassian, CrowdStrike, Dassault Systèmes, Salesforce, and Synopsys because customized open models can outperform generic frontier models for their specific use cases.
- Separately: data center developers (Google, Amazon) are disclosing projects earlier and offering community investments to defuse local opposition, with mixed success; AI infrastructure financing is under strain as rising borrowing costs hit heavily leveraged players like CoreWeave; and specialized AI liability insurance remains capped far below potential damages (Munich Re tops out at $50 million) even as AI developers face potentially hundred-billion-dollar exposure.
- Shopping/commerce agents (Meta's Muse, OpenAI's Dots) are increasingly making purchase decisions and auction bids for users, creating new adaptation challenges for e-commerce companies accustomed to optimizing for search and social traffic rather than agent-driven traffic.

## Quote

> This is a message to developers and our partners and enterprises that they can depend on us. — Kari Briski, Nvidia VP of generative AI software for enterprise
