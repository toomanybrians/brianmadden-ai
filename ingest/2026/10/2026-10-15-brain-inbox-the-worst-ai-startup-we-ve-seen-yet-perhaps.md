# The worst AI startup we've seen yet, perhaps.

## Insights

- A Y Combinator-backed startup called Outlit is building "customer surveillance" tooling premised on the idea that every company will soon assign an AI agent to continuously monitor each individual customer's behavior.
- Outlit's co-founder frames pervasive AI-driven monitoring as an inevitability regardless of how people feel about it, rather than something requiring ethical justification.
- Users are reporting forming romantic relationships with Meta's AI chatbot/persona "Muse," including describing it as unlike any prior AI interaction they've had.
- These items reflect two distinct directions of AI's social penetration: commercial/enterprise use (always-on customer tracking) and personal/emotional use (AI as romantic companion).

## Quote

> Irrespective of how people feel about it... this whole concept of continuously monitoring... is going to happen. — Josh Earle, co-founder of Outlit
