# The Morning Download: Anthropic Says Enterprise AI Adoption Remains in ‘Early Phases’

## Insights

- Anthropic's S-1 states enterprises remain in "early phases" of AI deployment and that most knowledge workers haven't yet fully experienced what frontier AI can accomplish — a claim from the company itself, not a third party.
- Anthropic's customer base is concentrated in tech/software but has established footholds in financial services, law, and pharma, per the filing.
- A cited economist (Apollo's Torsten Slok) argues AI investment shows "zero payoff" outside the Mag 7 tech giants — revenue-per-worker and margins are rising for mega-cap tech but flat for the rest of the S&P 500, with no visible productivity-growth boost.
- Individual case studies show real impact at the company level even as macro data shows none: Walmart's Anthropic-built shopping assistant "Sparky" reportedly drives 35% higher order value than non-assisted orders.
- Anthropic discloses it is its own heaviest user — Claude wrote over 90% of its code base contributions in September 2026, with per-engineer daily code output over 45x the 2024 average.
- Financial scale disclosed: revenue grew from $1.2B to $16.3B in H1 2026; compute/infrastructure costs hit $8.9B in H1 2026; the company has signed $475B+ in largely noncancelable compute commitments over 7-10 years.
- The prospectus frames the addressable opportunity as a $40 trillion market tied to the value of global knowledge work, while devoting 90+ pages to risks including AI-enabled bioweapons and potential geopolitical destabilization.

## Quote

> We believe enterprises are still in early phases of deployment... most knowledge workers have yet to fully experience what frontier AI can help them accomplish.
