# 10 Ways to Make Money With the Newest AI Models

## Insights

- Opinion AI's own newsletter revenue grew from $17,755 annualized to $206,673 in six months, while the author's separate AI consulting business (building agents, testing frontier models, helping companies adopt AI) has surpassed $4 million ARR.
- Author claims to know individuals earning $60K–$130K in a single month running solo businesses built around "a handful of AI agents."
- Frames the current moment as one where new AI model releases are outpacing people's ability to recognize the business opportunities they create.
- Specific new models/tools cited as enabling this: Claude Opus 5.5 (product animation generation), GPT-6 Astra (long-horizon complex task execution), OpenAI Dots, Grok Bot, Muse, and Hermes (agent usability), and Kimi K3 (cost reduction for large jobs).
- Teases ten small-scale, low-capital business models built on these tools, including a fictional AI persona selling real products, a $5/month micro-app, an AI agent that recovers money companies failed to collect, and a niche shop built around Christmas gifts.
- Emphasizes a pattern of examples that don't require large teams or large capital to start, positioning solo/small-operator AI businesses as a current growth category.

## Quote

> I genuinely think we're living through one of those rare moments when the opportunities are growing faster than people can even understand them.
