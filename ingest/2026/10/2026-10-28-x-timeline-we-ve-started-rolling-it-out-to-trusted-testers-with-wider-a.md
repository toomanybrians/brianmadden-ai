# We’ve started rolling it out to trusted testers, with wider access coming soon.

## Insights

- Genie 4 is Google DeepMind's new real-time interactive "world model" — it generates explorable, physically consistent environments from text/image prompts, combining real-time physics, reasoning, and native audio generation.
- It's built on the Gemini family: Gemini Omni grounds visual realism/behavior while other Gemini models supply the reasoning needed to maintain coherence and continuity across a simulation over time.
- Beyond passive video generation, Genie 4 supports live steering — users can modify a running simulation in natural language (change weather, move camera, add objects/people) and the model adapts the scene contextually.
- A notable new capability is native audio generation, including ambient sound and interactive, adaptive dialogue with in-world characters that respond to user input in real time.
- DeepMind pairs Genie 4 with Gemini 3.8 Flash acting as an agent-in-the-loop: it observes generated frames, decomposes goals into steps, verifies task progress, and replans as the world or user changes — enabling multi-step, causally reliable simulated events.
- DeepMind frames this as infrastructure for AGI development broadly: training environments for robots/agents, testing how AI systems navigate environments before real-world deployment, and new interactive media experiences.
- Rollout is staged: starting with trusted testers/research preview, then expanding to Google AI Ultra subscribers (Project Genie) and early API/Google AI Studio access partners via waitlist.
