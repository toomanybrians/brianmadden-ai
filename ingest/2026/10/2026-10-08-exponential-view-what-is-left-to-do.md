# 🔮 What is left to do

## Insights

- OpenAI released results from an unreleased frontier model: 722 mathematical manuscripts across 372 families spanning number theory, complexity theory, and mathematical physics, each averaging roughly three hours of equivalent ChatGPT Pro compute to produce.
- Scientist Derya Unutmaz claims this output represents 81% of major math discoveries from the past three years — a figure the piece flags as staggering even if halved.
- Verification is partial: many results have been checked in the Lean formal-proof system, but not all of them.
- A proposed framework suggests mathematics may bifurcate into two layers: "machine mathematics" (vast, formally verified, consumed mainly by AI systems) and "human mathematics" (a compressed, comprehensible subset — an "effective theory" of the machine frontier).
- The piece frames this as a potential epistemic turning point comparable in scale to the Enlightenment's shift from supernatural to natural/empirical explanation — raising the question of how humans retain understanding when formal verification outpaces human comprehension.

## Quote

> Humans won't have enough context to understand the underlying web of machine-invented concepts.
