# What happens to your AI strategy if the #AI bubble pops?

Here, Brian Madden exp

## Insights

- A bubble burst would mean one of two things: AI capability gains stall, and/or AI costs stop falling — these are the two legs of the current "progress narrative" that could break.
- Unlike past infrastructure bubbles (railroads, dot-com fiber), leftover AI hardware/models may not remain accessible — serving frontier models today may not even be profitable, so post-pop access could become far more expensive, slower, or restricted.
- Governments (US/China) could classify frontier models as national-security assets and restrict access, citing the existing precedent set with Mythos, Fable, and GPT-5.6.
- The proposed hedge is open-weight models (downloadable, independent of the lab that built them) — current best-in-class options cited are GLM-5.2 (Z.ai, MIT-licensed), with Kimi K3 (Moonshot) and Qwen3.8 (Alibaba) noted as emerging but not yet confirmed open.
- Open-weight models in the Sonnet-class range are described as already sufficient for most enterprise ROI use cases, which today centers on AI orchestrating administrative/work processes rather than frontier-level synthesis.
- Recommended strategy elements: build a personal/organizational "second brain" or "knowledge factory," govern the workspace rather than the specific model, manage model routing/token economics, and keep data and context portable across vendors and model types.
