# 📈 What is bigger than AI?

## Insights

- GLP-1 drug sales reportedly surpassed generative AI revenue by the end of 2025 ($76B vs. $63B), framed as a possible "crossover year" in revenue growth trajectories.
- More than twice as many Americans reportedly pay for GLP-1s as pay for AI products/subscriptions.
- Pricing dynamics show steep divergence: US list price has stayed flat since 2021 (~$1,349/month), but cash/self-pay prices have dropped to $199–499/month, and Indian generic pricing is near $13/month after patent expiry — framed as a volume-over-price strategy by manufacturers.
- Adherence is a major open question: real-world persistence rates are low (Danish and UK studies show 50-85% drop-off within one to two years), despite drugs being approved/intended for indefinite use.
- Beyond weight loss, GLP-1s are described as a "temperance technology" with documented effects on alcohol/substance-abuse behavior, and possible (unconfirmed, preprint-stage) cardiovascular aging effects independent of weight loss.
- Economic impact evidence is mixed: one study found reduced sick leave worth ~$722/employee/year, while another found no net medical cost savings for insurers over five years — actual real-world value remains contested across different populations (e.g., larger projected lifetime gains for those without a high school diploma vs. smaller gains for college graduates).

## Quote

> GLP-1s are a temperance technology with wide societal impact.
