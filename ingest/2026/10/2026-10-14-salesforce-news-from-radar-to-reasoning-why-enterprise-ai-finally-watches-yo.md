# From Radar to Reasoning: Why Enterprise AI Finally Watches Your Business in Real Time

## Insights

- Salesforce frames "operational intelligence" as AI that continuously reasons over live business data rather than summarizing historical dashboards — positioned as a new product category, attributed to Chief Scientist Silvio Savarese.
- Two named technical components: Deep Insights (reasons over structured business data, performs root-cause tracing, recommends next steps) and Moirai (a time-series forecasting model that predicts demand/flags anomalies with business context like launches or renewal cycles built in).
- Internal dogfooding example: Salesforce's own "Engineering 360" system uses Deep Insights across 90 billion records spanning dozens of internal systems to find cross-dataset correlations and patterns described as beyond human-scale detection capability.
- A second internal use case: Salesforce's Contact Center and Voice org applies the same forecasting approach to staffing/demand planning, aiming to anticipate problems before they manifest operationally.
- Savarese's stated ambition is to extend this beyond engineering/support into sales pipeline health, marketing performance, and supply chain shortage detection — any domain where data volume outpaces human monitoring capacity.
- The piece is structured around a historical analogy (WWII radar development) to argue AI's value is making existing signal "legible" and actionable in time, not replacing human judgment — worth noting as framing/marketing device rather than technical claim.

## Quote

> Deep Insights gives Engineering 360 something no human would be able to accomplish at scale.
