# I tried four personal AI agents. I still have to call the bank

## Insights

- Tested four personal AI agent products (Meta's Muse, OpenAI's Dots, X/xAI's Grok Bot, and Instinct) hands-on to assess real-world friction for an ordinary (non-technical) user, not just feature claims.
- Each agent required linking personal accounts (email, payment) before doing anything useful, and the author overrode her own privacy hesitations each time to proceed — suggesting onboarding friction is less about distrust in principle and more about habituated willingness to share data once a platform is already familiar.
- Payment/checkout remains a hard failure point: Meta's Muse (Pip) got partway through an independent purchase but failed at the payment gateway (Stripe Link), killing the task entirely.
- OpenAI's Dots (requiring a $100/month Pro upgrade vs. $20 Plus) succeeded at breaking down a tedious bureaucratic task (401k transfer paperwork) into manageable steps, but still punted the actual unresolved step (calling the bank) back to the human.
- Grok Bot succeeded at a simple, bounded task (drafting and sending an email) with a believable tone match, but this is functionality already available via existing chat assistants.
- Instinct (invite-only, used via WhatsApp/text) got furthest toward an actual purchase (ballet tickets), escalating from search to cart to a final $404 payment-approval prompt, but the tester stopped before completion, so full end-to-end success was never confirmed — and no agent demonstrated unsupervised, friction-free task completion from start to finish.

## Quote

> I want help, but I'm not yet willing to hand over access to everything.
