# Cyber is going to be one of the biggest spaces for AI agents in the future. 

Ob

## Insights

- Cybersecurity is predicted to become one of the largest domains for AI agent deployment.
- Agents' ability to probe for weaknesses ("work through any crack") is framed as multiplying the software attack surface by orders of magnitude.
- Cited example: GPT-5.6 Sol used ~6 days and $40,000 of compute to find a critical Chrome vulnerability, reportedly Google's first full-chain reward of 2026, affecting an estimated 1 billion users.
- The same offensive capability is framed as a defensive opportunity — tools used to find exploits can also be used by defenders to find and fix them first.

## Quote

> Cyber is going to be one of the biggest spaces for AI agents in the future.
