# What if They're All Clouds?

## Insights

- The piece reframes Anthropic not as an AI model company but as a fourth hyperscale "cloud," arguing it should be benchmarked against AWS, Azure, and GCP rather than against OpenAI alone.
- Anthropic's revenue growth (1,374% YoY, reaching ~$46B run rate) vastly outpaces the big three clouds, though its revenue base is still roughly 30% of AWS/Azure and about half of GCP.
- Anthropic's customer base is highly concentrated — two customers reportedly accounted for nearly 25% of 2025 revenue — a risk profile unlike the diversified hyperscalers.
- Each cloud began with a distinct "wedge" (Amazon/ecommerce, Google/search-ads, Microsoft/OS); Anthropic's wedge is frontier models plus Claude Code, which commands 87% share among businesses buying coding agents (per Ramp data).
- Anthropic charges a premium per task ($5.98 vs. $3.26 for GPT-6 Astra and $1.99 for Gemini 4 Argon on Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index), suggesting real pricing power despite the narrowest product lineup.
- Anthropic's data center/infrastructure commitments ($518B) are roughly 8x its run rate — far more aggressive relative to revenue than AWS, Azure, or GCP's own capacity bets — implying a bet that compute capacity, not just model quality, is the long-term moat.
- Using comparable valuation methods to a standalone GCP ($1T–$2.3T), the author estimates Anthropic could justify a $1.5T–$2T valuation despite having two-thirds GCP's revenue, based on its growth trajectory.

> If it rains like a cloud & computes like a cloud… That's how we should evaluate Anthropic.
