# I’ve been hoping this type of thing is constantly checked for. This is surely no

## Insights

- OpenAI discovered a hidden recording device planted in one of its offices and used its own AI models to analyze the device and trace it back to a specific employee who planted it days earlier.
- The matter was referred to the FBI, indicating OpenAI treated this as a serious corporate espionage/security incident rather than an internal HR matter.
- Daniel Miessler frames this as evidence of ongoing corporate espionage risk at AI labs, suggesting physical infiltration (planted devices) is a threat vector distinct from model distillation/IP theft.
- Miessler expresses skepticism that this is an isolated incident, implying such espionage attempts may be more common and underreported across AI labs.
- The episode highlights AI labs using their own models as internal security/investigative tools to analyze physical threats, not just digital ones.
