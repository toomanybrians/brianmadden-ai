# The AI Arms Race Is Not Inevitable. It’s Just Profitable.

## Insights

- The piece frames the AGI race as driven by a specific prize: whoever first automates AI R&D itself could convert a temporary technical lead into a permanent one, since AI that "makes labor itself" removes the historical constraint that automation could only substitute capital for labor "up to a point."
- Frontier AI is actually a concentrated, trackable industry (a handful of companies, reliant on Nvidia/TSMC/ASML chip supply chains), making "you can't regulate math" arguments against AI governance misleading — compute governance is technically feasible without mass surveillance.
- Existing US legal authorities (national security/emergency powers, export controls, federal contract terms) could already halt or restrict frontier training runs, but enforcement has been undermined by industry lobbying — Nvidia specifically is described as aggressively fighting export controls, including alleged efforts to get opponents fired from think tanks/government roles.
- Verifying an international AI agreement is harder than verifying nuclear arms control because data centers are general-purpose (unlike distinctive nuclear facilities); proposed solutions include "confidential computing" built into AI chips and tamper-evident hardware logging, both potentially deployable within 1-3 years.
- Comparative claim: China's stated AI ambitions (per a 2017 industry revenue target) are more modest than often portrayed, and Chinese leadership reportedly expresses more institutional openness to AI safety cooperation and willingness to restrict/shut down AI systems than the US government, based on interviews with researchers (Russell, Hinton, Bengio) and policy comparisons.
- A concrete policy proposal is floated: a bilateral US-China agreement banning development of systems designed to fully automate labor/AI R&D ("the Obsoleting Machine") until there's public buy-in and scientific consensus such systems can be built safely — distinct from broader bans on "superintelligence."

## Quote

> The Obsoleting Machine really begins when it obsoletes the people building it.
