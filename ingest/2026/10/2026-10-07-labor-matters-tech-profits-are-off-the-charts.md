# Tech profits are off the charts

## Insights

- Profits of the information/computer/electronics sector hit 2.4% of GDP in Q2, a record, up from roughly 1% through the 2010s — more than doubling since 2020.
- The climb in tech profit share steepened further after ChatGPT's release, suggesting a link between the generative AI boom and sector profitability.
- Argues this profit growth is evidence the AI investment boom is "paying for itself" rather than being purely speculative/bubble-driven, at least so far.
- Suggests some of these gains may be a transfer rather than new value creation — surplus generated in other industries (hospitals, banks, stores) shifting to the tech firms supplying AI tools as "West Coast tech" expands into more of the economy.
- Concludes that a boom this profitable is self-funding and predicts investment will not slow soon.

## Quote

> A boom these profitable keeps funding itself.
