# AI Agents Replacing Visa Consultants

## Insights

- The piece frames AI agents as distinct from chatbots: rather than answering isolated questions, an agent works through a connected sequence of steps toward a goal, adjusting its next action based on what it discovers.
- Visa preparation and travel planning are used as a concrete case study: an agent can organize documents, check itineraries, draft cover letters, compare flight routes, and flag problems before they become costly mistakes.
- With appropriate permissions, agents can extend into action-taking, not just advice — managing bookings and reminders rather than just surfacing information.
- The author positions this as part of a broader shift in AI's value proposition: from generating answers to getting real work done.
- The piece suggests future AI systems may be judged less on how intelligently they respond and more on how reliably they help people act.
- Human judgment is noted as still mattering somewhere in this process, though the excerpt doesn't specify where (it's flagged as covered "inside the full guide").

## Quote

> AI may be judged not just by how intelligently it responds, but by how reliably it helps people act.
