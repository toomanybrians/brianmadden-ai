# The most convincing chart

## Insights

- Employment in FIIPB industries (finance, insurance, information, and professional/business services) has fallen for three consecutive months and was down ~2% since end of 2022, with recent data revisions pushing earlier months lower.
- Real output in these same industries rose 16% over the same period, breaking a prior ~20-year pattern where employment and output moved together.
- The author argues this divergence is the clearest evidence yet that something structurally unusual is occurring in these sectors.
- The explanation offered: these industries concentrate exactly the back-office and junior tasks that current AI handles well, so firms are meeting steady/growing demand with fewer workers.
- This dynamic is linked to struggles among young college graduates, since FIIPB has historically been a primary entry point for new graduate hiring.
- Data sourced from BEA real value-added by industry (through 2026Q2) and BLS CES employment figures (through September 2026).

## Quote

> For twenty years the two lines moved together. To me this chart remains the most convincing evidence that something unusual is happening.
