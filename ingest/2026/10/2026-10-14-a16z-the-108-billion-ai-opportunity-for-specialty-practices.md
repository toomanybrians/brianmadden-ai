# The $108 Billion AI Opportunity for Specialty Practices

## Insights

- Prosper AI's study (drawing on $3B+ in managed care data, MGMA benchmarks, and a 2,000-practice phone-access study called CallIQ) estimates ~$125,000 in annual recoverable/incremental revenue per physician across four AI-addressable levers.
- The four levers: capturing missed-call demand (18% daytime missed-call rate, 81% after hours), re-engaging patients who fall out of the care cycle (only 54% of follow-ups occur on schedule), preventing avoidable claim denials (10% average first-submission denial rate, driven largely by front-end administrative errors), and recovering patient-owed balances (patients now cover 36% of care costs, with ~8% going unpaid).
- Framing is explicitly about AI as a revenue lever, not just a cost/efficiency tool — positioned against structural margin pressure from stagnant/declining reimbursements (inflation-adjusted Medicare payments down 33% since 2001) and rising operating costs (84% of practices report costs up ~11% year-over-year).
- Case example: a dermatology/plastic surgery practice using AI call-handling saw a 17% lift in booked appointments, wait times during peak hours drop from 7 minutes to zero, and simultaneous call capacity rise from 15 to 50.
- A randomized comparison found AI-driven outbound billing calls recovered 31.2% of outstanding patient balances versus 21.1% with standard practice follow-up over ~50 days.
- The piece frames the real opportunity as levers compounding together: an AI system that verifies benefits and informs patients of costs pre-visit improves show-rates, payment rates, and denial prevention simultaneously, rather than each lever working in isolation.

## Quote

> The bigger opportunity is what happens when the four levers work together.
