# Why people HATE automation

## Insights

- The term "Luddite" is commonly used today as an insult for anyone who resists technology, but this is a misreading of the original movement.
- The historical Luddites were motivated by a desire to preserve their jobs and way of life "at all costs" — not blanket opposition to technology itself.
- Shapiro draws a direct parallel between the Luddites and "mathematicians" today, implying a specific knowledge-work group currently facing similar displacement pressure from automation.
- The Luddite history is framed as a case study in how power intersects with progress — illustrating a recurring pattern of who benefits and who gets left behind when technology advances.

## Quote

> They wanted to preserve their jobs and their way of life, at all costs.
