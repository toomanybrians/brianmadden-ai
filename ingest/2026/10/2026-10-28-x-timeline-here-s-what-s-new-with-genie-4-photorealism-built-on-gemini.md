# Here’s what’s new with Genie 4:
🔵 Photorealism: Built on Gemini Omni for lifelik

## Insights

- Genie 4 is described as DeepMind's most realistic and interactive "world model" to date, generating dynamic environments rather than static scenes.
- The model is built on Gemini Omni, which the announcement credits for photorealistic rendering of spaces and subjects.
- Claimed physics capabilities include aerodynamics and collision handling, with people depicted moving "naturally" within generated environments.
- The system supports real-time prompting to modify scenes, add elements, or interact with objects/characters as they unfold.
- Generated people in these environments are described as able to move, react, and speak, suggesting simulated agents with behavioral responsiveness, not just visual assets.

## Quote

> This is Genie 4, our most realistic and interactive world model yet.
