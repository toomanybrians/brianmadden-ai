# You Have No Idea How Close the AI Bubble Is to Popping

## Insights

- Anthropic's quarterly revenue reportedly leaked via FT from a private investor meeting: $16+ billion in a single quarter, 46% QoQ growth, and ~$74B run-rate revenue — figures described as historically unprecedented for a company this young.
- Despite these numbers, investors are reportedly panicking because growth decelerated sharply from a prior ~145% QoQ rate, reading the slowdown as an exponential curve flattening into a sigmoid.
- The piece argues Anthropic has effectively replaced OpenAI as the AI industry's "too big to fail" lynchpin in 2025, meaning its performance is treated as proxied for the broader tech-dependent US economy (citing BofA/Bloomberg on SP500 exposure).
- Naive extrapolation suggests Anthropic will miss its purported $100B run-rate revenue target unless Q4 growth re-accelerates; the author is skeptical the upcoming "Fable 5.5" model will unlock enough new use cases to restore exponential growth.
- Anthropic's rumored $2 trillion IPO valuation and $518 billion in infrastructure commitments (80% non-cancelable) are framed as hard to justify against decelerating growth.
- The current AI investment boom is characterized as the largest in history, larger than railroads, dot-com, or canal booms — raising the stakes of any slowdown.

## Quote

> It only takes some finite amount of time to turn a prideful exponential into a disappointing sigmoid.
