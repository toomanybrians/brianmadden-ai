# Called to serve: Tech, research, and positive impact

## Insights

- Memex, a DARPA project White developed, connected disparate pieces of information across hard-to-index parts of the web to help build evidence for prosecutions; it reportedly helped increase trafficking prosecutions tenfold in New York City.
- White frames "problem selection" (sometimes called "taste") as the most consequential part of research — more than execution — because it determines which future gets imagined and worked backward from.
- Current Microsoft Research Catalyst Lab priorities described include cyber defense/AI security (protecting digital infrastructure against AI-enabled attacks) and tools that help humans analyze large volumes of content to augment creativity and capability.
- White cites Project Natick (an experimental underwater datacenter) as a model for productive research culture: radical, out-there thinking tested empirically, which generated outsized recognition and surprising results (e.g., no disruption to marine ecosystem, efficiency gains).
- He describes Microsoft Research's value proposition as combining "freedom" in problem selection with structured mechanisms for proving value, usability, and policy/integration fit — a claimed differentiator for turning research ideas into adopted technology.

## Quote

> "Problem selection... it's the place where the angle of the whole thing changes." — Chris White
