# Endpoint hardware prices went up. Your budget did not. 🥴

How much have your lap

## Insights

- The pitch centers on a cost squeeze: endpoint hardware (laptops, thin clients) prices have risen while IT budgets have not kept pace.
- Citrix positions UniconOS (formerly eLux) as a lightweight, hardened OS that lets organizations repurpose existing/aging hardware instead of refreshing it, claiming over 35% reduction in hardware/IT costs.
- Framed explicitly around Windows 10 end-of-support and IGEL OS 11 end-of-maintenance as forcing functions pushing enterprises toward costly hardware refreshes — UniconOS is offered as a way to avoid that by virtualizing into Windows 11 via Citrix DaaS instead.
- New "dual boot" feature: a second isolated, hardened UniconOS partition sits alongside Windows so that if Windows fails (ransomware, bad patch, corruption), users can reboot into UniconOS and resume work via Citrix DaaS in minutes, without spare hardware or reimaging.
- Broader claims include device lifecycle extension, sustainability/e-waste reduction, centralized endpoint management across diverse hardware, and support for hybrid/remote work security via boot-to-VDI architecture.
- The product is bundled into existing Citrix Enterprise licensing (UHMC & CPL), removing the need for a separate third-party endpoint OS purchase.
