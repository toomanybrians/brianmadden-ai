# The only way the AI boom doesn’t go bust is if it takes most people’s jobs. http

## Insights

- Argues the AI boom can only avoid a bust if AI ends up displacing most people's jobs — implying current spending/valuation levels only make sense in a mass-automation scenario.
- Cites a claim that for the AI buildout to pay off financially, Americans would need to spend roughly 9% of GDP annually on AI services.
- Frames that 9% figure by comparison: it's roughly equivalent to total U.S. spending on food, meant to convey how implausibly large the number is.
- References a Columbia paper presented at Brookings estimating the AI revenue required to hit roughly $3.5 (trillion, figure cut off in source) to justify the buildout.

## Quote

> The only way the AI boom doesn't go bust is if it takes most people's jobs.
