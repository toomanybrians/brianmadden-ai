# Salesforce Signs Definitive Agreement to Acquire Rune Technologies

## Insights

- Salesforce is acquiring Rune Technologies, an edge-native logistics platform for military/defense use, to extend its Missionforce portfolio into "tactical edge" operations where connectivity is denied, degraded, intermittent, or limited (DDIL).
- The stated architecture frames defense operations as three layers — Enterprise systems of record, Strategic HQ planning, and Operational Edge — with Salesforce claiming it already owns the first two and acquiring Rune to cover the third, positioning itself as an "edge-to-enterprise" platform.
- Rune's technology uses forecasting, simulation, and optimization models to predict supply/resupply needs and recommend resource allocation and routing, syncing data back to central systems once connectivity resumes.
- Salesforce cites rapid growth in its year-old defense business (80% YoY) and a 10-year, $5.6B-ceiling U.S. Army contract as evidence of momentum, framing this as its largest national-security-focused acquisition to date.
- Rune was founded in 2024 by ex-Anduril leaders and is already deployed with the U.S. Army and Marine Corps; deal is pending customary closing conditions, expected to close Salesforce's fiscal Q4 2027.
- Integration claims include "no custom integration" needed for edge data to land in enterprise systems of record — a strong vendor claim without technical detail to substantiate it.

## Quote

> "Salesforce is doubling down on national security with our most significant acquisition in this space." — Kendall Collins, President and CEO, Missionforce and Government Cloud
