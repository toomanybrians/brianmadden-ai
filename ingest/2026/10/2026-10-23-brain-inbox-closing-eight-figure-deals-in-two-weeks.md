# Closing Eight Figure Deals in Two Weeks

## Insights

- AI sales splits into two distinct markets with different dynamics: infrastructure (e.g., inference providers like Fireworks AI) and applications (e.g., Granola) — and the sales motions differ structurally between them.
- Infrastructure deals can close at eight figures in roughly two weeks because AI-native customers are growing extraordinarily fast, but the same speed invites intense competition since every inference provider chases the same accounts.
- Rep quota capacity in infrastructure sales is described as "multiples higher" than in traditional software sales, changing how comp plans need to work.
- Rapid product and model evolution (new capability ceilings roughly every 45 days) is pushing comp planning from an annual to a quarterly cadence.
- Infrastructure sales teams pair AEs with FDEs (forward-deployed engineers) and SEs (sales engineers) to handle complex product delivery, while application-layer teams pair AEs with CSMs for software delivery that resembles prior eras but with faster adoption/expansion.
- Risk of understaffing in infrastructure sales shows up specifically as under-investment in outbound and expansion effort.
