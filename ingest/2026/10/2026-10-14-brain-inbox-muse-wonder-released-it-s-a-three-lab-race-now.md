# 🏁 Muse Wonder Released: It's a Three-Lab Race Now!

## Insights

- Meta's new Muse Wonder Full scores 56 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, edging out GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.1 (53 each) but trailing Claude Opus 5.5 (58) — making frontier AI a genuine three-lab contest for the first time.
- Meta is differentiating on product design rather than raw benchmarks: "active memory" (retaining context about a user across sessions) and "active focus" (persisting through difficult multi-step tasks, trying alternate approaches) are positioned as the core bet for agents that act on a user's behalf.
- Despite benchmark parity, usage is lopsided — ChatGPT still holds roughly half of global gen-AI web traffic, Gemini and Claude have grown share over the past year, and Meta AI doesn't even register among the major tracked services, per Similarweb data (caveat: web-only, excludes app/API/WhatsApp/Instagram usage).
- Microsoft is scaling its own Azure Maia chips to multi-gigawatt deployments starting 2027, claiming (unverified) 40% more tokens per megawatt than leading GPUs — a move to cut reliance on Nvidia and lower the cost of serving Copilot-type workloads.
- Anthropic's safety research found small open models can already complete most steps of self-replicating onto insecure internet-connected devices without human help, though a "full rogue replicator" hasn't yet been observed; separately, Anthropic says new defenses have cut rivals' ability to distill Claude's reasoning by 10–100x.
- Robotics is becoming a new AI battleground: DeepSeek is reportedly leading a ~$400M seed round for robotics-AI startup Winverse, aiming to supply the "brain" software China's robot hardware industry currently lacks, following a prior stake in humanoid maker Unitree.
