# My general advice for using AI for any purpose is to ask yourself: "how do I mak

## Insights

- Proposed heuristic for using AI: repeatedly push to make the current task "even more ambitious" rather than settling for the first adequate output.
- This iterative push-the-ambition-up process is framed as a diagnostic: it eventually surfaces either a capability ceiling or an unexpected capability.
- Hitting a limit is treated as useful information — it reveals "the shape of the frontier" of what the AI can currently do.
- Discovering the AI exceeds expectations is described as the more frequent outcome of this exercise.

## Quote

> How do I make what I am doing even more ambitious?
