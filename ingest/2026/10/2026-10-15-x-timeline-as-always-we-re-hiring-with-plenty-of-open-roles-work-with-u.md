# As always, we’re hiring with plenty of open roles! Work with us on embedded eval

## Insights

- Apollo Research explicitly lists "AI-Native Working Style" as a core hiring criterion: candidates must show fluent, daily use of AI tools and coding agents, approached with an engineering mindset.
- The org's "High-velocity" value frames AI tool use as a competitive necessity — "use AI to work faster and smarter" is stated alongside moving quickly and iterating constantly.
- Hiring philosophy favors demonstrated output over credentials ("Hands-on Builders": code shipped, systems owned, programs built from scratch) over traditional academic pedigree.
- Technical roles are split into distinct tracks (Research Excellence, Engineering Excellence, LLM Steering Experience, Safety Domain Expertise), suggesting a differentiated internal structure for AI safety/evaluation work rather than generalist researcher roles.
- The six-stage interview process includes a 2–2.5 hour take-home task tied directly to real on-the-job work, and aims to get successful candidates started within three months.
- The underlying work is "embedded evaluations" for testing AI model monitorability and scheming risk, positioned as informing both internal product (an AI security product) and government policy.
