# Ilya doesn’t believe scale is all anymore; Hinton is behind. https://t.co/frswW7

## Insights

- Geoffrey Hinton reportedly acknowledges that Ilya Sutskever was right to argue that simply scaling up models would drive progress, a position Hinton initially doubted as "too easy."
- Hinton's original view held that real AI progress required new architectural ideas, not just more data and compute.
- Hinton now concedes that new architectures did contribute, but credits scale (data and computation) as a major driver of progress.
- The framing (via Gary Marcus's post) suggests Sutskever himself may now be shifting away from a pure scaling-is-all-you-need stance, per the tweet's opening line.

## Quote

> Geoffrey Hinton says Ilya Sutskever kept insisting the answer was simply to make models bigger.
