# Why Salesforce Lets You Pick the Right AI Model for the Job

## Insights

- Salesforce's model strategy is explicit multi-model orchestration rather than a single proprietary LLM: customers can mix frontier models (Anthropic, OpenAI), open-source models, and Salesforce's own custom model depending on task needs, cost, and speed.
- Koa is described as Salesforce's first "CRM reasoning model," built with NVIDIA, run inside Salesforce's own trust boundary, and trained on simulations derived from 27 years of Salesforce CRM workflow data — positioned to work alongside frontier models, not replace them.
- Salesforce frames the choice criteria concretely as speed of resolution and cost, implying different models get routed to different task types (e.g., precise multistep reasoning tasks vs. fast/cheap tasks).
- CDO Michael Andrew frames AI agents as fundamentally nondeterministic ("big artificial brains") in contrast to deterministic business software, explicitly comparing agent reliability to a trainable-but-imperfect new employee rather than a repeatable function.
- Slack is positioned as a "multiplayer AI" environment: agents operate in shared team channels with full conversational context (deals, customer issues) rather than in a private 1:1 user-agent tab, with permissions/security scoped to the user's channel access.
- The strategic rationale given for model flexibility is competitive/market-driven: models are "changing all the time" as vendors compete, so lock-in to one model is framed as a liability Salesforce wants to avoid for customers.

## Quote

> "AI is a fuel; it's more fuel into your engine, and we want to let that fuel come from anywhere." — Michael Andrew, Salesforce CDO
