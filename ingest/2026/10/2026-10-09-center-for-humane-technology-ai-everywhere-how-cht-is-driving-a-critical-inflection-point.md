# AI Everywhere: How CHT is Driving A Critical Inflection Point

## Insights

- The piece frames a perceived "critical inflection point" in AI discourse, citing converging signals: accumulating cybersecurity incidents, a high-profile resignation from Anthropic (Jacob Coxon), and Dario Amodei's public call to slow AI development.
- Amodei's slowdown call reportedly drew backing from Sam Altman, Demis Hassabis, and Elon Musk — framed as a notable moment of cross-company alignment on AI risk.
- CHT describes deliberately pursuing cross-ideological media reach, with Tristan Harris appearing across outlets spanning the political spectrum (CNN, Fox, Steve Bannon's War Room, Glenn Beck, CNBC, ABC World News Tonight) in the same news cycle following Amodei's CNN interview.
- CHT's documentary "The AI Doc: Or How I Became an Apocaloptimist" reached Netflix's US Top 10, positioned by CHT as a tool for communicating AI race "narratives" and underlying "incentives" to a mass audience.
- Harris and Aza Raskin engaged in public dialogue with Bill Gates (following a Gates essay described as sparking a "zeitgeist shift") and appeared alongside Bernie Sanders and Steve Bannon at a "Pro-Human Assembly," signaling an explicit strategy of building left-right coalition pressure on AI policy.
- CHT states it is working on unspecified "coordinated action" behind the scenes, suggesting organizational moves beyond media/public messaging are in progress.
