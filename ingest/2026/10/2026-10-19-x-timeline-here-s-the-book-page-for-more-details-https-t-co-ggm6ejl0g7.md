# Here’s the book page for more details https://t.co/GGm6EJL0G7

## Insights

- Paul Roetzer's new book, "The AI Transformation Blueprint," proposes a framework called the AI Transformation System (AITS) meant to move organizations from AI experimentation to sustained innovation.
- The book splits into two parallel frameworks: an 8-pillar organizational blueprint (Vision, Strategy, Data, Technology, Governance, Literacy, People, Performance) and a 5-pillar individual blueprint (Knowledge, Application, Judgment, Impact, Mindset).
- Underlying philosophy is explicitly "human-centered": AI is positioned to unlock human potential and enable more creative/strategic work rather than replace people.
- The book includes two practical artifacts — a "Responsible AI Manifesto" (principles for trust/accountability) and an "AI-Forward CEO Memo" (template communication for leaders managing workforce change).
- A companion assessment platform is offered to pair with the book, intended to help organizations/individuals establish AI maturity baselines, identify gaps, and measure progress over time.
- Frames the core leadership challenge as balancing responsible, human-centered AI adoption against pressure for near-term efficiency and profitability gains.

## Quote

> The winners will not be those who chase every new tool, but those who build the leadership, literacy, systems, and culture to continuously evolve.
