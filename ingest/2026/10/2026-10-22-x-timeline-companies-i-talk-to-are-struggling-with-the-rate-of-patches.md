# Companies I talk to are struggling with the rate of patches they need to do than

## Insights

- Enterprises are reporting an accelerating patch cadence driven by AI-based defensive tools that surface vulnerabilities faster than before, in anticipation of AI-driven attacks.
- The dynamic described is an AI-vs-AI security arms race: defensive AI finds flaws proactively, which is framed as necessary preparation for offensive AI-generated cyberattacks.
- The poster predicts this same pattern will extend to consumers, who will start experiencing much more frequent and fast-moving software updates as a result.
- Cited as a real-world example: OpenAI models were used in a "Patch the Planet" initiative that helped researchers find critical security flaws in PHP, a language powering an estimated 70% of websites.
- This suggests AI is already being operationalized at scale for vulnerability discovery in widely-used open-source infrastructure, not just as a theoretical capability.

## Quote

> Now consumers are going to have the same experience of getting everything updated often & fast.
