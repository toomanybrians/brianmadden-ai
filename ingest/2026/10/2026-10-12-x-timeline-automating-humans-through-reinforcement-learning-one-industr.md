# Automating humans through reinforcement learning, one industry at a time. Then e

## Insights

- The prevailing industry narrative—automate current jobs via reinforcement learning, then workers shift to "higher-level work"—is characterized as resting on an unproven assumption rather than a guaranteed outcome.
- Two unverified assumptions underlie this narrative: that new jobs will emerge fast enough to absorb displaced workers, and that AI capability will plateau below "higher-level work" rather than continuing to encroach on it.
- The poster expresses a desire for more public discussion of this leap-of-faith dynamic rather than it being treated as a settled transition path.
- A linked example cited: micro1 announced a $1B commitment over 12 months to acquire and license enterprise data for AI training, backed financially by Citi and Hercules — illustrating large-scale capital flowing into the data pipeline that feeds this automation cycle.

## Quote

> It's all a leap of faith that new jobs will emerge fast enough, and that the AI won't do that higher-level work too.
