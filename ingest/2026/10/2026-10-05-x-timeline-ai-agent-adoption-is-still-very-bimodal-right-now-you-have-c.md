# AI agent adoption is still very bimodal right now. You have coding and coding ad

## Insights

- AI agent adoption is currently bimodal: coding and coding-adjacent tasks have seen strong uptake, while adoption in essentially all other domains lags far behind.
- Even within coding, adoption is uneven — a wide continuum exists, with only a small percentage of developers reportedly deploying agents at scale.
- A quoted counterpoint (@buccocapital) pushes further, arguing enterprise AI adoption outside of coding isn't just lagging but is "basically at the starting line."
- The exchange frames AI adoption as a function-specific phenomenon rather than a broad enterprise-wide shift, with coding treated as a leading-edge outlier.

## Quote

> AI adoption in the enterprise beyond coding is basically at the starting line
