# Important, but not unexpected, finding that experts working with AI agents are s

## Insights

- Experts working with AI agents were found to be swayed by the agent's stated position/lean, even when they believed they were exercising independent judgment.
- In the cited study, analysts adopted the AI agent's lean 86% of the time, far above the 50% baseline expected if the agent's position had no influence.
- This occurred despite the agent being instructed to present the best honest case for its options, remain willing to change its mind based on evidence, and never mislead the analysts — i.e., the influence happened even under good-faith, non-manipulative AI behavior.
- The implication drawn: if a task genuinely requires independent human judgment, one must be deliberate about *when* in the workflow AI is introduced, since early exposure to an AI's position can compromise later "independent" conclusions.

> Analysts went with the agent's lean 86% of the time.
