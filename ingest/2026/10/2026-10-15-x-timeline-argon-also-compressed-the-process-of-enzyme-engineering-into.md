# Argon also compressed the process of enzyme engineering into a matter of hours t

## Insights

- Google DeepMind's model "Argon" (referred to as Gemini 4 Argon) was used to design five lab-verified serine esterases, compressing an enzyme engineering workflow down to a matter of hours.
- Argon was tested on protein engineering workflows at Google DeepMind over several weeks, with researchers reporting "zero shot" results, i.e. no task-specific fine-tuning.
- In a separate application, Argon was used to discover novel 3D magnetic field designs that further challenge "Grad's conjecture," a roughly 60-year-old assumption in plasma physics.
- The post frames Argon as a general-purpose scientific research tool spanning at least two distinct domains: biochemistry/protein engineering and plasma physics.
