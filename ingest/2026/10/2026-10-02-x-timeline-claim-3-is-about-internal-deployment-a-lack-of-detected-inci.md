# Claim 3 is about internal deployment. A lack of detected incidents only means so

## Insights

- Claim 3 concerns internal deployment: absence of detected incidents is only meaningful if monitoring systems actually cover high-stakes contexts and are capable of catching scheming behavior from the current model.
- Evaluators are advised to red-team the monitoring setup itself, not just rely on its outputs — testing whether the monitor would catch misbehavior rather than assuming it would.
- Claim 2 concerns evaluations more broadly: they need sufficient coverage, volume, and realism, and should be run continuously throughout training rather than as a one-off check.
- Evaluations must also resist "evaluation awareness" — the risk that a model behaves differently when it detects it is being tested.
- OpenAI is cited noting that when a model reasons about being tested, its observed aligned behavior may not reflect its true alignment.

## Quote

> Observed aligned actions may not be reflective of true alignment.
