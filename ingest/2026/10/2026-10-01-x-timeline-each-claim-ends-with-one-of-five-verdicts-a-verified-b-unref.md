# Each claim ends with one of five verdicts:
 A. Verified
 B. Unrefuted
 C. Self-r

## Insights

- Apollo Research uses a five-verdict taxonomy for classifying AI safety claims: Verified, Unrefuted, Self-reported, Evaluator-found, and Blocked.
- The framework distinguishes claims by the strength and source of evidence behind them, rather than treating all claims as equally substantiated.
- A core methodological point: failing to find a counterexample to a claim is insufficient evidence of truth — evaluators must also establish confidence that their testing methods were capable of surfacing one if it existed.
- Example given for monitoring claims: evaluators can test monitoring systems by inserting realistic evasion attempts and measuring how many are correctly flagged, as a way of calibrating confidence in the monitor's detection capability.

## Quote

> Not finding a counterexample is not enough to establish a claim as true.
