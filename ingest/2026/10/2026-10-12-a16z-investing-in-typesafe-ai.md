# Investing in TypeSafe AI

## Insights

- Jev is positioned as a new class of "System One" model: built to integrate directly with code rather than be conversed with by people, returning typed decision outputs instead of text that software must parse.
- Its claimed advantage is economic and architectural, not just capability — roughly 1/100 to 1/500 the cost of frontier models and 100x faster for classification tasks at comparable accuracy.
- The framing contrasts this approach with big AI labs' trajectory of more parameters, longer context, and "human-like" reasoning, arguing instead for ubiquity through cheap, embeddable intelligence.
- Adoption metrics cited: 1 trillion tokens generated within 3 days of launch, and 25% of Fortune 500 companies reportedly integrating Jev within the same period.
- The piece explicitly frames this as pro-developer and pro-SaaS, positioning TypeSafe's philosophy against labs' rhetoric about replacing software engineers and "killing SaaS" — AI as a tool to make existing software smarter, not replace it.
- a16z announces it is leading investment in TypeSafe, citing long-standing alignment with founder Diogo's vision of giving builders "primitives of intelligence" rather than building autonomous agents.

> Make intelligence cheap enough to call anywhere, and it gets called everywhere.
