# RT @tavus: Introducing Griffin, the first model to pass the video Turing test.

## Insights

- Tavus announced "Griffin," which it claims is the first AI model to pass a video Turing test for live conversational interaction.
- In live tests, 48% of people who talked to Griffin believed they were speaking with a real human.
- This is presented as a dramatic jump from prior systems, which reportedly had pass rates below 3%.

- Tavus frames Griffin as a new category: a "Human Interaction Model" (HIM), distinct from existing video/avatar AI labels.
