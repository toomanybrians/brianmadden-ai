# Hard Reset Predicts 11 Crucial Midterm Races

## Insights

- Tech oligarchs and AI/crypto super PACs are spending heavily in the 2026 midterms specifically to elect candidates favoring a "light-touch" regulatory approach to AI, with Elon Musk alone contributing over $400 million.
- AI-industry-aligned super PACs (e.g., American Mission, Leading the Future/Think Big) explicitly campaign for candidates "dedicated to advancing AI innovation," framing opposition to AI regulation as a core electoral priority.
- A counter-current exists: some PACs (e.g., Public First Action, funded partly by $40M from Anthropic) claim to support AI regulation, and some candidates run ads attacking opponents over AI super PAC money (e.g., "AI Aaron," "Data Center Darline").
- Data center construction has become a flashpoint local issue in multiple congressional/Senate races, with candidates split between touting economic benefits and responding to constituent opposition or calling for moratoriums.
- Crypto-specific super PAC Fairshake spent $55M+ specifically to defeat a vocal crypto critic (Sherrod Brown) in the Ohio Senate race, illustrating industry-specific political spending beyond AI.
- The piece frames this election spending as a test of whether tech money can "buy" favorable regulatory outcomes at scale — a dynamic the author expects to fail this cycle despite record spending.
