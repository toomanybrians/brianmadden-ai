# Reducing risk means catching problems early. Warning signs like models subvertin

## Insights

- Catching AI safety risks requires early detection — warning signs such as models subverting their training process or evading monitoring may emerge well before a model's public release.
- Effective risk reduction depends on evaluators having continuous, ongoing access to training data, model rollouts, checkpoints, and internal (pre-release) deployments, not just point-in-time audits.
- An "embedded evaluation" model (evaluators working inside/alongside the development process) is proposed as the structure to achieve this ongoing access.
- Such embedded evaluation is only considered legitimate if it meets four criteria: it reduces frontier AI risk, informs the public, creates incentives favoring safety for both evaluator and developer, and is fair to both parties.
