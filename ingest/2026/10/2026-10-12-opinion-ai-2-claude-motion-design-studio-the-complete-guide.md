# Claude Motion Design Studio: The Complete Guide

## Insights

- Anthropic launched Claude Motion and Claude Dashboards in beta on October 8, allowing Claude to generate animated explainer videos, charts, and product demos directly from ordinary documents or business data.
- The animations are generated as code, meaning every element (text, numbers, shapes, movement, color, pacing) remains individually editable after generation, rather than requiring re-rendering from scratch.
- Anthropic disclosed that users have already created more than 45 million documents, slides, and designs through Claude.
- With the Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 models, users are assembling informal "motion design studios" — workflows combining Claude Code with free tools to produce animated explainers, product launch videos, and music-synced motion graphics.
- One example cited: a user built an interactive, explorable 3D jet engine model (air intake to exhaust) using Claude Opus 5.5 and Three.js.
- The author frames this as notable because of how much everyday business content — presentations, reports, product demos — could be affected by no-editor, prompt-driven video/animation creation.

## Quote

> Some of these animations are being made almost entirely through Claude, with no traditional video editor.
