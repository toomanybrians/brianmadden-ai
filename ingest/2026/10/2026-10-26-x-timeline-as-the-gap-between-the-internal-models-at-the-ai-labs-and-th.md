# As the gap between the internal models at the AI labs and the ones they release

## Insights

- Posits a growing gap between the internal (unreleased) models AI labs develop and the models they actually ship publicly, driven by safety concerns and a desire to preserve research/competitive advantages.
- Argues this gap creates a strategic incentive for leading labs (specifically OpenAI and Anthropic) to monetize their more advanced internal capabilities directly rather than through public releases.
- Suggests the likely path for this is vertical market entry — labs using their internal-only model advantage to compete directly in specific industries rather than just selling general-purpose model access.
- Names finance and biotech as the two sectors seen as especially attractive for this kind of direct entry, implying these are domains where a capability edge translates into outsized commercial value.

## Quote

> it will be increasingly tempting for OpenAI & Anthropic to exploit that advantage directly by entering new markets
