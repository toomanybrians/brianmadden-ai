# The Dominos are Falling

## Insights

- Microsoft offered buyouts to 60,000 employees (~27% of global workforce) as it shifts operations to AI, framed as a take-it-now-or-risk-worse-terms-later decision for employees.
- American Express laid off 3,850 workers despite rising profits, suggesting layoffs are occurring independent of financial distress.
- The Dutch government announced plans to replace 25,000 civil servants (~16% of its civil workforce) with AI — a public-sector adoption move the author notes would be equivalent to ~400,000 jobs in the U.S. context.
- Getty Images filed for bankruptcy, attributed to AI-generated imagery undercutting demand for paid photography, raising doubts about photography as a viable livelihood.
- A Goldman Sachs study found industries adopting AI see lower job growth, cited without further elaboration.
- Anthropic filed its S-1 ahead of an IPO targeting a ~$1.5 trillion valuation, with revenue at $16.3 billion for H1 (up 14x), alongside noted risks: heavy long-term compute cost commitments, decelerating growth projections, and risk-factor disclosures referencing "catastrophic or existential risks to humanity."
