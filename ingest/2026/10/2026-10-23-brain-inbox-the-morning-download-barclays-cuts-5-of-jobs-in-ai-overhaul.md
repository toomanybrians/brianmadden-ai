# The Morning Download: Barclays Cuts 5% of Jobs In AI Overhaul

## Insights

- Barclays is cutting 5,000 jobs (~5% of workforce) as part of deeper AI integration; CEO C.S. Venkatakrishnan said ~60% of the cuts hit support functions, including "fewer developers as AI makes our people more productive."
- Barclays joins a wave of banks restructuring around AI: HSBC is weighing cutting up to 20,000 roles, Standard Chartered plans to cut corporate function roles by 15%+ by 2030, and Germany's Commerzbank and Norway's DNB have also announced job cuts this year.
- Large UK banks reportedly still run on dozens of often decades-old tech systems, framing AI adoption partly as a modernization/cost-cutting response to legacy infrastructure.
- At a Gartner conference panel, Gartner's Tina Nunno argued boards and senior executives are often driven by AI hype and downplay risk, leaving CIOs as the "adult supervision" pushing back on reckless investment — a politically difficult position.
- Mastercard's CTO Ed McLaughlin described a governance practice for AI deployment: new fraud-detection models run in "silent mode" alongside existing ones, and are sent back for revision if they create too many false positives — illustrating "speed comes from precision" as a counter to move-fast pressure.
- Context note: this appears amid broader market "AI jitters," with AI-linked stocks (Nvidia, SpaceX, Intel, CoreWeave) dropping and no clear single catalyst, alongside unrelated macro pressures (oil price spikes, France's debt crisis).
