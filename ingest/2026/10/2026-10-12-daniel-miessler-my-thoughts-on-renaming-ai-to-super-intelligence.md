# My Thoughts on Renaming AI to Super Intelligence

## Insights

- Miessler expresses ambivalence about renaming "AI" to "Super Intelligence," comparing the rebrand to politically-motivated renamings (e.g., "Gulf of America") while also seeing merit in it.
- His core objection is to the word "artificial," which he views as inherently derogatory/dismissive, rather than to the renaming concept itself.
- He references his own earlier terminology choice from "The Real Internet of Things" (2016), where he used "synthetic intelligence" instead of "artificial intelligence" for the same reason.
- His argument: AI's capabilities are "real" despite non-biological origins, so labeling it "artificial" undersells its legitimacy.
- He concedes that "Super Intelligence" is strategically superior branding — more optimistic and descriptive — and credits unnamed figures ("Our Great Leader and others") with making this marketing case.
- He lands on tentatively endorsing the renaming on strategic/marketing grounds while remaining personally uncomfortable with it.

## Quote

> Artificial is just a bad name for anything real. And AI is real.
