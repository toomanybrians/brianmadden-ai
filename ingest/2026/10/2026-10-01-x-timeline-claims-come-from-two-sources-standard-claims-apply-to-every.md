# Claims come from two sources.

Standard claims apply to every developer and cove

## Insights

- Proposes "claim-based assessment" as an alternative to holistic evaluation of AI developers: instead of an overall judgment, evaluators assess specific, pre-fixed claims.
- Claims fall into two categories: standard claims (baseline properties every developer should demonstrate) and recommended claims (specific to a given developer, drawn from its own commitments, safety case, or past incidents).
- Gives an example of a specific claim type: verifying that models never attempted to disable or evade monitoring during internal deployment.
- Framework assumes claims are defined in advance of evaluation, aiming for more precise, falsifiable assessment rather than subjective overall trust ratings.

## Quote

> Evaluators assess specific claims fixed in advance, such as "Models never attempted to disable or evade their monitoring during internal deployment."
