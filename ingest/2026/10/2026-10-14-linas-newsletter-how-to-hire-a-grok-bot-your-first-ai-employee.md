# How to Hire a Grok Bot: Your First AI Employee 🤖

## Insights

- Grok Bot is xAI's (now SpaceXSI's) AI agent product that runs on a cloud computer, logs into a user's tools, and continues operating on a schedule even when the user is offline.
- Adoption reported at 418,000+ weekly users by mid-September, per company figures cited via Bloomberg.
- The underlying model has changed twice in six weeks and is now moving to Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5, which the author frames as a reason to distinguish between instructions (the bot usually follows) and controls (which hold regardless of model changes).
- The piece advocates a staged trust model for AI agents: start with a narrowly defined role, run a short probation period, and expand permissions/access only as the agent proves reliable — rather than granting broad app access and vague goals on day one.
- Agents described as capable of joining team Slack, proactively suggesting work, reading bank data, and sending email from a dedicated address — illustrating expanding scope of autonomous agent permissions.
- The full guide (not included here) reportedly covers a 5-level promotion/demotion ladder for agent trust, a scorecard and test for re-evaluating agents after model updates, and templates for defining an agent's role and limits.

## Quote

> The best way to hire a Grok Bot is the way you'd hire a person.
