# As AI accelerates scientific discovery, we’re helping researchers keep pace by q

## Insights

- Google DeepMind is releasing tools that authenticate whether biological designs (e.g., synthetic proteins) were created with built-in safeguards, aimed at researchers working with AI-accelerated scientific discovery.
- The verification approach works like a watermark — described as a "digital ID card" — embedded in AI-generated biological designs.
- Stated use case: DNA labs can use the watermark to verify synthetic proteins and keep sequence databases accurate.
- The tools are being released on an open basis for research use, explicitly framed as strengthening biosecurity rather than slowing down research.
- The stated rationale is that as requests to design biological sequences scale up, labs need ways to automate verification of AI-originated designs to maintain safeguards at scale.

## Quote

> Acting like a digital ID card, the watermark can help DNA labs verify synthetic proteins and keep sequence databases accurate.
