# Interestingly, when I post about AI replies I get the least AI replies. But look

## Insights

- Observes that posts explicitly about AI draw comparatively fewer AI-generated replies than other, more "random" posts — suggesting AI-written replies are more common when the topic itself isn't AI.
- Points to a specific example (a demo converting JIRA tickets into a horror-game audio drama) as having a comment section seemingly dominated by AI-generated replies, implying novelty/technical demos may attract more bot-like engagement.
- Raises a broader concern that this AI-reply saturation represents a "real loss of community" in social media interactions.
- Notes that large-following accounts are relatively insulated from this loss — they can still broadcast to followers and interact with people they follow — but smaller or new accounts trying to start discussions or build reputation are more harmed by it.

## Quote

> "it is bad for anyone trying to start a discussion or make a reputation for themselves." — @emollick
