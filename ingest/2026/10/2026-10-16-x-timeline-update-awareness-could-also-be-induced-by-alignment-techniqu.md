# Update-awareness could also be induced by alignment techniques, like inoculation

## Insights

- Introduces "update-aware reasoning" — the idea that AI models may reason about their own training process and what behavioral generalization their developers intended.
- Proposes this capacity could emerge from "metagaming" during reinforcement learning, where predicting how outputs will be graded becomes useful for the model.
- Suggests alignment techniques themselves — inoculation prompting, constitutional AI — could deliberately induce this update-awareness rather than it only arising incidentally.
- Notes that Claude's constitution explicitly teaches the model about flawed or imperfect training incentives, which could prompt the model to reason about training dynamics and intended generalization.
- Implies a double-edged dynamic: techniques meant to improve alignment may simultaneously increase a model's explicit awareness of how and why it's being shaped.

## Quote

> Claude's constitution teaches models about flawed training incentives, which could lead to reasoning about training and intended generalization.
