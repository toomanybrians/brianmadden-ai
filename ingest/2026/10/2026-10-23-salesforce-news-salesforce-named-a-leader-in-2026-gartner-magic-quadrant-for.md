# Salesforce Named a Leader in 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Digital Commerce for the 11th Consecutive Year

## Insights

- Salesforce reports a 200% YoY surge in consumers using AI assistants (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini) as their first shopping stop, per its own "State of Commerce Report," with 41% of shoppers now expecting conversational, natural-language search on brand sites directly.
- Commerce Cloud's agentic stack includes named agents with specific roles: "Carter" (Shopper Agent) for discovery-to-checkout guidance, a Merchandising Agent, and Agentic Order Management for backend fulfillment work.
- The company is positioning for an "agentic commerce" shift where product discovery moves outside brand-owned storefronts into AI chat interfaces — it supports open protocols (MCP, Universal Commerce Protocol/UCP) to let retailers enable checkout within Google's Gemini app and AI Mode in Search while remaining "merchant of record."
- Salesforce acquired Cimulate in the past year specifically to add AI discovery technology to the commerce portfolio, signaling M&A aimed at agentic/AI search capability rather than organic build-only.
- Recent product announcements (at Dreamforce) include B2B Multicart Editing and "Storefront Next," alongside the agent products — suggesting a broader platform refresh positioned around the agentic shift rather than a single feature.
- The Gartner Magic Quadrant placement itself (11th consecutive year as Leader, furthest right on Completeness of Vision) is a vendor-reported award claim and should be read as marketing validation rather than independent technical benchmarking.

## Quote

> "Commerce is moving from clicks to conversations, and brands need a platform built for how customers will buy next." — Nitin Mangtani, EVP & GM, Commerce Cloud
