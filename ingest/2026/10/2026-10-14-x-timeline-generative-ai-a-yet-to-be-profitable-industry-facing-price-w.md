# Generative AI: a yet-to-be-profitable industry facing price wars, increased comp

## Insights

- Generative AI is characterized as an industry still not profitable, despite scale and investment to date.
- Competitive dynamics are intensifying: price wars and increased competition among providers are cited as current pressures.
- Liability and safety concerns are flagged as rising issues for the industry, alongside declining customer confidence.
- A cited survey found that respondents expressing worry, skepticism, or caution about AI rose to nearly 100%, up over 20 points from the prior year.
- The same survey reportedly concluded that materially measurable financial impacts from AI remain very difficult to find.
- The framing explicitly invokes "the AI bubble" as an ongoing phenomenon.
