# 🤖 I predicted AGI between 2025 and 2028. I think it showed up in 2026, inside Sl

## Insights

- Author revises an earlier prediction (AGI by 2025–2028, most likely 2027) to say AGI effectively arrived in 2026 via "Claude Tag," a persistent AI layer embedded in workplace tools like Slack/Teams.
- Defines AGI operationally as "a product that can replace an average knowledge worker" — not a test of general reasoning, but of economic substitutability for roles like data/business analysis or coding.
- Describes the tool's core capability: it can ingest a worker's entire history (emails, meetings, Slack, calls, docs, code) and then offer to take over all of it — not just one task, but the full scope of a role, including scaling to a whole team.
- Argues the key UX shift is that AI is no longer a separate "app" people tab out to — it's ambient, invoked inline via @mentions inside existing workplace chat tools.
- Explicitly distinguishes average performers (easily replicable, high layoff risk) from top performers (harder to replace due to tacit, hard-to-articulate skills).
- Frames the practical consequence as layoffs or reduced rehiring after attrition, while acknowledging uneven, gradual real-world adoption despite the technical capability existing now.

## Quote

> AGI already happened; it's just not—as they say—evenly distributed.
