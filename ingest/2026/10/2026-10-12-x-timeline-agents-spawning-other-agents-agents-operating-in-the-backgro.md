# Agents spawning other agents, agents operating in the background on every task,

## Insights

- Predicts a shift from single-agent, human-paced prompting to agents spawning other agents and operating continuously in the background across tasks.
- Projects that agent swarms will consume roughly 1,000x more tokens than the current model of people prompting agents one at a time.
- Cites current data: agents already burn nearly 5x the tokens of human users, and that multiple has grown 14x over six months.
- Frames the shift as a change in who/what counts as a "user" of software — moving from humans to machines as the primary consumers of AI output.
- Implies the chat-interface paradigm (bounded by human prompting speed) is a transitional, soon-to-seem-quaint phase of AI usage.
