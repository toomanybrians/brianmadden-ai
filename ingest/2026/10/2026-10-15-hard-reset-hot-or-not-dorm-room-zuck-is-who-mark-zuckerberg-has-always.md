# “Hot or Not” Dorm Room Zuck Is Who Mark Zuckerberg Has Always Been

## Insights

- The film frames Facebook's internal "Meaningful Social Interactions" (MSI) metric as the mechanism that systematically pushed users toward extremist and fringe content, prioritized over employee concerns about harm.
- Depicted leadership response to an internal team's harm-reduction proposal: when told a safety fix would cost 1% engagement, Zuckerberg's character rejects it and has the team removed — illustrating engagement-metric optimization overriding integrity research.
- The piece links founder psychology to product design, citing Zuckerberg's own stated motivation ("I make it for myself, because it's something that I want") as evidence that some tech leaders build products primarily for personal gratification rather than public benefit or accountability.
- Raises a governance critique: the real failure isn't just one CEO's temperament but a structural pattern of unaccountable power ("boys with toys") in companies that shape public discourse at scale.
