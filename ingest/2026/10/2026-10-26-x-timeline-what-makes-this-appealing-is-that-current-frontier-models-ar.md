# What makes this appealing is that current frontier models are remarkably capable

## Insights

- Current frontier AI models are described as highly capable "out of the box" for monitoring/analysis work when connected to company data, requiring little specialized customization or fine-tuning.
- A key dynamic identified: AI expands the span of control of senior management, enabling them to directly monitor an entire firm's operations rather than relying on layered reporting structures.
- This expanded oversight capability is framed as a significant change to how company operations function.
- This approach (expanding management monitoring via AI) is positioned as easier to implement than full organizational/firm redesign, making it an appealing near-term path for enterprises adopting AI.

## Quote

> A thing I see happening at large organizations as a result of AI is the expansion of the role and span of control of senior management.
