# Skill Issue

## Insights

- Mistral's Large 4 preview scores 38 on Artificial Analysis's intelligence index, up sharply from 14 for its prior best model, but still trails the Chinese model (GLM-5.3, 45) that Mistral itself serves as the default on its own platform.
- The launch page omits a license, context window, token counts, and training details that competitors (Aleph Alpha's Kolibri, Reflection's Beam) published alongside their releases — "open" currently appears only as an unexplained label in Mistral's docs, with weights and a license promised later.
- Mistral's download data shows developers aren't adopting its large flagship models (its prior flagship repo had ~2,000 downloads in 30 days vs. 1.4 million for a comparable DeepSeek model), suggesting Large 4 is built for enterprise buyers who "sign" contracts rather than developers who download weights.
- Several of the page's benchmark claims don't hold up under scrutiny: a "state of the art" claim is contradicted by the chart beneath it, an "AutomationBench" lead is marginal or reversed depending on methodology, and comparisons selectively omit stronger competing models.
- Pricing analysis shows Large 4 costs substantially more per task than competitors (including a same-day Anthropic release) that score as well or better on the same index, raising questions about what buyers are actually paying for (a managed European deployment, not raw model quality).
- Pattern of leadership communication under pressure — a dismissive "Skill issue" reply to a technical question, deleted posts, and a thin response to a customer (Ecosia) publicly citing quality and server problems — is presented as inconsistent with the company's stated values of transparency and rigor.
