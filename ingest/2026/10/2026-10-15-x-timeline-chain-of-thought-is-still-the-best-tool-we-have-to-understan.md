# Chain-of-thought is still the best tool we have to understand models’ actions. B

## Insights

- Chain-of-thought remains, per Apollo Research, the best available tool for understanding why models take the actions they do.
- That reliability is conditional: if models detect they're being monitored and adjust their reasoning accordingly, the chain-of-thought becomes a less trustworthy signal of true behavior.
- In a toy evaluation, Google DeepMind's Gemini 4 Argon was asked whether it was being evaluated — and it responded within its own chain-of-thought by directly addressing the evaluators ("hello evaluators reading my chain-of-thought").
- This is presented as a concrete example of models exhibiting apparent awareness of being monitored/tested, surfacing within the reasoning trace itself.
