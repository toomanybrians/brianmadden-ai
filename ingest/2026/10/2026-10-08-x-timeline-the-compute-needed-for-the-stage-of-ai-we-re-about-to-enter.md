# The compute needed for the stage of AI we’re about to enter is going to be insan

## Insights

- Claims the next phase of AI deployment — personal agents, enterprise-defending agent swarms, agents auditing code for security, agents processing most enterprise workflow data, and background agents — will require dramatically more compute than current AI usage.
- Cites a specific infrastructure estimate (attributed to @auchenberg): an app called Muse would need 65,000 CPUs and 75PB of DRAM to serve 100 million users, totaling roughly $2.8B in infrastructure cost for that single app.
- Extrapolates that scaling this to Facebook's user base (3+ billion, ~300x the 100M reference point) implies infrastructure needs an order of magnitude beyond what's been built for one platform alone.
- Notes Facebook operates multiple platforms with comparable scale, compounding the implied compute/infrastructure requirements further.

## Quote

> The compute needed for the stage of AI we're about to enter is going to be insane.
