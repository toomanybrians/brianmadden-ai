# Anthropic ARR is flatlining post tokenmaxxing (as many of us predicted) just as

## Insights

- Claims Anthropic's annual recurring revenue (ARR) growth has flatlined following a period of rapid token-usage growth ("tokenmaxxing"), a trend the author says he and others predicted.
- Notes the timing coincides with Anthropic's planned IPO, suggesting a tension between slowing growth and the valuation being sought at IPO.
- Cites third-party data (via @Silicon_Data/@tickerplus) showing Anthropic's ARR flattened around the same time a "token index" tracking frontier labs peaked.
- The data source itself flags a caveat: the token index has incomplete coverage of frontier labs, limiting how much can be inferred from it.
- Frames this as a reason for prospective investors to scrutinize the last three months of performance against the prior six months before evaluating a reported $2T market cap.
