# Anthropic’s Numbers May Never Look This Good Again

## Insights

- Anthropic filed to go public despite a market turning against AI — seven data center companies have pulled, postponed, or cut prices on their IPOs since September, and CoreWeave/Nebius have lost ~30% of value in a month.
- The article's core thesis: Anthropic is filing now because its fundamentals may never look this strong again — revenue grew 3x faster than costs in H1, and it posted its first operating profit in Q2.
- Anthropic holds $112.4B in cash against $6.2B in debt, but revenue growth is slowing and the big three cloud platforms (nearly half its revenue) are raising fees that will erode margins.
- Anthropic has signed close to $1 trillion in compute deals that haven't yet hit its balance sheet or income statement — a major looming liability not fully reflected in the S-1.
- Frames S-1 filings as marketing documents as much as legal disclosures, arguing Anthropic's flattering self-portrait is already becoming outdated given how fast the company's financial position is shifting.

## Quote

> Anthropic is going public now because its business may never look this good again.
