# Karpathy argues that enjoying educational clips can fool us into thinking we und

## Insights

- Karpathy distinguishes "shortification of learning": short educational videos on YouTube/TikTok often look like education but function as entertainment.
- The appeal is mutual — viewers enjoy feeling educated, and creators benefit from content that satisfies that feeling without requiring real effort.
- Genuine learning, by contrast, requires sustained effort and active processing, not passive consumption of enjoyable clips.
- Enjoying a piece of educational content is not a reliable signal that understanding has actually occurred.

## Quote

> There are a lot of videos on YouTube/TikTok etc. that give the appearance of education, but... they are really just entertainment.
