# a16z's State of Markets 2026: The AI Playbook for Founders and Investors 💸

## Insights

- a16z's "State of Markets II" deck (100+ charts, 90 pages) argues the AI boom is real and earnings-backed rather than hype-driven, framing it as still early-stage despite scale of investment.
- Hyperscaler capex is projected at roughly $780B in 2026, nearly double the prior year, reflecting an aggressive race to build AI infrastructure capacity.
- A stark adoption gap exists: 69% of S&P 500 companies report running AI in production, but only 2% actually track an AI performance metric over time — suggesting most deployments lack measurement or accountability.
- The piece frames this investment-vs-adoption gap as the central battleground for value creation over the next decade, determining which companies become category leaders versus mere feature additions.
- Leading AI companies are reportedly cutting operational costs 35–90% through techniques like model routing, caching, and fine-tuning — pointing to maturing agent economics.
- The deck reportedly covers capex shifting "from bits to atoms" (supply-chain bottlenecks with pricing power), compute pricing trends, SaaS repricing under AI, and diverging agentic-commerce strategies (e.g., Amazon vs. Instacart).

## Quote

> The AI boom is real, it's earnings-backed, and still super early.
