# Thinking in terms of dashboards is not big enough, AIs don’t just passively gath

## Insights

- Argues that framing AI's role in business monitoring as "dashboards" undersells its capability — AI systems don't just passively surface KPIs.
- Claims AI can actively conduct research, identify loose ends, and surface what matters — not just at a high summary level but in granular detail.
- Suggests the frontier is moving toward AI taking action, not just reporting or analyzing information.
- Notes that current frontier models already perform this kind of proactive analysis well out of the box when given access to company data, without requiring significant custom engineering.
