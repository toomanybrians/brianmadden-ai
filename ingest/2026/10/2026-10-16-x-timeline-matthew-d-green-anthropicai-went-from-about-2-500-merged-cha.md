# @matthew_d_green 🌿 @AnthropicAI went from about 2,500 merged changes a day in Ma

## Insights

- Anthropic's rate of merged code changes grew roughly 12x in four months, from about 2,500 per day in May to over 30,000 per day in September.
- This growth outpaced existing developer infrastructure (git), which the team says was built for human-scale development rather than agent-scale development.
- Anthropic responded by building an in-house source control system and migrated their entire monorepo off git, completing the transition in about five months.
- The scale of change is attributed implicitly to AI agents generating or contributing code, pushing merge volume far beyond typical human-driven engineering throughput.
