# Robot once meant forced labor🎯

## Insights

- Mistral previewed Mistral Large 4, a 1-trillion-parameter open-weight model trained on 4,000 Nvidia GPUs over two months, which the company claims is the strongest open model built outside China but trailing top closed systems in coding (unverified by outside tests).
- Meta, Walmart, Stripe, and Sierra are developing an open standard to let businesses distinguish authorized AI agents from unauthorized bots; OpenAI and Anthropic have not yet joined, and Amazon already blocks Meta's agent.
- Google will restrict free Gemini users to its smallest model (Flash-Lite) starting October 9, pushing paid tiers ($4.99 and $19.99/month) as the way to access larger models like Pro and Deep Think.
- Anthropic is investing $100 million in a "Claude Frontier Academy" to train 10,000 enterprise AI engineers, and separately offers startups a free year of Claude Team plus $1,000 in API credits.
- OpenAI is reportedly in talks to raise at least $30 billion at a $1.4 trillion pre-money valuation (per Bloomberg).
- Infrastructure buildout continues: Google signed a 3,590 megawatt power deal with Constellation, including 890 megawatts from nuclear, signaling the scale of energy demand behind AI compute.

## Quote

> Robot once meant forced labor.
