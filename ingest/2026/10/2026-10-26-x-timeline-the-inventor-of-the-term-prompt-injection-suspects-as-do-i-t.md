# The inventor of the term "prompt injection" suspects (as do I) that this may not

## Insights

- The person credited with coining "prompt injection" now suspects it may be less of a problem in the newest, strongest AI models than previously assumed.
- Anthropic is described as confident in its models' ability to resist prompt injection attacks.
- The concern is characterized as still real, but more specifically tied to cheaper, weaker models rather than top-tier ones.
- Independent testing (by the poster) found that a specific prompt-injection attack — attempting to get an AI grader to award higher grades on a paper — no longer works against current models.
