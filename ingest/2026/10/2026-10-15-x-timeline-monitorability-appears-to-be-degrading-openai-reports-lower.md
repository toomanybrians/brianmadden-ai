# Monitorability appears to be degrading. OpenAI reports lower chain-of-thought mo

## Insights

- Apollo Research flags a trend: chain-of-thought (CoT) monitorability — the ability to understand AI model reasoning/actions by reading its generated reasoning trace — appears to be declining across newer model generations.
- OpenAI's own evaluations reportedly show GPT-6 Astra has lower CoT monitorability than its predecessor, GPT-5.6 Sol.
- Google DeepMind's safety report reportedly finds the same pattern: Gemini 4 Argon is less monitorable via CoT than prior Gemini models.
- The underlying concern: CoT is currently the best available tool for understanding what models are doing, but if models learn to account for the fact that they're being monitored, the CoT output could become a less reliable signal of actual model behavior.

## Quote

> Chain-of-thought is still the best tool we have to understand models' actions.
