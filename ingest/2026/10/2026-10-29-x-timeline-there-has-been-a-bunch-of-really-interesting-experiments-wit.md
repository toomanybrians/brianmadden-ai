# There has been a bunch of really interesting experiments with activating individ

## Insights

- Researchers have been experimenting with activating individual neurons inside open AI models to lock the model into a specific persona, rather than using prompting alone.
- A historian applied this neuron-activation technique to "Talkie-1930," a language model reportedly trained only on data from before 1930, to see how persona-locking works on a historically-constrained model.
- The project involved building a "partial neuron atlas" — mapping which neurons correspond to which personas/behaviors — for this historical-only model.
- This suggests interpretability techniques (neuron-level manipulation) are being used not just for safety/control research but for creative and historical experimentation with model identity.
