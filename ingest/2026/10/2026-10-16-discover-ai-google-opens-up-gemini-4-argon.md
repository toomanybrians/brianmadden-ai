# Google opens up Gemini 4 Argon

## Insights

- Google opened Gemini 4 Argon to AI Pro/Ultra subscribers and the paid API, pricing it at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with a max output of up to 1 million tokens (up from 64,000 in earlier Gemini models).
- Google's own benchmarks claim Argon scores 77.9% on DeepSWE (a long-coding-task test) and leads the Vals Index across finance, coding, legal, and tax domains.
- Anthropic reported Q3 revenue of $16.8 billion (up from $11.6B in Q2 and $4.7B in Q1), with sales more than tripling over the year and an adjusted profit, ahead of a planned IPO.
- OpenAI will retire GPT-4o, GPT-4o mini, GPT-4.1, and GPT-4.1 mini (including fine-tuned versions) from its API on January 14, 2027, recommending gpt-6-luna as the replacement.
- Security firm XBOW used human researchers plus AI agents (via GPT-5.6 Sol) to build a Chrome exploit capable of taking over a Windows PC from a single webpage, earning a $250,000 Google bounty after ~6 days and $40,000 in compute costs.
- More than 400 scientists, including Geoffrey Hinton and Yoshua Bengio, signed an open letter calling for a global body to track AI containment failures.
