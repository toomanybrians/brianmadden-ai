# Salesforce Signs Definitive Agreement to Acquire Braze

## Insights

- Salesforce will acquire Braze, a customer engagement platform, for ~$5.66B enterprise value ($47.50/share cash), expected to close in Salesforce's FY2028.
- Braze's technical core is an event-streaming architecture that processes real-time customer behavior signals into personalized messages/journeys across mobile, web, email, SMS, and WhatsApp, at high volume (described as "trillions of messages and journey actions" annually).
- BrazeAI adds AI-driven optimization of content, offers, channel selection, and timing — positioned as "decisioning" layered on top of the engagement infrastructure.
- Salesforce frames the deal as strengthening all four layers of its stated architecture (Data 360, Customer 360, Agentforce, SIForce), specifically boosting Marketing Cloud's cross-channel personalization at "hundreds of millions" of customer scale.
- Strategic rationale is framed explicitly around agentic AI: marketers "now need agentic solutions that can understand customers, make decisions, and act in real time" — positioning this as a capability acquisition for real-time decisioning, not just a feature bolt-on.
- Braze will continue operating somewhat independently post-close ("Braze and investing in the Braze community"), serving ~2,770 existing customers (Canva, Instacart, Venmo, etc.); no change to Salesforce's FY27 guidance, no FY28 guidance given yet.

## Quote

> "AI is raising the bar for marketers, who now need agentic solutions that can understand customers, make decisions, and act in real time."
