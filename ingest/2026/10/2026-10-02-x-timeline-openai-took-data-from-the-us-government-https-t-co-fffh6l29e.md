# OpenAI took data from the US government https://t.co/Fffh6l29eL

## Insights

- Financial Times reporting (per FT's own post) found that OpenAI's models drew on data scraped from 55 websites belonging to businesses, non-profits, and government agencies.
- Named government/institutional sources include the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), the US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), and the International Energy Agency (IEA).
- Gary Marcus frames this as OpenAI having "taken data from the US government," amplifying the FT finding as evidence of improper or unauthorized data sourcing.
