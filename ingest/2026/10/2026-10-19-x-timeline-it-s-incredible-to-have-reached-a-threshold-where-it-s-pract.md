# It’s incredible to have reached a threshold where it’s practical that AI will be

## Insights

- Anthropic's Head of Global Affairs, Michael Sellitto, reportedly told the IMF that Anthropic projects AI could be responsible for 80-90% of frontier model training within a year.
- This would be a dramatic jump from an estimated 3% of frontier model training being AI-led as of April.
- The claim is framed as evidence of recursive self-improvement becoming practically visible rather than a distant hypothetical.
- The poster (@levie) frames this as a rapid, surprising threshold shift — from "recursive self-improvement feels far off" to seeing early signs of it in a short span of time.
