# A bonus class on the two executive orders and the AI accord

## Insights

- President Trump signed an executive order ("Inaugurating The Era of Super Intelligence," Sept 29) directing the executive branch to replace the terms "Artificial Intelligence"/"AI" with "Super Intelligence"/"SI" in federal usage, including in existing statutory language (citing 15 U.S.C. 9401(3)).
- Governor Newsom responded the next day (Sept 30) with California Executive Order N-10-26, directing state agencies in an apparent counter-move to Trump's order.
- Neither executive order creates enforceable legal rights — they are framed as largely symbolic/administrative rather than substantive policy changes.
- Separately, the President and six leading AI companies signed a one-page "White House Accord on Super Intelligence," signaling coordinated terminology/framing between the administration and frontier AI labs.
- The piece frames this as a dispute over naming and definitional control of "AI" itself, invoking the Humpty Dumpty "words mean what I choose them to mean" framing.

## Quote

> It is therefore the policy of my Administration... the executive branch shall use the terms "Super Intelligence" and "SI" in place of "Artificial Intelligence" and "AI"
