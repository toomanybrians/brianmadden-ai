## Insights

- The claim under dispute: Anthropic's Sholto Douglas projected AI capex rising from $1 trillion this year to $4 trillion in 2028, with AI beginning to double humanity's GDP by the early 2030s.
- Douglas's reasoning rests partly on historical compute scaling trends — AI compute capacity has been doubling or tripling annually over the past four to five years.
- Gary Marcus rejects the GDP-doubling prediction outright, calling it a claim that "never comes true."
- Marcus's critique is also aimed at media/platform incentives — he questions why such forecasts get amplified and taken seriously despite a track record of not materializing.
