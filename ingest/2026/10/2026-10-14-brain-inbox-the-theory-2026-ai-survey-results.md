# The Theory 2026 AI Survey Results

## Insights

- Security and governance jumped from the sixth-ranked pain point in 2025 to first in 2026 (43% rate it top concern), while only 10% say security tooling currently delivers the most value — the largest gap between perceived importance and current satisfaction of any category surveyed.
- Context limitations, the dominant complaint in 2025, appear largely resolved in 2026 per the survey framing.
- Model routing has become mainstream: 45% of teams now route queries across multiple models (split roughly evenly between self-trained and proprietary routers), while just over half still send every query to one model.
- Among the 259 teams whose agents write and execute code, Cloudflare hosts 62% of sandboxes — three times its nearest competitor (Vercel and "Other" tied at 17%) — suggesting sandbox infrastructure is consolidating around one provider positioned as compute rather than security.
- Agent performance analysis is being done through a mix of tools rather than dedicated platforms: BI tools (56%), coding agents themselves (48%), observability tools (41%), and spreadsheets (30%) all see meaningful use, pointing to category convergence rather than a clear winner.
- Evals remain the second-highest-rated pain point (22%) behind security, and are described as still in early adoption despite their prominence as a concern.

## Quote

> Security rose from sixth to first among pain points in twelve months.
