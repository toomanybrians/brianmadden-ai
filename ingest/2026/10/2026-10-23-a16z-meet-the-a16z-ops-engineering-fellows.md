# Meet the a16z Ops Engineering Fellows

## Insights

- a16z ran an 8-week "Ops Engineering Fellowship" cohort of 50 leaders from companies including Ramp, Decagon, Luma AI, Opendoor, and ElevenLabs, selected from thousands of applicants.
- The cohort clusters into three recognizable AI-adoption roles emerging inside companies: (1) hybrid ops/engineering people building internal tooling, consolidating tech stacks, and designing "context layers" that AI agents draw on; (2) COOs and chiefs of staff who decide which workflows to automate first and define KPIs to prove automation is working; (3) product builders shipping new AI-native software and teams.
- A recurring pattern described is deploying agentic workflows with human checkpoints rather than full automation, suggesting a human-in-the-loop design norm for operational AI rollouts.
- The framing treats "ops engineering" as a distinct, named discipline — operations work increasingly requires engineering-level tooling and data/context infrastructure to support AI agents.
- The existence of a dedicated fellowship signals a16z sees enough demand/maturity in this operator-plus-AI hybrid role to invest in formalizing and networking a cohort around it.
