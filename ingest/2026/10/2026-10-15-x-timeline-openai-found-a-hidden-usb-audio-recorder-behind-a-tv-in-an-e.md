# OpenAI found a hidden USB audio recorder behind a TV in an exec conference room

## Insights

- OpenAI discovered a hidden USB audio recorder (disguised in toilet paper, containing encrypted data) plugged in behind a TV in an executive conference room used for confidential research, product, business, and government-related discussions.
- OpenAI stated it used its own AI models to analyze the device and trace it back to the employee who planted it, then referred the case to the FBI — an example of an AI company using its own tools for internal security investigation.
- The accused engineer allegedly used a productivity/chatbot tool (and a related account tied to his spouse) to research building layouts, meeting calendars, leadership locations, and to get technical help with covert recording, concealment, remote access, and encryption.
- A second, similar recording device was found in the employee's own bag alongside his wallet and ID, which investigators cite as linking evidence.
- The case documents alleged physical eavesdropping, not a confirmed network breach or proof that recordings reached an external party — prosecutors have not identified a foreign recipient.
- Charges carry up to five years in prison and a $250,000 fine; the employee had joined the company about a year before the alleged incident.

## Quote

> We discovered a sophisticated recording device that was hidden inside one of our offices. We used our models to understand the device and where it had come from.
