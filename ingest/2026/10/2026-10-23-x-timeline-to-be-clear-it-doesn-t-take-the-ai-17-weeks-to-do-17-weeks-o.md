# To be clear, it doesn’t take the AI 17 weeks to do 17 weeks of human equivalent

## Insights

- A widely cited "AI can now do 17 weeks of human-equivalent work" framing is misleading: it doesn't take the AI 17 weeks of wall-clock time to complete that work — the comparison is about task scope/duration a human would need, not AI's actual time cost.
- The AI's success rate on this benchmark is higher than 50%, correcting an implied lower figure from the original framing.
- The study being referenced found the AI could replicate ~17 weeks of human engineering work for roughly $241 in token costs.
- The author tracks a personal benchmark across AI book editions: AI capability moved from ~4 minutes of human-equivalent work (50% success) in 2024, to 17 hours by early/mid this year, to 17 weeks by publication — illustrating a fast-compounding capability trajectory.

## Quote

> To be clear, it doesn't take the AI 17 weeks to do 17 weeks of human equivalent work.
