# SynthID has been named one of @TIME's Best Inventions of 2026.

With over 180 bi

## Insights

- Google's SynthID watermarking tool was named one of TIME's Best Inventions of 2026, and has now watermarked over 180 billion AI-generated images and videos.
- SynthID embeds imperceptible digital watermarks (described as small pixel perturbations) into AI-generated images and video that survive cropping, compression, or cutting.
- Adoption extends beyond Google: SynthID is used by OpenAI, Nvidia, and Kakao, with Apple adoption reportedly coming soon — suggesting a cross-industry watermarking standard forming around AI content provenance.
- Google reportedly decided years ago against building AI-detection tools that try to spot synthetic content after the fact, betting instead on embedded watermarking, because detection models would eventually be unable to distinguish real from synthetic media.
- In October 2026, Google launched a public website (synthid.com) letting anyone upload content to check for a SynthID watermark.
