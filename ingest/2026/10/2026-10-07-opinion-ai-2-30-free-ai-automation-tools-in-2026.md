# 30 Free AI Automation Tools in 2026

## Insights

- Frames a shift in AI usage from a single-turn "ask → answer → you do the rest" pattern to persistent automation loops: "something happens → AI thinks → something gets done," with systems retaining state across days.
- Cites specific models positioned as enabling this shift: GPT-6 Astra (computer/browser control built into the model), Claude Opus 5.5 (harder judgment-based tasks), Kimi Work (local files, browser tasks, long-running jobs), and small local "decision models" inspired by something called Jev, run via Ollama for fast local choices.
- Argues that meaningful automation no longer requires paid platforms like Zapier or Make — free tools can now replicate much of that functionality.
- Describes automation building blocks as covering research, file handling, browser work, email triage, agents, memory, and API/repetitive tasks.
- Suggests practical adoption doesn't require accumulating many tools — most people only need a small stack (e.g., three tools) rather than all 30 listed.
- Positions "building your own automation system" as a distinct, learnable AI skill separate from simply prompting models.

## Quote

> That is AI automation in its simplest form: Something happens → AI thinks → something gets done.
