# Our concrete proposal is claim-based assessment. Instead of an overall judgment

## Insights

- Proposes "claim-based assessment" as an alternative to holistic evaluator judgments of AI developers: evaluators check specific, pre-registered claims (e.g., "models never attempted to disable or evade monitoring during internal deployment") rather than issuing an overall verdict.
- Argues good incentives and fairness in these evaluations require three conditions set in advance: agreed scope of assessment, evaluator independence, and protection of confidential IP for both the evaluator and the developer being assessed.
- States that voluntary commitments to this kind of evaluation are unlikely to be sufficient, and advocates that embedded evaluations should eventually become a legal requirement rather than optional practice.

## Quote

> Models never attempted to disable or evade their monitoring during internal deployment.
