# SynthID Detector can quickly identify our watermarks in video, audio and images

## Insights

- Google DeepMind's SynthID Detector, a tool to identify AI-generated watermarks, is now publicly available to everyone rather than limited access.
- The detector works across multiple media types — video, audio, and images — not just a single format.
- Watermark detection reportedly survives post-generation edits, such as filters applied to the content.
- The tool checks for watermarks from content generated by Google's own AI as well as industry partners, including OpenAI, NVIDIA, and Kakao, with Apple support coming soon.
- AI-identification capability is also being built directly into existing consumer surfaces: Google Chrome, Google Search, and the Gemini app.

## Quote

> SynthID Detector can quickly identify our watermarks in video, audio and images – even after edits, like adding filters.
