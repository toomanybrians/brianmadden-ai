## Insights

- Chamath claims AGI has effectively been achieved in a lab sense but "cannot diffuse" into the broader economy — not a technology limitation, but a physical build-out limitation (datacenters, power, cooling) that has to be completed before a plurality of people can feel it.
- A speaker notes Anthropic's recent S-1 reportedly lists, as a risk factor, that hyperscalers will not guarantee it compute — called "a really good indicator" that compute scarcity is real even for the most heavily funded labs.
- Dave Blundin claims roughly 15 gigawatts of purchased AI chips — including older, non-Vera-Rubin-generation hardware — are sitting idle in inventory, waiting for liquid-cooled datacenter capacity to come online.
- Chamath cites Microsoft reportedly triggering a liquidated-damages clause and exiting a datacenter deal with N-Scale after two separate coolant-leak incidents, as evidence that liquid cooling is harder to execute at scale than assumed.
- Chamath argues the binding constraint on AI diffusion over the next five years is as much political/regional as physical: regional power co-ops controlled by established utilities (he names Oklahoma Gas & Electric as an example) can block new entrants from securing power offtake agreements even where buildable capacity and permitting exist.
- Citing an "AI data sovereignty" argument attributed to Palantir's Alex Karp, Chamath claims non-hyperscaler enterprises (his example: an insurer like Humana) will be forced into private VPCs or fully air-gapped on-prem facilities rather than wait in line for shared cloud AI capacity, driving a wave of terrestrial datacenter buildout.
- Chamath frames this as an open business opportunity: executives at any company should be pushing to "unlock a billion dollars" to prepay for future power/compute capacity now, warning that many firms have already been "frozen out" of compute by quant trading firms and frontier labs that scooped up available supply.
- A speaker states roughly 100GW of total compute capacity is targeted globally for AI by 2030, with Chamath's own ~6GW power position representing a meaningful share of that target, assembled in about two years.
