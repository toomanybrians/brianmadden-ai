# Google NotebookLM: The AI Tool for Business Growth

## Insights

- NotebookLM (now rebranded as Gemini Notebook) lets users aggregate disparate source materials — videos, articles, documentation, GitHub projects — into one workspace and query across them with cited, traceable answers.
- Google reported 30 million users and over 600,000 organizations using the tool as of July 2026, indicating significant enterprise and individual adoption.
- The rebrand added expanded capabilities: more powerful underlying AI, web search, deeper research functions, presentation generation, and code writing/execution on supported plans.
- The core value proposition highlighted is synthesis across multiple sources simultaneously — e.g., asking what's buildable from a tool's docs, tutorials, and examples combined, rather than consulting each separately.
- The author frames this as a business-building tool: turning scattered personal knowledge into applications, paid workshops, services, or improved professional output.
- Positioned as accessible — free to start, with a "surprisingly simple" setup, lowering the barrier to using AI-assisted research synthesis.

## Quote

> Notebook reads across those materials and shows you the evidence behind its answer.
