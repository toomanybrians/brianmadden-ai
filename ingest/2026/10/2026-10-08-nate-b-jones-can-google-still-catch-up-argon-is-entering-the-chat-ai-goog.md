# Can Google still catch up? Argon is entering the chat #AI #Google #Argon #AInews #tech

## Insights

- Google's upcoming model "Argon" reportedly derives its name from the Greek word for "idle," which the piece frames as ironic given speculation about Google's AI trajectory.
- Google is said to have gone months without releasing a frontier model, leading to speculation it has deprioritized frontier AI in favor of lightweight consumer- and Search-oriented models.
- Internal leaks reportedly suggest some Google engineers are unenthusiastic about Argon.
- Early third-party benchmark speculation places Argon's capability near ChatGPT's "Sol 5.6" tier, not at the more advanced 6 or 6.1 level.
- Other labs continue shipping — a "Fable 5.5" is in preview and another ChatGPT model is reportedly coming — framed as evidence that frontier model scaling and competition show no signs of slowing.
- The piece raises a secondary question distinct from the competitive race: whether current users/organizations are actually utilizing the AI capability already available to them.
