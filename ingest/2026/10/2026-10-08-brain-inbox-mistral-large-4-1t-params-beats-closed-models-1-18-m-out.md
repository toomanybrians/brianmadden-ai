# 🧠 Mistral Large 4 1T params beats closed models, $1.18/M out

- Mistral claims this is the strongest open-weights model from the US or Europe on aggregated benchmarks, positioned as beating closed frontier models; it's built entirely in Europe on Mistral's own GPU cluster.
- Pricing undercuts typical frontier-model API costs ($1.36/M input, $4.18/M output tokens via Mistral Studio), with a 1M-token context window, native image understanding, 160+ language support, and willingness to run cybersecurity tasks closed models often refuse.
