# Academics have to invest tremendous effort into a single paper, so we often work

## Insights

- Academic research is shaped by resource constraints: because each paper demands enormous effort, researchers gravitate toward topics they predict will yield interesting or significant results, which biases the field toward "predictable work" rather than open-ended exploration.
- AI removes that constraint by lowering the cost of individual research efforts, which the author argues should force a broader rethinking of how scientific work is selected and conducted.
- Cited as a concrete example: a new working paper reportedly shows AI can forecast treatment effects in economics studies directly from pre-registration documents (before results are known).

## Quote

> AI has no such constraints, forcing us to rethink a lot of how science is done.
