# Cyber will be one of the most defining domains for AI in the coming years, and a

## Insights

- Cyber security is framed as one of the most consequential domains for AI application over the coming years, with heavy enterprise focus expected.
- AI is creating new categories of security work: issues from "vibe coded" software, agentic attacks, and accidental harms caused by autonomous agents.
- Data cited (via quoted a16z post) across 21 major software companies (Apple, AWS, Microsoft, Google, etc.) shows reported critical vulnerabilities stayed under 100/month for four years.
- Since spring, that figure has jumped to over 600/month — a roughly 6x increase, implicitly linked to AI-driven coding and agent proliferation.
