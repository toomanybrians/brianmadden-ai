# This is a good way to understand how early we actually are in AI diffusion right

## Insights

- The large majority of current AI users engage with it as a simple chatbot, using it as a next-generation search substitute rather than tapping into its broader capabilities.
- This framing is presented as evidence that AI diffusion is still at a very early stage despite widespread chatbot usage.
- A referenced data point claims roughly 6 billion people (~75% of the population) have only minimally engaged with AI tools so far.
- The gap in understanding between people who grasp full LLM capability and those using it only for basic interactions is described as widening over time, not narrowing.
- This shift is characterized as moving from "two groups talking past each other" to a more structured, sharp divide ("funnel") in awareness/capability.

## Quote

> "The vast majority of people are using AI as a chatbot for simple interactions that approximate next-generation search." — @levie
