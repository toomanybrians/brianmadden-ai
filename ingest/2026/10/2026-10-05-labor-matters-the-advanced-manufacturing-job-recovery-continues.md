# The Advanced Manufacturing Job Recovery Continues

## Insights

- Manufacturing's overall job numbers (9,000 added in September, 40,000 over the past year) mask a sharp bifurcation between two segments of the sector.
- "Advanced manufacturing" — chemicals, machinery, computers/electronics, and transportation equipment, ~38% of manufacturing employment — added 56,000 jobs over the past year and is up 66,000 since bottoming out at the end of 2025.
- The remaining ~62% of manufacturing lost 16,000 jobs over the same period and has been declining since early 2023, now barely above 2016 employment levels.
- The author attributes advanced manufacturing's growth specifically to industries supplying equipment for the AI and energy buildout, plus reshoring-driven industries.
- The piece frames this as a narrow, not broad-based, manufacturing recovery, with no expectation that the non-advanced segment will start hiring again soon.

## Quote

> It is happening in the industries that make the equipment for the AI and energy buildout and in the ones most affected by reshoring.
