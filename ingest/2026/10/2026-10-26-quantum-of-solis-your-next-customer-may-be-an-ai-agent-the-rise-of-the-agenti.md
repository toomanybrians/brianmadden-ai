# Your Next Customer May Be an AI Agent: The Rise of the Agentic Web

## Insights

- AI agents are shifting from answering queries to actively navigating the web, comparing vendors, verifying claims, coordinating with other agents, and completing transactions — described as a "machine-native market."
- Cited data (HUMAN Security 2026 report): AI-driven traffic grew 187% in 2025, while traffic from AI agents/agentic browsers grew 7,851% year over year, signaling rapid early-stage adoption.
- Interoperability standards are emerging to let agents discover, coordinate, and transact: MCP (Anthropic), A2A (Google), and commerce protocols like ACP (Stripe/OpenAI), plus Google's agent-payment authorization work.
- The traditional marketing "funnel" is being replaced by a "decision graph" — a network of parallel/sequential agent-driven queries, verifications, and handoffs that only escalate to humans at judgment thresholds.
- Trust must become "machine-readable": structured, verifiable evidence (pricing, certifications, policies, proof points) rather than the emotional/experiential trust signals built for humans; introduces concept of "Agent Experience" (AX) and "Agent Experience Debt" as a new organizational liability.
- Despite automation, cites conflicting Gartner stats (67% of B2B buyers prefer rep-free experiences vs. 69% turn to reps to validate AI insights) to argue human roles will concentrate on judgment, empathy, and ambiguous/emotional moments rather than disappear.
- Proposes a "Market OS" framework for leaders spanning search/discovery, context, protocols, transactions, and trust as the new organizing structure for go-to-market work across marketing, product, IT, security, legal, and service teams.
