# RT @ashleyrgold: Scoop: How Zuckerberg shaped Trump’s AI industry pledge
https:/

## Insights

- The White House "Accord on Super Intelligence," signed by Meta, OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Google, and Nvidia, originated from a conversation between Mark Zuckerberg and House Speaker Mike Johnson at a state dinner, not from White House policy staff.
- Zuckerberg drafted the principles himself and enlisted Nvidia's Jensen Huang to build support among other AI executives before the document was presented to Trump.
- The agreement is brief (~300 words) and commits signatories to "robust internal controls" and outside audits for frontier model safety, but offers no specifics on implementation.
- The accord references a voluntary government clearance framework (from a prior Trump AI order) that has never been publicly released, raising transparency questions.
- The piece frames this as evidence of Zuckerberg's rehabilitated political standing in Washington after years of "techlash," positioning Meta as cooperative with government to preempt heavier regulation.
- Note (tangential): Meta is separately pushing into enterprise AI with a new agent product called Muse.
