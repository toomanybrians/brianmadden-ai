# Unsupervised Learning NO. 548

## Insights

- Barclays is cutting ~5,000 jobs (5% of global workforce) as part of an AI-driven restructuring, with 60% of the cuts hitting support functions including developers; CEO frames AI as supplemental rather than core to the decision.
- Banks are pulling back on AI spending after costs ran over forecast, shifting workloads to smaller and Chinese models — Santander reportedly cut per-loan-approval AI cost from ~5 euros to ~0.03 euro.
- Anthropic's two most visible Claude Code community/developer-relations builders (Thariq, Andrew Morris) left for SpaceX, reportedly to work on xAI's "GrokBot" — described as a sign of GrokBot's fast rise as a personal/admin assistant (calendar, email) after only a few weeks of serious use.
- Anthropic shipped "Claude Duet," a proactive cloud-based agent that works autonomously while the user is away, delivers morning briefings, accepts tasks via message, and asks permission before sending messages or making payments — positioned as a direct competitor to OpenAI's and xAI's similar agent products.
- A tracked claim (via Steve Krouse) holds that AI cost-per-capability drops ~10x per year, roughly seven times faster than Moore's Law, reinforcing that capability-per-dollar economics are shifting rapidly.
- Analysis from Bernstein Research suggests only ~35% of announced US AI data center capacity will actually get built, with many "bragawatt" projects from undercapitalized newcomers lacking power, permits, or customers — a caution against overbuilding/overcommitting to the AI infrastructure boom.
