# If this is correct, Anthropic is not nearly as close to profitable as many folks

## Insights

- Gary Marcus argues that if reported figures are accurate, Anthropic is far less close to profitability than widely assumed.
- He frames Anthropic's financial position as dependent on a large subsidy from Amazon, which he characterizes as likely not sustainable long-term.
- Cited data (from an investor presentation, via @rdd147): Anthropic spent $7.2 billion for 1.4 GW of compute in 2025, roughly $5.14 billion per GW.
- Anthropic reportedly told investors it is tracking toward 5 GW of compute in 2026, with associated spending around $20 billion.

## Quote

> If this is correct, Anthropic is not nearly as close to profitable as many folks seem to believe.
