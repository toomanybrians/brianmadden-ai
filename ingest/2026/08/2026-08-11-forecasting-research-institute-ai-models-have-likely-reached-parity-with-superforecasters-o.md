# AI models have likely reached parity with superforecasters on ForecastBench

## Insights

- On ForecastBench, several AI systems (Cassi AI, xAI, Google DeepMind) are now statistically indistinguishable from human superforecasters on tournament-level accuracy, though confidence intervals still overlap substantially with human performance.
- For the first time, an AI system (Cassi AI) outranked the superforecaster median on market-based questions — judgment calls about novel, one-off events — not just data-lookup-style questions where AI had an inherent edge.
- A preliminary leaderboard shows 17 submissions now ranking above superforecasters on dataset questions (derived from time-series sources like FRED, ACLED, Yahoo Finance), up sharply from essentially one leading system a few months prior.
- Leading approaches (Cassi, Torchcast) rely on multi-agent/multi-stage pipelines: generating sub-questions, retrieving current context, filtering for relevance, ensembling model outputs, and using a separate LLM to synthesize a final probability.
- FRI frames this as parity rather than clear outperformance, cautioning that their human superforecaster baseline is from 2024 and growing stale, and that results remain statistically noisy as more questions resolve.
- The trajectory — from parity to leading on harder judgment-based questions — suggests forecasting/probabilistic judgment, long considered a distinctly human expert skill, is becoming an area where AI systems are closing in on or matching top human performance.
