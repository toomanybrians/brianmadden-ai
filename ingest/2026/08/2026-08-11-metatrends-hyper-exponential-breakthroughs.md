# HYPER-EXPONENTIAL BREAKTHROUGHS

## Insights

- The White House OSTP's "Golden Age of Science" and "Genesis Mission" reports propose closed-loop autonomous labs — AI proposes hypotheses, robots run experiments, models interpret results and design the next round, continuously and without human intervention.
- Kratsios envisions democratizing access to this infrastructure: anyone (including a high schooler) could submit a hypothesis online and have a federal autonomous lab execute it, removing institutional gatekeeping from science.
- The reports diagnose declining scientific productivity despite rising budgets (NIH at $45B) and attribute it partly to process: ~45% of researcher time goes to administrative overhead rather than actual science.
- Proposed structural fixes include five-year up-front grants, fast-turnaround "few page" grant reviews, and "golden tickets" letting individual reviewers unilaterally fund radical ideas that a committee would otherwise reject.
- The vision extends to AI-native funding mechanisms: prediction markets, AI agents sourcing and vetting research leads, DAOs/smart contracts releasing milestone-based payments to autonomous labs — a fully machine-speed alternative to traditional grant funding.
- Diamandis flags two gaps in the government plan: no dedicated focus on longevity/aging research, and biotech is nested under the Genesis Mission rather than being a standalone national priority.
