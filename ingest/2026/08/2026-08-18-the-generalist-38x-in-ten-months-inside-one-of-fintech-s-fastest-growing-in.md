# 38x in Ten Months: Inside One of Fintech’s Fastest-Growing Infrastructure Companies (Farooq Malik, CEO of Rain)

## Insights

- Malik argues Rain's stablecoin-based financial infrastructure (card issuance, payments) is positioned for a future shaped by AI agents, though the piece doesn't detail the specific mechanism beyond flagging it as a strategic bet.
- Rain deliberately built stablecoin infrastructure years before the market matured, betting tokenized money would become a foundational layer of global finance — an early-mover/patience thesis reinforced by lessons Malik draws from The Art of War.
- Malik frames the addressable opportunity in monetary/financial infrastructure as larger than several major sectors combined (AI, food, oil and gas, water, government spending, defense), positioning money movement as an unusually large infrastructure layer.
- The piece frames stablecoins as combining properties of cash and electronic money, addressing friction and cost in cross-border money movement — a problem Malik says is still poorly solved by existing infrastructure.
- Malik contends the payments market is large enough to support multiple winners beyond incumbents like Stripe, suggesting infrastructure-layer competition rather than winner-take-all dynamics.
- Company operating details mentioned: co-founders Malik and Charles Yoo-Naut deliberately limit meetings to stay aligned, and Rain has specific internal mantras guiding hiring and management.

## Quote

> Money, quantifiable... is larger than AI, it's larger than food, it's larger than oil and gas. —Farooq Malik
