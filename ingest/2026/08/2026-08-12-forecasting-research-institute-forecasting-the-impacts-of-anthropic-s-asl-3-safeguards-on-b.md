# Forecasting the Impacts of Anthropic's ASL-3 Safeguards on Biosecurity Risks

## Insights

- A forecasting survey of 22 biosecurity/national-security experts and 20 superforecasters estimated that Anthropic's ASL-3 safeguards, if applied to all general-purpose AI (GPAI) models, would cut the probability of a $100M+ human-caused outbreak (2026–2028) from 16% to 9.2% — roughly a 40% relative reduction.
- Forecasters judged that ASL-3 applied universally would capture about 71.7% of the total risk reduction theoretically achievable (using a "no GPAI ever existed" world as an upper-bound benchmark), though this figure is likely biased upward since it ignores AI's defensive benefits (e.g., pandemic surveillance, vaccine R&D).
- Restricting ASL-3 to frontier models only (rather than all GPAI models) captured meaningfully less risk reduction (~18–20% vs. ~40%), with disagreement among forecasters tied to how capable/accessible near-frontier models — especially Chinese open-weight models — are assumed to be.
- Of tested variations to ASL-3, faster jailbreak-patching (5x speed) produced the largest additional risk reduction (8% decrease in expected damages), more than harder-to-find jailbreaks or greater capability degradation from jailbreaks — consistent with bioweapon development requiring sustained iterative model access.
- Both experts and superforecasters saw state actors as the most likely cause of a major human-caused outbreak, and expected ASL-3 to further concentrate risk toward well-resourced actors (states, organizations, lab accidents) while screening out lone-wolf/nonexpert actors.
- When unauthorized use was assumed to have caused an outbreak, no single misuse pathway (own jailbreak, public jailbreak, black-market jailbreak, trusted-user exemption) dominated — all were rated at least 15% likely, with superforecasters weighting "trusted user exemption" more heavily than experts did.

## Quote

> Respondents believe ASL-3 safeguards demonstrate high efficacy... the median respondent's forecasts imply that ASL-3 would mitigate roughly 70% of GPAI-attributable risk.
