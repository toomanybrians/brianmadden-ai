# OpenAI Pays $280,000 For This Job. You Don't Have To Be An Engineer.

## Insights

- Forward deployed engineer (FDE) roles are described as one of the hottest jobs in AI, with pay bands that vary widely and reportedly don't agree with each other.
- DXC and Anthropic are cited as having promised to train tens of thousands of FDEs but have so far trained only 86, suggesting a large gap between hiring ambition and actual output.
- The job is framed as having three components, only one of which requires coding — the other two rest on finding "leverage points" in real workflows and owning deployment, meaning non-engineers with domain/industry knowledge can qualify.
- Evals (evaluation work) are positioned as a technical skill required for the role but distinct from traditional coding.
- The underlying argument: AI labs are hiring humans rapidly not despite automation but because deploying models inside real companies ("the last mile") still requires human work that current AI can't do alone.
- Enterprise AI deployments are said to consistently hit the same wall, implying a structural, recurring bottleneck in taking AI from lab to production inside organizations.

## Quote

> Forward deployed engineering pays what it pays because the last mile of AI is still human work.
