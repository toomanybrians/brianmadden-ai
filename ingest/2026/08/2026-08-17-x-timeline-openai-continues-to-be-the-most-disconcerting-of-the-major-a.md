# OpenAI continues to be the most disconcerting of the major AI companies. https:/

## Insights

- OpenAI reportedly dissolved a safety team whose sole function was identifying catastrophic risks in its own models, according to a claim cited by @aakashgupta.
- This marks the third internal safety team OpenAI has eliminated within a two-year span.
- The Superalignment team, co-led by Ilya Sutskever, was the first to be dissolved, in May 2024.
- The moves are framed as contradicting OpenAI's 2023 public pledge to dedicate 20% of total compute resources to AI safety work.
- Gary Marcus characterizes OpenAI as the most "disconcerting" of the major AI labs based on this pattern.
