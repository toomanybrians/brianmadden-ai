# The China Shock 2.0

## Insights

- Setser frames a "China Shock 2.0": unlike the 2000s shock (China dominating low-end manufacturing — clothes, toys, electronics), the new shock is China's dominance of advanced/high-end manufacturing — EVs, batteries, solar panels — outcompeting most of the world.
- The disruption is spreading beyond manufacturing into software: China's frontier AI models are described as neck-and-neck with American ones, suggesting AI competitiveness is now part of the broader trade/economic competition, not a US-only race.
- The piece frames this as an emerging global threat, with other countries only beginning to feel economic and political destabilization from China's advanced-manufacturing and tech competitiveness.
- The episode poses an open policy question — what should other countries do in response — without the extracted content providing Setser's actual proposed answers.

## Quote

> China now dominates advanced manufacturing: electric vehicles, batteries, solar panels.
