# MAICON 2026: You can wait. AI won’t.

## Insights

- References findings from "100+ controlled AI experiments" run with major enterprises (Kroger, AT&T, General Motors, Shell, The Home Depot), positioned as evidence of what's "actually producing ROI" from AI in marketing — though no specific results are detailed in this piece.
- Cites a case of one marketing team building 211 discrete "AI teammates" (agents), with 57 put into daily workflows across 150+ people, reportedly driving 2–3x higher campaign engagement.
- Frames enterprise AI maturity as a shift "from experimentation to scale," implying many organizations are still stuck in pilot/experiment mode rather than operationalizing AI broadly.
- Describes a returning attendee example of individual AI adoption: building AI agents, creating an "AI Activation Coach," preparing an executive summary for leadership, and developing team training — a template for how one person can drive internal AI change.
- Anecdotal evidence suggests conference attendees gain enough AI fluency to become the de facto internal expert/leader on AI within their organization.
