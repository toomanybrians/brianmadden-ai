# Protect your family from voice AI scams. Here's how  #AI #scams #voicecloning #deepfakes

## Insights

- Proposes a low-tech countermeasure to AI voice-cloning scams: families pre-agree on a private verification word/phrase never used in normal conversation, to confirm identity during suspicious calls.
- Argues voice cloning has become good and cheap enough that a few seconds of audio (e.g., pulled from a video) is sufficient to convincingly impersonate someone's voice.
- Describes the typical scam pattern: a cloned voice calls a family member sounding panicked, claiming to be in trouble and needing money wired immediately.
- Key claim: a clone can replicate vocal sound but cannot know a private, pre-shared code word, making this a reliable (and free) verification method.
- Frames the fix as trivial to implement — a five-minute family conversation — positioned against a threat described as underappreciated ("almost nobody has done it").

## Quote

> The clone can copy how you sound. It cannot know the word.
