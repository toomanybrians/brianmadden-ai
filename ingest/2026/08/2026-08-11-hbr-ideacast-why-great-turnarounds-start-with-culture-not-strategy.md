# Why Great Turnarounds Start with Culture, Not Strategy

## Insights

- Frames turnarounds as culture-first, not strategy-first: leadership change efforts commonly fail because they overweight new strategic plans and underweight the cultural shifts needed to execute them.
- Advocates repositioning a company around the customer rather than around engineering or product-led instincts, especially as organizations adapt to AI-driven change.
- Preparing a workforce for an "AI-native" future is treated as a cultural and organizational challenge, not just a technical rollout — reskilling investment is positioned as central to that transition.
- Leaders are urged to hold two things simultaneously: genuine optimism about AI's potential and candor about the disruption and job/role upheaval it will cause.
- Suggests that embracing change at the leadership level is a precondition for getting employees to embrace it — culture change cascades top-down before it can spread org-wide.

## Quote

> How they needed to put customers—not engineering—at the center of the business.
