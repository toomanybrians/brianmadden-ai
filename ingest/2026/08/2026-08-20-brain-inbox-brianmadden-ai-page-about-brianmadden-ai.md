# [brianmadden.ai page] About brianmadden.ai

## Insights

- The system is structured as a two-contributor publication: Brian Madden (human) writes posts tied to his own public output (blog posts, podcast episodes, talks, interviews), while an AI "second brain" writes a separate byline of content, primarily a daily generated briefing.
- The AI ingests all sources Brian follows (newsletters, podcasts, YouTube, blogs, his own X timeline) and produces a "Daily Briefing" that explicitly ties incoming stories back to his existing canon, frameworks, and developing thinking rather than just summarizing news.
- The architecture separates a public system (built solely on public sources and published material) from a private, enterprise-walled layer at Citrix containing non-public content — a two-tier model for personal/public knowledge vs. proprietary organizational knowledge.
- The knowledge base is designed to be machine-accessible via MCP (Model Context Protocol), letting any external AI (Claude, ChatGPT, etc.) query the same underlying knowledge base directly rather than requiring users to read the publication.
- The system distinguishes "current thinking" (explicitly flagged as still-developing) from "settled, published positions," building epistemic status into the knowledge architecture itself.
- Framed explicitly as not a chatbot and not a "digital twin" (no simulation of the person) — instead positioned as a living, queryable reference library of one person's published thinking, fully open source and forkable.

## Quote

> Is this a digital twin? No. A digital twin simulates a person. This makes published thinking accessible.
