# Stripe Paid $7.5 Billion For OpenRouter. You Are Living In The Age Of Startups.

## Insights

- Stripe reportedly paid $7.5 billion for OpenRouter (a router for 400+ AI models), roughly 5x its $1.3 billion valuation from May 2026, a jump attributed to shifts Stripe saw in its own internal data.
- Stripe internally marked January 1, 2026 as an inflection point ("the singularity") based on changes in its metrics.
- A "new Moore's law for intelligence" is described: token volume reportedly doubling every 11 weeks, with OpenRouter's growth characterized as a 24,000x token curve.
- The piece cites an example of agent-to-agent commerce: an AI agent charging another agent two dollars, framed as evidence that small teams can now assemble and sell agent-based services.
- Framed as a "Stripe shaped problem": routing intelligence between models is positioned as adjacent to Stripe's core payments infrastructure business.
- Argues the deal signals founders should reassess their base-case assumptions, and serves as a "wake-up call" for incumbents whose competitive moats may already be eroding in ways not yet visible on typical market maps.
