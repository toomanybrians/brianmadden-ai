# Excellent new Bill Gates essay on the urgency of having a coherent AI plan

## Insights

- Bill Gates's essay reportedly frames AI risk broadly: bioterrorism, deepfakes, disinformation, and cyberattacks, with particular concern that low-skill criminals will gain capability to target victims "at every scale."
- Gates raises a less commonly discussed risk: AI could stunt children's development and displace human relationships — a harm framed as social/developmental rather than economic or safety-related.
- Gates argues society currently lacks a systematic, coherent plan for addressing AI, and frames closing this gap as urgent.
- He emphasizes that civil society (not just government and tech companies) must be centrally involved in shaping that plan; Marcus notes Gates does not explicitly call for independent scientists to be included, which Marcus sees as a gap.
- Gates calls for both domestic and international frameworks for governing AI.
- Gates proposes tax policy changes — rebalancing how labor and capital are taxed — as a mechanism to keep humans employed amid AI-driven disruption.

## Quote

> Even criminals with very limited skills will be able to target victims at every scale.
