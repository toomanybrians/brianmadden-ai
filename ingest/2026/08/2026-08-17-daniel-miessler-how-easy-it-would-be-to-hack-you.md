# How Easy It Would Be to Hack You

## Insights

- Miessler predicts unrestricted (uncensored) open-source AI models will reach the capability level of leading closed models (referenced as "Sol/Mythos/Astra") within 3-12 months.
- He frames this as a "Thanos Gauntlet" scenario: once such models are freely available, anyone could direct one to "ruin" a specific named target with minimal effort.
- The core concern is personal/organizational attack surface — accounts, finances, business operations, customer data, personal information — becoming trivially exploitable once high-capability unrestricted models are commoditized.
- The piece is a call to start seriously assessing one's own vulnerability now, before this capability threshold is reached, rather than treating it as a distant hypothetical.

## Quote

> "Ruin their life." snap And all the power of Mythos++ is unleashed on every attack surface you have in life.
