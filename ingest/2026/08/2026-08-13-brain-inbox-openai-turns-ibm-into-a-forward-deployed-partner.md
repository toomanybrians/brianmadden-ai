# ⚙️ OpenAI turns IBM into a forward deployed partner

## Insights

- OpenAI and IBM announced a partnership embedding OpenAI's tech into IBM Consulting; IBM joins OpenAI's "elite partner tier" and is launching a dedicated OpenAI Practice with thousands of consultants trained on OpenAI certifications — effectively giving OpenAI a forward-deployed enterprise sales/implementation arm.
- The partnership targets three areas: modernizing legacy workflows (finance, procurement, customer ops, HR) with GPT-5.6/Codex/ChatGPT Work; modernizing legacy software/engineering processes; and combining OpenAI's cyber capabilities with IBM's agentic security platform (Project Daybreak) for AI/cyber risk management.
- IBM's stated rationale: enterprises no longer struggle to access AI models — the real bottleneck is deploying AI securely at scale across large, fragmented legacy systems, while AI-enabled cyber threats grow more sophisticated.
- Framed as a "forward deployed partner" model, echoing forward-deployed engineering as a hot AI role — suggesting a template for how frontier labs may need consulting/systems-integration muscle to actually land enterprise adoption.
- Tension noted: this kind of deep partnership could help enterprises balance adoption, security, and cost pressure — or could lock organizations into OpenAI's stack over cheaper/open alternatives.
- Adjacent items: Google's Pixel 11 push toward an "agentic phone" (task/goal-based interaction replacing app-centric use, with human-approval checkpoints for sensitive actions) and Mozilla's strategy of offering model choice in-browser to avoid AI vendor lock-in and preserve an open ecosystem.

## Quote

> "Most organizations don't struggle with getting access to AI tools or models anymore. The real challenge is putting AI to work securely at scale." — Andy Baldwin, IBM Consulting
