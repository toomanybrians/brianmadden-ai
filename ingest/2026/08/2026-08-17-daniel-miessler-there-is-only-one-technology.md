# There Is Only One Technology

## Insights

- Miessler frames all technology — from stone tools to AI to hypothetical neural implants/consciousness uploads — as a single continuous "technology," not discrete inventions or choices.
- The variation between different technologies is just which branches of a "tech tree" get explored first and how far each is pursued before combining or abandoning it.
- He casts humans as "apes powered by evolution" compulsively pushing the boundaries of what's possible, implying the process is closer to a biological/evolutionary drive than deliberate decision-making.
- This framing is explicitly offered as a way to remove guilt and blame from discussions of technology's downsides (TV, Facebook, iPhone, AI) — these weren't "bad choices" society made but inevitable steps on the tree.
- Concludes that this technological progression won't stop on its own — only an external force could halt it.

## Quote

> We are apes moving through a tech tree. And we will not stop unless something stops us.
