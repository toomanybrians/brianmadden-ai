# Another Crazy Experience with AI

## Insights

- Author used an AI assistant ("Kai") to diagnose and troubleshoot chronic macOS performance issues, identifying that a custom-built web app was causing inefficient GPU usage — fixed in ~10 minutes.
- With AI assistance, he rebuilt a paid file-sharing utility as a self-hosted replacement in about an hour, eliminating a recurring subscription cost and reducing system overhead.
- He also replaced a menu-bar system stats app (iStat Menus) with a custom-built alternative ("ulbar.io"), again built in under an hour, including a website for it.
- The cumulative effect: lower ongoing costs, a faster system, and a repeatable "skill" (automated routine) that now regularly checks for performance-draining processes.
- Core claim is about speed of individual capability: one person replacing multiple commercial macOS utilities, complete with hosting and a public-facing product, within roughly an hour each — framed as a marker of accelerating change in what's possible with AI-assisted development.
- Piece frames this as evidence of a broader shift in the pace of technological capability, not just a personal anecdote.
