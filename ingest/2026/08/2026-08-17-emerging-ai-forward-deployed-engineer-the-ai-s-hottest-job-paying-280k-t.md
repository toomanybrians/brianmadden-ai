# Forward Deployed Engineer: The AI's Hottest Job Paying $280K to $1M+ (Full Course with resources)

## Insights

- "Forward Deployed Engineer" (FDE) is emerging as a distinct, fast-growing AI job category, with rapid hiring across Salesforce, Databricks, Anthropic, MongoDB, OpenAI, and startups, spanning new-grad to senior levels and multiple countries.
- The role exists because deploying AI inside real companies is messy: enterprise data is scattered across Salesforce, PDFs, databases and internal software, and someone must handle permissions, tool integration, rule compliance, answer testing, and long-term maintenance.
- Compensation is notably high: Palantir new-grad FDE roles start around $135K–$145K base, OpenAI lists $162K–$280K base plus equity, some total packages reportedly exceed $500K, and senior equity-heavy roles can approach seven figures.
- AWS has committed $1 billion to build an FDE organization embedding thousands of engineers directly with customers, signaling large-scale institutional investment in this function.
- A survey of 113 FDE job descriptions found consistent patterns: 90% involve direct customer work, 87% involve building/deploying production systems, and 62% involve API, data, or system integration work.
- The piece frames FDE skill-building as more accessible than frontier AI research — emphasizing deployment skills (Python, SQL, APIs, cloud, Docker, RAG, MCP, agents, evals) over model training expertise, achievable via an 8-week focused learning path.

> An AI engineer who works very close to the customer and owns the problem until the AI system actually works.
