# ⚙️ Salesforce taps Claude as enterprise AI layer

## Insights

- Salesforce and Anthropic launched "Claudeforce," a partnership embedding Claude as a reasoning layer across Salesforce and Slack, including a plugin with 37 pre-built sales skills for reasoning over live revenue data and automating pipeline updates.
- The integration is designed for centralized setup: an admin connects Salesforce to Claude once, with authentication and permissions managed centrally so every seller gets access without per-user configuration.
- Claude becomes the default model inside Agentforce (powering the Atlas Reasoning Engine, Agentforce Vibes, and Agentforce Coworker) and the default model across all of Slack's AI features, including Slackbot and Slack Code.
- The deal involves reciprocal adoption — each company becomes a customer of the other — which the piece frames as enabling each to build products informed by direct use of the other's platform, and as a possible model for less siloed AI industry collaboration.
- Separately, Z.ai's GLM-5.3-Flash release illustrates that Chinese labs are targeting enterprise token-cost pain points, with the piece noting many enterprise AI tasks don't require frontier-level model performance to deliver ROI.
