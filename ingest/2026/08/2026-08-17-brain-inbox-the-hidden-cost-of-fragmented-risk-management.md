# ⚙️ The hidden cost of fragmented risk management

## Insights

- CISOs commonly report that risk data is fragmented across disconnected systems: third-party risk in one tool, IT risk in another, compliance evidence in spreadsheets and institutional memory.
- AI adoption is cited as a direct driver of increased risk complexity — more vendors, models, and systems touching sensitive data, each requiring new access, breach-impact, and compliance answers.
- Regulatory frameworks (DORA, NIS2, EU AI Act, and future rules) are multiplying alongside AI-driven vendor growth, while risk team headcount stays flat.
- The piece frames risk management maturity as a curve from siloed/reactive programs to integrated risk management that actively supports business decisions, rather than a binary fragmented-vs-integrated state.
- Distinguishes "teams drowning in evidence collection" from "teams the board actually trusts on exposure" as the practical outcome gap between low and high risk-management maturity.

## Quote

> The vendors multiply, the frameworks multiply... and the team asked to keep up stays the same size.
