# The hyping of Anthropic’s IPO

## Insights

- Anthropic is in an SEC-mandated "quiet period" ahead of its fall IPO, which formally limits company communications — but hasn't stopped a wave of leaked financial claims from third parties.
- A Reuters report cites unnamed sources claiming Anthropic is projecting 2028 revenue of roughly $190–200 billion, with the underlying math undisclosed.
- Separate leaked documents reported by Bloomberg reportedly show a strong Q2, and Gavin Baker claimed on the All-In podcast that Anthropic is profitable on every token — both claims presented without disclosed methodology.
- Dwarkesh Patel projected Anthropic could end the year with $100–150 billion in revenue, a figure the author notes Patel later partially walked back.
- Marcus frames the pattern as a coordinated-feeling flow of bullish, unverifiable financial claims surfacing precisely when the company itself is constrained from speaking — positioning himself to scrutinize the gap between hype and disclosed fact ahead of a piece dissecting the specific numbers.

## Quote

> Anthropic may be "quiet," but its fans are not.
