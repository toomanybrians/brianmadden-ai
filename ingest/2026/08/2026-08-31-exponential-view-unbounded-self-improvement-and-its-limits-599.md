# 🔮 Unbounded self-improvement and its limits #599

## Insights

- Philosopher Toby Ord argues recursive self-improvement (RSI) is gated by "generation time" — the loop time for an AI to help design and train its successor — and concludes that while unbounded RSI is mathematically conceivable, real-world constraints (experiment time, training runs, chip manufacturing) prevent generation time from reaching zero, and physical limits (speed of light, Bekenstein bound, Landauer's energy cost of irreversible computation) impose hard ceilings even beyond that.
- Open-weight models are gaining real enterprise traction: Vercel's open-weight token share hit 62% (up from 28% two months prior), and Western firms like Thomson Reuters are building in-house models on Chinese open weights (Qwen) to cut costs.
- Fine-tuned small open models are beating frontier closed models on narrow tasks at much lower cost — Bridgewater's fine-tuned Qwen model (with Thinking Machines) had ~30% fewer errors than the best closed model at 1/14th the inference cost; a 27B Qwen model reportedly outperformed GPT-5.6 Sol on specific fine-tuned tasks.
- Frontier providers' edge is shifting from raw model performance to service guarantees, harness quality, and reliability — which is why Anthropic and OpenAI still capture significant business despite cheaper open alternatives.
- OpenAI's own models helped design its new "Jalapeño" chip (writing kernels, cutting ~10% from a compute block), enabling a ~16-month chip design cycle that beats comparable Nvidia silicon 1.5–1.9x on tokens per megawatt — suggesting AI is compressing chip design cycles and driving toward a more heterogeneous, specialized compute market (e.g., Cerebras for low-latency inference, Fractile's deal with Anthropic).
- Noted in passing: harness/scaffolding quality can matter as much as the underlying model (a system called SwarmOS reportedly pushed GPT-5.6 Sol from 13.3% to 100% on a benchmark called ARC-AGI-3 Public); a University of Chicago economist's guest post found only micro-level, not aggregate-level, productivity evidence for AI so far.

## Quote

> The Universe, it seems, agrees with me. Unbounded RSI has its limits.
