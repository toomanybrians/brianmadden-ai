# Claude Cowork: The Simple Guide to AI Workflows

## Insights

- Claude Cowork is positioned as distinct from Claude Chat: Chat is for thinking through problems and getting answers, while Cowork is for executing multi-step tasks to a finished deliverable (e.g., files, spreadsheets, presentations).
- Cowork's workflow model: give it a defined job (files, instructions, desired output format) and it plans the work, reads/compares source documents, and produces finished artifacts autonomously while the user does other work.
- Anthropic frames Cowork around longer, multi-step tasks that draw on files, connectors, browser actions, and sub-agents rather than single-turn responses.
- Recent feature additions expanding Cowork's capability set include Projects, scheduled tasks, plugins, computer use, and (as of August 26) a built-in browser.
- The piece frames the shift as moving AI use from "help me think" toward "finish this job for me" as the more consequential use case worth adopting.

## Quote

> Chat helps you think. Cowork helps you finish work.
