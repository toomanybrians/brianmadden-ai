# ⚙️ As models commoditize, Replit embraces routing

## Insights

- Replit's new Intelligent Model Routing (now default for all accounts) automatically matches coding tasks to the most cost/quality-appropriate model rather than always using frontier models; Replit claims it matches prior top-tier "Max Mode" output quality at 65% lower cost.
- Free-tier Replit users are notified before being escalated to costlier, higher-powered models and can override; enterprise admins can restrict routing to an approved model set — preserving cost control and transparency alongside automation.
- Snowflake launched a similar dynamic model-routing feature in its Cortex AI Gateway/CoCo/CoWork products in mid-August, suggesting model routing is becoming a broader enterprise AI infrastructure pattern, not a one-off.
- Cohere released "Parse," a vision-language model built specifically for enterprise document processing, pitched as a cheaper, more accurate middle ground between expensive frontier vision models (~$10/1,000 pages) and traditional OCR — Parse runs at $1.50/1,000 pages and outperformed frontier competitors on price-performance in Cohere's internal evaluation.
- Cohere argues that as enterprises push agents and RAG into production, the quality of input data (structured tables, diagrams, visual layout) becomes a critical bottleneck that generic OCR fails to handle — driving demand for task-specific models.
- Broader trend noted: enterprises are growing "weary" of expensive, general-purpose frontier models and increasingly favor niche/domain-specific/small models and routing systems, raising an open question about whether AI industry economics built on ever-growing spend can survive model commoditization.
- Separately, Agility Robotics CTO Pras Velagapudi says manufacturing (not homes) is the better proving ground for humanoid robots, aiming to fill "islands of automation" — manual gaps between existing automated systems — and target dangerous/repetitive "3D" jobs (dull, dirty, dangerous) like bulk material handling; household deployment is a longer-term goal requiring higher safety bars.
