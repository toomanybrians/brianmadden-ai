# You're About to Live Like a Millionaire

## Insights

- Diamandis frames "demonetization" as the mechanism by which today's millionaire-only goods and services (concierge medicine, private tutors, private aviation, domestic staff) become near-free, driven by AI, robotics, and energy cost curves.
- He cites Elon Musk's "universal high income" concept as distinct from UBI: not a floor guaranteeing minimum survival, but a continuously rising ceiling of abundance as marginal production costs approach zero.
- Specific projected enablers: AI tutors reaching "2-sigma" personalized learning outcomes for free, humanoid robots cheaper than a car handling household labor, robotaxis eliminating car ownership (~$50/month), and AI diagnostics plus wearables delivering physician-grade healthcare via phone.
- He pins 2031 as a tipping point when four "basics" (food, shelter, energy, healthcare) and four "luxuries" (education, transport, assistance, entertainment) converge into being fully demonetized, contingent on solar+battery costs collapsing by 2030 and 3D-printed construction pushing home costs under $80K.
- For executives specifically: he argues employees will soon have AI assistants and robot helpers, and claims "the workplace you're managing today won't exist in 5 years" — urging active planning now.
- For policymakers, he frames the central lever not as redistribution/taxation but as accelerating AI/robotics/autonomous transit deployment to speed universal access to abundance.

## Quote

> The millionaire lifestyle doesn't trickle down. It collapses down.
