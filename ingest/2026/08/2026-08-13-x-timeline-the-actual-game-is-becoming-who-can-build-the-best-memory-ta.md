# “the actual game is becoming: who can build the best memory, take the most usefu

## Insights

- Frames the emerging competitive axis for AI agent products as three dimensions: quality of memory, usefulness of actions taken, and token efficiency.
- Implies a shift in evaluation criteria — away from raw capability demos and toward operational efficiency and practical utility.
- Quoted post argues most AI agent products are converging on the same basic pattern: connecting to email, calendar, docs, Slack, and cloud storage to extract context.
- That extracted context — how a person works, who they work with, how they communicate, what they care about — becomes the raw material agents turn into personalization/action.
- Suggests the differentiation between competing agent products may increasingly come down to execution (memory quality, efficiency) rather than the underlying integration approach, which is becoming commoditized.

## Quote

> the actual game is becoming: who can build the best memory, take the most useful actions, and do it while burning the fewest tokens.
