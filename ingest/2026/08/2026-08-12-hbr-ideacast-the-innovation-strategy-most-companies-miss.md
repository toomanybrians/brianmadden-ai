# The Innovation Strategy Most Companies Miss

## Insights

- Govindarajan and Winter argue companies typically design products for wealthy markets first and adapt them downward, but designing for the toughest constraints (affordability, portability, limited infrastructure) first can produce products that perform better across all markets, not just constrained ones.
- They emphasize deep field research as the method for uncovering real customer needs, rather than assuming needs based on existing product lines or wealthy-market use cases.
- Successful global innovation requires balancing performance with cost as a core design constraint, not an afterthought.
- The biggest barrier to global innovation is organizational mindset, not technological capability — companies default to premium-first thinking rather than constraint-first thinking.
- AI is discussed as having both potential and limits in accelerating the design process, though specifics on where it helps versus falls short aren't detailed in the summary.

## Quote

> designing for the toughest constraints first ... can lead to products that perform better everywhere
