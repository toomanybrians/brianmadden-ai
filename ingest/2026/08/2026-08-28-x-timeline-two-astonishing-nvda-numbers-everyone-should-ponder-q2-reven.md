# Two astonishing $NVDA numbers everyone should ponder:

Q2 Revenue: $96 billion

## Insights

- Nvidia's Q2 revenue was $96 billion, but its disclosed future commitments total $366 billion — supply/capacity commitments alone jumped from $119 billion to $279 billion quarter-over-quarter.
- Beyond the $366 billion, Nvidia disclosed additional exposure: $56 billion in AI-cloud and third-party lease commitments, $108.5 billion in land and power guarantees, and MOUs for "more than $500 billion" of third-party capital over time.
- Nvidia's free cash flow fell sharply, from $48.5 billion to $21.3 billion quarter-over-quarter, even as revenue and stock price surged (stock up 8.74% following earnings).
- Saxo's chief investment strategist argues these commitments don't prove circular financing but do mean Nvidia is increasingly financing the very ecosystem it sells into — adding customer credit risk, leases, and equity stakes as new variables investors must weigh alongside GPU shipments.
- The scale of forward commitments relative to current revenue is framed as making Nvidia's risk profile "more complex," raising questions about sustainability of the AI infrastructure buildout it is helping fund.

## Quote

> This does not automatically make the revenue circular. It does mean Nvidia is increasingly helping to create and finance the ecosystem into which it sells.
