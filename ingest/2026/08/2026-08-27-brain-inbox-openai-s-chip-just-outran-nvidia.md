# 🌶️ OpenAI's Chip Just Outran Nvidia

## Insights

- OpenAI published first public benchmark results for Jalapeño, its custom inference chip, showing 1.5–1.9x more work per watt and 1.7–3.6x lower latency than Nvidia's GB200/GB300, across GPT-OSS 120B, DeepSeek R1, and Kimi K2.5; OpenAI claims the gap widens further on its own frontier models (unverified externally).
- Notably, OpenAI says earlier GPT models helped design and bring up the chip, and current models now optimize and program it — AI increasingly building its own infrastructure.
- Results are OpenAI's own runs; SemiAnalysis verified only some on-site, and the chip hasn't cleared production qualification — framed as a signal of possible Nvidia independence, not proof of deployed advantage.
- Anthropic is reportedly telling IPO investors it sees a $30 trillion total addressable market (more than the entire S&P 1500 tech sector's combined revenue), while targeting a ~$2 trillion valuation and up to $100 billion raise, with a listing possibly in September.
- XPeng's robotics unit raised $900M+ at a $6.3B valuation to mass-produce its IRON humanoid (76 DoF, in-house Turing chips) by end of 2026, backed by Tencent and Alibaba — a vertically integrated hardware/model/factory bet.
- Figure's Index app pays people to film everyday chores to train its Helix robot model — 264,000 downloads, 16 million videos, $15M paid to contributors, with $1B+ planned spend on data/compute, reflecting real-world data scarcity as a bottleneck in robotics.
- A modeled study (Duede, Gross, Crockett, Bergstrom) argues AI time-savings in science often lead researchers to start more projects rather than deepen existing ones, degrading paper quality in two of three modeled scenarios even with a "perfect" AI assistant.

## Quote

> "As a labor-augmenting technology, LLMs increase the opportunity cost of our time, impelling us to do more, less well." — Duede et al.
