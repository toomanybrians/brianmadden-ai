# 🤖 Elon Just Hired A Bot Into Your Company

## Insights

- xAI (now operating inside SpaceX as "SpaceXAI") launched Grok Bot in beta on August 11: agents that run persistently on their own cloud computer, sign into a user's existing tools, keep working after the laptop closes, and return with finished work — a shift from prompted assistants toward autonomous "staff."
- Grok Bot is the first consumer product from SpaceX's $60B acquisition of Cursor's parent Anysphere (agreed June 16), and is distributed through Cursor's paid tiers ($120–$300/month), suggesting the deal bought a product team and distribution channel, not just coding-agent tech.
- The genuinely new capability is persistence and delegation: a bot can save a task as a repeatable routine, run it on a schedule, and hand off work to other bots in shared threads — unlike prior browser agents that handled one session and forgot it. Early reviews flag an underwhelming, unpinnable model router.
- Infrastructure and tooling are scaling to support this "agent labor" shift: Anthropic signed a 20-year, $9.1B lease for 191MW of data center capacity from a Bitcoin miner (Riot Platforms), and Nvidia released an open-weight model (Nemotron 3.5 Lightning) explicitly optimized for the cheap, repetitive execution steps agents grind through, rather than frontier reasoning.
- Terminal-Bench 3.0, a new benchmark for professional computer-work tasks, shows the best model/agent pairing (Claude Opus 5 + mini-SWE-agent) resolving only 43.5% of tasks — while costing thousands of dollars per run — indicating agent "hiring" is outpacing actual task competence.
- Separately, Google/DeepMind's ResidencyRL trained a Gemini model through ~50,000 simulated patient consultations; diagnostic accuracy under adversarial conditions rose from 81% to 88%, and the learned interviewing habits transferred to unseen oncology cases, suggesting procedural/interactional skill (not just knowledge) can be drilled via simulation.

## Quote

> "Bots are AI teammates that do real work for you. They sign in to your tools, use them just like you do, and come back with finished work." — xAI
