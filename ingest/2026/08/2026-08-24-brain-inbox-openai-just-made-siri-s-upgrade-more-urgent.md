# ⚙️ OpenAI just made Siri's upgrade more urgent

## Insights

- OpenAI's new ChatGPT plugin for Apple Messages (Mac-only via ChatGPT Work/Codex at launch) moves AI from answering questions to actively managing tasks — e.g., reviewing a day's message threads and drafting suggested replies, or checking a calendar and composing texts with proposed times.
- This plugin puts pressure on Apple's Siri overhaul (previewed at WWDC, expected with iOS 27 in September), since Apple's version would work across the whole device ecosystem rather than being confined to one app/machine.
- An enterprise infrastructure exec (Vultr's CMO) argues large-company AI adoption is still nascent: a typical Fortune 500 has thousands of internal/external applications that all need AI modernization, describing this as a "10-year build cycle" requiring sustained compute growth.
- Growing compute demand is colliding with real-world constraints (power, land, water), pushing chipmakers and infrastructure players toward efficiency gains rather than pure scale — including much smaller/cheaper reasoning models as one path to lower compute/energy costs.
- Separately cited research (from a sponsor, JumpCloud) claims non-human identities (AI agents) now outnumber humans in 83% of organizations, but only 21% of those orgs have governance controls in place for them.
- On-device AI tooling (fast voice dictation, larger foldable screens for remotely steering/monitoring in-progress AI agent tasks) is emerging as a distinct value proposition for technical/builder users, separate from consumer chat assistants.

## Quote

> "That's a 10-year build cycle, and that's going to demand a tremendous amount of compute capacity all around the globe." — Kevin Cochrane, Vultr CMO
