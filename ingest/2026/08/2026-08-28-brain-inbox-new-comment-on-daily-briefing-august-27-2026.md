# New comment on Daily Briefing: August 27, 2026

## Insights

- AI labs increasingly using their own compute for internal model development rather than selling it as inference capacity is flagged as an underappreciated shift.
- Two possible explanations offered: labs may be less worried about the looming financial pressure to generate hundreds of billions in revenue to service infrastructure investment and debt, or they believe a major capability breakthrough is close enough that inference revenue is no longer the best use of compute.
- Speculates this could mark an approach to "RSI" (recursive self-improvement) — the point where AI systems begin meaningfully improving themselves.
- Notes that in the prior month or two, most major AI lab leaders ("AI oligarchs") issued public statements about AI danger and the need to slow down.
- Interprets these warnings as a possible signal of shared awareness among labs that they're near an RSI threshold, framed as an implicit coordination dynamic ("ok bros, are we gonna do this or what?") where no one wants to be the one to say no.
- Suggests a possible motive of pushing compute toward one final capability gain before broader economic strain (tied to AI infrastructure financing) potentially hits.

## Quote

> I truly wonder if this is the RSI (recursive self improvement) moment.
