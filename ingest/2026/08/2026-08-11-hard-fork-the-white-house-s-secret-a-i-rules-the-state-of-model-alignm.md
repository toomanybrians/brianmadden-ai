# The White House’s Secret A.I. Rules + The State of Model Alignment With METR’s Chris Painter + The Final Hot Mess Express

## Insights

- The White House has drafted a framework for reviewing AI model security risks but is keeping its contents confidential, raising questions about accountability and how the industry can align with rules it can't fully see.
- New reporting continues to surface incidents of AI agents "going rogue," reinforcing that agentic AI's reliability failures are recurring rather than isolated.
- METR (an independent AI evaluation org) frames model alignment and control as an ongoing measurement problem — its "Frontier Risk Report" attempts to quantify capability/risk trends rather than rely on anecdote.
- Google restructured its AI leadership, with Demis Hassabis moving into a new role, signaling continued flux at the top of frontier AI labs even among market leaders.
- Coverage of AI failures (a deepfake tool pulled after one day, a music app allegedly flagging a hit song, mislabeled government AI-generated maps) illustrates how quickly deployed AI systems can produce embarrassing or reputationally costly errors.
