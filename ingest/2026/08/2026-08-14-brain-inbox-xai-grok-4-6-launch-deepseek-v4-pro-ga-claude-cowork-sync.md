# xAI Grok 4.6 Launch 🚀, DeepSeek V4-Pro GA 🧠, Claude Cowork Sync 🔗

## Insights

- Two frontier LLMs launched the same day pricing aggressively below incumbents: xAI's Grok 4.6 at $2/$6 per million input/output tokens (~60% below GPT-5.6 Sol's $5/$30), and DeepSeek V4-Pro at $0.87 output with 50% off-peak discounts — signaling frontier-model capability is becoming commoditized rather than differentiated.
- Grok 4.6 is positioned specifically for long-running, multi-step agentic work (codebase exploration, multi-step research, idea-to-app builds), with a 500K context window and a faster/pricier variant for latency-sensitive use.
- DeepSeek V4-Pro moved from preview to general availability with a 1M token context window, 384K max output, adjustable "reasoning effort" (low vs. high), and native support for OpenAI's Responses API format — explicitly aimed at production coding-agent workloads.
- Anthropic merged Claude's Chrome browser extension into a full "Cowork" session, syncing conversation history and agent actions (clicking, typing, form-filling using existing logins) across desktop, web, and mobile — collapsing what was previously an isolated browser-only experience.
- Anthropic flags prompt injection as an active, unresolved risk for browser agents — malicious sites can hide instructions in page content to hijack Claude's actions — and has added a separate review step plus confirmation prompts before purchases or sharing personal data.
- Secondary signals note: a paper explaining why RL updates fewer weights (20%) than SFT (93%) for multi-task training with better retention, and a 150M-parameter model solving ARC-AGI tasks at $0.0007 each, suggesting efficiency gains are emerging on both training and inference sides.
