# Dylan Patel – Anthropic & OpenAI will have most of the world’s compute by 2028

## Insights

- Compute is rapidly centralizing: OpenAI and Anthropic took ~30% of incremental new compute in 2025, a projected 40-50% in 2026, and could control most of the world's usable FLOPs by 2028 — driven by their ability to monetize compute (via inference revenue) far above what other buyers can pay.
- Lab economics have flipped: compute that once ran at negative gross margins now generates $50-100M+ per megawatt in revenue for frontier labs, versus a base cost of ~$10-15M/megawatt — creating huge margin that funds further compute buildout.
- A described trend: labs are allocating a shrinking share of compute to revenue-generating inference and a growing share to internal R&D/training, because the internal value of frontier models for accelerating research now reportedly outweighs near-term inference revenue.
- Regulatory and safety-driven throttling (delayed releases, paused training, undeployed internal models, state-level datacenter moratoriums/taxes) is flagged as a real constraint that could suppress revenue-per-megawatt growth and slow the compute land-grab by the labs.
- Compute pricing is described as being reshaped by a "bullwhip effect": prices for scarce inputs (memory, substrates) are rising fast, actors like SpaceX/Meta are hoarding compute to resell at markups ($25-50M+/megawatt) rather than sell at base cost, and full supply-chain price adjustment (e.g. fabs) lags years behind demand signals.
- China is estimated to be receiving under 10% of new global AI compute (vs. ~30-35% in 2022), a gap attributed to export restrictions and slower domestic buildout, with China's compute potentially reaching around 30 gigawatts by 2028 versus a much larger US/lab-controlled base.

## Quote

> By the time you're towards the end of 2028... you've got them just controlling most of the usable flops in the world on their own.
