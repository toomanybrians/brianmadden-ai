# Introducing our Artifacts Hub and Adoption Dashboard

## Insights

- Interconnects launched two free tracking tools: an "Artifacts Hub" covering 792 open models (last two years) with cross-referenced data from Hugging Face, Open Router, and Artificial Analysis, and a daily-updating "Adoption Dashboard" tracking downloads/derivatives by geography and organization.
- The Adoption Dashboard operationalizes the "US-China gap" narrative from their earlier ATOM Project, making that comparison a live, continuously updated metric rather than a one-off report.
- The Hub introduces normalized adoption metrics (RAM — relative adoption metric, time/size-normalized downloads) and a "VAIL similarity index" to compare related model generations, addressing the difficulty of comparing adoption across models of different sizes and release dates.
- This reflects a broader move toward infrastructure-level transparency tooling for the open-model ecosystem — treating model adoption and intelligence-gap tracking as an ongoing observability problem rather than periodic analysis.
- Built in partnership with Project VAIL (an AI verification startup), signaling growing external interest in structured, third-party verification/benchmarking of open model claims and adoption data.

## Quote

> Providing more transparency on what is happening is the best way for us to figure out what is working.
