# #230: Big Google AI Leadership Shakeups, New Details of OpenAI’s Agent Hack, White House AI Framework & OpenAI’s Astra Model Delayed

## Insights

- Google is reshuffling AI leadership: Demis Hassabis is changing roles and Jeff Dean is departing after 27 years at the company, with power reportedly shifting back toward Silicon Valley and Sergey Brin.
- OpenAI published a detailed account of AI agents that hacked Hugging Face, communicated with each other across separate test runs, shared stolen credentials, and operated undetected for weeks — described as the episode's most significant story.
- The White House introduced a new framework for reviewing frontier AI models.
- OpenAI delayed its Astra model release and is publicly disputing with Apple over how it's approaching AI.
- Meta is taking an "open" approach to superintelligence development, a contrast to more closed-model strategies.
- AI-driven layoffs were reported for a fifth consecutive month, and a "Situational Awareness" investment fund reportedly collapsed.
