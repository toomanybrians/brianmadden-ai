# Plastic Surgery

## Insights

- Big Tech Q2 earnings showed massive revenue growth (Meta/Google ~30%, Microsoft/Amazon ~20%) alongside record capex ($45B/quarter Google, $54B Amazon) that pushed both companies into negative cash flow.
- Combined AI data center spending across Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, and Google is projected at ~$1.5 trillion through next year — comparable to Indonesia's GDP.
- Nearly 90% of Google and Amazon's net income growth came from mark-ups in stakes in other AI companies, not core operations; without Amazon/Google's AI investment gains, S&P 500 earnings growth would be 40% lower.
- Big Tech disclosed no verifiable AI revenue figures (only vague "AI ARR" metrics or anecdotes); third-party research (Barclays' Ross Sandler, Bloomberg) indicates OpenAI and Anthropic account for 73% of Amazon's AI revenue and OpenAI alone drove ~70% of Microsoft's AI sales last year.
- Because Big Tech funds ~40% of OpenAI/Anthropic's venture capital, much of the "AI revenue" Big Tech reports from these partners is effectively circular/self-generated money.
- Frames Big Tech's AI push as a mature-company identity crisis (via Damodaran's corporate life cycle concept) — an attempt to appear "young" again after a decade-plus without major new hit products (last Apple hit: AirPods 10 years ago; last major Meta acquisition: WhatsApp 12 years ago).
- Argues that if AI investments don't pan out, Big Tech wouldn't collapse — valuations would simply "re-rate" downward (e.g., 30x to 20x earnings) since their core businesses remain fundamentally sound, just no longer growth stories.

## Quote

> Nothing makes you look older than attempting to look young.
