# Will the AI boom continue? Forecasting the trajectory of the AI industry

## Insights

- A panel of AI experts and superforecasters (LEAP, Wave 11) expects private investment in data center structures to grow 76% in real terms from 2025 to 2028, and 120% by 2035 — even accounting for headwinds like New York's hyperscale data center moratorium and slow electricity interconnections.
- Forecasters expect much slower growth in the broader categories that support AI buildout (IT equipment and electrical/communication infrastructure) than in data centers specifically, since those categories are economy-wide and less AI-sensitive; short hardware lifespans (vs. decades-long data center structures) are seen as a key driver of IT equipment investment.
- Forecasters expect semiconductor stocks (SMH) to grow much more slowly through 2028 than their 2024–2026 trajectory — median forecast of only 1.5x mid-2026 value vs. a trend-extrapolated 3.37x — with more experts predicting a decline than a return to trend.
- Software stocks (IGV) are expected to modestly outperform their recent historical trend, with forecasters split on whether AI will let software companies sell more (bullish) or render them obsolete (bearish).
- Superforecasters place a 34% chance that OpenAI and Anthropic's combined annualized revenue run rate exceeds $400B by 2030 (median forecast ~$300B, rising to $770B by 2040), but researchers suspect many experts/public respondents anchored on outdated figures and effectively predicted revenue *declines* relative to already-reported mid-2026 numbers — a likely artifact of survey design rather than genuine belief in a slowdown.
- Reaching $200B+ revenue (as OpenAI and Anthropic project for 2028–2030) would place either company among just 30 firms globally with that scale of revenue (per 2026 Fortune Global 500 data).

## Quote

> Forecasters expect data center investment to continue growing quickly, despite the prospect of moratoria.
