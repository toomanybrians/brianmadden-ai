# Stop Building What We Don't Understand

## Insights

- John Sherman (The AI Risk Network) argues AI safety advocates focused on extinction risk should join forces with other AI-harmed constituencies — anti-data-center communities, workers facing AI-driven layoffs, families worried about chatbots, and seniors targeted by AI fraud — rather than treating existential risk as the only legitimate concern.
- His reasoning: past tech backlashes (social media, smartphones) never succeeded in constraining big tech, but the data-center opposition movement did succeed largely by accident, suggesting broad coalitions — not narrow, single-issue framing — are what actually move institutions against AI companies.
- Cited labor data: Challenger, Gray & Christmas reports AI as the top-cited reason for layoffs for four consecutive months (April–June 2026), with 101,743 AI-attributed cuts year-to-date (~23% of all announced cuts) and 173,568 cumulative since 2023.
- Data center opposition is substantial and bipartisan: $130B in projects blocked/delayed nationwide, 142+ groups active across 24 states, roughly even Republican/Democrat opposition, and polling showing 59-70% of Americans oppose data centers near their homes.
- Other cited harms bolstering the "big tent" case: an FTC inquiry into AI companion chatbots and minors, a bipartisan Senate letter pushing enforcement, the Kids Online Safety Act advancing, and a Senate Aging Committee hearing on AI-enabled elder fraud (projected to reach $40B in losses by 2027).
- Pew data shows a generational shift in AI sentiment: 55% of US adults under 30 are now more concerned than excited about AI (up from 31% in 2021), driven mainly by job-loss fears rather than existential risk.
- Sherman's proposed remedy — an "immediate, indefinite, international ban on building advanced AI" — is broader than the existing Statement on Superintelligence petition (72,000+ signatures), which calls for a conditional, reversible prohibition pending safety consensus and public buy-in.

## Quote

> In human history, the people have never stood up to big tech. We have never been able to flex our agency against big tech.
