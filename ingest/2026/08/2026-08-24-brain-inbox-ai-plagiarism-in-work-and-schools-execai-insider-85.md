# AI Plagiarism in Work and Schools (ExecAI Insider #85)

## Insights

- Anthropic will begin watermarking Claude-generated content, aimed at surfacing low-quality "AI slop," but the author argues this misses the bigger issue.
- The author frames "AI plagiarism" as the real problem: people presenting AI-generated ideas and words as their own without any critical thought or engagement.
- AI-assisted writing (drafting, editing, inspiring ideas) is described as a legitimate and expanding practice, especially for non-writers who have something to say, and shouldn't be stigmatized via watermarks.
- A distinction is drawn between using AI to augment writing/thinking versus using it as a full replacement for one's own thought — the latter is called "a slippery slope for society."
- The piece calls for teaching responsible AI use in both schools and business, including preserving methods to teach and test for critical thinking and writing skill, rather than banning AI outright.
- Note: schools are flagged as often still treating AI use as straightforward cheating, which the author considers not the right answer either.

## Quote

> AI has the potential to make us better writers, deeper thinkers, and more inspired creatives, but only if we use it to augment our abilities rather than replace them.
