# ⚙️ Crusoe’s inference pitch: Own more, charge less

## Insights

- Gartner estimate cited: inference costs are projected to grow fivefold by 2028, driven largely by rising token usage from agentic workflows.
- Crusoe's pitch centers on vertical integration — owning energy, data centers, and cloud infrastructure end-to-end — as the lever for cheaper, faster inference rather than software optimization alone.
- Company claims its "MemoryAlloy" technology achieves 9.9x faster time-to-first-token and 5x greater throughput than vLLM on GPT-OSS-120B, though this is a vendor-provided benchmark.
- Managed Intelligence portfolio spans four tiers of abstraction: fully serverless inference (no infra management), tailored custom deployments, self-serve dedicated compute, and serverless fine-tuning on customer-uploaded datasets.
- Company frames its role as absorbing "operational hassle" so customers can focus on building with AI rather than managing infrastructure — positioning infra ownership/management as a service layer, not just a cost play.
- Crusoe also contributes open-source tooling (e.g., "fastokens" tokenizer) integrated into vLLM, SGLang, and NVIDIA Dynamo, suggesting a strategy of shaping the inference-serving ecosystem beyond its own paid platform.

## Quote

> There's also a real shift in appetite for who does the work. We're the cloud partner that offloads the operational hassle. — Michael Yen-Chi Ho, Crusoe
