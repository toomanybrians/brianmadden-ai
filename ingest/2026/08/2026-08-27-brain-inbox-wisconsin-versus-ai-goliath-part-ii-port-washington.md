# Wisconsin versus AI Goliath Part II: Port Washington

## Insights

- A Vantage Data Centers facility under construction in Port Washington, WI (three square miles) is slated to have OpenAI and Oracle as tenants, though residents say no one has given clear answers on what will actually be built, stored, or run there.
- No environmental impact study was conducted before approval; FOIA records reportedly show Vantage told the mayor the project wouldn't proceed if an environmental impact study were required, after which the requirement was dropped and the project was approved by the governor's office.
- Residents describe intensive surveillance infrastructure surrounding the site (seven license-plate-reading cameras on one exit route) and private security empowered to confront and threaten residents on public streets, raising questions about the purpose of such security for a "data center."
- Concerns center on physical/environmental externalities of AI infrastructure: use of local residential well water without proper permitting or capping, warm-water discharge into Lake Michigan from associated power generation, ambient heat effects on local climate, and possible reliance on diesel or methane gas turbines for on-site power due to delayed grid transmission lines.
- Property values near the site have collapsed (residents cite $150,000–$300,000 losses) and homes have become effectively unsellable, with financing/contingency issues compounding the impossibility of relocating.
- Local organizing (Great Lakes Neighbors United, originally a Facebook group) formed in direct response to a viral video revealing the deal, and has since expanded to advise residents in other towns facing similar data center proposals; community members report deteriorating social cohesion, harassment, anonymous threatening letters, and mental health strain tied to the fight.
