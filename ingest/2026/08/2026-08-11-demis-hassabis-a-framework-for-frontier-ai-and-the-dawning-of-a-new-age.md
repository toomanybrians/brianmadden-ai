# A Framework for Frontier AI and the Dawning of a New Age

## Insights

- Hassabis positions AGI as imminent ("a few short years away") and frames its impact as potentially 10x the Industrial Revolution at 10x the speed — a scale claim relevant to how enterprises should plan workforce and capital investment horizons.
- He explicitly names near-term risks beyond misuse: loss-of-control scenarios involving agentic, recursively self-improving systems, alongside cyber, bio, and nuclear threat vectors — a framing enterprises evaluating AI deployment risk should track.
- Proposes a concrete governance mechanism: a US-based "Frontier AI Standards Body" modeled on FINRA (public-private, industry-funded, National Labs involvement) that would certify "Frontier Labs" and "Frontier Models" against evolving benchmarks.
- Governance mechanics described: voluntary 30-day pre-release model sharing initially, moving toward mandatory pass/fail certification for US market deployment; quarterly-updated benchmarks; provisions for coordinated slowdown if risk escalates.
- Notably exempts startups/academic (non-frontier) models from the regulatory framework, suggesting a bifurcated compliance burden that would concentrate scrutiny on large incumbent labs — a structural point relevant to competitive dynamics in enterprise AI adoption.
- Raises unresolved second-order questions for a "post-scarcity" economy — new economic models, meaning/purpose, and the human condition — without answering them, signaling these as open strategic questions rather than settled predictions.
