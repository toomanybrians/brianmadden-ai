# Unconventional Thought Is the Differentiator

## Insights

- Core claim: as AI becomes proficient at "average" tasks/knowledge, unconventional, contradictory, and novel thought becomes the primary human differentiator.
- Argues novel thought requires deep self-connection — understanding one's own personal differences and views on how the world should be different.
- Suggests this inner sense of self is often suppressed by upbringing, education, peers, and parental pressure, causing people to adopt others' ideas and goals instead of forming their own.
- Frames the suppression of individual perspective as something that "needs to be reversed" as AI capability increases.
- Ties the ability to generate novel ideas to having a strong, distinct sense of self combined with broad reading and varied inputs.
- Positions this as a general imperative: everyone needs to actively work on cultivating unconventional thought as a skill/practice.

## Quote

> Unconventional thought is becoming way more of a differentiator and something that sets you apart.
