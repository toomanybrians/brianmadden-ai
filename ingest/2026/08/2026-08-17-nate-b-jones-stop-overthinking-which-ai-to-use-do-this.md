# Stop overthinking which AI to use. Do this.

## Insights

- Argues there's no objectively "best" AI model — the search for one via benchmarks or comparisons is misguided.
- Proposes a personal-fit test instead: choose the model that feels most comfortable during your hardest, most difficult work.
- The right model is one where you don't have to translate or reframe your thinking into its language — it matches how your brain already works.
- Frames model choice as a subjective, felt experience rather than a measurable performance ranking (benchmark scores, trending names).
- Suggests most people already intuitively know which model fits them, even if they haven't admitted it.
- Mentions a self-built tool (linked via newsletter) for matching model choice to individual working style.

## Quote

> Pick the model that makes you feel most comfortable doing your hardest work.
