# Awesome day in terms of new model releases from both Deepseek and Grok. Both of

## Insights

- Three frontier AI models launched on the same day: Grok 4.6, Qwen3.8-Max, and DeepSeek-V4-Pro-0813, prompting the claim of a "huge jump in capability at insanely low costs."
- Grok 4.6 is described as matching a prior "Fable 5" capability level while being roughly 85% cheaper.
- Qwen3.8-Max is a 2.4T-parameter model with 95B active parameters, and its weights have been released openly.
- DeepSeek-V4-Pro-0813's weights were expected to release imminently, with early word describing it as genuinely strong rather than optimized narrowly for benchmarks.
- The author frames this cost/capability compression as a real-time example of Jevons paradox in AI: falling costs are expected to drive higher overall demand rather than shrink the market.
- Increased competition among model providers is characterized as beneficial for both consumers and businesses.

## Quote

> This is truly Jevons paradox for AI playing out. By bringing down the cost of AI, demand will continue to [grow]
