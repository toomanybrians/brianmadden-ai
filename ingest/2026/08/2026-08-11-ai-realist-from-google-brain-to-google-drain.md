# From Google Brain to Google Drain

## Insights

- Google's Gemini co-lead structure (Dean, Vinyals, Shazeer) fully dissolved within roughly two months in mid-2026, with all three departing to competitors or startups — read as a leading indicator preceding today's reorg rather than a cause of it.
- The destinations of departing talent function as an informal signal of insider confidence: Shazeer went to OpenAI, the Dean/Ghemawat/Vinyals/Le group founded an architecture-search startup (Discovery Loop), and AlphaFold's Nobel-winning core moved to Anthropic — each choice implicitly a verdict on Google's internal roadmap.
- Pichai's reorg memo mentions "Gemini 4" but omits any reference to the intermediate release the trade press expected, suggesting the in-between model cycle stalled or was scrapped internally.
- Dissolving the AlphaFold science team to reinforce Gemini product work is framed as evidence of resource scarcity, not strategic confidence — companies "long on model progress" don't cannibalize their research bench.
- Google's handling of departures (equity stake, Cloud partnership, compute hosting for the new startup) converts a talent-drain expense into a customer/investment relationship — a financial-engineering pattern the piece compares to Nvidia-CoreWeave and Amazon-Anthropic circular deals.
- The analysis separates Google's genuine strengths (custom silicon, datacenters, distribution via Search/Android, ~950M app users) from its weaker current position: infrastructure moat buys time but doesn't itself produce a competitive frontier model.

## Quote

> Insiders didn't publish model evaluations. They published destinations.
