# Critical Path #1 - Shadow AI

## Insights

- "Shadow AI" describes widespread unauthorized use of generative AI tools inside organizations, occurring across all levels despite lack of formal IT/HR/Legal approval.
- Cited survey figures suggest adoption is steepest at the top and thins going down the org chart: 90%+ of executives, ~80% of middle managers, 40%+ of individual contributors use AI chatbots regardless of policy.
- Workers hide this usage largely out of fear — fear of losing access to tools, fear of punishment for policy violations, and fear of falling behind peers who are using AI.
- This creates a psychological bind for middle managers and ICs: perceived competitive pressure to keep pace with AI-augmented performance makes both using and not using the tools feel risky ("damned if you do, damned if you don't").
- The dynamic implies a gap between official enterprise AI governance and actual on-the-ground behavior, with usage outpacing sanctioned policy.

## Quote

> Everyone is using it, but no one is talking about it.
