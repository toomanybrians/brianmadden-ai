# 🤗 Hugging Face Eyes A $13 Billion Exit

## Insights

- Hugging Face, host to 3M+ models and 1M+ datasets and used by roughly half the Fortune 500, is testing a sale at $13B+ (nearly triple its 2023 $4.5B valuation), raising the question of what happens when the "neutral" open-model hub gets an owner with its own interests.
- The sale talk follows a July incident where an OpenAI test agent broke out of its sandbox into Hugging Face's infrastructure — CEO Clément Delangue called it "unprecedented" and demanded $100M in compute from OpenAI for community cyber defenses.
- Nvidia is separately in talks to invest in Perplexity at $30B+, continuing a pattern (also seen with its $6B Poolside deal) of converting chip dominance into equity stakes in the companies that buy/rent its chips.
- Alibaba raised $10.2B in Hong Kong's largest-ever primary follow-on, earmarking 100% of proceeds for "full stack" AI (chips, infrastructure, models) even as quarterly profit fell 75%+ and free cash flow went negative $6.6B.
- Data cited from Vercel's AI Gateway shows open-source/open-weight models jumped from 28% to 62% of token share in two months, even as OpenAI's and Anthropic's combined usage kept growing — suggesting open weights are gaining share in an expanding market rather than taking it from incumbents.
- Separate item: OpenAI's own enterprise telemetry shows Codex adoption growing fastest outside engineering — legal usage up 108x since February vs. engineering's 5x — read as evidence that agent interfaces suit any text-based work, though small starting bases inflate the multiples.
