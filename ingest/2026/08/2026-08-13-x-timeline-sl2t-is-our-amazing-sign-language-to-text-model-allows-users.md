# SL2T is our amazing sign-language-to-text model allows users to sign directly to

## Insights

- Google DeepMind released SL2T, a sign-language-to-text model that lets users sign directly into their phone rather than typing.
- Initial rollout covers American Sign Language-to-English, integrated into Gboard and Live Transcribe on Pixel 11 (Android).
- The model was developed in close collaboration with the Deaf community, positioned as a co-designed accessibility tool rather than a top-down build.
- Framed by Demis Hassabis as an example of AI's potential for direct social good, distinct from productivity or enterprise use cases.

## Quote

> Built in close collaboration with the Deaf community, it's a great example of the good that can be done with AI.
