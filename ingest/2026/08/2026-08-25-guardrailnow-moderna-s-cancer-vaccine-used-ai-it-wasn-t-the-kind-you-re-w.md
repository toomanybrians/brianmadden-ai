# Moderna's Cancer Vaccine Used AI. It Wasn't the Kind You're Worried About.

## Insights

- Moderna's melanoma vaccine breakthrough (paired with Merck's Keytruda) used narrow AI only for a specific classification/ranking task — sequencing tumor DNA and predicting which molecular targets a patient's T cells should be trained to recognize — not general intelligence; commentators stress this is a task narrow AI has been suited to for years, not a new AGI capability.
- Stock market reaction was extreme and volatile: Moderna surged 176% then fell 25% the next day, a net $18B swing, showing how thin the line is between hype and correction even for genuine narrow-AI medical results.
- A leaked NRSC memo frames data center backlash as a "sleeper issue" and potential liability across races (cited: Ohio Senate race), with tech companies urged to address the "toxic brand" of the industry themselves rather than relying on campaigns to do so.
- Circular financing arrangements are spreading across the AI infrastructure buildout — e.g., Nvidia reportedly in talks to help fund an OpenAI data center lease while also supplying its chips; AMD investing in Anthropic which commits to buying AMD chips — raising concern that labs, chipmakers, and cloud providers are simultaneously funders, suppliers, and customers of each other, potentially eroding external market discipline and incentives to slow down for safety work.
- Public friction with AI's physical footprint is showing up in adjacent forms: vandalism against Flock Safety license-plate-reader cameras, a 69-year-old activist serving jail time for chaining herself to OpenAI's HQ (with AI safety researcher Stuart Russell testifying at her trial), and a viral video of a humanoid robot crashing during training for a robotics competition.
- The piece's framing: none of these individual stories resolve the AI risk debate, but together they show the industry's physical footprint, financial structuring, and human/political costs becoming broadly visible before core questions about frontier model capability are settled.

## Quote

> "The incentive to slow down for safety work collapses completely."
