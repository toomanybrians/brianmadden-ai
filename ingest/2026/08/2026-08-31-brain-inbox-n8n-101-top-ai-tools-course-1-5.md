# n8n 101: Top AI Tools Course 1/5

## Insights

- The piece argues that connective/orchestration tools (like n8n) matter more for practical AI adoption than frontier models themselves, since they let multiple AI tools and data sources work together in automated sequences.
- n8n is positioned as a "fair-code" visual workflow automation platform with native AI capabilities, citing over 200,000 GitHub stars and 400+ integrations as evidence of adoption.
- The core use case described: chaining triggers (emails, form submissions, new orders, document changes) to AI models, data sources (Google Drive, databases), and actions (Slack messages, spreadsheet updates) into repeatable workflows requiring no manual re-triggering.
- Frames a shift in how AI is used at work — from single-prompt chat interactions that end when a tab closes, to persistent, recurring automated processes tied to real business events.
- Mentions that a workflow can include an AI agent checking or approving actions before they execute, and can incorporate human approval steps.
- Notes a "newer 2026 way" of building workflows using AI itself to construct the automations, though this is only flagged, not elaborated in this excerpt.

## Quote

> Once you see n8n working properly, a lot of separate AI tools start looking like pieces of one bigger machine.
