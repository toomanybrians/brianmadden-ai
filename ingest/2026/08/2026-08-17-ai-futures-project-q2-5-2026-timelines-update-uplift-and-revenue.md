# Q2.5 2026 Timelines Update: Uplift and Revenue

## Insights

- The Automated Coder (AC) milestone is defined as the point where a leading AI company would rather fire its human software engineers than forgo AI for coding — a direct proxy for enterprise-level labor substitution, not just a benchmark score.
- The forecasters now anchor AC predictions on "coding uplift" (the speedup AI gives engineers) rather than relying solely on METR's time-horizon benchmark, because the uplift threshold for AC is far less ambiguous than an equivalent time-horizon value.
- Internal Anthropic employee surveys reportedly show coding uplift rising from ~1.25x to ~4x in seven months, implying a doubling time (of uplift minus 1) as short as 3.5–5 months, though authors caveat this is likely biased upward by employee self-selection.
- Three independent methods (time horizon, coding uplift, and AI-company revenue extrapolation) converge on similar AC arrival dates, which the authors treat as a meaningful — though not conclusive — sign of robustness in their modeling.
- Grading their earlier "AI 2027" scenario against reality, the authors estimate actual AI progress is running at roughly 70–90% of the pace they originally predicted, with AC now projected around mid-2027 to early 2028 depending on assumed slowdown.
- A revised model change accounts for the fact that deploying software improvements still requires retraining runs, which reduces the plausibility of extremely fast "takeoff" scenarios once automation begins.
- Individual forecasters' median AC dates diverge somewhat (e.g., one author's median moved from Dec 2028 to closer to 2030) due to differing weight given to "unknown unknowns," data bottlenecks, and confidence in the underlying model.

## Quote

> Reality seems to be going about 70-90% as fast as AI 2027 predicted.
