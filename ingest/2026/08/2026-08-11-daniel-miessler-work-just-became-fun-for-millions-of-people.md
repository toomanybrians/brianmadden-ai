# Work Just Became Fun for Millions of People

## Insights

- Argues the "AI makes us work more" narrative is a framing error: what's actually happening is that work itself has become enjoyable, so more hours don't feel like a burden.
- Reframes most traditional knowledge work as "scaffolding" — meetings, process, bureaucracy, workflows, knowledge bases, and formatting for professional output — rather than the actual work itself.
- Claims AI removes the scaffolding, exposing the underlying creative/building work as the genuinely engaging part that was always there, just obscured.
- Suggests this shift is happening for "millions of people," implying a broad, not niche, change in how knowledge workers experience their jobs.
- Acknowledges overwork is possible with AI-enabled work but treats that as a secondary risk, not a reason to doubt the core claim.
- Notably self-referential: the post discloses AI (Kai) handled formatting, subtitle, illustration, and publishing — an applied example of scaffolding being offloaded.

## Quote

> AI Unmasked Our Work as Scaffolding
