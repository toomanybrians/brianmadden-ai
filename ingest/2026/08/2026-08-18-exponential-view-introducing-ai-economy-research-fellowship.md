# 🔮 Introducing: AI Economy Research Fellowship

## Insights

- Exponential View is framing its AI Economy research agenda around five core open questions: how to value AI companies/infrastructure from first principles, where economic surplus is created and captured across the AI value chain, AI's long-term effects on wages/employment/productivity/worker bargaining power, microeconomic effects inside firms, and what indicators would signal "transformative AI" has arrived.
- The organization explicitly positions itself against academic-pace research, emphasizing "a good answer within hours or days" via tightly framed questions, visible assumptions, and willingness to revise views quickly rather than waiting for consensus.
- Their broader AI Economy programme tracks the full physical/financial/organizational build-out of AI: semiconductors, energy, data centers, model economics, corporate investment/adoption, firm-level performance, labor markets, and value distribution across the stack.
- The role requires distinguishing between what is observed, estimated, inferred, and unknown — treated as a core discipline for judging claims about AI's economic impact.
- Candidates are asked to test propositions from economists like Anton Korinek and Daron Acemoglu on "transformative AI" by translating them into observable, measurable indicators over a two-year horizon.
