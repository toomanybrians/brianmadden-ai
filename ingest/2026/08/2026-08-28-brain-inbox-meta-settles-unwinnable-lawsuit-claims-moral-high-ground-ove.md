## Insights

- The Wall Street Journal published an op-ed by billionaire investor Stanley Druckenmiller that editors failed to recognize as AI-generated; Druckenmiller later confirmed he used AI to write it.
- WSJ Editorial Page Director Paul Gigot defended publishing the AI-assisted op-ed, arguing that AI use for research, editing, and drafting is now normal and that the piece still reflects Druckenmiller's "genuine opinion" based on his longstanding relationship with the paper.
- The newsletter author pushes back on that defense, arguing that when an LLM contributes substantial content, it becomes impossible to distinguish the author's actual views from AI-generated material.
