# 🎙️ Ground Level AI Podcast | Synthesia CEO Victor Riparbelli on turning AI video into an ‘LLM with a face’

## Insights

- Synthesia has shifted from one-way, pre-recorded AI avatar videos to real-time conversational avatars — Riparbelli calls the current product "basically an LLM with a face."
- Enterprise customers use AI role-play to rehearse difficult management conversations (harsh feedback, conflict resolution) at scale; one customer with 2,000-3,000 managers uses this, with average sessions running ~18 minutes.
- The system scores employee performance after role-play/simulated calls (product knowledge, adherence to sales methodology, next-steps setup), giving executives aggregate data to diagnose whether underperformance is a product issue or a training issue.
- Riparbelli argues not all AI agents should have a face/video interface — simple transactional queries (e.g., refund status) suit text, while complex teaching/selling scenarios benefit from a visual, interactive presence that can demonstrate and show materials like a teacher.
- He predicts most computer interactions will become agent-mediated within a couple years, but rejects the idea that companies will eventually be run mostly by AI agents overseen by a small human board — he maintains "great people" remain core to company success.
- He speculates that as AI-mediated interaction becomes ubiquitous, human interaction itself becomes scarcer and more valuable, potentially shifting economic value toward experiences built on genuine human connection (dining, live events, relationship-driven sales) and away from purely logical/numerical work.

## Quote

> "Whatever becomes scarce in society, the value of that goes up." — Victor Riparbelli
