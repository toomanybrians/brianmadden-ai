# Why Microsoft is Quietly Abandoning China

## Insights

- Microsoft has spent five years retreating from China — closing offices and retail stores and winding down thousands of jobs — while continuing to sell Azure and AI services to major Chinese firms like ByteDance and Shein.
- The pullback raises the question of whether this constitutes the broader US-China tech decoupling that's been anticipated, even as commercial AI/cloud ties persist selectively.
- Microsoft's former Beijing R&D center is noted as having trained alumni who now lead Chinese AI rivals, including DeepSeek and SenseTime — a talent pipeline that indirectly built competitors.
- The episode frames this as a split strategy: physical/organizational retreat from China paired with continued deep commercial engagement in AI and cloud infrastructure with Chinese enterprises.
