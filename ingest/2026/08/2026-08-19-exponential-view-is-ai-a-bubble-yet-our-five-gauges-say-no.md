# 🫧 Is AI a bubble yet? Our five gauges say no

## Insights

- Exponential View's five-gauge "boom-or-bubble" dashboard shows no red signals, two amber, and the rest green, with the verdict still "boom, not bubble."
- Trailing twelve-month AI revenues reached $126 billion as of July, continuing to rise even as public valuations cooled after a sharp semiconductor stock correction.
- AI demand for compute is outstripping supply, driving heavier infrastructure investment and pushing hyperscalers beyond cash reserves into debt and increasingly complex financing vehicles.
- Funding quality has been deteriorating since September 2025; the base case expects funding quality and economic strain gauges to turn red sometime in 2027.
- The reliance on debt, leases, and guarantees to fund the buildout is framed as rational "gaming of the system" as long as revenue keeps compounding — but these financing structures are described as brittle if growth slows.

## Quote

> This "gaming of the system" is not only rational; it is necessary, as long as revenue is compounding.
