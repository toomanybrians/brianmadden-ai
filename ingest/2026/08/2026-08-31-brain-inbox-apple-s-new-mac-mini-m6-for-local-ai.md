# Apple’s new Mac mini M6 for Local AI

## Insights

- Apple's M6 Mac mini starts at $899 and supports up to 32GB of unified memory, positioned as hardware capable of running AI models locally rather than via cloud APIs.
- Local open-weight models have reached sizes that fit consumer hardware: Qwen3.8-27B (~18GB), Qwen3-Coder 30B (~19GB), Gemma 4 12B (~7.6GB), and OpenAI's gpt-oss-20B (~14GB).
- The pitch for local AI is avoiding both round-trips to remote data centers and per-token metering costs for routine/small tasks.
- The author flags a caveat: the base $899 configuration is reportedly not the right one to buy for AI use (reasoning not included in the excerpt).
- Framing suggests a shift in perception — "running your own AI" moving from niche/lab experiment toward a mainstream desktop-computing capability.
