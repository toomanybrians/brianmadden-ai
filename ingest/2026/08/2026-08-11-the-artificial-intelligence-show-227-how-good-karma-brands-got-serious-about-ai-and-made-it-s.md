# #227: How Good Karma Brands Got Serious About AI and Made It Stick

## Insights

- Good Karma Brands achieved 65% daily AI usage within roughly a year of launch, driven by top-down urgency rather than a slow, bottom-up rollout.
- The rollout followed a deliberate cadence: a 50-person pilot, then a structured "seven Tuesdays" training program before company-wide deployment of ChatGPT Enterprise.
- The company layered in additional tools (Claude, Codex) beyond the initial ChatGPT Enterprise rollout as use cases matured.
- A dedicated innovation team/role emerged as key infrastructure — turning specific three-hour tasks into ten-minute ones and freeing up partner-level time, suggesting internal automation-building capacity matters as much as tool access.
- Reported gains of 10–15 hours/week saved per teammate on certain workflows, attributed to sustained training and internal culture change (e.g., recurring newsletter, reinforcement) rather than a one-time tool rollout.
- Framed adoption success as requiring both a permissive/experimental culture and organizational urgency — access to tools alone wasn't sufficient to move usage from shallow to deep.

## Quote

> Less than a year later, 65% of the company uses AI daily.
