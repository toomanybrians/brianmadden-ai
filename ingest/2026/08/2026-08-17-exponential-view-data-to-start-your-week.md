# 📈 Data to start your week

## Insights

- AI economy revenue growth continues accelerating: July figures were 3x higher year-over-year, with annualized run-rate now exceeding $210 billion.
- Enterprise AI usage is highly concentrated: the top 10% of companies using OpenAI's products consume 8.3x more tokens than a typical firm, suggesting a widening gap between AI power-users and laggards.
- There are early signs of a ceiling on demand for top-tier models — usage of a newer/higher-end model ("Fable 5") among businesses has plateaued, representing just 6% of total token usage and 11% of spend, indicating businesses may be capping their willingness to pay for frontier-level model performance.
- Together these signals point to bifurcation in enterprise AI adoption: intensifying usage among leading adopters alongside apparent budget/utility limits for the most expensive models.
