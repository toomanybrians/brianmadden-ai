# 📈 Data to start your week

## Insights

- Open-weight AI model usage is catching up to closed-weight models: open-weight inference tokens have doubled over the past 12 months, approaching a 1:1 ratio with closed-weight tokens — though closed-weight token volume still grew sevenfold in the same period, meaning total usage of closed models is expanding faster in absolute terms even as open models gain share.
- Youth employment in AI-exposed occupations continues to decline relative to trend: workers aged 22–25 in these roles are now 19% below trend, up from 15% the prior year, suggesting a widening and accelerating gap rather than a one-time shock.
- AI agents have overtaken humans in raw token consumption: as of February, agents used more tokens than humans, and agent token usage has since grown 14x versus only 2.8x growth in human token usage — indicating agent-driven compute demand is scaling far faster than human-driven usage.
