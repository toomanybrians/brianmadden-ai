# 🐉 China Strikes Back With Two Open Models

## Insights

- Two Chinese labs (Z.ai and Alibaba's Qwen team) released open-weight models the same day — GLM-5.3-Flash and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next — both priced around $0.15/million input tokens yet scoring within a few points of closed flagship models costing many times more.
- GLM-5.3-Flash is a 320B-parameter MoE (18B active), MIT-licensed, natively multimodal, and reportedly approaches Claude Opus 4.8 on coding/agentic benchmarks at one-tenth the price of its predecessor.
- Qwen3.8-Flash-Next previews a new Qwen4 architecture, activating only 6B of 125B parameters per token; Alibaba claims ~1/9 the training cost of its prior model and 8.6x prefill throughput at million-token context.
- Closed flagship models still lead on the hardest reasoning benchmarks (e.g., Humanity's Last Exam), but the gap in "price per point of intelligence" is closing fast, which the piece frames as pressuring closed labs' margins and startups' build-vs-buy decisions.
- Nvidia agreed to buy Hugging Face for $12.9B (~80x forward revenue), described as a hedge to keep demand tied to its hardware as OpenAI and Anthropic pursue their own chips — positioning Nvidia as owner of "neutral ground" in open-source AI infrastructure.
- Separately noted: Anthropic opened a privacy-preserving research tool (Anthropic Insights) letting outside researchers study ~250,000 real Claude conversations; early finding is that over half involve "consequential tasks" affecting others or hard to undo.
