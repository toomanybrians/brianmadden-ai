# Big Tech Defied Tradition With a Risky Bet on Trump. It’s Not Looking Like a Winner.

## Insights

- Opposition to AI has become a live midterm campaign issue, with Republican-aligned candidates (e.g., Texas Governor Abbott, Florida's Byron Donalds) now playing defense specifically on data center construction amid public souring on the buildout.
- Multiple competitive 2026 races are being framed around tech/AI infrastructure backlash: Ohio's Senate race involves attacks on a candidate for being "too supportive of data center construction," and Florida's race brands the GOP candidate "Data Center Donalds."
- The Anthropic IPO S-1 has drawn sharply divided expert reactions — NYU's Aswath Damodaran values the company near $1.475 trillion (close to its $1.5-2T range), while investor Roger McNamee calls the filing "speculative fiction" and a dangerous bet for investors.
- A brief market note: big tech earnings this week were mixed, with Google and Amazon shares dropping while Microsoft's rose, and a passing mention that "China's AI bubble starts to deflate."
- Tech and VC leaders (Musk, Andreessen, Horowitz, Brockman, and others) have made unprecedented direct financial bets on Trump and Republican campaigns, a break from Silicon Valley's historical bipartisan hedging strategy.
