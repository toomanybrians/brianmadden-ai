## Insights

- Gary Marcus argues the bull case for Anthropic's IPO rests on an assumption of continued exponential revenue growth, which he calls the "trillion-pound baby fallacy" — the idea that few exponential growth curves sustain indefinitely.
- He claims Anthropic's revenue growth already decelerated after Q2, which he attributes to the end of "tokenmaxxing," increased competition from Chinese models, and customers becoming more cost-conscious about token spend now that they have more options.
- Marcus singles out Dario Amodei's promises about AI's impact on medicine as "absolutely absurd" and unlikely to be delivered, comparing their likelihood to being hit by a meteor.
- He frames this as both an investment critique and a moral argument, explicitly urging people not to buy Anthropic stock "for the sake of humanity," though the specific moral reasoning is behind a paywall.
- The piece positions itself against a specific bull-case chart/argument reportedly made by Brad Gerstner in October, which projected continued exponential revenue growth for Anthropic.
