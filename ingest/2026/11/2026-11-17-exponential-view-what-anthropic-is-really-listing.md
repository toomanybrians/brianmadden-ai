# 🔮 What Anthropic is really listing

## Insights

- Frames the bear case on Anthropic (falling token costs, open-weight competition eroding margins) as missing the point — it treats the company as a token vendor rather than an intelligence producer.
- Argues Anthropic's real asset is "machine intelligence" capable of generating intangibles — science, information, software — which is where most economic value now sits.
- Cites a shift over the past 50 years in S&P 500 value composition from tangible assets to intangibles (brand, patents, software, data), claiming intangibles now represent ~92% of S&P 500 value.
- Suggests this positions Anthropic to compete for that 92% of value without needing physical deployment (e.g., robotics).
- Points to market reactions — stock price moves in sectors each time Anthropic announces a product encroaching on them — as evidence AI firms could extract a "permanent tithe" on the broader economy.
- Outlines (incompletely, piece cuts off) three ways Anthropic captures intangible value, the first being charging companies to defend their assets, citing Anthropic's "Mythos" as a security-oriented AI tool tied to AI-exposed software security weaknesses.

## Quote

> AI companies might be able to extract a permanent tithe on the economy.
