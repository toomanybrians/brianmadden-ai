# the machine is not taking credit for the work done by the human

it is the tech

## Insights

- The author argues that AI systems themselves are not responsible for obscuring human contributions to a given work or output.
- Instead, the blame is placed on industry figures/executives ("tech bros") who downplay or ignore the human labor behind AI-assisted or AI-generated work.
- The distinction drawn is one of incentive: AI models have no stake in self-promotion, whereas humans profiting from AI narratives have financial and reputational motives (stock hype, marketing narratives) to obscure human contribution.
- The post frames this as a human/business-incentive problem rather than a technological limitation or behavior of the AI itself.

## Quote

> the machine doesn't have a narrative to sell, or a stock to hype; the humans do.
