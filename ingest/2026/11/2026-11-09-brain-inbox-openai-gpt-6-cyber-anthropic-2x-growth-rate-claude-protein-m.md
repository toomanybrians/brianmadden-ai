# OpenAI GPT-6 Cyber 🛡️, Anthropic 2x Growth Rate 📈, Claude Protein Map

## Insights

- OpenAI released GPT-6 Cyber, a specialized (non-chatbot) model for offensive/defensive security work, accessed only via a vetted program (Daybreak, red/blue tiers); it found 81 high-severity Firefox vulnerabilities and reproduced 948 working exploits from 1,724 Linux kernel bugs, while staying in-scope 99%+ of the time.
- METR (an independent evaluator) spent 11 weeks inside Anthropic and found its models are improving at roughly 2x the historical rate (range 1.3x–3x), with ~80% of that speedup attributed to Anthropic using its own AI internally for research rather than more compute/data.
- METR found no clear bottlenecks to this self-accelerating improvement loop, though the most critical research steps are still not fully automated; the full report was heavily redacted by Anthropic before release.
- Anthropic used Claude to help solve a 40-year biology mystery around "vault" protein structures — searching 15 billion predicted proteins, identifying vault-forming relatives in bacteria for the first time, and cutting structure-mapping time from months to days.
- The protein-mapping work included a real error: Claude generated a flawed lab protocol (wrong additive) that cost two weeks, caught only by human oversight — offered as an example of AI as a fast but imperfect research collaborator still requiring supervision.
