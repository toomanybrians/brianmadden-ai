# Elon Blew Half a Billion Dollars on Nothing

## Insights

- Voters identified AI and data centers as a top-tier political issue, with an NBC exit poll finding one in four voters cited AI/data centers among their biggest concerns.
- Post-election, several states (Texas, Iowa, Pennsylvania) elected governors opposed to unchecked data center buildout, and Texas's new governor campaigned on a data center moratorium — Bloomberg reports half of all planned U.S. data centers now face delay or cancellation.
- The piece frames public backlash as being partly about AI's effect on "the dignity of work" and people's "agency to form independent thoughts without the help of large language models," suggesting cultural anxiety about AI's encroachment on cognition and labor.
- Tech CEOs are described as shifting rhetoric in response to political pressure — Sam Altman reportedly softened his "doomsday" tone on AI following the election results.
- Google is reportedly denying schools search engine access if they restrict AI usage, raising a flagged antitrust concern about tying one product's market power to another.
- Wells Fargo is shifting liability for "rogue AI agents" with account access onto customers, an early example of enterprise risk-transfer policy for agentic AI.
