# Klarna builds its own AI Shopping Agent after ChatGPT Wallet left it out 🛍️🤖; Anthropic just raised a record $107 billion at a $1.5 trillion valuation 🤯📈

## Insights

- Klarna launched an AI assistant (17 November, alongside Q3 results) that shops, handles returns, and books restaurant tables and babysitters via WhatsApp, iMessage, and the Klarna app, building on an in-app browser that has driven $34B+ in spend since 2020.
- The launch came just 4 days after OpenAI's ChatGPT Wallet debuted with AmEx, Chase, Mastercard, and Visa — Klarna was notably excluded as a partner, which appears to be a direct trigger for Klarna building its own agent.
- Klarna's strategic bet: checkout buttons lose value once AI agents mediate payment decisions, so the company is moving to own the agent itself rather than just be a payment option within someone else's agent.
- Contrast in risk models: OpenAI's ChatGPT Wallet leaves the risk of bad purchases with merchants and issuers, while Klarna's assistant has Klarna itself carrying that risk — "pay after it arrives" — and marketing that risk ownership as the trust feature.
- Framed as resolving two prior arguments: that agent-economy money flows to whoever owns user intent or completes the transaction (Klarna is pursuing both), and that large banks are locking out AI agents to protect deposits while Klarna, having no deposits to protect, can build an open "front door."
- Piece flags unresolved threads for a fuller read: a discrepancy between Klarna's two accounts of how purchases get paid, an EU credit rule taking effect November 20, and a WhatsApp policy risk that could cut off Klarna's primary distribution channel.

## Quote

> Klarna is offering the opposite deal now - it carries the risk itself and sells that as the reason to use it.
