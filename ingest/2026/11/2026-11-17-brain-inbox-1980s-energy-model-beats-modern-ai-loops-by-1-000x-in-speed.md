# 1980s energy model beats modern AI loops by 1,000x in speed

- A 1985-era "energy function" model (CRAM) reportedly trains reasoning tasks 1,000x faster than modern looped reasoning models, solving Sudoku in 42ms on a single GPU — framed as evidence that newer isn't always better architecturally.

- WorkOS is marketing "Airlock," an authorization layer for AI agents built on the premise that agents currently inherit the full permissions of whoever triggered them; it adds human-in-the-loop approval (e.g., in Slack) for risky actions like refunds over a limit.
- DeepSeek opened its consumer-app tooling (web search, deep-thinking search, file/PDF parsing, URL reading, safety screening) to API developers, reducing the need to stitch together external services to build agentic apps.
