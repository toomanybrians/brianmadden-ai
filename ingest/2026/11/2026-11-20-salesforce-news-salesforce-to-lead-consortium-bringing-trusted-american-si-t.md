# Salesforce to Lead Consortium Bringing Trusted American SI to Global Markets

## Insights

- The U.S. Department of Commerce has designated Salesforce's consortium as one of 33 pre-set groups under a new "American SI Exports Program," which packages American AI/tech offerings for promotion to foreign government and business buyers via Commercial Service channels.
- Designation confers concrete government benefits beyond marketing: priority government-to-government engagement, priority export control review, and priority routing for federal financing referrals — signaling this is a formal trade/industrial policy mechanism, not just a PR label.
- Salesforce frames its "SI" (Super Intelligence) export package as a bundle of existing products: Hyperforce (cloud infra/data residency), Data 360/Zero Copy plus Informatica (governed data), Atlas Reasoning Engine with xGen and third-party LLMs plus Einstein Trust Layer/Shield (AI and security), and Agentforce (pre-built agents for service/case management/operations).
- The company positions itself around existing scale in government: it cites current operations across 30+ countries, 60 U.S. federal agencies, all 15 Cabinet departments, and all 50 U.S. states as the basis for credibility in this export push.
- Messaging emphasizes "sovereignty" and customer control over data/models/deployment even as the product is being positioned as a tool of U.S. government soft-power/export strategy — a tension between local data control claims and the program's framing as American technology leadership abroad.
- "Super Intelligence" here is used as a government/export-policy label for a packaged enterprise AI offering, not a technical claim about model capability — worth treating as branding/terminology rather than a capability signal.

## Quote

> American technology leadership grows when governments and businesses around the world put it to work with trust.
