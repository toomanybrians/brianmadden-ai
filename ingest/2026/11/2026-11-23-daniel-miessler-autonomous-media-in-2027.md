# Autonomous Media in 2027?

## Insights

- Miessler argues AI voice and AI video quality are both converging rapidly and will combine in 2027 to produce media formats he can't yet characterize, describing the outcome as "pretty strange."
- He observes fully AI-produced YouTube content already circulating at high quality, where a human supplies the ideas/script but production (voice, visuals, editing) is automated — alongside a growing volume of low-quality "slop" that will need curation/filtering services.
- Appearance-based creators (he cites OnlyFans) are reportedly already facing competition from AI-generated models/avatars, suggesting looks-based content monetization is vulnerable to synthetic alternatives.
- He anticipates individuals using avatars or self-modification (removing flaws, enhancing appearance) to improve how they present in video content, whether or not AI generates the content itself.
- His preferred personal outcome: an AI avatar of himself performing content he writes, with his actual mannerisms/voice, decoupling idea generation from production labor and enabling much higher output volume without sacrificing quality.
- He frames this as solving a longstanding bottleneck where only top-earning creators could previously afford top-tier production quality, referencing his own 2023 writing on commoditized production quality.

## Quote

> A usable clone of someone's voice now takes only a few minutes of recorded speech.
