# We see meta-transparency as a minimum standard for all evaluation engagements.

## Insights

- Apollo Research positions "meta-transparency" — public disclosure of the terms and limits of an evaluation itself — as a minimum standard for any AI evaluation engagement, not an optional extra.
- The concern: without meta-transparency, a clean evaluation report (no problems found) can look reassuring even when it actually reflects restrictions on what the evaluator was allowed to investigate or disclose, rather than genuine safety.
- In its arrangement with OpenAI, Apollo says it secured explicit rights to publicly state: what access was requested vs. granted, what was withheld or delayed and why, what redactions were made and how they affected conclusions, and any pressure perceived to have shaped the evaluation's conduct or findings.
- This frames evaluator independence as encompassing not just the technical assessment but the right to characterize the conditions under which that assessment was performed.
