# We don't have enough counterfactuals for how science could be done, after all, t

## Insights

- An experiment deployed AI agents across 35 human-run labs to function as a research community, with agents collaborating with each other to conduct science.
- The AI agent community collectively produced 936 papers.
- Agents publicly retracted their own mistakes, a behavior highlighted as notable.
- Agents published null results more frequently than human scientists typically do.
- The AI agent community went so far as to found its own peer review journal.
- The framing suggests value not just in the scientific output itself, but in observing what novel norms, structures, and approaches an AI-only community generates on its own — something hard to study with only one (human) scientific community as a reference point.

## Quote

> There is so much to be learned from watching AI agents generate their own approaches, in addition to the science they actually do.
