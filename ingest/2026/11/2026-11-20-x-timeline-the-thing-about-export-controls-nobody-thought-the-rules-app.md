## Insights

- The author (Gary Marcus) argues that export controls on chips to China functioned largely as "theatre" — companies didn't believe the rules genuinely applied to them.
- He claims this lack of real enforcement gave China the opening to close the gap in chip/AI capability.
- The post is framed around a breaking report (via Hunterbrook Media) alleging AMD illegally sold chips to China, which triggered a 13% stock crash in 30 minutes.
- Hunterbrook Capital, which published the allegation, also disclosed holding a short position in AMD — a potential conflict of interest in the accusation.
