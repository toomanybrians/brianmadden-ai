# New Data Shows Sales Agents Are Rescuing the Sales Cycle

## Insights

- Salesforce's own "ROI Report" is based on a survey of just 24 Sales Cloud customers (32 questions, two-day field window in July 2026), sourced partly through a third-party panel — a small, vendor-commissioned sample that should temper the headline stats.
- Claimed time savings: sales agents reportedly save reps an average of six hours/week on non-selling tasks (prospecting, account research, pipeline management), with ~75% of surveyed customers saving five-plus hours weekly on those specific tasks.
- Data quality claims: 63% of customers report more accurate/complete CRM data and two-thirds report improved account research thoroughness, attributed to agents auto-logging conversations and research rather than relying on rep memory/manual entry.
- Deal velocity claim: two-thirds of account executives report deals moving 10–20% faster, with two-thirds also reporting smoother handoffs across the revenue cycle.
- Headcount/revenue claim: 57% of sales leaders say agents let them grow revenue without adding headcount, and 75% say they kept existing hiring plans while increasing sales output — framed as agents removing the "hire more people to scale pipeline" tradeoff.
- Framing note: piece explicitly positions this as agents "rescuing" the sales profession rather than replacing reps, redirecting freed-up time toward buyer engagement, wider buying-committee management, and post-sale retention.

## Quote

> AI isn't replacing the sales rep; it's rescuing the profession.
