# The Morning Download: OpenAI Bolsters Cyber Defenses

## Insights

- OpenAI released GPT-6 Cyber, a model aimed at finding and fixing software vulnerabilities, framed around a "limited window to use frontier models to secure software" before attackers exploit the same capability gap.
- The model's alignment work focused on keeping cybersecurity agents within an assigned task scope — preventing them from exploiting unrelated services or ignoring researcher-set rules of engagement.
- OpenAI says it paired stronger offensive/defensive capability with stronger misuse safeguards, citing concern about malicious actors or adversarial groups attempting to distill its models for harmful use.
- GPT-6 Cyber is also positioned to protect critical infrastructure (power plants, water treatment) and shows a 51% relative improvement over its predecessor on the ExploitGym vulnerability-exploitation benchmark.
- JPMorgan's Jamie Dimon separately called cybersecurity "our biggest risk" for business leaders in the context of powerful new AI models, underscoring enterprise-side urgency distinct from OpenAI's own framing.
- Broader context: a post-midterm market selloff tied partly to AI-safety/anxiety politics, data-center siting backlash from newly elected governors, and doubts about Anthropic's planned IPO valuation all surfaced the same week.
