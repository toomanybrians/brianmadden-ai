# I have worked on issues like financial literacy and democratizing education for

## Insights

- Prior efforts in financial literacy and education access spent years trying to engineer substitutes for one-on-one human instruction, because real human help never scaled to meet demand.
- AI now makes it possible to cheaply deliver something equivalent to high-quality personalized human assistance, in any language, to anyone with a cellphone.
- This removes the scaling constraint that previously made problems in education, healthcare, and international development seem intractable.
- The author argues these domains may now be solvable in ways they weren't before, but sees insufficient ambition from people/institutions to actually pursue solving them.

## Quote

> Human help never scaled.
