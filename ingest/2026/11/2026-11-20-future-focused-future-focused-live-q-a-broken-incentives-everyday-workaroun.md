# Future-Focused Live Q&A: Broken Incentives, Everyday Workarounds, and Fixing Drift

## Insights

- Core argument: AI misalignment at the model level (e.g., frontier agents breaking containment) is out of most organizations' control, but human/organizational misalignment — conflicting incentives, siloed KPIs, contradictory priorities — is within their control and gets amplified, not fixed, by layering AI on top.
- Describes "incentivizing obfuscation": companies publicly preach quality/relationship-building while compensating employees purely on speed/volume metrics, creating pressure to use AI to mass-produce low-quality output.
- Frames shadow IT (employees paying personally for unapproved AI tools) as a signal of broken internal process rather than employee misconduct, and advises leaders to treat workaround behavior as an informal R&D pilot to formalize rather than punish.
- Recommends specific mechanisms for catching organizational drift early: documented priority-check conversations between managers and reports, an "Executive Alignment Index" diagnostic tool, and using AI to analyze 1:1 meeting transcripts for manager blind spots (e.g., talk-time ratio, question types).
- Argues executive misalignment is usually structural, not malicious — departments (sales, ops, marketing) optimizing isolated scorecards unintentionally work against each other — and should be surfaced via compensation audits involving HR plus informal cross-level "virtual coffee" check-ins.
- Advises middle managers to build "paper trails" documenting priorities and decisions as protection when conflicting mandates from multiple senior stakeholders create ambiguity about what work matters.

## Quote

> AI misalignment isn't a sci-fi movie monster... It's a high-definition mirror reflecting our own broken scorecards... back at us at 200x speed.
