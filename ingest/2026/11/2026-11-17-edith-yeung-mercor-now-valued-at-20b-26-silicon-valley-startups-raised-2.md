# Mercor Now Valued at $20B. 26 Silicon Valley Startups Raised $2.6B Last Week

## Insights

- Mercor, founded in 2023 by three teenage college dropouts as an AI recruiting platform, pivoted to AI training data and has hit a $20B valuation ($750M Series D) and ~$3B annual revenue run rate after 2x'ing its valuation in 12 months.
- Mercor's model relies on a network of 5M+ human experts (medicine, law, engineering, finance) who help AI labs train and evaluate models — positioning it as "intelligence and training infrastructure" alongside competitors Scale AI, Surge AI, and Handshake AI.
- Mirendil, founded by former Anthropic/DeepMind/xAI/OpenAI researchers, raised $1.2B in just five months (seed to Series A) to build AI agents ("Lor" platform) that coordinate hundreds of agents to automate AI research itself — writing code and improving models.
- Broader funding snapshot: 26 Silicon Valley-area startups raised $2.6B in one week, with AI infrastructure startups capturing the largest share ($12.7B across just 4 companies), far outpacing enterprise AI apps ($550.7M/5 startups) or healthcare ($160M/1 startup).
- Enterprise AI tooling funding spans agent infrastructure (LangChain at $3B valuation, agent memory/security/identity startups) and enterprise AI applications (AI sales agents, localization, website optimization, B2B pricing) — signaling fragmentation across many narrow agent-tooling niches rather than consolidation.
- M&A activity shows incumbents and AI-native firms absorbing niche AI capabilities: OpenAI acquired an AI travel agent (Odessia), Vanta acquired AI-agent runtime security (Pillar Security), and Wolters Kluwer acquired AI clinical decision support (AgileMD) for up to $100M.
