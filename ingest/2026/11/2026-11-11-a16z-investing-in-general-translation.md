# Investing in General Translation

## Insights

- Localization today requires fragmenting text across codebases, docs, marketing, and media, and coordinating internationalization libraries, translation management systems, and external agencies — a process described as slow and brittle.
- A common failure mode: localized copies drift out of sync with source content and UI components break visually when translated text changes layout.
- General Translation's pitch is "full-stack localization" — instead of extracting translatable strings out of design/code, developers integrate translation primitives directly into React apps, docs, and existing tools like GitHub, Figma, and Slack.
- The approach reframes localization from a discrete downstream step into something embedded in the normal product development cycle, aimed at removing it as a separate bottleneck.
- Customers cited (Cursor, Cognition, Ramp, Clickhouse) are framed as fast-growing companies already using the tool to scale products across languages.
- Founder background is highlighted as a mix of classical languages training and computer science, framed as suited to bridging linguistic nuance with scalable software systems.

## Quote

> We believe more companies should be able to serve global audiences earlier without having to deal with the headache of localization.
