# AI could do 1000x more research but the jagged frontier currently runs through t

## Insights

- Argues AI could enable roughly 1000x more research output, but the limiting factor ("jagged frontier") is that discerning what makes an interesting story remains a distinctly human skill.
- Draws a parallel between journalism and academic research: both face the same bottleneck of human judgment/taste determining which findings are worth pursuing or publishing.
- Frames this as a potential "golden age of investigative journalism," since LLMs can now analyze filings, records, and public data at a scale that previously required a large team of journalists.
- Suggests LLMs can roughly 10x the pace of investigative research (digging into companies, governments, etc.), even though human taste still gates which investigations get greenlit.
- Qualifies the claim about human taste as the bottleneck with "at least for now," leaving open that this may be a temporary rather than permanent constraint.

## Quote

> AI could do 1000x more research but the jagged frontier currently runs through the fact that the taste for what is an interesting story almost certainly remains human
