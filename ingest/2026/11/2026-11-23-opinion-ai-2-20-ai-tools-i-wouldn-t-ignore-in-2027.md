# 20 AI Tools I Wouldn’t Ignore in 2027

## Insights

- The piece frames the shift in AI tools as moving from "giving good answers" to reducing the manual work of using those answers — likening it to the leap from verbal directions to a map pin.
- New model releases cited as of November: OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Sol and Astra (API), Google's Gemini 4 Neon, and Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2.
- Argues the specific model name matters less than what the tool does with its output — i.e., evaluate tools by task-completion capability, not branding/version.
- Proposes a workflow example of chaining specialized tools (one to find source material, one to generate an image, one to produce a video clip) rather than relying on one all-purpose tool.
- Advises selecting tools based on the actual job at hand, suggesting most people need only a few tools (e.g., three), not a large stack of subscriptions.
- Frames a forthcoming list of 20 tools for 2027 as curated around distinct, clear-cut job functions rather than general-purpose use.
