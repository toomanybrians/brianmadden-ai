## Insights

- Draws a distinction between "vibe coding" and simply using AI to code — vibe coding specifically means having no understanding of what the AI is producing.
- Frames vibe coding as "separation from understanding," positioned as the direct opposite of engineering.
- Allows some value in vibe coding for early-stage builders experimenting or prototyping ("messing around"), rather than dismissing it entirely.
- Argues that real engineering requires developers to bring their own taste, preferences, and deep understanding of how they implement important systems.

> Vibe Coding is when you have no idea what your AI is making for you. It's separation from understanding.
