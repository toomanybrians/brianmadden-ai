# One imaginable outcome is that AI Labs, unwilling or unable (due to more careful

## Insights

- Speculates on a scenario where frontier AI labs stop releasing their most advanced models publicly, either by choice or due to tightening government oversight.
- In this scenario, labs would instead apply their most powerful "internal" models directly within their own ventures in high-value domains like finance and biotech, where frontier-level intelligence confers decisive advantage.
- This could effectively create (potentially regulated) monopolies of intelligence, with labs becoming operators in specific industries rather than just model providers.
- The framing is tied to a separate observation (from a quoted post) that frontier labs report their internal models are becoming too powerful to release, while also improving fastest on coding and math capabilities.

## Quote

> One imaginable outcome is that AI Labs... become (regulated?) monopolies of intelligence.
