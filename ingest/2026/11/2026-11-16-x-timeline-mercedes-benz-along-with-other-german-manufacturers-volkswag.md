# Mercedes-Benz, along with other German manufacturers Volkswagen, BMW and Porsche

## Insights

- German automakers (Mercedes-Benz, Volkswagen, BMW, Porsche) are cutting production costs domestically due to competitive pressure and falling demand from China.
- Mercedes has reportedly halted new investments in Germany and signaled potential factory shutdowns.
- One commentator argues legacy automakers' only viable path forward is to license Tesla's Full Self-Driving (FSD) technology or manufacture vehicles for Waymo.
- The claim frames legacy automakers as "dead men walking" against autonomous-driving competition, with FSD expected to go "unsupervised" and Tesla's Cybercab poised to scale.
- A rival autonomy approach ("Alpamayo") is acknowledged as a possible future contender, but seen as too far off to offset the near-term threat to legacy manufacturers.
