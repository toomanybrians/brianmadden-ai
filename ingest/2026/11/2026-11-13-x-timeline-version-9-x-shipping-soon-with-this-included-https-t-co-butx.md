# Version 9.x shipping soon with this included.

https://t.co/BUtXyCs3Zb

## Insights

- LifeOS is an "AI-powered Life Operating System" framework/harness designed to let a user state goals and have AI systems execute toward them, spanning coding, writing, health, and habits.
- It introduces a "Router" system that defaults to one model (Opus 5.5) but escalates to a higher-capability model (Fable) for tasks requiring more intelligence or scrutiny, such as security decisions, and can route to other models in its harness as needed — a model-routing-by-task-sensitivity approach.
- The core workflow is framed as a repeatable loop: Articulate (define done with checkable criteria), Climb (take the next step), Verify (require tool-based evidence of progress), and Learn (feed results back into the system).
- The system is built around capturing a user's persistent goals/mission/beliefs ("TELOS") once, then carrying that context into every subsequent AI task rather than re-prompting each time.
- Positioned as shifting from "prompt engineering" to "intent engineering" — conveying the *what* to an AI rather than manually specifying the *how*.
- The project is open-source, with a large list of named subsystems (memory, security graphing, dashboards, eval/judgment layers, etc.) suggesting a full modular architecture around AI task delegation and verification, not just a single tool.
