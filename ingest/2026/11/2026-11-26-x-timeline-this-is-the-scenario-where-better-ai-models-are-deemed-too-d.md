# This is the scenario where better AI models are deemed too dangerous to release

## Insights

- Posits a scenario in which frontier AI models become "too dangerous" in the eyes of government, triggering direct regulatory intervention in release decisions.
- Argues this would effectively end the era of unregulated, freely available frontier open-weight models.
- Suggests AI labs, if restricted from public release by stricter oversight, could pivot to becoming regulated monopolies of intelligence.
- Describes labs potentially applying frontier AI directly within specific high-value domains like finance and biotech, where superior intelligence confers decisive advantage.
- Frames this as a structural shift from "AI as product distributed broadly" to "AI as a controlled capability deployed selectively by its creators."

## Quote

> AI Labs...become (regulated?) monopolies of intelligence: launching their own efforts in areas like finance & biotech.
