# I know many people in the industry, have published research on it, built games m

## Insights

- Predicts that indie game studios and solo/small designers will feel pressure to publicly deny using AI tools, out of fear of fan backlash, regardless of actual practice.
- Argues larger studios and (paradoxically) solo developers will escape the same scrutiny or punishment that indie studios face for AI use.
- Claims a strict "no AI code" standard is unenforceable/impossible given that AI will be effectively embedded in all coding workflows going forward.
- Suggests the backlash dynamic disproportionately harms indie studios specifically, rather than the industry as a whole.
- Proposes reframing the fan response: instead of policing AI use, reward and highlight games that center human artists and designers.

## Quote

> Almost every indie studio and game designer is just going to lie about using AI and live in fear of their fans.
