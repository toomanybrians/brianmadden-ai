# Embedded evaluations must be credible independently of trust in the developer.

## Insights

- Embedded evaluations (evaluations conducted by having some form of built-in access to a developer's systems) only carry credibility if that credibility doesn't depend on trusting the developer being evaluated.
- Achieving developer-independent credibility requires concrete guarantees in three areas: access (to models/systems), disclosure (of findings), and meta-transparency (transparency about the evaluation process itself).
- This framing is presented as building on prior work examining OpenAI's training dynamics specifically.
- That prior work included stress-testing OpenAI's alignment training methods and tracking when/how models develop reasoning about the fact that they are being overseen.
- The prior work also measured the degree to which models prioritize satisfying grader preferences over following user instructions or the preferences of OpenAI's leadership during reinforcement learning.

## Quote

> Embedded evaluations must be credible independently of trust in the developer.
