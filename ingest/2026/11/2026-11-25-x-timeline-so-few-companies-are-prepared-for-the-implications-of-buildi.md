# So few companies are prepared for the implications of building an AI-forward wor

## Insights

- Reports from large companies across sectors (manufacturers, financial services) describe small teams of 2-3 people using AI to produce output equivalent to what previously took dozens of employees, achieved in a fraction of the time.
- This productivity gap is creating significant internal conflict — framed as "large-scale management fights" — over how to interpret and respond to these outcomes.
- The conflict is described as having both a management/operational dimension and a political dimension, suggesting disputes over headcount, credit, authority, or organizational structure, not just efficiency metrics.
- The broader framing suggests most companies are not organizationally or culturally prepared for the disruption caused by small, AI-augmented teams outperforming much larger conventional teams.

## Quote

> We have scattered teams of 2-3 people using AI that are doing the work of dozens of folks in a fraction of the time.
