# RT @markgurman: As Apple develops its own computer-use agent, it’s making it har

## Insights

- Apple is reportedly developing its own computer-use AI agent for macOS, a category currently led by third-party tools like ChatGPT's agent mode.
- Apple has made it significantly harder to enable third-party computer-use agents (e.g., ChatGPT) on Macs: in macOS 27.3, the setup process grew from 4 steps to 14.
- Apple's official justification for the added friction is security.
- The framing suggests a platform-level dynamic where the OS owner can disadvantage competing AI agents relative to its own in-development offering, prompting accusations of anti-competitive behavior from some observers.

## Quote

> As Apple develops its own computer-use agent, it's making it harder for rival agents to control Macs.
