# Open-source AI without Jensen

## Insights

- Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's resignation removes the open-source AI ecosystem's primary institutional protector; Nvidia has financial motive to keep supporting open models (to prevent customer concentration/chip competition) but lacks Jensen's personal willingness to intervene and "stop a lot of shit from going wrong."
- The open model economy's optimistic case rests on "ownership of intelligence" and enterprise post-training as a viable lane; the pessimistic case is that true commoditization of open intelligence remains far off while compute stays scarce and inference margins stay high (cites DeepSeek's DeepInfer release as evidence open models aren't yet priced as commodities).
- Closed labs (Anthropic, OpenAI) benefit from superior economies of scale and vertical integration (model-to-datacenter-to-chip), giving them a structural advantage over open-weight competitors in the current compute-constrained environment.
- Per Dylan Patel (SemiAnalysis), the US open-model gap isn't a funding problem but a talent problem — top talent clusters at Anthropic/OpenAI/DeepMind, leaving open efforts (Nemotron, Thinking Machines) under-resourced versus Chinese labs (Moonshot/Kimi, DeepSeek) that match skill despite compute/deployment deficits.
- A major risk is regulatory: rising political appetite (especially Democratic state/national efforts) for AI "safety" regulation driven by people unfamiliar with open-model tradeoffs; author warns one high-profile Chinese open-weight-enabled hack could trigger a regulatory crisis long in the making.
- Media dynamics are described as driven by timing relative to political cycles rather than new information — the Jensen story and an earlier "Jacob Coxon" safety-culture story are cited as cases where long-known facts became news only when politically opportune.
- The piece concludes the open ecosystem needs a new champion willing to intervene proactively (potential candidates: Zuckerberg, Andy Jassy, Satya Nadella) since no single actor has stepped up to replace Huang's role.
