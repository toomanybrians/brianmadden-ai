# Why is Marvell Paying $115B for Lumentum? 19 Silicon Valley Startups Raised $16.2B Last Week

## Insights

- Marvell's $115B all-stock acquisition of Lumentum combines semiconductor chip processing with optical components, aiming to speed data movement between GPUs, servers, and data centers — positioning the combined company against Broadcom, NVIDIA, Cisco, Coherent, and Chinese optical transceiver makers.
- OpenAI reportedly raised $11B at a $1.25T valuation (first close of a round that could reach $20B), up sharply from $852B in March, while Anthropic's planned IPO valuation is reportedly being discussed at $1T-$1.3T, down from earlier $1.5T-$2T targets.
- fal.ai, founded by two Turkish founders in 2021, raised a $1.3B Series F at $16B (2x its April valuation) and has surpassed $800M in annualized revenue, underscoring strong demand for generative media model infrastructure.
- In one week, 19 Silicon Valley-area startups raised $16.2B total, with AI infrastructure capturing the largest share ($12.7B across 4 startups), followed by drone technology ($2.7B across 2 startups).
- The same week saw 15 M&A exits across AI-adjacent categories: voice AI (Salesforce/Cresta, $2.7B), AI observability (CrowdStrike/Fiddler AI, $400M), AI storage (Dropbox/Tigris Data, $350M), and several smaller agentic-AI and coding-tool acquisitions (Meta/Conductor, OpenAI/Pierre, Sierra/Anon, Factory/HumanLayer).
- Other notable raises include Zipline ($1.5B, autonomous drone delivery) and Skydio ($1.2B, autonomous drones for public safety), alongside niche enterprise AI tools like Larridin (AI value measurement across human and agent workforces) and Nooks (AI revenue agent platform for sales teams).
