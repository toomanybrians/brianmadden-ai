# AI agents can do more. That also means they can access more. The real enterprise

## Insights

- Citrix frames the core enterprise problem with agentic AI as an access/security tradeoff: more capable agents necessarily require broader access to enterprise systems, data, and tools, which raises governance stakes.
- SecurSpaces is positioned as a "secure execution layer" — an isolated, centrally governed environment where both human employees and autonomous AI agents interact with corporate apps, data, and models, rather than each team building its own agent infrastructure.
- The product has expanded from a developer-focused secure coding environment to covering three personas: developers (parallelized AI coding agents), AI engineers (connecting private models/data/tools), and knowledge workers (corporate agents automating business processes).
- Specific technical controls are emphasized as differentiators: restricted filesystem access, credential protection that keeps session tokens out of the agent's execution environment, and centralized visibility into agent activity, AI token consumption, and security events.
- A collaboration is underway to let managed Claude agent workflows use SecurSpaces as a private execution environment, with autonomous code migration/modernization cited as a concrete long-running agentic workload example.
- Deployment is offered flexibly (self-hosted, cloud-based "Flex," and a free Community Edition) to let organizations move from experimentation to enterprise-wide agentic AI adoption at their own pace, alongside release of an open-source "Knowledge Worker Agent."

## Quote

> AI agents can do more. That also means they can access more.
