# I was watching the new Apple HomeView reviews and seeing that they hallucinate a

## Insights

- Observed that Apple's new HomeView (Siri-based smart home interface) hallucinates and misreads what's happening in the physical environment it's monitoring.
- Characterizes this failure as "bitter lessonable" — i.e., a case where a hand-built, rules/expert-system-style approach underperforms compared to what general-purpose learned models can do.
- Contrasts this with claims about current frontier AI models, which can reportedly reason about a household's state, flag anomalies or safety/maintenance issues, and propose solutions.
- Notes that frontier models already have fairly strong spatial understanding derived from image inputs, which supports this kind of environmental reasoning.
- Frames the gap as evidence that Apple's approach to building an "expert system" for the home is architecturally mismatched with where capability actually comes from (general model scale/reasoning vs. bespoke engineered logic).
