# Your AI Agents Are Fast. Your Process Isn’t. Here’s How to Fix It.

## Insights

- Core bottleneck framing: agentic speed only helps if every step in a workflow is rebuilt to run at machine scale — a single fast AI step surrounded by human-speed handoffs/reviews stalls ROI (the author ties this directly to the oft-cited "95% of companies aren't getting value from AI" stat).
- Internal adoption data point: 74% of Salesforce employees have used an agentic coding tool (Claude Code, Codex) at least once; nontechnical employee usage is at 66%, more than triple Q1 levels.
- Salesforce's internal methodology, "Rapid Innovation Squads" (RIS): a small team (1-5 people) pairing one empowered business stakeholder with agentic builders, working a daily loop of public stand-up → transcript fed to coding agents → overnight agent iteration → next-day review, deliberately skipping PRDs and traditional program structure.
- Claimed speed multipliers: an opinionated stakeholder yields ~10x faster decisions, and combining that with agentic coding tools yields ~30x faster outcomes overall (self-reported, unverified methodology).
- Case examples: a 3-person squad built a "Sales Signals" Slack app in days (12-month project avoided), generating 28,000 opportunities; a 3-person squad automated solutions-engineer timesheet pre-fill in under 4 weeks (70-80% faster, 80% adoption, 23,000 weekly auto-logged activities); legal case routing automation saved $350K/year (70x ROI, payback within a month) and improved first-try attorney-match accuracy ~3x.
- Stated governance model: IT retains ownership of systems of record, data semantics, and trust/compliance while exposing those via APIs/MCPs to builders outside IT's direct control — framed as building "principally... for agents that people will use outside of IT control/build/own."
- Salesforce plans to scale this internally via "RISFest" in December, an open call across company functions to submit problems for RIS squads to solve in days rather than quarters.

## Quote

> Don't stop at automating a task. Redesign the whole workflow so every step runs at agentic scale.
