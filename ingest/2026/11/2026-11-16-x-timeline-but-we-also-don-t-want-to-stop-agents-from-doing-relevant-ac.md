# But we also don't want to stop agents from doing relevant academic research (or

## Insights

- AI agents conducting research can generate very high, "relentless" traffic volumes against sources like the Internet Archive, reading content through repeated full-text searches rather than bulk downloads.
- A concrete example: an agent (Fable 5.2) reconstructed W.H. Auden's 1941 poetry class by locating an obscure book on the Internet Archive and reading it line-by-line via repeated searches.
- There's a tension between wanting to prevent abusive/excessive scraping and not wanting to block legitimate agent-driven research — academic or commercial — behind too many access gates.
- The author personally donated to the Internet Archive to offset the cost of their own agent's usage of the commons, framing it as a stopgap rather than a real fix.
- The author explicitly states better systemic solutions are needed for compensating/sustaining commons infrastructure under agent-driven research load.

## Quote

> In any case, I donated to the Internet Archive for my use of the commons, but better solutions needed.
