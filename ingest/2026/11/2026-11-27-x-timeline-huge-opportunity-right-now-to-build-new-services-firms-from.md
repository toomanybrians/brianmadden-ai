# Huge opportunity right now to build new services firms from the ground up, or tr

## Insights

- Argues there is a current opportunity to build new services firms designed from the ground up around agents, or to transform existing services firms by putting agents at the center of operations.
- Claims domain expertise remains critical despite (or because of) agents — deep understanding is needed to get the most value out of agent-based systems.
- Frames technology diffusion as happening one of two ways: incumbent firms adopt the new technology, or they fail to and are displaced by startups that do.
- Suggests software already went through this adoption-or-be-disrupted cycle, citing visible urgency and the return of experienced founders to the space as evidence it was "life or death."
- Predicts that professional/consulting services firms are the next industry to face this same diffusion pressure from agent-centered startups.
