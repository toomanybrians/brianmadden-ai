# I think you’re just not getting the right people. 1000x builders are out there.

## Insights

- Claims highly productive "1000x" builders using AI exist in meaningful numbers, but most prefer working independently over being employed.
- Frames the scarcity problem in hiring not as a lack of elite AI-augmented talent, but as a mismatch between where that talent wants to work (own ventures vs. someone else's company).
- Cites a quoted claim that founders coding with AI are 10-100x more productive than hired developers using AI on the same tasks.
- Implies a structural issue for companies trying to hire for AI-augmented engineering roles: the most capable people self-select out of traditional employment.

## Quote

> The hard part is finding one who wants to work for someone else.
