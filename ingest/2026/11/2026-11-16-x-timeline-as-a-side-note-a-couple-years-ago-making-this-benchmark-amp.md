# As a side note, a couple years ago, making this benchmark &amp; analysis would’v

## Insights

- A new benchmark tracker ("SatBench") measures how quickly major AI benchmarks become saturated (effectively beaten by models); of 173 benchmarks cited by AI labs since 2023, 63% are now saturated.
- The rate of benchmark saturation is increasing year over year, suggesting model capability is outpacing the field's ability to measure it.
- The author notes that producing this kind of benchmark analysis would previously have required significant manual statistical work (days in STATA), plus extra time for polished presentation/visualization.
- This entire analysis (data work plus formatting) was instead produced with roughly four prompts to an AI tool called "Fable 5.2," illustrating a large compression in analyst time for this type of quantitative research task.

## Quote

> AI is getting better faster than our measurements are
