# Claude Sheets, or how Anthropic plans to turn Microsoft Office into a backend 🤖📊; Monzo goes from £10B takeover talks to a £7.5B funding round in just six weeks 😳📉

## Insights

- Anthropic launched Claude Sheets, Whiteboards, and Claude Data (beta/GA) deliberately scoped below Excel/Power BI/Miro capability — mirroring its earlier Personal Finance feature, which also shipped intentionally limited versus ChatGPT's version.
- The strategy targets where work *starts* rather than feature parity: Anthropic wants documents, boards, sheets, and dashboards to persist inside Claude so "Office becomes the backend," since inside Microsoft's own products Claude is just one interchangeable model option (90% of Copilot queries run in auto-select mode).
- Anthropic's business model pressure is explicit: ~80% of its revenue is usage-based and it holds ~$498B in noncancelable compute commitments, so it needs products that keep consuming tokens continuously (live dashboards, shared boards) rather than one-off queries.
- Shared/live artifacts (a dashboard or financial plan viewable by colleagues without their own data-connector access) are the mechanism for spreading usage from one user to many within an org.
- Microsoft's Office revenue growth increasingly comes from per-seat price increases and premium tiers (Copilot, E5/E7, E3 price hikes) rather than new seats (only 5% seat growth last quarter) — making it vulnerable if teams do core work in Claude and merely export files to keep their Office seat.
- Author flags a key metric to watch: whether Microsoft's revenue-per-user growth slows while seat counts hold, which would indicate Claude is capturing the "AI premium" Microsoft has priced into Office.
