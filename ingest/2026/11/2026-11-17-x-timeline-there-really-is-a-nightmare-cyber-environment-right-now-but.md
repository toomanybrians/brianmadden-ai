# There really is a nightmare cyber environment right now, but also people are pay

## Insights

- Current computing/cyber infrastructure is described as largely improvised and prone to cascading failures, independent of any AI involvement — citing Microsoft and FAA outages as pre-AI examples of large-scale disruption.
- Argues two things are simultaneously true: the cyber threat environment is genuinely severe right now, and public perception of outages is amplified because people are now more attentive and vocal about incidents on social media.
- Notes that offensive AI-driven cyberattacks are now a real added factor, but counterbalanced by the emergence of defensive AI systems.
- Suggests that dramatic social-media narratives about outages may overstate novelty — similar-scale incidents have long occurred due to systemic fragility, not just new AI threats.

## Quote

> You didn't need offensive AI cyberattacks to have big outages like Microsoft/FAA.
