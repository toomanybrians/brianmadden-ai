# What could possibly go wrong? Nationwide FAA Ground Stop edition

## Insights

- Marcus frames a nationwide FAA ground stop and a major Microsoft outage (both occurring the same week) as possible instances of the AI-related failures he's been warning about since 2022 — though he explicitly notes he doesn't know whether either incident is actually tied to AI, malicious cyberattack, or generative-AI/LLM failure modes specifically.
- He references having warned in 2022 about "sticking generative AI" into critical systems, and recalls being told at the time that no one would be "stupid enough" to do so — implying that integration has since happened anyway.
- He also cites a January 2025 Politico piece where he warned about malicious cyberattacks, distinguishing that threat vector from the LLM-failure threat vector he raised in 2022.
- The broader claim is cultural/structural rather than technical: infrastructure fragility is attributed to "the arrogance of Silicon Valley" combined with "the ineffectiveness of Washington" in regulating or safeguarding critical systems.
- No technical evidence, root-cause analysis, or sourcing is provided connecting the outages to AI specifically — the piece is speculative commentary/reaction rather than an investigated claim.
