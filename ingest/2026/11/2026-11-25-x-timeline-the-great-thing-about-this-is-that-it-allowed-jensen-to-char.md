# The great thing about this is that it allowed Jensen to charge American companie

## Insights

- Argues that allowing Nvidia (Jensen) to sell AI chips to China let the company charge American companies higher prices, since those China sales worsened a chip shortage in the US market.
- Claims this same chip-export policy simultaneously strengthened Chinese AI competitors' technical quality, which in turn cut into American companies' profits.
- Frames this as a case where a single policy decision produced a lose-lose outcome for US companies — paying more while also losing competitive ground.
- Quoted post (Romney) raises the broader contradiction: the US fast-tracked AI development citing a race against China, while also selling China the chips needed to compete in that race.
