# Nice to see a successor to GDPval to evaluate AI work but really needs human bas

## Insights

- A new benchmark, GDPx, has launched as a successor to GDPval for evaluating AI agentic work across high-value domains, but it lacks human performance baselines for comparison, which the commenter sees as a significant gap.
- Even frontier models score low on GDPx, with the best model achieving only 12% (1/7).
- GPT-6 Pro was used to identify errors in the benchmark's own grading process, suggesting quality-control issues in how GDPx scores are assessed.
- The commenter argues that reporting "0%" scores obscures real differences in success rates between models that may be economically significant even at low absolute performance levels.
- Despite these critiques, the commenter still regards GDPx as a valuable benchmark for measuring AI capability.
