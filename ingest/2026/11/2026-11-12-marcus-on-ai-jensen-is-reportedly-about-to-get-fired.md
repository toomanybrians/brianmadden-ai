# Jensen is reportedly about to get fired

## Insights

- Reports (via WSJ, as cited by Marcus) suggest Nvidia's board may be preparing to remove Jensen Huang as CEO, with Marcus framing this as following "further evidence of poor judgement" on Huang's part.
- Marcus argues Nvidia may feel compelled to act regardless of preference, citing potential legal liability, reputational damage, and risk of investigative scrutiny if the board doesn't remove him.
- The piece frames Huang as central to what it calls "insane circular financing" across the AI industry — implying his departure could destabilize that financing structure, not just Nvidia's stock price.
- A specific near-term question raised: whether Nvidia (potentially under an interim CEO, speculated to be the CFO) would still honor billions in committed funding to Anthropic's IPO — and what happens if that commitment is paused or reversed.
- Note this is speculative/early reporting-based commentary, not confirmed fact — Marcus is extrapolating fairly aggressive downstream consequences ("all hell may break loose") from an unconfirmed personnel move.

## Quote

> The bigger issue for the AI world at large is that Jensen is the ringmaster of all the insane circular financing.
