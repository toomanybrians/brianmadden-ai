# The model excels in both robotic control and spatial awareness:
🔘 It matches our

## Insights

- Google DeepMind introduced a robotics-focused model (referenced alongside "Gemini 4 Hydrogen") paired with a new robotics plugin for Google Antigravity, aimed at teaching robots new tasks via natural conversation rather than traditional programming.
- The workflow described: connect a supported robotic arm, practice/train in MuJoCo simulation, then execute tasks in the real world.
- On performance, the model reportedly matches a larger "Argon" model on standard robotic control tasks like pick-and-place, but at lower computational/operational cost.
- The model is claimed to outperform "Gemini Robotics ER 2" specifically on spatial reasoning tasks — judging 3D space, camera angles, and movement.
- The framing suggests a push toward making robot task training more accessible and cheaper, decoupling capability gains from needing the largest/most expensive model.
