# In a proof of concept with @Apptronik and @MercedesBenz Gemini Robotic 2.5 helpe

## Insights

- Google DeepMind, Apptronik, and Mercedes-Benz ran a proof-of-concept where Gemini Robotic 2.5 directed the Apollo 2 humanoid robot to pick and organize car parts for assembly.
- The system achieved 96% accuracy on this automotive parts-handling task.
- DeepMind frames this as an advancement in both breadth of tasks and dexterity, claiming it approaches human-level finesse.
- Additional dexterity benchmarks cited: tying a bow (manipulating deformable objects), pipetting liquids (handling delicate items), and serving food with tongs (advanced tool use).
- These examples are positioned as tests of distinct manipulation skills — softness/deformability, precision/fragility, and tool-mediated action — rather than a single narrow capability.

## Quote

> The result is a significant advancement in both task breadth and dexterity - approaching human-level finesse.
