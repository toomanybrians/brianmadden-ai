# Also the models are getting stranger and more divergent at the frontier. And no

## Insights

- Frontier AI models are becoming more divergent and "stranger" in behavior/capability rather than converging on a single trajectory of improvement.
- Standard benchmarks are losing their usefulness for comparing or evaluating frontier models.
- Model naming and versioning conventions no longer reliably signal capability level — names are more confusing (e.g., "neon" vs "carbon") and version numbers don't track model families or progress (e.g., a point-release can diverge unpredictably from its base version).
- Despite this confusion, a practical heuristic still works for most users: pick the latest model from OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google and set it to the second-highest available thinking/reasoning level.

## Quote

> Model names and numbers have never been less useful in determining ability levels.
