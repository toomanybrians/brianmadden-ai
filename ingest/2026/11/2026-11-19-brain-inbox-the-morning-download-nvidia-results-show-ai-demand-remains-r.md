# The Morning Download: Nvidia Results Show AI Demand Remains Robust

## Insights

- Nvidia raised its fiscal 2028 revenue growth forecast from 70% to 75%, reporting $115.8B quarterly revenue (beating estimates by 6.3%) and $65.3B net income, with data-center revenue at $107.6B, up 110% year-over-year, driven by new Vera Rubin servers.
- This growth acceleration nearly four years post-ChatGPT is framed as evidence that large-enterprise AI investment commitment remains strong at the CEO level, even though some studies suggest only a minority of non-tech companies are seeing significant AI leverage.
- A wave of major system outages (Microsoft, FAA, banking, United Airlines, NSA, Apple) is linked to AI accelerating the discovery of software vulnerabilities — Microsoft's monthly patch count jumped from ~170 flaws a year ago to ~1,400 last month, forcing faster, more automated patching that itself introduces new failure risk.
- AI is cited as a double-edged factor in cybersecurity: it helps defenders find bugs faster but also arms attackers, contributing to what's described as a "pressure cooker" of accelerated vulnerability discovery and patching.
- The piece frames the outages less as a technology failure story and more as a leadership-communication failure story: officials' slow, non-specific explanations after the FAA ground stop let public speculation (including viral "cyberattack" theories) fill the information vacuum.
- Nvidia is also facing an SEC investigation into whether its disclosures misled investors, with the specific focus not yet disclosed.
