# AI Score for Business Leaders (ExecAI Insider #95)

## Insights

- SmarterX launched AI Score in public beta: a free assessment tool with separate tracks for organizations (Business AI Score) and individuals (Personal AI Score), designed to benchmark AI maturity via plain-language self-rated statements, producing an overall score plus pillar-level grades.
- The tool is positioned as a change-management instrument, not just a diagnostic — it's meant to surface perception/experience gaps across roles and departments, build "blueprints" of priority actions, and serve as ongoing KPIs for tracking AI transformation progress over time.
- Roadmap items previewed include Custom Blueprints and integrating Personal AI Score directly into business accounts, suggesting a move toward unifying individual and organizational assessment data.
- United Airlines announced it is restricting/prohibiting third-party personal AI agents from accessing its booking and account systems, citing security risks and potential FAA/DOT regulatory violations — an early example of an enterprise pushing back against uncontrolled agentic AI access.
- HubSpot's co-founders (returning CEO Brian Halligan, CTO Dharmesh Shah) announced a strategic shift toward a "headless" HubSpot, making more product capability consumable by AI agents/harnesses (Claude, ChatGPT) via MCP, reflecting a broader enterprise-software pivot toward agent-accessible infrastructure.
- Within one week, Anthropic (ahead of a planned IPO) and Google both shipped new frontier model releases, with Google's Gemini 4 Carbon explicitly marketed around "long-horizon agentic workflows across complex knowledge work, software engineering, and scientific reasoning."
