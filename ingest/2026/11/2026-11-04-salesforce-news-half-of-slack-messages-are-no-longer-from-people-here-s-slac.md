# Half of Slack Messages Are No Longer From People. Here’s Slack by the Numbers.

## Insights

- Slack reports 50% of messages now come from apps/agents rather than humans, framed as evidence of a shift from Slack-as-chat to Slack-as-execution-layer for human-agent collaboration.
- Via MCP (Model Context Protocol), agents reportedly perform 127 million "tool calls" per week in Slack — automated actions pulling context from and acting across platforms like GitHub, Salesforce, and Jira — with that volume said to be doubling quarter over quarter.
- Anthropic is cited as shipping 65% of its code changes through Slack, and Vercel claims to run its entire company (engineering through go-to-market) inside Slack channels, including an inbound sales agent handling work previously requiring ~10 reps.
- Salesforce claims frontier AI company spending across its products grew 435% year-over-year, and that 9 of the top 10 frontier AI companies run operations on Slack — positioning Slack as infrastructure for AI-native companies specifically.
- Internally, Salesforce cites high self-adoption: 83% of employees are weekly Slackbot users and 90% of sellers use Slackbot to update Salesforce records, alongside a customer claim of 312% ROI (methodology unstated).
- Slack is positioning itself for a "multiplayer era of AI" where agents and humans co-occupy the same channels, with a new "Slack Code" product/partner track and a "Slack Frontline" offering for deskless workers mentioned as adjacent expansions.
