# Your logs say the app launched. They don’t say your team spent the afternoon ret

## Insights

- Standard IT logs capture that an application launched but not what a user actually did inside a session, leaving a blind spot for both incident investigation and process understanding.
- Citrix is rebranding/evolving "Session Recording" into "Session Insights," with a new "Flex" tier in preview for Citrix DaaS that analyzes session content rather than just recording it.
- The product frames manual, repetitive work (e.g., copying data between two applications, maintaining a shared spreadsheet) as invisible to process maps because it's only known through employee interviews, which yield inconsistent accounts.
- Positions session-level visibility as the basis for identifying which workflows are "ready to automate," based on observed behavior rather than self-reported process descriptions.
- Visibility is selective and governed: organizations choose which users/groups/personas to monitor and can toggle it on/off (e.g., turning it on for finance only during an audit), with customer-managed encryption keys for control.
- Roadmap includes optional AI-assisted analysis to help review long recordings efficiently (planned Q1 2027 preview) and future AI-driven automated compliance alerting using customer-chosen models.

## Quote

> Session context turns an incident into evidence.
