# As robots move into more real-world spaces, they need greater safety measures. W

## Insights

- Gemini Robotics 2.5 is Google DeepMind's new vision-language-action model aimed at expanding robot dexterity, reliability, and safety for unstructured, real-world environments beyond lab settings.
- In a production-line pilot with Apptronik's Apollo 2 humanoid and Mercedes-Benz, the model enabled autonomous "kitting" (sorting parts into trays for assembly) with a 96% success rate across factories in Hungary and Germany.
- Benchmarked across 200 distinct tasks (e.g., using tongs, tying bows, pipetting liquid), the model shows 30-100% higher success rates and 20-30% faster completion than prior baselines.
- New safety mechanisms include inference-time "reasoning guidance" to steer robots away from hazards in real time, improving safe task completion by 33% around obstacles and 43% near people.
- Google released ASIMOV-Jailbreak, an open-source benchmark testing robot resistance to disguised unsafe prompts; their newest model (Gemini 4 Argon) scored 99.5% jailbreak resistance vs. 82% for the prior baseline.
- Gemini 4 Argon also functions as a simulation/research accelerator — generating synthetic training scenarios and digital twins from real camera footage, which sped up development of the robotics model itself.
- Framing positions this work as progress toward "physical AGI," with robots intended to take over repetitive/monotonous physical tasks so human workers can focus on more complex work.
