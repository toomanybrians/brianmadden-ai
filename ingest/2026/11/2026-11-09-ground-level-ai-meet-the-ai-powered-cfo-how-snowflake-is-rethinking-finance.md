# Meet the AI-powered CFO: How Snowflake is rethinking finance

## Insights

- Snowflake's CFO uses a "Good Morning CFO" routine where AI agents pull from roughly 40 data sources, analyze business performance, and draft emails for his review each morning.
- Snowflake is using AI agents to accelerate financial close, preparing preliminary quarterly numbers for the board within hours of quarter-end, moving toward what's described as a "zero-day close."
- The CFO argues traditional ROI measurement frameworks don't work well for AI investments, and that the discipline needs to rethink how it evaluates returns on AI spending.
- A recurring theme: applying AI effectively requires redesigning work itself, not simply automating existing tasks within unchanged workflows.
- Finance poses distinct constraints for AI deployment — accuracy, auditability, and access to sensitive data are treated as nonnegotiable, which shapes where agents can act autonomously versus where humans must retain final sign-off.
- Snowflake reports achieving near-100% AI adoption within its finance organization, and the company is explicitly prioritizing adoption over near-term ROI as a strategy.
- The conversation frames the next 12 months as potentially more transformative for finance work than the prior 30 years, with future-state ambitions including AI agents operating continuously, including while humans sleep.
