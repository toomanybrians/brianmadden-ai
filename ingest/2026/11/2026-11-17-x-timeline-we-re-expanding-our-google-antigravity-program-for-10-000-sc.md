# We’re expanding our Google @Antigravity program for 10,000 scientists to include

## Insights

- Google released Gemini 4 Carbon, a frontier model positioned above Gemini 4 Argon for the hardest tasks (complex multi-step coding, open-ended scientific research), using more tokens to reason and run extended verification loops.
- Carbon is being used for formal software verification: an "AlphaProof Code" agent framework used it to mathematically prove security properties of Signal's cryptographic protocol in weeks, a task described as normally taking expert teams years.
- In scientific research contexts, Carbon diagnosed subtle wet-lab errors (e.g., incomplete mixing, sample carryover) directly from lab notebooks/raw data and increased retrieval of protein homologs 40-fold on low-data protein families, aiding structure prediction without retraining.
- Google is expanding a free-access research program (originally Argon-only) to include Carbon for 10,000 scientists via "Google Antigravity," with thousands already onboarded, explicitly framed as accelerating scientific research.
- A new multi-agent "/swarm" feature in Antigravity coordinates teams of Carbon agents on complex projects (e.g., 3D reconstructions, game development, financial/legal document analysis), with Carbon assigned to high-reasoning roles like architecture and auditing while lighter models handle other tasks.
- On cybersecurity benchmarks, Carbon showed large safety/robustness gains over Argon (e.g., 0% prompt injection attack success on one benchmark, lower breach rates under adaptive red-teaming), alongside improved honesty/alignment scores on agentic tasks.
