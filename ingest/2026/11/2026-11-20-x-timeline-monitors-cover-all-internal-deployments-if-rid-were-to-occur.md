# Monitors cover all internal deployments

## Insights

- A proposed framework for evaluating AI safety monitoring consists of six specific claims: full coverage of internal deployments, reliable detection of "rogue internal deployment" (RID) incidents, monitor trustworthiness, appropriate disclosure of detected incidents, a clean track record (no missed attempts), and confidence that monitoring will hold going forward.
- The framing treats AI monitors as the primary safeguard against risks from AI agents operating within a company's own internal deployments (as opposed to external/customer-facing deployments).
- The source advocates for third-party "embedded evaluators" — external assessors with insider access — to independently verify frontier AI developers' monitoring infrastructure, rather than relying on self-assessment.
- The six claims function as an auditable checklist, implying monitoring efficacy should be verifiable incident-by-incident and claim-by-claim rather than taken as a general assurance.

## Quote

> AI monitors are the main safeguard against threats from agents during internal deployment.
