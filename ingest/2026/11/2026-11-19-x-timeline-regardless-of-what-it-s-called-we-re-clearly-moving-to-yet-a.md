# Regardless of what it’s called, we’re clearly moving to yet another era of AI ag

## Insights

- Levie argues AI agent capability is evolving quickly: from single-shot answer generation, to agents orchestrated across multi-step tasks, to agents now capable of "fanning out" and working in parallel.
- The quoted timeline frames a yearly progression of AI-for-coding paradigms: autocomplete (2022) → chat (2023) → AI IDE (2024) → coding agents (2025) → agent orchestration (2026).
- The quoted post predicts 2027 will shift focus to "outcome engineering" — implying the unit of work shifts from managing agent tasks/orchestration to defining and engineering desired outcomes.
- The framing suggests naming/labeling of the current era ("agents," "orchestration") matters less than the underlying trajectory: increasing agent autonomy and parallelism in actual work execution.
