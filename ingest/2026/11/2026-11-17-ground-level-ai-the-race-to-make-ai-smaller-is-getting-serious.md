# The race to make AI smaller is getting serious

## Insights

- Liquid AI argues the industry's focus on ever-larger models misses a parallel opportunity: smaller, specialized "decision models" that handle everyday tasks faster and cheaper than LLM-style reasoning requires.
- Always-on personal AI agents that continuously monitor and act face a cost problem; on-device processing and smaller decision models are positioned as a way to make that economically viable.
- Smaller, efficient AI isn't framed as a replacement for data centers — Hasani expects large-scale compute demand to keep growing, just with some tasks shifting off the cloud.
- Liquid AI frames handing personal data to cloud-based AI as a loss of control over one's own decisions, tying on-device AI to privacy and autonomy concerns rather than just efficiency.
- The company is already embedding its models in Mercedes-Benz vehicles and states an explicit goal of running on half of all newly manufactured devices (cars, phones, robots) by 2029.
