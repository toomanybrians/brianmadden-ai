# Coding gives you a window into what the future will look like for a large amount

## Insights

- Coding is framed as an early indicator/preview of how AI will transform knowledge work more broadly, since it's one of the domains where AI tools are most advanced.
- The predicted trajectory: workers shift from personally doing research, writing/typing, and value creation (even with digital tools) to coordinating work that AI performs.
- This shift is described as "the first step" — implying coordination is an intermediate stage, not necessarily the endpoint.
- A quoted perspective (Nate Silver) adds that working directly with AI coding tools gives a "front-row seat" to how fast AI capabilities are advancing, describing the moment as disorienting ("insane") precisely because of that visibility.
- Notes a duality: AI coding tools are simultaneously highly useful for business operations and a source of unease about the pace of capability growth.

## Quote

> Coding gives you a window into what the future will look like for a large amount of knowledge work. — @levie
