# The AI News Cycle is Relentless. We Can Help.

## Insights

- Reported high/critical CVEs across 21 major vendors/open-source projects have climbed sharply since spring 2026, coinciding with Anthropic's Claude Mythos cyber capabilities release; AI-assisted researchers found critical PHP flaws (affecting ~70% of websites) that attackers exploited before patches were public.
- Open-weight models (e.g., Kimi K3.1) are approaching closed-frontier cyber capabilities; unlike Kimi, other open models aren't deliberately detuned, raising risk of anonymous malicious use and shifting the attacker-defender dynamic toward a "cat-and-cat" game.
- Anthropic's S-1 filing shows revenue up 1300% YoY (H1 2026) but $132B cash against ~$900B in obligations through 2045 ($70B due within a year); seeking IPO valuation of $1.26–1.41 trillion. Only ~27% of compute served paying customers; the rest went to training, research, internal use, and free tier.
- Anthropic's S-1 explicitly warns that as models advance, the company may lose ability to detect misalignment, potentially deploying models whose behavior can't be adequately verified.
- A new benchmark (TasteVal) shows AI models rapidly improving at choosing which research experiments to prioritize — a judgment/"taste" skill often assumed to require human experience — with performance curves steepening since early 2026.
- In the US midterms, 40 of 41 local ballot measures on data centers were decided against development, often by lopsided margins, and anti-data-center candidates won office despite heavy industry PAC spending — signaling public backlash against AI infrastructure's physical/local impacts.
- Goldman Sachs data shows hiring slowing and unemployment rising in AI-exposed sectors beyond tech; Microsoft offered voluntary buyouts to 27% of its workforce; Anthropic's internal hiring shows shrinking junior-employee share and AI agents logging 6x the hours of human employees, with many human roles shifting to managing agent teams.

## Quote

> If we become less able to detect or assess misalignment as our models advance, we may deploy models whose behavior we cannot adequately verify.
