# ⚙️ Adobe turns rival AI into a reason to stay

## Insights

- Anthropic launched a suite of workplace tools (Meetings, Whiteboards, Sheets, Data) plus expanded interactive connectors, extending beyond existing Docs/Slides/Design/Motion tools that have reportedly produced 45M+ docs, decks, and designs.
- Claude Meetings joins video calls as a visible participant (like Otter/Fireflies) but goes further by acting on meeting content directly — building whiteboard timelines or pulling connected-app data, not just summarizing.
- The strategic bet is eliminating context switching: because Claude already holds organizational context and user trust/familiarity, companies may adopt Claude-native tools (e.g., Claude Sheets) even over entrenched incumbents like Excel, since work can still export to standard formats.
- Adobe's Creative Cloud update (Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere, After Effects) adds agents (Creative Agent, Always-on agents) and new editing tools, but the most significant piece is local MCP integration — letting users bring any third-party AI model (e.g., ChatGPT) to edit native project files while preserving editability of layers/vectors/sequences.
- This MCP move is framed as reducing lock-in to any single AI assistant: users don't have to re-explain brand/style context to a new tool just because they're in Adobe's ecosystem.
- Separately, UC Berkeley/Nvidia research on "agentic robotics" uses LLM agents to write/test robot control code on the fly (rather than relying solely on data-trained vision-language-action models), aiming to break past an observed ~80% reliability plateau in data-driven robotics approaches.

> "For years, we've learned to work around software. Now Claude works around you." — Ami Vora, Anthropic CPO
