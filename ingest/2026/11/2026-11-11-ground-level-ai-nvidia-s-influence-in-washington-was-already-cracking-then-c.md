# Nvidia's influence in Washington was already cracking. Then came the spy scandal

- Reporting describes Nvidia as having helped shape Trump administration AI chip export policy, with Jensen Huang characterized as having become a significant Washington power broker.

- New restrictions on AI chip exports to China are described as gaining political momentum, independent of the scandal.
