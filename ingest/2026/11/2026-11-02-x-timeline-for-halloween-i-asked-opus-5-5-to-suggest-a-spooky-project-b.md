## Insights

- An AI model (Opus 5.5) was prompted to independently generate a creative project idea based on the user's prior work, and produced "The Ghost Census" — a data visualization estimating historical deaths near any location.
- The resulting project demonstrates AI's capacity to go from a casual prompt to a fully realized, methodologically rigorous data product: it synthesizes multiple academic datasets (HYDE population grids, historic city population estimates, life-table death rates, battle/epidemic tolls, historical border maps) into a single interactive tool.
- The project runs probabilistic modeling (1,500 simulation runs per query, varying death rates, population, and event tolls) to produce confidence ranges rather than point estimates — an example of AI-assisted tools embedding statistical rigor and uncertainty quantification into a public-facing product.
- The tool is transparent about its modeling assumptions and limitations (e.g., "modelled averages, not records of individuals"), suggesting a design pattern for AI-generated analytical tools: show methodology and caveats alongside outputs.
- This is a case study in rapid, low-effort creation of a polished, data-rich interactive product — built seemingly as a one-off novelty exercise rather than a sustained work project — indicating a drop in the effort threshold for producing professional-grade analytical/creative tools.
