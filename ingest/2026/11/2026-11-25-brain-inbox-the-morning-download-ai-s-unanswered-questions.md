# The Morning Download: AI’s Unanswered Questions

## Insights

- Jensen Huang's reinstatement as Nvidia CEO hasn't resolved underlying uncertainty; analysts note it exposes "deeper questions around corporate governance, leadership succession, and regulatory oversight" with lasting competitive implications.
- Nvidia is tightening financing/allocation standards for "neoclouds" (specialized cloud providers renting its chips to AI labs), straining what one analyst calls AI's "circular financing chain" — exposing unfinanced neocloud builders most.
- White House involvement in Huang's return was explicit: officials reportedly saw Huang as a U.S. champion of open-weight AI models (cheaper, more customizable) seen as strategically important in the AI race against China.
- Huang faces continued multi-front scrutiny: Nvidia board probe (Wachtell Lipton) into alleged sharing of sensitive info with a former Megaspeed executive, an SEC disclosure investigation, closed-door House interviews (Dec 1) and Senate Banking testimony (Dec 3), a DOJ/Commerce criminal probe into Megaspeed's China ties, and a California AG subpoena.
- Commerce Department moved to add Megaspeed to the Entity List, barring it from buying AI chips/advanced tech without a government license — signaling tightening export-control enforcement around AI hardware supply chains.
- Enterprise AI infrastructure demand remains strong independent of the Nvidia turmoil: HPE raised its outlook on data-center/AI product demand (Cloud & AI unit revenue up 69% to $11.3B); Amazon paid ~$7.5B for AI startup Decart AI's team and cross-hardware model-execution software; Cisco and OpenAI launched an open-source tool (Shirudo) to secure agentic AI systems without requiring reboots during patching.
