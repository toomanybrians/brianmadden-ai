# Between the realities of this number relative to negligible profits and today’s

## Insights

- Anthropic has disclosed $895 billion in future AI infrastructure spending commitments, combining purchase commitments and leases for computing power and related infrastructure.
- Roughly 50% of that figure ($448 billion) is expected to be incurred by the end of 2030.
- Gary Marcus argues this spending level, set against what he characterizes as negligible profits, undermines the case for a $2 trillion valuation at Anthropic's IPO.
- Marcus connects this to a broader market correction occurring at the same time, suggesting the combination threatens the IPO's prospects.
- He predicts a delay to the IPO is likely, at minimum, given these conditions.

## Quote

> $2T as a valuation is probably off the table. A delay seems likely, at a minimum.
