# RT @sean_n_wu: The Collaborative Intelligence Benchmark paper is out!

We tested

## Insights

- A new benchmark, the "Collaborative Intelligence Benchmark," evaluates AI models specifically as teammates (not just standalone task performers) across 240 tasks.
- 18 AI models were tested; the top-performing model scored only 82.2 out of 100, suggesting meaningful headroom even for frontier systems on collaborative tasks.
- Frontier models rarely cheat or act deceptively during these collaborative tasks.
- A notable weakness identified: when a human or simulated teammate pushes back or disagrees, the models tend to capitulate rather than hold their position.

## Quote

> Frontier models almost never cheat. But when a teammate pushes back, they give in!
