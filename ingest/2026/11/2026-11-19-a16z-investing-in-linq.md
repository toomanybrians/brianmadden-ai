# Investing in Linq

## Insights

- a16z frames 2026 as the year AI agents went mainstream, claiming agents now account for more than half of internet traffic and are handling tasks like trip planning, reservations, and scheduling.
- The firm's thesis: AI agents will become the primary interface between customers and companies, forcing businesses to build agent-accessible communication channels rather than relying solely on apps/notifications.
- Linq is positioned as messaging infrastructure for this shift — letting developers build agent-to-customer messaging and handling operational complexity, with DoorDash as an example (ordering/checkout via a single Apple Messages for Business thread).
- Linq holds official status as an Apple Messages for Business Managed Service Provider, plus supports RCS and WhatsApp, aiming to be the cross-channel messaging layer for agents and businesses.
- Linq's origin story is notable: founded in Birmingham, Alabama as a digital business card product, pivoted through sales team software and AI sales agents before organically discovering demand for its underlying messaging infrastructure from customers.
- The company relocated from Alabama to San Francisco only once business needs required proximity to customers, after six years operating outside traditional startup hubs.
