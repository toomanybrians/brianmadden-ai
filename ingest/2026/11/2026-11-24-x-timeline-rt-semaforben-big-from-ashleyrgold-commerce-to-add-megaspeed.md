# RT @semaforben: Big from @ashleyrgold: Commerce to add Megaspeed to trade blackl

## Insights

- The US Commerce Department added Singapore-based Megaspeed and several affiliates (MSFusion, Speedmatrix, Apex Enterprise Solutions, Swiftdata) to its trade entity list, barring them from obtaining US semiconductors without a license, with licenses presumed denied.
- The action follows the Nvidia scandal involving CEO Jensen Huang's relationship with Alice Huang, a Megaspeed executive suspected of being a Chinese spy; the same day, Nvidia's board reinstated Huang as CEO at Trump's urging.
- The Justice Department is investigating whether Megaspeed illegally shipped Nvidia chips to China, circumventing export controls.
- Treasury, the Pentagon, State Department, and USTR reportedly pushed for Megaspeed's blacklisting "for months," while Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick held sole authority ("holds the pen") and faced bipartisan pressure to act.
- The listing extends beyond US-origin products: it also bans export of advanced AI chips to Megaspeed even if manufactured outside the US, closing a potential loophole.
- Technology already in transit to the targeted companies is allowed to complete shipment under the new rule.

## Quote

> Commerce will continue to vigorously enforce export controls, including these new Entity Listings.
