# Introducing Emergent: The Founder-Generated Startup List

## Insights

- Newcomer and Felicis crowdsourced a list of 29 seed-stage (or earlier) AI startups by having established AI founders — many past Cerebral Valley Summit speakers — nominate peers they see as having "generational" potential.
- The largest cluster of nominated companies builds tooling to run, monitor, and control AI agents — including agent security/discovery (Arrakis Security), agent payments (Agentcard), agent action verification/audit trails (Mnemom), and agent evaluation/reliability infrastructure (Convergent, Ampersand).
- Open-source/open-weight models and on-device (local) inference recur as a theme, framed as a way to cut costs and enable AI to run locally on phones and laptops rather than in the cloud (e.g., Mirai Labs, Hercules, Phylo).
- AI-native "services and support" companies — AI systems replacing traditional service businesses (event planning, maritime logistics engineering, power infrastructure engineering, film production) — are described as a major investor theme this year.
- Hardware and physical-world applications are called out as "hot," spanning brain-computer interfaces (Alljoined), robotics for space labor (Icarus Robotics), and general-purpose robot intelligence (Enigma).
- Several nominated companies target enterprise/knowledge-work functions directly: a "foundation model for predictions" powering business decisions (Conway), a model that builds a living operational model of a business to rearchitect workflows and evaluate agents (Ampersand), and an AI tool capturing how users and agents interact with products via chat interfaces (Armature).
