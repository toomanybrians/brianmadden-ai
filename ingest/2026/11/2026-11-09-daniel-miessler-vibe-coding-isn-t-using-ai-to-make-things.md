# Vibe Coding Isn't Using AI to Make Things

## Insights

- Miessler argues "Vibe Coding" is specifically defined by lack of understanding of how the built thing works internally — not by the mere use of AI to generate code.
- He traces the term to Andrej Karpathy (February 2025), who described it as code that "grows beyond my usual comprehension."
- He frames engineering as the direct opposite of vibe coding: full comprehension of how everything works, regardless of how much AI assistance was used.
- Per this framing, using AI to write 100% of code while shipping many features/products per month can still count as engineering, if the builder understands the system.
- Conversely, shipping a working product without knowing the stack, language, or security implementation qualifies as vibe coding, even with minimal AI involvement implied otherwise.
- The core distinction he draws is the builder's level of understanding and intentionality behind decisions, not the AI/non-AI nature of the work itself.

## Quote

> Vibe Coding is making things where you have no idea how it works on the backend.
