# Personal Finance in Claude: The Complete Beginner’s Guide

## Insights

- Anthropic has launched "Personal Finance in Claude," a feature connecting Claude directly to users' bank and credit card accounts.
- The tool lets users ask natural-language questions about spending, savings, bills, debt, and unusual charges without needing to understand banking terminology or do manual calculations.
- In Anthropic's own demo, Claude identified $4,309.37 in monthly spending (including $1,024.51 on food and drinks) and estimated $1,393.22 remaining before payday after upcoming payments.
- The feature is framed as valuable for catching things humans tend to miss — e.g., small recurring charges that silently persist unnoticed, unlike memorable large purchases.
- A key value-add noted is conversational follow-through: one question (e.g., "why did this bill increase") naturally leads to deeper investigative questions (duplicate payments, savings opportunities from habit changes).
- The author frames the real skill as learning how to ask Claude the right questions to get useful financial insight, positioning this as a new literacy.
