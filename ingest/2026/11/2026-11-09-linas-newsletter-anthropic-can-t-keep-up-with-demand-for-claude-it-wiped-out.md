# Anthropic can’t keep up with demand for Claude, & it wiped out its profit right before its $1.5T IPO 📉; Stripe now lets AI Agents spend your money while you sleep, on stables merchants never see 🪙

## Insights

- Anthropic's amended S-1 shows Q3 2026 revenue of ~$17B but a swing from $974M operating profit to a $567M loss, driven by compute costs rising to 58% of revenue (up from 48% in Q2) — compute grew 77% while revenue grew only 47%.
- About $5B of Anthropic's Q3 compute spend was short-term capacity (pricier than long-term contracts), and roughly half of that ties to a SpaceX compute deal worth up to $120.5B that either party can exit with 90 days' notice starting early 2027 — Bloomberg reports Anthropic was ~$5B of SpaceX's Q3 revenue.
- Anthropic's total compute commitments now total $895.4B, with $69.8B due in the next 12 months — roughly equal to its annualized Q3 revenue — raising questions about whether a $1.5T IPO valuation holds if compute costs keep climbing toward/above 60% of revenue.
- Stripe's new "Link Balance" feature lets AI agents spend a user's prefunded money autonomously (no per-purchase approval) below a set limit — rolling out first to consumers via the Instinct agent, ahead of giving equivalent autonomous-spend permissions to businesses.
- Under the hood, Link Balance converts user dollars into a stablecoin (via Stripe's Crypto Onramp, held in a Privy wallet on Tempo) and spends via a Stripe Issuing virtual card, so stablecoins are invisible to both consumers and merchants, who just see an ordinary card transaction.
- Liability for agent-driven purchases remains unresolved: Stripe's terms shift responsibility toward users, and existing card dispute processes assume a human clicked "buy" — a gap not fixed by spending caps, only bounded by them.
- Agentic purchase volume through Stripe's Link grew 38x in a month, with over 25% of one merchant's (Muse) weekly users already shopping via an AI agent before autonomous spending was even enabled.

## Quote

> Stripe gave that power to consumers before it gave it to businesses.
