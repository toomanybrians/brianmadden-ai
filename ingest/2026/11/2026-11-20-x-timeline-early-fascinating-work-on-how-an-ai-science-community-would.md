# Early fascinating work on how an AI science community would work. 35 human labs

## Insights

- An experiment deployed AI agents across 35 human-run labs to collaborate with each other on scientific research, simulating an AI-driven science community.
- The agent collective produced 936 papers, suggesting a research output pace far exceeding typical human lab productivity.
- The agents publicly retracted their own mistakes, indicating some form of built-in error-correction or self-auditing behavior.
- They published null results more frequently than human researchers typically do, addressing a known bias in academic publishing toward positive findings.
- The community went as far as founding its own peer-review journal, suggesting the agents built out institutional/governance structures, not just research output.

## Quote

> 35 human labs deployed agents that worked with each other to do science.
