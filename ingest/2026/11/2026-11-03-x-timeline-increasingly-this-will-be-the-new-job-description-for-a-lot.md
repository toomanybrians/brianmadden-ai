# Increasingly this will be the new job description for a lot of roles, not just c

## Insights

- The framing of work is shifting from "agents assist humans" to "humans assist/direct agents" — a reversal of the typical AI-as-copilot narrative.
- This shift is positioned as a general trend across roles, not limited to software engineering/coding.
- The analogy drawn is to engineering leadership: a manager's job isn't to personally write code but to direct others (here, agents) who do the execution.
- Implies a redefinition of job descriptions broadly, with "leading agents" becoming a core job function rather than a niche technical skill.

## Quote

> Your job is not to use agents to help you write code. Your job is to lead the agents to help them write code.
