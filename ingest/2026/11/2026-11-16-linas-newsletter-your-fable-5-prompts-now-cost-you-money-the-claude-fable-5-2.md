# Your Fable 5 Prompts Now Cost You Money: The Claude Fable 5.2 Playbook 🤖

## Insights

- Claude Fable 5.2 (released Nov 9, 2026) reached #1 on both the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and the Vals Index within a day, and built a full game from scratch (models, sounds, textures) in one-shot with no human feedback for roughly $550 in API costs over 8 hours.
- Pricing is $8/million input tokens and $40/million output tokens — 20% cheaper than Fable 5.1 but twice the cost of Claude Opus 5.5 — and free inclusion on Pro/standard Team seats ends November 16, 2026, after which usage bills to credits.
- Anthropic's updated prompting guidance says several previously-recommended instructions (including one from the author's own June guide) now increase cost, suppress routine work, or make the model less willing to flag its own errors — meaning prompts optimized for earlier models actively hurt performance/cost on 5.2.
- Anthropic recommends defaulting to Opus 5.5 for most coding and knowledge work, reserving Fable 5.2 for the most ambitious, open-ended technical tasks; on the Remote Labor Index (freelance-style projects) Fable 5.2 scored 41.3% vs Opus 5.5's 21.3%.
- The model has five selectable "effort levels," with cost swings observed as dramatic as $24 vs. $642 for the same prompt depending on effort setting.
- Model capacity: 1M-token context window, up to 128K output tokens; a parallel "Mythos 5.2" variant exists with fewer safeguards for vetted organizations.
