# You can even test ideas before touching hardware.

Upload a photo or prompt to b

## Insights

- Google DeepMind's Gemini Robotics model ("Gemini 4 Hydrogen") supports a workflow where users upload a photo or text prompt to generate a 3D simulation scene in MuJoCo before deploying to physical hardware.
- Objects and movements within the simulated scene can be adjusted via chat interface, then deployed directly to a robotic arm.
- The claimed workflow takes a whiteboard sketch to a functioning conveyor sorter in approximately one hour.
- On everyday robotic control tasks like pick-and-place, the model reportedly matches the performance of DeepMind's larger "Argon" model, but at lower cost.
- The model is claimed to outperform "Gemini Robotics ER 2" specifically on spatial reasoning tasks: judging 3D spaces, camera angles, and movement.
