# Q3.5 Timelines Update: Looks Like the Intelligence Explosion Will Happen in 2027 After All

## Insights

- AI Futures Project revised its forecasts sharply after a METR report estimated Anthropic's AI R&D progress is now accelerating ~2x historical rate, with ~80% of that acceleration attributable to AI itself (implying a 1.7x AI-attributable speedup) — far higher than their models predicted as of three months earlier.
- All contributors now forecast "Superhuman AI Researcher" (SAR) — the point where a company would prefer an all-AI research team over the best human team — arriving sometime in 2027, with a 35-65% chance that AI capable of automating all white-collar work arrives before 2028.
- The piece stresses that a 1.7x acceleration in *overall AI progress* is a much bigger deal than a 1.7x productivity boost to individual researchers, since overall progress also depends on compute and is subject to diminishing returns — self-reported labor uplift at Anthropic was ~12x, but that doesn't translate directly into 12x overall progress.
- Multiple independent modeling approaches (linear extrapolation of research taste, labor-uplift extrapolation, automation-level extrapolation, toy models) converge on SAR dates ranging from mid-2027 to mid-2028, with a filtered version of their main model giving a median SAR date of February–March 2027.
- Key uncertainty: Anthropic redacted "a majority of the evidence" behind METR's conclusions, and it's unclear whether recent acceleration reflects genuine automation of AI research (coding + "research taste") versus progress confined to well-specified, easily-gradable tasks that may not generalize to messier real-world R&D work.
- The authors argue that given these timelines, pausing or substantially slowing frontier AI development is "incredibly urgent," since companies are not on track to align a superhuman AI researcher before potentially using it for recursive self-improvement.

## Quote

> This level of acceleration due to AI is very unexpected.
