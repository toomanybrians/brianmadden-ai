# jobs have barely moved; productivity has barely changed.

and yet we just listen

## Insights

- Marcus asserts that despite heavy AI hype, actual job numbers and productivity statistics have shown little movement so far.
- He criticizes the practice of accepting CEO projections about AI's economic impact at face value, characterizing them as unsubstantiated ("pull numbers out of a hat").
- The quoted tweet describes Anthropic's CEO forecasting 10% annual economic growth alongside 20% of people being jobless.
- When Anderson Cooper restated this scenario back to him in an interview, the CEO affirmed it ("That's exactly what I was getting to").
- The CEO is also reported to have said AI could eliminate half of entry-level white-collar jobs.

## Quote

> jobs have barely moved; productivity has barely changed. and yet we just listen to CEOs who pull numbers out of a hat.
