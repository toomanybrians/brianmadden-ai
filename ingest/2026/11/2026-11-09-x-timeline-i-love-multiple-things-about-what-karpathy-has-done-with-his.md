# I love multiple things about what @karpathy has done with his last couple of pos

## Insights

- Karpathy built "TristanTube," a custom video-browsing app for a specific person's curated list of ~47K niche videos, including a "TV mode" for passive viewing.
- The app was "one-shotted" with Claude Code in roughly 3 minutes, built in direct response to seeing someone else's list of favorite YouTube channels.
- Miessler frames the significance not as the app itself but as the category: custom software that didn't exist before, built purely because one person wanted it to exist.
- He notes that reactions dismissing it as "he just built it for himself" miss the point — personal, bespoke software-on-demand is presented as the actual value, not a limitation.

## Quote

> It's custom software that didn't exist before... It's something that HE wanted to exist
