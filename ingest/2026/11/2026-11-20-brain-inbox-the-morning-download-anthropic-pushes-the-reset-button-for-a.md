# The Morning Download: Anthropic Pushes the Reset Button for AI

## Insights

- Anthropic's IPO disclosed $900.9 billion in aggregate compute commitments (with $70.7 billion due within 12 months), giving investors a public-filing-based demand signal for AI infrastructure spending rather than inferred capex guidance.
- Analysts frame the central unresolved question as "margin against growth" — whether AI labs' profitability will eventually justify current spending levels.
- Nvidia's $500 billion pledge (with six Wall Street firms) to help finance customers' chip purchases remains undeployed; its >$100 billion investment portfolio is concentrated in companies that in turn buy its chips (including a committed $10 billion stake in Anthropic's IPO), raising circularity concerns.
- Since Oct. 6, the eight publicly traded companies in Nvidia's investment portfolio lost a combined $820 billion in market value, signaling investor unease even amid strong demand data.
- Enterprise adoption signals are showing up in earnings: Walmart's e-commerce (+17%) and ad business (+37%) grew sharply; Alibaba's AI cloud and e-commerce profitability tripled net profit; Lenovo's AI-server-driven data-center revenue jumped 48%; industrial robot orders rose 25% amid factory labor shortages.
- At a CMO Council summit, marketing leaders described AI restructuring their function: OpenAI's Colin Fleming proposed a "20-60-20" workflow where AI handles 60% of execution but human judgment remains critical in framing (20%) and refining (20%); Visa's Frank Cooper III described marketing shifting toward "B to AI," with product catalogs and brand websites regaining importance as AI shopping agents become customers.
