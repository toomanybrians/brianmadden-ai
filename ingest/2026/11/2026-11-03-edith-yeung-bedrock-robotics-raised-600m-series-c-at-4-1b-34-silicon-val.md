# Bedrock Robotics Raised $600M Series C at $4.1B. 34 Silicon Valley Startups Raised $2.56B Last Week

## Insights

- Bedrock Robotics raised a $600M Series C at $4.1B (total funding near $1B), retrofitting existing heavy equipment like excavators with hardware/AI to make them autonomous — betting that "the most valuable robot brains sit inside machines contractors already own" rather than requiring new purpose-built robots.
- Sebastian Thrun's Dulo raised $130M seed to build foundation models for hardware/engineering design, framed as "Engineering Superintelligence" — applying AI directly to the physical design process, not just software.
- Weekly Silicon Valley funding snapshot: 34 startups raised $2.56B total, with "Enterprise" the largest category ($902M across 14 startups), followed by Construction ($617M, concentrated almost entirely in Bedrock) and BioTech ($352M).
- Within enterprise AI funding, a cluster of startups targets AI-agent infrastructure specifically: agent security (Upwind, Infisical, Northstar), agent identity/management (Mintive), agent training environments (Plato), and agents building internal apps (Reflex) — suggesting the "AI agent" category is fragmenting into specialized tooling layers.
- Notable M&A: Anthropic acquired Mithril Medicine (rare muscular dystrophy therapies) — an AI lab acquiring a biotech/therapeutics company, and Salesforce acquired Rune Technologies, an AI logistics software firm for military sustainment, for $375M.
- Other acquisitions show AI-native companies consolidating adjacent tooling: Cognition (AI coding agents) acquired Corridor for security controls on coding agents; Harvey (legal AI) acquired an AI sales execution platform (Aida).

## Quote

> Bedrock believes that the most valuable robot brains sit inside machines contractors already own.
