# Investing in First Person Labs

## Insights

- The piece frames personal AI agents as requiring a "total understanding" of the user — an ongoing, cross-application model of relationships, priorities, habits, and intentions.
- Whoever controls that personal context model has significant leverage over what a person's agents actually do — framed as a power/control problem, not just a technical one.
- a16z is backing First Person Labs and its product "Second," which builds a continuously updated personal model designed to be portable across tools/agents rather than locked into one vendor.
- Architectural choices are presented as the mechanism for user control: the engine runs on-device by default, the model is inspectable and portable, and users choose which intelligence powers it and which apps/agents can access it.
- Founders Ken Liu (Stanford/Google DeepMind, AI and data privacy/model memory) and Erik Chi (Michigan, network security/privacy/censorship resistance) previously built the Open Anonymity Project for more private AI access.
- The underlying thesis: as personal context becomes "an increasingly valuable part of your digital life," infrastructure that keeps that data user-controlled and portable becomes a distinct investable category.

## Quote

> Whoever controls that model will have considerable influence over what your agents do for you.
