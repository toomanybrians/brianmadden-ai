# How AI agents are rewriting the rules of enterprise software

## Insights

- Individual-level AI productivity gains don't automatically translate into organizational results — without coordination, security, and governance, local efficiency can undermine broader business goals.
- AI agents require business context (relationships between projects, people, resources, budgets), not just raw intelligence, or they risk automating workflows without improving actual decisions.
- Enterprise CIOs are shifting from open-ended experimentation to demanding measurable outcomes, stronger governance, and willingness to kill underperforming AI projects.
- Singh argues enterprise software won't disappear but its role changes — AI becomes the interface layer, while contextual intelligence underneath becomes the real competitive differentiator.
- Software vendors lacking valuable proprietary contextual data may face consolidation or revenue decline as customers offload simpler tasks directly to AI models.
- The conversation frames the current AI shift as comparable in magnitude to the earlier transition to cloud computing.

## Quote

> business context, governance and accountability will be essential to making AI agents effective
