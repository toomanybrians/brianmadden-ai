# Breaking: Anthropic stock is crashing, and AI stocks have lost over 50% in 5 months.

## Insights

- Anthropic's IPO is framed as a flop: opened at 111, closed day one at 97, and premarket data pointed to a further ~9% drop toward 88.
- The decline isn't Anthropic-specific — SpaceX was set to open below its $135 IPO price, SoftBank fell 14.5% overnight, and Asian markets saw broad overnight losses.
- Marcus claims the current downturn is unfolding faster than the dot-com crash, citing an external tweet/video as evidence.
- He predicts OpenAI cannot IPO at a $1 trillion valuation in this environment and raises the possibility it could run out of cash.
- He extends the forecast to infrastructure players, expecting Nvidia and "Neoclouds" to be hit hard in the near term.
- Frames this as confirmation of a long-predicted "death spiral" in AI markets, and references a separate piece alleging Anthropic's IPO structure disadvantaged retail investors.
