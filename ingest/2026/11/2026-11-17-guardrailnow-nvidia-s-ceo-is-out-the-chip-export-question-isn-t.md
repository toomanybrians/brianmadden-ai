# Nvidia's CEO Is Out. The Chip Export Question Isn't

## Insights

- Nvidia's board accepted CEO Jensen Huang's resignation on November 11, ending his 33-year tenure; the 8-K filing discloses no reason for the departure.
- CFO Colette Kress has been installed as interim CEO.
- The resignation follows reporting on Megaspeed, a Singapore-based cloud company that purchased over $4.8 billion in Nvidia chips over two years.
- US and Singapore authorities are investigating Megaspeed over suspected diversion of export-restricted chips to China.
- The piece frames the chip-export control question as unresolved and ongoing regardless of the leadership change.
