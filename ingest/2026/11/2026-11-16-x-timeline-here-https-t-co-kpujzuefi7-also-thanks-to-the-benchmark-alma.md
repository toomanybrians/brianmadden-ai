Also thanks to The Benchmark Almanac: https://t.c

## Insights

- SatBench, a new analysis tool, tracks how quickly the 173 AI benchmarks most cited by labs since 2023 become "saturated" (best reported score ≥85%); 63% (109 of 173) are now saturated.
- Saturation accelerated sharply: the solved share held near 30% from 2023 to early 2025, then jumped 20 points in the last 12 months — the original 2023 cohort of 44 benchmarks itself crossed 85% saturation in September 2026.
- New benchmarks are being solved faster each year: the share solved within a year of release rose from 17% (2023 releases) to 30% (2024) to 43% (2025), with median time-to-85% falling from ~20 months to ~14.
- OpenAI has been first past 85% on the most benchmarks overall (20), while Anthropic led in 2026 (11), based on a restricted set of 71 benchmarks multiple frontier labs report (to avoid rewarding niche self-reported ones).
- A 2023-era "canon" of benchmarks (MMLU, HumanEval, GSM8K, MATH, BBH, HellaSwag, DROP, MGSM) is now essentially extinct in frontier launch posts — cited in nearly every 2023-24 post but only 2 of 134 in 2026 — and benchmarks are typically saturated before labs stop citing them (28 of 32 extinct benchmarks were already past 85% when last mentioned).
- Caveats flagged directly: most "solved" claims rest on labs' own self-reported launch-post scores rather than independent verification (independently-verified saturation is only 24%, not 63%); score coverage is sparse (only ~40% of the model-benchmark matrix is filled); and extrapolated saturation timelines (full fixed-set saturation ~2028, live/growing index ~2030) are explicitly framed as extrapolation, not forecast.
