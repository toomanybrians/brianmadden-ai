## Insights

- Six of Nvidia's seven largest customers — Google, Microsoft, Meta, Amazon, Anthropic, and OpenAI — are developing their own chips, rather than relying solely on Nvidia GPUs.
- SpaceX is the lone exception among the seven, not reported as building custom silicon.
- These seven companies collectively account for the vast majority of GPU deployment, meaning the shift toward in-house chip development is happening among the buyers that matter most to Nvidia's business.
