# It does best on the stuff nobody watches. Partner and supplier portals, mobile b

## Insights

- Frames AI agent effectiveness in security/attack contexts around a "beat a 3 out of 10" threshold — mediocre, tireless agents succeed not by being excellent but by being persistent against low-quality targets.
- Identifies the attack surface where this applies: partner and supplier portals, mobile backends, employee apps, legacy services, and third-party integrations — systems that are poorly monitored or "nobody watches."
- Notes that attacker-run AI instances (referencing a tool called ARTEX) predominantly use DeepSeek Flash models rather than higher-end models like Claude or GPT, even though compatibility with those exists.
- Attributes this model choice to cost and lack of guardrails — DeepSeek Flash is "good enough," cheap, and doesn't visibly resist being told it's conducting a pentest.
- Implies attackers optimize for economics and low friction over raw capability, undercutting the assumption that more advanced models are the primary threat vector.

## Quote

> A mediocre agent that never gets bored finds your forgotten partner.
