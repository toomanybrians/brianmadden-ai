# AI is a general purpose technology has good and bad impacts, the general model s

## Insights

- Argues AI should be understood as a general-purpose technology with both good and bad impacts, not treated as a monolithic single thing.
- Frames the productive path forward as amplifying beneficial uses while actively mitigating harmful ones, rather than accepting or rejecting AI wholesale.
- Claims that treating "AI" as one undifferentiated category is itself an obstacle to clear thinking and productive debate about it.
- In the specific case of gaming, a reply distinguishes developer use of AI for coding (less objectionable) from AI-generated art and "low effort shovelware" (the actual source of backlash) — conflating the two makes advocating for legitimate uses harder.
- Separately contends that the games industry already has a poor track record on worker treatment (crunch, instability), and calls for positive visions of rebuilding the industry stronger with AI rather than defending an already-flawed status quo.
