# Y Combinator Just Showed You What to Build. Here Are 33 Startup Ideas Worth Building Now 🚀

## Insights

- YC's Winter 2027 Requests for Startups shifts focus from AI applications to AI infrastructure bottlenecks, framed around the line "AI's biggest bottlenecks are moving beyond the models."
- The 11 requests span physical/structural constraints: space access, the US robot supply chain, data center equipment, financing for physical AI, government benefits verification, city permitting, air-gapped AI, owned/vertical foundation models, AI-backed expert services, and models that write well.
- The argument is that intelligence (AI models) is becoming cheap, while physical and institutional constraints — materials, permits, verified data, capital, trust — remain scarce and are where future value will concentrate.
- This marks a shift from YC's prior list (Fall 2026), which centered on consumer AI products, multiplayer AI, and crypto; Winter 2027 drops almost all consumer-app framing in favor of supply-chain-level plays.
- The piece positions this as consistent with a16z's State of Markets 2026, describing a broader industry "rotation from bits to atoms," with pricing power moving to supply-chain bottlenecks.
- Funding context cited: AI captured $102B of $159B in global VC funding in Q3 2026, and hyperscalers are expected to spend roughly $1T in capex this year, concentrated in a small number of mega-funded companies — leaving gaps (e.g., actuator supply shortages, Medicaid renewal processing) for smaller startups to fill.
- Notes specific deadline-driven opportunities, e.g., Medicaid work-requirement verification needs across 40 states and DC starting January 1, 2027, and a July FCC action affecting robot actuator supply in the US.
