# Just say no to Anthropic’s IPO

## Insights

- Anthropic is pursuing an IPO valuation of "up to" $1.4 trillion despite having no sustained profitable quarter (one exception called "anomalous" and "heavily subsidized") and nearly $900 billion in future liabilities.
- The trillion-dollar valuation narrative traces back to a growth projection made in May, at what the piece calls the peak of "tokenmaxxing mania" — a projection characterized as unrealistic.
- Revenue growth has sharply decelerated: quarter-over-quarter growth was 147% in Q2 but fell to 47% in Q3, undercutting the trajectory the valuation hype depended on.
- The slowdown is attributed partly to the fading of "tokenmaxxing" dynamics and partly to the emergence of lower-cost, "good quality (if not quite top-tier)" competitors from China, which are eating into growth.
- The piece frames Anthropic's own IPO filing as unusually candid about these risks, suggesting the company itself acknowledges the fragility of the bull case even as it seeks the high valuation.

## Quote

> Q2 growth was 147% compared to Q1, but Q3 wound up being a mere 47% compared to Q2.
