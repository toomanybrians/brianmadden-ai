# Apollo Research may discuss confidential OpenAI information only with authorized

## Insights

- Apollo Research's access to confidential OpenAI information is restricted to specific authorized OpenAI staff, selected by OpenAI itself, rather than open-ended access.
- Apollo does not receive access to general internal communication channels (e.g., Slack) that an internal OpenAI safety researcher would have.
- Formal conversations between Apollo and approved OpenAI staff must be logged, suggesting an auditable but constrained communication protocol.
- OpenAI retains the right to withhold "in-scope" items from Apollo when it judges this reasonably necessary for technical, security, or IP reasons — but not merely because the information is unfavorable.
- When OpenAI withholds information, it is obligated to disclose that withheld items exist and provide written reasons for the withholding.

## Quote

> OpenAI can withhold in-scope items where it deems it reasonably necessary for technical, security, or IP reasons, but not simply because they're unfavorable.
