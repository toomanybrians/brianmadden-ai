# Our latest frontier model Gemini 4 Carbon is rolling out today! Amazing progress

## Insights

- Google DeepMind announced Gemini 4 Carbon as its newest frontier model, released less than a year after Gemini 3, positioned as a major capability jump in a short timeframe.
- The announcement's headline demo: Carbon reportedly reverse-engineered old compiled game binaries (Theme Park, from the 90s), added new original content (a DeepMind-themed ride), and ported the result to run in a web browser — all described as accomplished "in one shot."
- This is presented as evidence of leap in coding/reverse-engineering capability versus the prior model generation, where the author's earlier Gemini 3 demo (quoted in the same thread) required manually vibe-coding a recreation over "a matter of hours" rather than one-shot automated output.
- The comparison implicitly frames progress in terms of reduced human effort/iteration needed: Gemini 3 needed hours of human-guided vibe coding; Gemini 4 Carbon is claimed to do a harder version of the task (binary reverse-engineering plus new feature plus platform port) autonomously.
