# Meta-transparency: OpenAI agreed to strong rights for us to publicly explain the

## Insights

- Apollo Research secured contractual rights from OpenAI for "meta-transparency" — the ability to publicly disclose not just findings, but the terms of its own access: what was requested, what was granted, and what was withheld or delayed.
- The agreement extends to disclosing redactions and their impact on Apollo's conclusions, plus any pressure Apollo believes influenced its work — effectively allowing the auditor to flag constraints on its own independence.
- A key limitation: Apollo cannot publish findings based on internal employee discussions unless OpenAI formally attests to them or Apollo can independently verify the finding through other means.
- If OpenAI refuses to provide an attestation, Apollo retains the right to state that refusal publicly and explain how it limits the audit's conclusions — making non-cooperation itself visible rather than silent.

## Quote

> If OpenAI declines an attestation, we can say so and explain how that limits our conclusions.
