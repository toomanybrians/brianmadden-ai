# After Anthropic Euphoria, Nvidia, AMD & the Broader Market Offer a Harsh Reality Check

## Insights

- Short-seller Hunterbrook alleges AMD has replicated Nvidia's "circular financing" model (chip makers investing in and financing their own customers) but without Nvidia's margins or pricing power, triggering a near-10% stock drop; AMD disputes the characterization.
- AMD is also reported to have shipped updated chip firmware to multiple US-blacklisted Chinese entities, adding a China-exposure risk on top of the financing concerns.
- Large Wall Street firms that pledged ~$500 billion in data-center loans back in August reportedly haven't disbursed any of it yet, signaling growing investor caution about AI infrastructure buildout.
- Despite Anthropic's IPO succeeding as a bullish "validator" for the AI industry, broader markets (including Nvidia, AMD) sold off the same week — a disconnect suggesting investors are selling into AI good news rather than broadly re-committing.
- Nvidia's post-Jensen-Huang leadership (interim CEO Colette Kress) is reportedly set to scale back Huang's aggressive startup dealmaking pace, even after Nvidia/NVentures hit a record 121 investments in 2026; this is described as a major concern for startup-land funding.
- Google's Gemini 4 Neon release is framed as disproving recent declarations (e.g., SemiAnalysis calling DeepMind "no longer a frontier lab") that Google had fallen out of the frontier AI race — read as evidence that labs with talent and compute are never durably out of contention.

## Quote

> If the smoke surrounding Nvidia becomes an inferno for the AI build-out, AMD will be engulfed by the flames.
