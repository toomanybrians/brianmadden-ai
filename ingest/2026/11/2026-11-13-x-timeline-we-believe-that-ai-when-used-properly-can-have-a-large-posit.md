# We believe that AI, when used properly, can have a large positive impact on educ

## Insights

- The team built an education-focused AI tool called "Primer" (named after the device from Neal Stephenson's *The Diamond Age*), arguing mainstream AI models aren't built for learning out-of-the-box.
- Design priority was giving educators control to make their own choices about how AI is used, rather than imposing one approach.
- The initial version was built in collaboration with teachers.
- The tool/paper is explicitly model- and technology-agnostic, meant to be adapted rather than tied to specific AI systems.
- The paper includes an appendix containing a prompt that hands the paper itself to an AI agent and instructs it to build its own version of Primer — described as "a paper you can run."
