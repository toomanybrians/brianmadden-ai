# It turns out the advancements we’re getting in coding and computer use, combined

## Insights

- Claims that current advances in AI coding ability and computer-use capability, combined with industry-specific knowledge and general reasoning, are now sufficient to automate significant portions of knowledge work across many domains.
- Frames this moment as the start of an "autonomous collaborators" era — AI systems acting as collaborators rather than just tools, across a number of fields.
- Cites an example (via quoted Claude/Anthropic post) of a pre-release model ("Fable 5.2") taking a single one-paragraph prompt and independently designing three working electronic devices, including a home voice assistant hub.
- The example is positioned as evidence of AI handling open-ended, ambitious, multi-step technical projects rather than narrow tasks.
