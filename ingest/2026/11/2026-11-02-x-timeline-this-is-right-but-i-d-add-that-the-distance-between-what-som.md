## Insights

- There are two distinct "gaps" being described: (1) the widening gap between frontier AI users and everyone else, and (2) a separate, growing gap between what frontier users actually do with AI and what today's AI systems are technically capable of doing.
- The claim is that even the most advanced practitioners are underutilizing current AI capabilities — the ceiling of "properly harnessed and applied" AI is far above current frontier usage patterns.
- This implies capability gaps aren't just about access or skill adoption lag, but about a fundamental lack of understanding of what current-generation systems can do.
- The quoted post (from @levie) frames the user-skill gap as non-converging and accelerating, explicitly rejecting the idea that it would stabilize if AI progress plateaued.

## Quote

> We don't really understand the full capability of today's systems when properly harnessed & applied.
