# hot take on how we got into this immense, correction-level generative AI market

## Insights

- Gary Marcus claims he predicted a market correction specifically for AI companies, framing it as the end of a "free ride" for the sector.
- Market reaction was initially mixed rather than uniform: neocloud/data-center-heavy companies (e.g., CoreWeave) sold off while large diversified tech companies (Amazon, Apple, Google) rose.
- One circulating theory: investors were rotating capital out of smaller, AI-infrastructure-pure-play "neocloud" firms into larger tech companies better positioned to absorb a cash crunch.
- A second theory ties the divergence to public/political resistance to data center buildouts, suggesting investors hoped hyperscalers would rein in capital expenditure on AI infrastructure.
- By the next day, the piece claims the correction broadened into a near-universal selloff ("a slaughter"), moving past the initial mixed/divergent pattern.
- Full reasoning and Marcus's acknowledged past wrong predictions are behind a paywall and not available in the extracted content.
