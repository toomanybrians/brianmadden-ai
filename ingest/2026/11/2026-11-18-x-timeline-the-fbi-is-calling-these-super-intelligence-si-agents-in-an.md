# The FBI is calling these "super intelligence (SI) agents." In an official FLASH.

## Insights

- The FBI issued an official FLASH advisory referring to AI systems as "super intelligence (SI) agents," marking a shift in official government terminology away from "AI."
- @DanielMiessler claims he had proposed renaming AI to "super intelligence" about a month prior and is surprised at how quickly a federal agency adopted similar language.
- The FBI corrected a separate FLASH advisory about a ConnectWise-related security campaign: the Nov 13 version stated the campaign "enabled access to more than 2,250 downstream tenants," while a reissued version walks this back to "resulted in attempted sign-ins" to those tenants.
- The correction is framed as significant because "enabled access" and "attempted sign-ins" imply very different severity levels for the actual breach/compromise.
