# It seems inevitable that Anthropic's best strategy is to become as vertically in

## Insights

- Argues Anthropic's optimal strategy is deep vertical integration: designing its own chips to avoid paying a "chip tax" to suppliers, then building both developer primitives (API/SDK) and end-user applications on top — likened to AWS's layered model.
- Cites Claude shipping four products (Whiteboard, Sheets, Data, Meetings) within three days as evidence of rapid, broad application-layer expansion.
- Frames this moment as analogous to the early Microsoft Office era, when bundling Word, Excel, and PowerPoint displaced standalone tools like Lotus 1-2-3 and WordPerfect.
- Suggests standalone note-taking apps are especially exposed to disruption as AI assistants bundle overlapping functionality natively.
