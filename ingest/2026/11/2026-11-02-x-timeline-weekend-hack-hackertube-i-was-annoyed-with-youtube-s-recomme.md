# Weekend hack: HackerTube. I was annoyed with YouTube's recommendations so I had

## Insights

- Demonstrates a practical use case of large language models for knowledge-work-style data curation: filtering an unstructured dataset (43M Hacker News comments spanning 20 years) down to a curated list of 6,454 recommended videos.
- The task was completed in roughly 2 hours using Claude Opus 5.5, illustrating the speed at which AI can perform large-scale extraction/curation tasks that would be impractical to do manually.
- The underlying motivation was dissatisfaction with an algorithmic recommendation system (YouTube's), with AI-driven curation from a trusted community (HN commenters) positioned as a better alternative.
- The output was shipped as a working product (hackertube.org) with a YouTube-like interface (Home, Longs, Liked, History, Watch later, Categories, Playlists), suggesting a full personal tool was built, not just a dataset.
