# Anthropic can sell more AI than it can supply, & that’s now the biggest risk to its $1.5T IPO 🤖📉; Shopify’s Q3 2026: a 66% profit jump that’s really 40%, Shop Pay in AI agents & a 9% drop 🛍️📉

## Insights

- Anthropic's amended S-1 (filed Nov 2) disclosed Q3 2026 revenue of nearly $17 billion, resolving a figure the author's prior valuation model had estimated.
- Despite strong revenue, Anthropic swung from a $974 million operating profit to a $567 million loss in the same quarter, driven by compute costs climbing to 58% of revenue.
- The filing discloses $895.4 billion in total compute commitments for the first time — a scale not previously made public.
- The author ties roughly $5 billion of the loss to a SpaceX compute contract that either party can exit starting in early 2027.
- With the IPO valuation under debate, investors are reportedly pricing Anthropic between $1.4 trillion and $1.6 trillion, against the previously floated $1.5 trillion figure.
- The piece frames Anthropic's core tension as demand exceeding supply capacity — the company can sell more AI than it can currently provision compute for.

## Quote

> Revenue landed where our model needed it. Costs did not.
