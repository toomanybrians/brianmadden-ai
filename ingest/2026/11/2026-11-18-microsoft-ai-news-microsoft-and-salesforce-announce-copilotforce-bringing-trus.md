# Microsoft and Salesforce Announce Copilotforce, Bringing Trusted Customer Context and Action into Microsoft AI Experiences

## Insights

- Microsoft and Salesforce launched "Copilotforce," embedding Salesforce CRM data, workflows, and 38+ prebuilt sales skills directly into Microsoft Copilot, Teams, and Fabric — positioning Copilot as a unified "front door" to business process rather than a standalone chat tool.
- The integration relies on Microsoft's "Work IQ" intelligence layer, which pulls context from Teams meetings, Outlook emails, and SharePoint/OneDrive documents, combining it with CRM data so sellers get context beyond what's manually logged in Salesforce.
- Access and governance are explicitly split: Salesforce retains control over data access and business actions (via authenticated MCP access and existing permissions), while Microsoft enforces its own compliance policies on communications/documents; CRM writebacks require explicit user confirmation.
- A new two-way "Zero Copy" data federation connects Salesforce Data 360 and Microsoft Fabric's OneLake, letting both platforms query/analyze shared data without duplicating datasets or building custom pipelines — framed as solving the problem of customer data (Salesforce) vs. operational/financial data (Fabric) living in separate systems.
- Accenture is both the implementation/design partner and the first large-scale pilot customer, running the integration across its internal sales org (740,000+ Copilot users, 55,000 Salesforce users) before broader rollout — a common vendor pattern of using a major partner as proof-of-concept.
- Specific sales workflows named: Pipeline Review, Close Planning, Deal Review, Customer Health, Renewal Radar, and Call Follow-Up — each designed to surface risk/opportunity signals buried in emails, Teams chats, or stalled CRM records rather than relying solely on recorded deal stages.
