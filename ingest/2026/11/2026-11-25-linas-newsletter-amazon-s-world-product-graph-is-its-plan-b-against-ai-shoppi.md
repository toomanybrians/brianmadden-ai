# Amazon’s World Product Graph is its Plan B against AI Shopping Agents 🤖🛍️; Shopify now pays AI Shopping Agents extra to use its checkout 🤖💸

## Insights

- Amazon is building "World Product Graph," an internal plan to own structured data on every product for sale — including items Amazon itself doesn't stock — so AI shopping agents can't bypass it even when skipping Amazon's own site.
- The strategic driver is Amazon's ad business: ads only monetize when shoppers view Amazon's pages, and ad revenue growth has slowed to $19.6B/quarter, its weakest growth in nearly four years, as agents route shoppers elsewhere.
- Meta's Muse (10M+ US/Canada users) now skips Amazon and sends shoppers to Walmart instead, in an escalating block-and-counterblock dynamic between Amazon and AI shopping agents.
- The piece frames this as a battle to "own intent at the research step" — noting only 16% of shoppers start product research on Amazon versus 57% on Google, which limits Amazon's natural leverage point.
- Draws a parallel to Klarna, which responded to exclusion from ChatGPT Wallet by building its own agent and product data — suggesting a pattern of platforms building proprietary data/agent layers when excluded from others' agent ecosystems.
- Amazon is positioning its 400M+ Alexa for Shopping users as the distribution base behind this product-graph strategy.

## Quote

> World Product Graph is therefore Amazon's attempt to own intent at the research step.
