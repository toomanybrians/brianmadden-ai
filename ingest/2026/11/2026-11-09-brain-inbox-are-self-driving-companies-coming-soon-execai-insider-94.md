# Are “Self-Driving Companies” Coming Soon? (ExecAI Insider #94)

## Insights

- Replit CEO Amjad Masad defines a "self-driving company" as one where AI agents execute and coordinate much of the cross-departmental day-to-day work, using company data/software, while humans set goals, make judgment calls, and remain accountable for outcomes.
- Masad predicts every Fortune 500 company will be a "Self-Driving Company" by end of 2030; Paul Roetzer disagrees with that timeline for large enterprises but agrees the model will emerge soon elsewhere.
- Roetzer's reasoning: four years after ChatGPT's launch, most companies remain in "understanding and piloting" phases, with only 5-10% of staff as advanced AI users — typically concentrated in a single department rather than organization-wide.
- He argues self-driving companies are more likely to emerge first among startups rather than large enterprises, given enterprises' slower pace of organizational redesign.
- SmarterX itself is prototyping early versions internally: building "agent analyst" prototypes in ChatGPT to automate performance monitoring/data analysis, and testing customer success agents that could "reinvent" existing roles.
- Masad's framing ties agentic execution to organizational redesign: as agents absorb execution/coordination work, companies must rethink decision-making structures, not just adopt new tools.

## Quote

> By the end of 2030, every company in the Fortune 500 will be a Self-Driving Company. — Amjad Masad
