# The State of Longevity

## Insights

- ChatGPT is identified as one of two forces (alongside COVID-19) that pushed health/longevity management from a fringe "biohacker" activity into the mainstream, by letting people query and interpret complex medical information themselves.
- The piece frames a "race to become a healthcare home" — described explicitly as the "Muse for healthcare" — where companies compete to be the AI-powered single interface for records, labs, wearables, and care coordination; value is expected to migrate from AI interpretation (cheap) to the "action layer" (scheduling, follow-up, prescriptions).
- Named contenders for this AI-driven healthcare hub include Function Health, Oura, WHOOP, General Medicine, Counsel, Ferry Health, and large-audience incumbents OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon, and Apple.
- Brain-computer interface section describes non-invasive neurotech companies (Conduit, Alljoined, Aleph Neuro) treating neural decoding as a "data scaling problem" — training AI models on large datasets to translate noisy brain signals into text/images, with a stated long-term goal of communicating directly with AI agents without verbalizing thoughts.
- Survey data: 39% of respondents would use a non-invasive brain-sensing device for sleep/focus/mood, and 29% would use thought-controlled devices, with higher interest among men — signaling early consumer appetite for AI-mediated brain interfaces.
- Broader consumer-health trends tracked (toxins/non-toxic products, peptides, cholesterol management, wearables "Act 3") are presented as adjacent markets where AI-enabled tools (wearable AI interpretation, AI-matched treatment) are expected to drive the next wave of company-building.

## Quote

> If this sounds like "Muse for healthcare," that's the idea.
