# We cannot publish information based on employee discussions unless OpenAI provid

## Insights

- Apollo Research describes a formal constraint: it cannot publish findings based on employee discussions about OpenAI unless OpenAI provides a formal attestation, or Apollo can independently verify the finding itself.
- If OpenAI refuses to attest to a finding, Apollo states it will disclose that refusal publicly and explain how it limits the conclusions they can draw.
- OpenAI retains the ability to request redactions of confidential information, including evidence underlying Apollo's conclusions — but redactions are not permitted solely because a finding is unfavorable to OpenAI.
- Apollo commits to explaining how any redactions affect both their conclusions and readers' ability to independently assess those conclusions.
- This outlines a transparency/oversight protocol governing how an external research group reports on internal practices at an AI lab, balancing confidentiality against accountability.

## Quote

> OpenAI may request redactions of confidential information... but not simply because a finding is unfavorable.
