# Nov. 24 - AI-linked hacks hit Check Point servers | US falls short on food cyber safety

## Insights

- At least 22 organizations were compromised in an AI-enabled campaign exploiting critical vulnerabilities in Check Point Security Management servers.
- Industry framing is shifting from "can AI run an entire attack" to where AI gives attackers leverage to change the cost, speed, or scale of an intrusion (per Intel 471 sponsored content).
- GAO found that USDA and FDA lack sufficient resources and staff to fully carry out food-sector critical infrastructure cyber protection responsibilities.
- CrowdStrike is acquiring Zscaler for $43.9 billion, consolidating security vendors amid what's described as a "turbulent threat environment."
- Separate coverage flags AI as a new threat vector for power grid infrastructure, and highlights ~40% of industrial serial gateways broadcasting insecure configurations.
- A sponsored Check Point message frames AI-driven attacks as autonomous ("don't need people") and able to "probe, adapt and attack without waiting on a human."

## Quote

> AI can probe, adapt and attack without waiting on a human. Stop threats early so security and the business can keep moving.
