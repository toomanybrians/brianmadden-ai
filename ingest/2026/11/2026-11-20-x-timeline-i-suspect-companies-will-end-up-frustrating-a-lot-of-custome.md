# I suspect companies will end up frustrating a lot of customers by telling them t

## Insights

- Apple is making it harder for third-party computer-use AI agents (e.g., ChatGPT) to control Macs, while simultaneously developing its own native computer-use agent.
- In macOS 27.3, enabling ChatGPT's third-party computer-use support requires 14 distinct steps, up from 4 previously — a significant friction increase.
- Apple frames the added friction as a security measure; some observers instead read it as anti-competitive behavior favoring Apple's own agent.
- The commentator predicts companies restricting users' ability to deploy their own AI agents on their own accounts/devices will increasingly frustrate customers.
- The commentator argues that using fear/security framing to justify these restrictions has a limited shelf life as a strategy.

## Quote

> Scaring them will only work so well for so long.
