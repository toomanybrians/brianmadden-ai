# Google seems well-positioned in the home hub space, but the abilities of Google

## Insights

- Google is seen as well-positioned in the smart home hub market structurally, but its underlying model (Google Home) is assessed as far weaker than a frontier model like "Hydrogen."
- Apple and Amazon are flagged as having lagging AI models for their home devices, with no clear path forward since adopting a Chinese open-weights model is considered unlikely for them.
- The quality gap between frontier AI models and current voice assistants is framed as experiential: using a PC with a strong model (e.g., Codex/Code/OpenClaw) versus using Siri or Alexa reveals a stark difference in intuitiveness.
- Frontier models are described as resembling the "household computer" trope from science fiction, in contrast to existing assistants.
- The author predicts that lacking a strong AI model to power a central home device will become an increasing competitive liability.

## Quote

> Not having a good AI model powering your central home device will be a growing liability.
