# This is what I've been worried about: the techniques are old, but agents make ma

## Insights

- A China-backed group ("Vapor Panda") used an agentic AI pentesting framework (ARTEX, paired with DeepSeek) to compromise ~140 of 200+ targeted organizations across 14 countries in just 11 days.
- The underlying attack techniques are not new — what's changed is speed and scale: agentic automation pushed command execution from a historical norm of ~40/hour to over 1,500/hour.
- Targets included foreign ministries, defense/intelligence agencies, telecom providers, and 19 US organizations across pharma, semiconductor, defense, nuclear, and financial sectors — roughly 70% showed confirmed or likely compromise.
- CrowdStrike reports agentic adversarial attacks nearly tripling month-over-month (45 in September to 126 in October), describing a broader shift toward "agentic adversaries" as the new normal.
- A separate but related campaign (Storm-0940, per Microsoft) used the same ARTEX framework in a supply-chain attack reaching 2,250+ tenants at 2,900 sign-in attempts per minute.
- CrowdStrike's view: this isn't an unsolvable problem, but defenders need better visibility into endpoints, identities, and cross-domain activity to match the new operating speed.
