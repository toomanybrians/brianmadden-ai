# Genie 4 is available! Looks like it is turtles all the way down then… Eligible U

## Insights

- Google DeepMind released Genie 4, an interactive "world model" that generates explorable, navigable environments from prompts, now available to Google AI Ultra subscribers via Project Genie.
- New capability: mid-session prompting lets users edit an environment in real time while navigating it (change weather, add characters, redirect action), paired with real-time native audio that adapts to the scene.
- Genie 3 also got updates: sessions extended from 60 seconds to 3 minutes (or chained world-to-world), 360-degree panorama image grounding to seed worlds, and real-time promptable in-world events.
- A new "Project Genie agent" turns world-building into a conversational process — it orchestrates multiple Google multimodal models to generate visuals, music/score, and context-aware suggestions or triggered events as a user explores.
- A community gallery lets users publish, explore, and remix worlds; examples include turning personal photos into explorable spaces, panorama-grounded prehistoric habitats, and collaborations with outside creators (a gothic-manor mystery, an ant's-eye-view colony sim).
- Responsible-deployment notes: all generated video/audio carries SynthID watermarking and safety screening; DeepMind flags unresolved challenges like camera latency, complex physical interactions, real-time audio precision, and long-term scene permanence.
