# This is one of the better litmus tests for whether you’re pushing your use of AI

## Insights

- The author proposes a litmus test for whether someone is using AI ambitiously enough: merely reproducing what you'd already make, just faster, no longer counts as pushing AI use.
- He claims this "faster but same output" ceiling was roughly the best achievable with AI until about six months prior, implying a recent shift in what's possible.
- The post is cut off before specifying what the new phase or capability is, but frames it as a transition point in how AI should be used.
- The quoted post (@sundeep) raises the bar further: AI-made output should be ~10,000x better than self-made work, not just incrementally better.
- Failing to meet that higher bar is framed as a signal of laziness rather than a reasonable use of a faster tool — i.e., mediocre AI output reads as a character/effort judgment, not a technical limitation.

## Quote

> If you're going to make something with AI, make sure it's 10,000x better than anything you would have made yourself.
