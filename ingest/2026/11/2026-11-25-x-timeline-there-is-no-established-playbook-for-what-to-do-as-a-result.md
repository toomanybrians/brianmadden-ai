# There is no established playbook for what to do as a result, and these teams are

## Insights

- Small AI-augmented teams (2-3 people) at large firms, across manufacturing and financial services, are reportedly producing output equivalent to dozens of workers in far less time.
- This productivity shift is triggering significant internal management conflict, framed as "large-scale management fights over the implications and politics."
- These small teams often operate across traditional department boundaries (marketing, legal, engineering, sales simultaneously), rather than fitting neatly into one function.
- The cross-functional nature of this work is a specific source of organizational friction because it threatens existing departmental structures, traditions, and rules.
- No established management playbook currently exists for how organizations should respond to or govern these emergent small high-output teams.

## Quote

> There is no established playbook for what to do as a result.
