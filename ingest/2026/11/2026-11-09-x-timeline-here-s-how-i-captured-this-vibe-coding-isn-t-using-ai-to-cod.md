## Insights

- Redefines "vibe coding" by the builder's level of understanding, not by whether AI was used: it's when the person has no idea how the backend works or why decisions were made.
- Engineering is framed as the opposite state — full understanding of how everything works — and is compatible with AI writing 100% of the code, even at high output volume (dozens/hundreds of features per month).
- Attributes the term's origin to Andrej Karpathy (Feb 2025), originally describing code that "grows beyond my usual comprehension."
- Pushes back on a counter-argument (from a quoted post) that "vibe coding" unfairly implies low effort/soul — the original poster insists the real distinction is comprehension, not effort or heart put into the work.
- Implies a spectrum: same AI tool use can land as either "engineering" or "vibe coding" depending solely on the operator's grasp of the system produced.

## Quote

> Vibe Coding is when you have no idea what your AI is making for you. It's separation from understanding.
