# The 10x Wall

## Insights

- Anthropic and SpaceX, the two largest IPOs in history, trade within 15% of each other's market cap and at an identical forward multiple of 9.5x EV/2027 revenue, despite SpaceX being projected to grow 466% vs. Anthropic's 288%.
- Across 24 mega-cap software/infrastructure names, both companies sit mid-pack on valuation multiple despite growing roughly 10x the median growth rate — the market isn't rewarding their outlier growth with outlier multiples.
- Above $1 trillion of enterprise value, no company trades much above 10x forward sales regardless of growth rate (16% to 466% growth all cluster near the same ceiling among hyperscalers).
- The real anchor at trillion-dollar scale is P/E, not revenue multiple: hyperscalers (Microsoft, Google, Meta, Amazon) all trade in a tight 20x–26x earnings band, which mathematically caps the forward revenue multiple based on margin structure.
- Implication for Anthropic/SpaceX: since multiple expansion appears to be over at this scale, further stock appreciation depends on "growing into" current earnings/margins rather than re-rating on growth.

## Quote

> Public markets are not debating whether Anthropic or SpaceX is the better business: at trillion-dollar scale, multiple expansion is over.
