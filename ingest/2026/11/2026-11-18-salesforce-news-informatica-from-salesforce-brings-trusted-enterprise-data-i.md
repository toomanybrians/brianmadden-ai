# Informatica from Salesforce brings Trusted Enterprise Data into Microsoft Copilot, Microsoft Foundry, and Microsoft Fabric to Accelerate AI & Analytics

## Insights

- Informatica (Salesforce) is integrating its enterprise data intelligence (catalogs, business glossaries, lineage, quality, governance) directly into Microsoft Copilot/Teams, Foundry, and Fabric, rather than keeping it as a separate governance tool.
- The stated problem being targeted: per a cited Deloitte 2026 report, 85% of organizations expect to customize AI agents, but only 21% of those planning agentic deployments report having mature agent governance models — a readiness gap this integration is pitched against.
- Foundry integration uses MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers to let developers ground agents in authoritative data definitions and enforce identity/access/governance controls on agent actions, with auditability and admin tracing of agent-data interactions.
- Fabric-side work extends scanning/lineage beyond stored data to notebooks and pipelines, aiming to track data transformation and orchestration steps, not just data at rest.
- New U.S. East Coast Azure deployment point added for data residency/compliance reasons, with Informatica purchases countable toward Microsoft Azure Consumption Commitments (MACC).
- Framing throughout: the next phase of enterprise AI is less about model capability and more about agents/assistants understanding business context, definitions, and trust/provenance of data before acting.

## Quote

> "The next frontier of enterprise AI isn't simply giving every employee an intelligent assistant... It's ensuring that those assistants and agents understand the business they're operating in." — Savinay Berry, Salesforce
