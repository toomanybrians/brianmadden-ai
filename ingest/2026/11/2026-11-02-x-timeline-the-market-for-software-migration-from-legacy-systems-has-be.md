# The market for software migration from legacy systems has been limited to $30B a

## Insights

- The legacy-system software migration market has historically been capped around $30B/year, constrained by the fact that migration is expensive while ongoing maintenance of old systems is comparatively cheap.
- This cost asymmetry has kept many migration projects shelved even when technically "scoped" and ready to execute.
- Conversations with older/legacy-heavy firms suggest AI is now unlocking a backlog of these previously-scoped-but-uneconomical migration projects faster than expected.
- The implication is a potential shift in the cost structure of migration work specifically, rather than a broad claim about AI's impact on software development generally.

## Quote

> there is a real backlog of scoped cheaper migration projects being unlocked by AI surprisingly fast
