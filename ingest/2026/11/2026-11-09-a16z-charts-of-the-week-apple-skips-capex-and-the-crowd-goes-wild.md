# Charts of the Week: Apple Skips Capex, and the Crowd Goes Wild (Sort Of)

## Insights

- AI and AI-adjacent companies (hyperscalers, neoclouds, semis, AI supply chain) have been the primary drag on Nasdaq returns over the trailing 30 days, while non-AI "household tech" names (Costco, Walmart) and Apple provided the upward offset.
- Apple is the only Mag7 company to beat the Nasdaq year-to-date; Nvidia's lead over the S&P 500 has evaporated — a reversal from last year's Mag7-concentration narrative.
- Apple's capex has actually declined since 2023 and fallen as a share of sales, even as the four major hyperscalers have increased capex roughly 6x-9x and capex-to-sales ratio 2-3x over the same period — the market appears to be rewarding Apple for largely sitting out the AI infrastructure buildout.
- Apple's core business (iPhone revenue growth rebounding, services compounding ~13% yoy) has stayed strong without heavy AI capex investment, suggesting a viable alternative path to the hyperscaler AI-spending strategy.
- Roblox reports concrete AI-driven creation metrics: over 80% of its top 100 games (by hours engaged) used AI tools in the past month, daily asset uploads tripled month-over-month, and two AI-built games broke into the top 20 by concurrent players.
- The piece contrasts this with a broader prior observation that AI-generated apps/content haven't generally translated into increased user engagement ("so many apps, so little time") — Roblox's AI-made games actually getting played is framed as a notable exception to that pattern.
