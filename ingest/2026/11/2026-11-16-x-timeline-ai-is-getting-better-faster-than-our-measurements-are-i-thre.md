# AI is getting better faster than our measurements are

I threw together SatBench

## Insights

- A tool called SatBench was created to track how quickly AI benchmarks become "saturated" — i.e., effectively beaten/maxed out by models.
- Of 173 benchmarks cited by AI labs since 2023, 63% are now saturated.
- The rate at which benchmarks become saturated is increasing year over year, implying benchmark lifespans are shrinking.
- The core claim is that AI capability is improving faster than the measurement tools (benchmarks) designed to track it, creating a growing gap between progress and our ability to assess it.
