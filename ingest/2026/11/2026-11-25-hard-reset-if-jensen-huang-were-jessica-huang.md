# If Jensen Huang Were Jessica Huang

## Insights

- NYT reporting (as described in the piece) alleges Jensen Huang used degradation and humiliation rituals as a management/motivation tactic once standard financial incentives stopped working on highly-paid staff.
- The piece flags a lack of succession planning at Nvidia despite Huang's age and controversies, framed as a structural governance risk tied to a "circular financing scheme" allegedly propping up valuations across Nvidia, OpenAI, Microsoft, and Oracle.
- Raises the question of chip export controls/national security being subordinated to stock market and executive interests, referencing alleged shipments of Nvidia chips to China and Russia.
- Argues a systemic double standard: male tech founders (Huang, Travis Kalanick, Vishal Garg of Better.com) retain or regain power after serious misconduct allegations, while female executives (cites Elizabeth Holmes, "girlboss"-era founders like Audrey Gelman, Steph Korey, Ty Haney) face swift ouster for lesser offenses.
- Suggests cult-of-personality dynamics around "singular visionary" founders enable behavior that would be disqualifying in any normal organizational or legal context, undermining workplace norms like harassment/ethics training.
- Notes employee reactions to Huang's reinstatement (described as enthusiastic, "sprinting to embrace him") as incongruous given broader tech industry layoffs and worker anxiety.
