# The 7x (!!) acceleration in pretraining loss reduction from the OpenAI chart is

## Insights

- OpenAI has published pretraining loss (log-perplexity) data across all its frontier models going back to GPT-3.5, providing a rare public, quantitative view into model scaling progress.
- The data shows log-perplexity falling roughly 7x faster since early 2026 compared to the rate observed from 2022–2025 — a sharp acceleration rather than a continuation of a steady trend.
- METR frames this disclosure as valuable for public understanding of the pace of AI R&D acceleration.
- The commentator notes this acceleration is especially notable because conventional expectations hold that pretraining loss improvements should decelerate (diminishing returns) over time, not speed up.

## Quote

> The 7x (!!) acceleration in pretraining loss reduction from the OpenAI chart is only more striking because that trend is supposed to bend the other direction.
