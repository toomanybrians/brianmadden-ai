# Why is everything shutting down at once? #cybersecurity #news #tech

## Insights

- Multiple near-simultaneous outages (bank outage, FAA ground stop, a "United Systems" takedown, global Outlook outage) are prompting public speculation about a common cause.
- Organizations have strong incentives not to disclose root causes when cyber risk is involved, since disclosure itself can expose systems to further risk — this secrecy erodes public trust.
- Raises, without confirming, the possibility that increasingly powerful AI systems could be actively probing or pushing against cyber defenses as a macro-level pattern behind these failures.
- Frames current outages as the visible surface of roughly 25 years of accumulated legacy software vulnerabilities, where "security through obscurity" is no longer sufficient protection.
- Notes he has separately identified non-AI-related technical causes for some incidents (Outlook, the airline issue), cautioning against assuming all outages share one cause.
- Recommends basic personal preparedness (emergency supplies, water, food) and emphasizes understanding the technical mechanics behind each incident rather than just theorizing.
