# Why I wouldn’t touch Anthropic’s IPO (requires free subscription):

 https://t.c

## Insights

- Anthropic is targeting an IPO valuation of up to $1.4 trillion despite having never turned a profit (with one possible subsidized-quarter exception) and carrying nearly $900 billion in future liabilities/commitments.
- An earlier Reuters report indicated Anthropic aimed to raise up to $69.3 billion in the IPO, with filing details briefly sent then retracted.
- Cited commentary (Ed Zitron) flags slowing Q3 revenue growth alongside rising costs, decelerating Q4 growth (per Ramp data) by as much as 50%, and warns of a potential liquidity crisis for Anthropic and the AI industry broadly.
- Anthropic's amended S-1 itself reportedly acknowledges rising costs, continuing losses, and an expectation that revenue growth will not continue at comparable rates, with $110 billion in annual commitments.
- Revenue growth trajectory is decelerating sharply: quarter-over-quarter growth was 147% (Q2 vs Q1) but fell to 47% (Q3 vs Q2), attributed to the fading of "tokenmaxxing" demand and rising competition from Chinese AI models.
- Trillion-dollar valuation projections are characterized as resting on now-invalidated assumptions of continued meteoric growth from earlier in the year.

## Quote

> The whole idea that they might be worth trillions mostly comes from projections like this absurd but popular one.
