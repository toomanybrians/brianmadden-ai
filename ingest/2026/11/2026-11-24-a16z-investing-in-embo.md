# Investing in Embo

## Insights

- Embo's core bet is that "world models" — systems that learn how the physical world works — are the next frontier for robotics, as an alternative/complement to vision-language-action models or repurposed general-purpose LLMs for robot control.
- The company targets two linked problems: generalization (working in environments never seen before) and resilience (recovering from slips, stuck drawers, and other unexpected failures).
- Its training approach: pretrain a world model on massive internet video plus robot demonstration data to encode general physical knowledge, then have an agent practice and refine skills via reinforcement learning inside that imagined/simulated world model.
- This design is meant to overcome robotics' long-standing data bottleneck — human demonstrations can't cover every task, environment, or failure mode, but internet video offers a much larger source of physical-world knowledge.
- Framed as compute-scalable: agents can accumulate far more practice (including failure recovery) inside a learned simulation than could ever be collected on physical hardware.
- Founder Danijar Hafner's prior work (Dreamer, Dreamer 4 learning Minecraft diamond-collection from offline data via "imagined experience") is cited as the research lineage; team also includes Wilson Yan (VideoGPT) and alumni of Google DeepMind, Meta Superintelligence Labs, NVIDIA Cosmos, Berkeley, and MIT.
- a16z explicitly frames the company's mission as building "the brain for the humanoid workforce," tying robot intelligence directly to future labor/production capacity.

## Quote

> Embo's conviction is that world models are the next generation of frontier AI.
