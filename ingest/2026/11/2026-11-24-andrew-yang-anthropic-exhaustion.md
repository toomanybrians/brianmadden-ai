# Anthropic Exhaustion

## Insights

- Anthropic's IPO valued the company at $1.5 trillion and raised $93 billion, but was followed by a broader market slump (NASDAQ down 5.28% in a day, tech stocks down 20% from peak) — framed as a sign the "AI trade" may be reversing.
- Between 44% and 65% of 2026's GDP growth is attributed to AI buildout, meaning a reversal in AI valuations could tip the broader economy into recession, not just hit investment portfolios.
- Despite low unemployment optics, 2025 saw 1.1 million job cuts (per Washington Post) — an unusually high figure for a non-recessionary year, and spending on data centers now reportedly exceeds spending on office buildings.
- Argues that a recession would give employers cover to make AI-driven layoffs/restructuring decisions they've been delaying, potentially producing "the most savage job market" ever seen, since AI capability keeps improving independent of valuation crashes.
- Notes open-weight models can already handle most tasks at a fraction of the cost of frontier models like Anthropic's, suggesting current frontier valuations may be inflated.
- Flags a wave of AI-enabled cybersecurity breaches (banking sites, airlines going down, password hacks) with an expert predicting rolling service outages for 18 months as systems are patched.

## Quote

> This will lead to the most savage job market any of us has ever seen.
