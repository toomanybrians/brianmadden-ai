# RT @HedgieMarkets: 🦔The IMF says circular AI deals, where companies fund the cus

## Insights

- The IMF estimates "circular" AI deals — where AI companies provide financing to the same customers who then buy their products/services — now exceed $1 trillion in total value.
- Roughly two-thirds of that $1 trillion is financed through borrowed money rather than cash, raising leverage concerns.
- IMF chief Kristalina Georgieva characterized the global economy as disproportionately dependent on AI-driven activity for growth.

## Quote

> The world economy is "standing on one leg on the AI leg." — Kristalina Georgieva (IMF)
