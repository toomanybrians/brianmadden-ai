## Insights

- Redefines "Vibe Coding" against common usage: it's not simply using AI to write code, but building something whose backend logic/mechanics are a complete mystery to the builder.
- Attributes the term's origin to Andrej Karpathy (February 2025), describing code that "grows beyond my usual comprehension."
- Frames engineering as the opposite of vibe coding: full understanding of how and why something works, regardless of how much AI was used to produce it.
- Argues AI usage percentage is irrelevant to the distinction — someone could use AI for 100% of code output and still be "engineering" if they understand every decision.
- The dividing line is comprehension and intentionality (knowing the stack, security choices, architecture), not the tool or the volume of AI-generated code.

## Quote

> Vibe Coding is making things where you have no idea how it works on the backend.
