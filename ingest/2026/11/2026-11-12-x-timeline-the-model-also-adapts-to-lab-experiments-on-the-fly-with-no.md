# The model also adapts to lab experiments on the fly with no retraining needed.

## Insights

- AlphaGenome 2 is a genomics AI model trained on over 100 million cellular profiles spanning 68 distinct tissue types.
- Unlike approaches that analyze whole tissues in aggregate, the model focuses on one cell type at a time, which reportedly allows it to detect nearly twice as many mutations.
- The model can adapt to new lab experiments on the fly without retraining — researchers can feed in data from just a few dozen cells to study how DNA changes behave in specific biological contexts.
- Example use cases cited include tracking immune cells responding to a vaccine and specific gut cells involved in some biological process.
- DeepMind frames the shift from whole-tissue to single-cell-type analysis as moving from a "blurry image" to detail at the level of individual pixels.

## Quote

> Looking at a whole tissue at once is like looking at a blurry image: you get the visuals, but miss what's happening inside individual pixels.
