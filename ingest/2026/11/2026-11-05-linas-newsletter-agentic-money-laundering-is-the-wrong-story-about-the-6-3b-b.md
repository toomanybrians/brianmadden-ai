# Agentic Money Laundering is the wrong story about the $6.3B Binance hack 🤖💸; Stripe now lets AI Agents spend your money while you sleep, on stablecoins merchants never see 🤖🪙

## Insights

- The October 29 Binance hack ($6.34B, attributed by the FBI to North Korea's TraderTraitor unit) is being labeled "agentic money laundering" by some, but Binance's own incident report only finds AI involvement in the intrusion phase, not in how funds were subsequently moved.
- Attackers compromised two wallet engineers' computers by exploiting Binance's internal AI tool, gaining access nine days before any funds were drained.
- The theft exploited a delegated permission structure that Binance could not quickly revoke, meaning disabling withdrawals did not stop the drain once access was established.
- Stablecoin issuers Tether and Circle froze $2.85 billion within hours through issuer-level intervention — a faster and larger response than any AML monitoring system achieved.
- The piece argues the real lesson for payments/stablecoin/AI-agent builders is to scrutinize AI tooling and permission/delegation structures inside organizations, rather than focusing on AI-driven laundering techniques, since no on-chain evidence of agentic laundering has actually been found.

## Quote

> The weak point was AI tooling inside the victim, and the theft ran through a delegated permission Binance couldn't quickly revoke.
