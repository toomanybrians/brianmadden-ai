# ⚙️ Your AI creative studio is here

## Insights

- Adobe Firefly is positioned as a unified "AI creative studio" combining Adobe's pro-grade creative tools, multiple third-party AI models (Google, ByteDance, Kling, OpenAI, ElevenLabs, plus Adobe's own), and an orchestrating agent in a single workspace, aimed at eliminating tool-switching across image, video, audio, and design tasks.
- The "Firefly AI Assistant," powered by "Adobe Creative Agent," lets users describe a goal in natural language and have the agent plan and execute multi-step creative workflows (e.g., producing assets for a social campaign), with a "Plan Mode" that surfaces the proposed approach before execution.
- Users retain control over delegation: they can review agent output, give feedback, redirect, or manually refine results — framed explicitly as keeping human "taste and judgment" central to the final product.
- A "Model Selector" feature lets users choose which underlying AI model the agent uses for a given task, and preferences can be saved across projects.
- "Quick Actions" offer pre-built, ready-to-use workflows (product ads, thumbnails, quick image edits) for simpler tasks without needing to configure a full multi-step workflow.
- The piece is sponsored/partnership content from Adobe, presented via The Deep View newsletter, rather than independent reporting or analysis.
