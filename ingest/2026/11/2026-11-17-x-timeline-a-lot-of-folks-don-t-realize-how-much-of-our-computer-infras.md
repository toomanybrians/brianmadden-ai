# A lot of folks don't realize how much of our computer infrastructure is an impro

## Insights

- Much of existing computer infrastructure is described as an "improvised system" that is inherently prone to cascading failures, not a robustly engineered one.
- Major outages (cited examples: Microsoft, FAA) have already occurred without any involvement of offensive AI cyberattacks — the fragility predates AI threats.
- The arrival of AI introduces a new dimension: offensive AI-driven attacks targeting this already-fragile infrastructure.
- This is paired with defensive AI capabilities, framing AI's cybersecurity impact as a two-sided development rather than purely a new risk.

## Quote

> A lot of folks don't realize how much of our computer infrastructure is an improvised system prone to cascading failures.
