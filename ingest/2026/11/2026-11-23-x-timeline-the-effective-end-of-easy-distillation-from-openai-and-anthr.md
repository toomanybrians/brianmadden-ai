# The effective end of easy distillation from OpenAI and Anthropic models may be b

## Insights

- Suggests the recent slowdown in open-weights model progress may be linked to frontier labs (OpenAI, Anthropic) closing off easy distillation pathways from their models.
- Frames distillation — training smaller/open models on outputs of frontier closed models — as a likely major driver of rapid open-weights improvement, though the actual extent is acknowledged as unknown.
- Notes that OpenAI is actively researching methods to detect and disrupt "coordinated attempts" at distillation, framing it as a safeguard/safety issue rather than just a competitive one.
- OpenAI's position: distillation lets others reproduce model capabilities without replicating the safety guardrails built around them.
- Implies an open empirical question ahead — whether restricting distillation access will visibly slow open-weights progress going forward.

## Quote

> Distillation can reproduce powerful AI capabilities without the safeguards built around them.
