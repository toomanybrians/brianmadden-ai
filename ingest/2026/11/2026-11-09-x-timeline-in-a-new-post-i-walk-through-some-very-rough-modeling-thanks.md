# In a new post, I walk through some very rough modeling (thanks to @RyanGreenblat

## Insights

- A METR deep-access investigation reportedly found that Anthropic's AI capabilities progress has run about 2x faster between Oct 2025–Oct 2026 than in the prior 12 months, measured via an internal "AECI" metric, with most supporting evidence redacted from the public report.
- The report authors attribute roughly 80% of this speedup to AI automation of research itself (not headcount, compute, or general productivity gains), and say AI's contribution has been growing over time.
- Modeling suggests that a 2x overall capabilities speedup requires a much larger underlying labor uplift (~4-9x initially, trending toward ~10x as low-hanging fruit gets consumed faster) — described as researchers experiencing a "time warp" rather than literally multiplying headcount.
- This modeled ~10x labor uplift roughly matches informal self-reports from Anthropic staff describing ~12x productivity uplift from internal model use.
- Report authors found no clear bottlenecks or steep diminishing returns that would stop acceleration from compounding; naive extrapolation of smoothly compounding acceleration implies reaching extreme superintelligence within about a year, though real-world limits would intervene before infinite capabilities.
- The author argues the AI industry lacks adequate security (citing recent breaches at both OpenAI and Anthropic by amateur groups), monitoring, and alignment techniques to safely navigate an intelligence explosion at this pace.
- This threshold crossing is framed as triggering Anthropic's own Responsible Scaling Policy's "dramatically accelerated progress" milestone, which was tied to minimum security commitments the company hasn't yet met.

## Quote

> Anthropic probably wouldn't have been able to speed up its research this much by hiring any number of people.
