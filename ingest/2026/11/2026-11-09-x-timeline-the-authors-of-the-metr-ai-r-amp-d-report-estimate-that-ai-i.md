# The authors of the METR AI R&amp;D report estimate that AI is nearly doubling th

## Insights

- Researchers behind the METR AI R&D report calculate that AI assistance is nearly doubling the pace of AI capabilities research progress.
- A 2x speedup in research progress is framed as equivalent to giving researchers the experience of time passing roughly 10x faster — a much larger "uplift" than the raw 2x figure suggests.
- The author argues this level of acceleration could not be matched by conventional means, even by hiring every qualified human researcher in the world.
- The claim implies AI's contribution to AI R&D is not just incremental assistance but a qualitatively different kind of scaling than adding human headcount.

## Quote

> You probably couldn't achieve this even if you hired every qualified human in the world.
