# OpenAI kills a lot of its products

Sora lasted 6 months, Agent Builder 8 months

## Insights

- OpenAI has shut down several of its own products quickly after launch: Sora (6 months), Agent Builder (8 months), Group Chat (8 months), Atlas (9 months).
- The pattern suggests that, with the possible exception of Sora, these products became obsolete from OpenAI's own perspective within a matter of months of release.
- This is framed as evidence that even OpenAI — the company often seen as driving the AI pace — struggles to correctly judge how fast its own technology and the market around it are moving.
- The implication is that rapid internal product obsolescence undercuts any assumption that leading AI labs have special immunity to misjudging exponential change.

> their own product became obsolete within months, in case you think they are immune to misjudging the exponential themselves
