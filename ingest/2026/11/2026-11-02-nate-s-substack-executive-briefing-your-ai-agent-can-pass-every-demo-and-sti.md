# Executive Briefing: Your AI Agent Can Pass Every Demo and Still Double-Credit a Customer. The 8 Tests to Run Before You Buy.

## Insights

- On three consecutive days in late October, Salesforce enabled sending work to Microsoft Copilot Studio agents, Oracle announced a workspace bringing its business apps into ChatGPT, and ServiceNow announced a "Control Tower Alliance" with founding members OpenAI, Anthropic, AWS, and NVIDIA.
- These partnerships serve a dual purpose: vendors help each other reach customers while simultaneously competing to become the single supplier customers depend on to coordinate work across all their other software.
- Historically, enterprises could buy distinct vendors for distinct domains (Microsoft for everyday work, Salesforce for customer relationships, Oracle for finance) with limited overlap; agentic AI is eroding that separation by giving vendors incentive to claim the work happening *between* applications.
- The piece frames an "AI control plane" as the real battleground — decisions made at that layer will determine how much autonomy and usefulness enterprise agents are ultimately allowed to have.
- The full briefing (behind a paywall/teaser) promises a breakdown of six separable responsibilities in agent orchestration (from receiving a request to reviewing performance), vendor-by-vendor positioning for Microsoft, Salesforce, Google, Amazon, OpenAI, Anthropic, ServiceNow, and business-application companies, and a practical framework (eight vendor-meeting questions, a standard task brief) for evaluating control-plane claims.

## Quote

> These companies are helping one another reach their customers while competing to become the supplier those customers depend on.
