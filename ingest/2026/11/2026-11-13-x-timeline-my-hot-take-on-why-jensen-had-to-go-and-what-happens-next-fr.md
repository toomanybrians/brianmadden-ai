# my hot take on why Jensen had to go and what happens next (free post) https://t.

## Insights

- Gary Marcus frames Jensen Huang's resignation as effectively forced by the Nvidia board, with CFO Colette Kress stepping in as replacement amid a sharp stock drop.
- Marcus credits Huang's historical strategic foresight (pivoting Nvidia from gaming to AI starting around 2014) while citing growing concerns over "hype" and circular financing practices in recent years.
- The stated reasons for ouster: enabling/lobbying for Chinese AI access, undermining US policy, and personal vulnerability to blackmail/espionage due to conflicts of interest — creating legal and reputational liability for Nvidia.
- Marcus raises a specific open question: whether Nvidia will still honor its promised multi-billion-dollar commitment to Anthropic's IPO under an acting CEO.
- He predicts the resignation could trigger a broader unwinding of "insane circular financing" propping up parts of the AI industry, with cascading effects on the sector.
- Anticipated fallout includes continued congressional investigations, a possible market crash, China emerging as a relative winner, and intensified political scrutiny (including of US officials involved in chip policy).

## Quote

> Jensen is the ringmaster of all the insane circular financing. And all that might collapse.
