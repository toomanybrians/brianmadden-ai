# Madness and mayhem

## Insights

- Azhar frames the current turbulence (AI stock correction, market shock, Nvidia/China spy controversy) as "a flight to quality, not a collapse" — capital is repricing risk, not fleeing the sector.
- Enterprise AI spending is still rising: AI revenue hit $188bn over the trailing twelve months, now running at a $345bn annualized rate, roughly 4.2x year-over-year growth (~43% per quarter).
- Anthropic's growth deceleration from 147% to 45% quarterly is reframed as the company falling back in line with the broader AI economy's growth rate, not a stall — the 147% figure was the anomaly, not the new 45%.
- Anthropic is still up roughly 1,000% year-over-year despite the sequential slowdown.
- Conversations with non-tech companies and consultancies serving them indicate large enterprises are still in the early stages of figuring out how to extract results from AI, but plan to increase spending next year versus this year.
- Azhar predicts Democratic policy will raise data center costs, which he expects to favor hyperscalers over Nvidia and the open-weight model camp, with "neoclouds" identified as the sector's weakest link.

## Quote

> This is a flight to quality, not a collapse.
