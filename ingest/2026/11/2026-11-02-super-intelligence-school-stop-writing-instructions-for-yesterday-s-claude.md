# Stop Writing Instructions for Yesterday’s Claude

## Insights

- Argues that as Claude's capabilities improve, the optimal interaction style shifts from heavily detailed, step-by-step instructions toward outcome-based descriptions — i.e., less prompt engineering, not more.
- Proposes replacing step-by-step oversight with self-verification: giving the model a way to check its own work rather than having a human check every step.
- Suggests organizing work around persistent "projects" rather than managing many discrete, disconnected conversations.
- Recommends treating instructions as provisional rather than permanent — periodically re-testing whether existing rules/instructions still add value as the model's capability changes.
- Frames the underlying goal as building an engineering environment where fewer instructions are needed overall, mistakes surface visibly, and finished work can be trusted without constant supervision.

## Quote

> The objective is not to make Claude follow more instructions. It is to create an engineering environment where fewer instructions are necessary.
