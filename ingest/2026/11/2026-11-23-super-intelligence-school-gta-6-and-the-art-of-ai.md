# GTA 6 and the Art of AI

## Insights

- The game deliberately creates an information asymmetry: the engine has full ground-truth knowledge of an event (who drove, who rode along, where they went), but in-game police characters only receive a partial witness account — a design choice that keeps investigations meaningful rather than instantly solved.
- This illustrates a general principle in game AI design: the interesting engineering problem isn't giving a system more data, but deciding how much of the system's full knowledge an in-world "agent" (like a police officer) is permitted to act on.
- Separately, animation uses "runtime retargeting" — a technique that adapts a single scripted motion (e.g., gripping a steering wheel) to fit different character body proportions, so a 5'6" and 6'2" character both look physically correct performing the same action.
- The piece stresses these are distinct, separately engineered systems — an artist sets body proportions, animation software handles physical fit, and game AI governs decision-making under incomplete information — not one unified "AI" doing everything.
- Notably, the article states Rockstar's parent company has said generative AI played no part in creating the game, positioning the described systems as traditional game engineering/AI rather than generative AI output.

## Quote

> Game AI governs what the officer can do with an incomplete report.
