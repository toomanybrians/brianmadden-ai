# Charts of the Week: There is No Gen Z

## Insights

- Navan's AI customer support agent "Ava" handled a ~5x spike in chat volume during the FAA ground-stop disruption while holding its resolution rate steady at ~80%, matching pre-disruption performance — presented as evidence AI support can scale under sudden load without degrading in a way human-only teams couldn't match.
- Enterprise AI spend/usage data is highly inconsistent across sources: Databricks reports proprietary (closed) models account for ~70% of token volume, while OpenRouter shows roughly the opposite split favoring open models.
- Per-employee AI token spend estimates diverge sharply between vendors — Ramp shows higher per-employee spend than Databricks but less acceleration, except at the top percentile — partly due to differing methodology (firm-level aggregation vs. individual human users).
- Investor sentiment on AI capex has reversed quickly: a BofA manager survey found a record 38% of respondents believe companies are "overinvesting" in AI, with a majority expecting capex cuts within six months, up sharply from ~12% two months prior.
- Smart money (hedge funds) recently executed one of the largest tech sector net-selling moves on record, described as a 4-sigma+ event, reflecting a rapid shift from bullish to bearish positioning.
- Veteran investor Stanley Druckenmiller cited unprecedented uncertainty about the 12-month outlook as reason to stay neutral, while still expecting AI to be a long-term "game changer" for medicine, cybersecurity, and outcome-based business models.

## Quote

> I can't remember a period of where we had less clarity on where we're going to be in 12 months. — Stanley Druckenmiller
