# WPP's CTO: We're making an AI-generated ad campaign every three to four days

## Insights

- WPP now produces a fully AI-generated advertising campaign (including for car brands) every three to four days — a pace its CTO expected to take years to reach when 2025 began.
- AI lets WPP bring production work in-house that was traditionally outsourced to film crews, directors, location scouts, and production companies, lowering costs for clients while increasing WPP's own revenue capture.
- The CTO frames this as workers adapting into new roles rather than being made obsolete — employees are shifting from executing production tasks to designing, monitoring, and optimizing agentic workflows.
- He predicts the creative brief itself will become AI-generated, with agents identifying marketing opportunities from real-time market data and initiating briefs rather than humans driving the process via intuition.
- Despite the automation, he insists professional AI filmmaking still requires deep creative skill — extensive scripting, storyboarding, art direction, and detailed instruction — rejecting the idea that a single prompt yields quality output.
- His career advice for young people is counterintuitive: study humanities (history, literature, psychology) rather than technology, arguing that will matter more than technical AI proficiency for future creative professionals.

## Quote

> Study the humanities, not technology.
