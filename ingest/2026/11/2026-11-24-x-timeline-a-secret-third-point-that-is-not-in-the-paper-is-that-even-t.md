# A secret third point that is not in the paper is that even the most advanced use

## Insights

- Even the most advanced current enterprise AI use cases are not very advanced, with coding as the notable exception.
- This gap implies a large "capability overhang" — existing AI capability far exceeds what organizations are actually deploying or using.
- The underlying Anthropic paper (referenced, not reproduced here) reportedly argues most AI value comes from organizational change rather than the tools themselves.
- Worker resistance to AI adoption is widespread, and is partly attributed to that organizational-change dynamic.
- Suggested mitigation: changes that expand what workers are able to do with AI, combined with sharing the resulting gains with them, could reduce resistance.

## Quote

> The capability overhang of AI is very, very large.
