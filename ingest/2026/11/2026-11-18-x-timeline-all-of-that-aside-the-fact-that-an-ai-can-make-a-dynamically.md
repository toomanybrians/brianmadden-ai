# All of that aside, the fact that an AI can make a dynamically lit explorable 3D

## Insights

- A pre-launch version of GPT-6.2 Astra was used to generate a fully coded 3D explorable scene (a space elevator ride) built with Blender and Unity, including dynamic lighting and realistic physics, with every object coded by the model.
- The author found the model "uneven and weird" in general use — specifically harder to steer and work with compared to other models.
- Despite that difficulty in everyday steerability, the model performed well on an ambitious, open-ended creative/technical project when given significant scope.
- The author frames the core takeaway as the raw capability demonstration itself — an AI producing a complete, physically realistic, explorable 3D environment via code — independent of the model's other quirks.

## Quote

> On one hand, it was harder to steer & work with than other models. On the other, when given an ambitious project... it could deliver well
