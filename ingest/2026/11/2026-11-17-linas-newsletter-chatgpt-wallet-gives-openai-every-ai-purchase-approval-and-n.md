# ChatGPT Wallet gives OpenAI every AI purchase approval and none of the liability 🤖💳; Meta’s Personal Agent Protocol reaches bank accounts before card payments 🤖🏦

## Insights

- OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet (Nov 13) with American Express, Mastercard, and Visa, shifting its strategy from hosting purchases inside ChatGPT to building the approval layer where users authorize agent purchases.
- Every purchase made by OpenAI's "dots" agents requires an OpenAI approval screen plus Face ID/Touch ID verification; OpenAI takes no fee and contractually places liability on merchants and card issuers rather than itself.
- With ChatGPT's 1.2 billion weekly users, the launch effectively settles (for now) how consumer AI agents transact: on existing card-network rails and under card-network rules, rather than via stablecoins or alternative payment rails.
- The piece frames this as confirming a prior argument that whoever controls checkout, identity, and authorization captures the take rate — and notes OpenAI is ceding that layer to card networks while gaining valuable purchase data for its ad business.
- OpenAI reportedly bypassed Stripe for this launch, a choice the piece contrasts with Meta's Muse and xAI's Grok Bot, which did not route around Stripe similarly.
- Separately, Meta and Sierra released "Poppy," version 1.0 of their Personal Agent Protocol, now live on every Shopify store and used first by Meta's Muse, with OpenAI joining as a design partner while governance remains with Sierra.

## Quote

> OpenAI takes no fee, and its terms leave the risk with merchants and card issuers.
