# 🐉 Tencent's Hy4 Trained an AI by Itself

## Insights

- Tencent's Hy4 model, paired with its Hyra 2.0 research agent, autonomously trained a new version of a third-party model (Jev) that beat the original on 6 of 8 benchmarks — framed by Tencent as groundwork for "recursive self-improvement" (AI building better AI).
- Despite this, Tencent's own published benchmarks show Hy4 far behind frontier models on hard reasoning/coding tasks (e.g., 13.0 vs. 97.5 for GPT-6.1 Sol on an internal research-math test), suggesting the self-improvement claim is more directional than competitive yet.
- OpenAI claims ChatGPT's search-triggering queries now equal roughly 20% of Google's search volume (self-reported, methodology undisclosed), a sharp contrast to Bing's ~3% share after a decade of trying — reframing the AI-vs-search competitive landscape.
- Google will disable Search entirely for K-12 student accounts unless schools also enable its Gemini AI features, prompting accusations (from Sanders, Mamdani) that bundling core infrastructure with AI adoption amounts to coercion.
- Apple's new investor filing adds a formal risk disclosure that third-party AI agents "may behave in unintended or unpredictable ways," including unauthorized actions, and that Apple's ability to control such agents "may be limited."
- A Stanford re-test of 50 widely-cited AI research findings across 20 models found 44% have already "expired" (no longer hold) within ~16 months on average; findings about capability failures (counting, arithmetic, context loss) fade fast, while behavioral tendencies (sycophancy, self-favoring judgment, political lean) persist or strengthen.
