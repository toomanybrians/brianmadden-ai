# Engineering Behind AI Agents: Harnesses, Loops & Graphs | Full Guide

## Insights

- Researchers from Heidelberg University and Singapore Management University searched 36,487 actively maintained repositories in August looking for agents running in autonomous loops.
- Only 256 repositories looked promising, and after individual inspection just 217 were confirmed as actual autonomous-loop agent implementations — roughly 0.59% of all repos searched.
- Almost every candidate repository contained code to launch/start an agent, but the deeper engineering needed for reliability was largely absent: verification, execution limits, persistent state, stopping conditions, and cost tracking.
- The findings suggest a significant gap between the volume of public discussion/advice about agent loop engineering and the actual state of implementation in real repositories.

## Quote

> The gap between launching an agent and engineering an autonomous system was bigger than the conversation suggested.
