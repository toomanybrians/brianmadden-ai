# Thing I am hearing from a number of big firms, from manufacturers to financial s

## Insights

- Small teams (2-3 people) using AI inside large organizations are reportedly producing output on the scale of what previously required dozens of employees, in less time.
- This pattern is being observed across industries, including manufacturing and financial services, suggesting it's not sector-specific.
- The productivity gap between AI-augmented small teams and traditional larger teams is triggering significant internal conflict at the management level.
- The conflict is described as being about "implications and politics" — not just technical adoption, but organizational power, roles, and structure.

## Quote

> We have scattered teams of 2-3 people using AI that are doing the work of dozens of folks.
