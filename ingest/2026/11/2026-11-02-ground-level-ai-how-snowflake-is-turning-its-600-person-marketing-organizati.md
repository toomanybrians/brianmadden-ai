# How Snowflake is turning its 600-person marketing organization into an AI-first team

## Insights

- Marketing was an early adopter of generative AI for writing, content creation, and campaign development, but Snowflake's effort targets a harder shift: moving from individual productivity gains to restructuring how an entire 600-person marketing organization operates.
- Snowflake has deployed AI agents that generate hundreds of ad variations automatically while enforcing brand guideline compliance.
- A separate AI agent can build and launch ad campaigns on platforms like LinkedIn, compressing what was previously a lengthy manual process into minutes.
- The transformation is framed as a joint effort between marketing and data leadership (CMO and chief data/AI officer), suggesting cross-functional ownership rather than marketing acting alone.
- The conversation (per topic list) also addresses a unified customer data foundation, adoption tactics across the marketing team, impacts on marketing jobs/skills, and concerns about brand trust and "AI slop."

## Quote

> AI agents that help employees create hundreds of variations of digital ads while staying within brand guidelines.
