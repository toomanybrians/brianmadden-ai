# I think there is way too much concern expressed about verification of AI output

## Insights

- Argues that concern over AI factual errors in everyday work tasks is overstated relative to current risk levels.
- Claims this concern was valid in the past but is increasingly outdated given newer model performance.
- Cites data suggesting recent AI models outperform human experts — including PhD-level specialists — at fact-finding and claim verification tasks.
- Frames the implication as: the common assumption that human review is needed to catch AI factual errors may now be backwards.

## Quote

> It used to be true, but data on more recent models suggest they beat human experts, including PhD specialists, in finding facts and checking claims.
