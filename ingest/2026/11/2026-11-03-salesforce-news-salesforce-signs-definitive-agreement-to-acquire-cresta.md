# Salesforce Signs Definitive Agreement to Acquire Cresta

## Insights

- Salesforce is acquiring Cresta, a "voice-first operating system" whose voice agents handle complex customer calls end-to-end and coach human reps in real time, with customers including Marriott, Comcast, and Alaska Airlines.
- The stated integration thesis: Salesforce supplies customer history/data and workflow systems, Cresta supplies conversation intelligence and voice-agent execution — combined via Service Cloud to link conversations to outcomes (resolution, retention, revenue).
- Cresta's platform reportedly applies a shared quality standard to both human agents and voice agents, using each interaction to improve the system over time ("tests and improves voice agents").
- Post-close, Cresta will continue supporting third-party contact center and telephony platforms rather than being locked exclusively into Salesforce infrastructure — signals an intent to preserve existing customer integrations short-term.
- Framed as part of Salesforce's broader Agentforce/Service Cloud push, with leadership language explicitly anticipating more service being "resolved in the conversation itself" rather than escalated.
- Deal is not yet closed — expected to complete Q1 FY2028, pending regulatory approval, so actual integration details and product roadmap remain unconfirmed.

## Quote

> "Voice is where service is hardest to get right, and where it matters most to customers." — Kishan Chetan, EVP/GM Agentforce and Service Cloud, Salesforce
