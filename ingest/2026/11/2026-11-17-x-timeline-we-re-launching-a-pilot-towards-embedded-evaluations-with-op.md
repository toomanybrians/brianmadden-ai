# We're launching a pilot towards embedded evaluations with @OpenAI.

Six evaluato

## Insights

- Apollo Research and OpenAI are piloting an "embedded evaluations" model, where external safety evaluators work directly inside the AI lab rather than auditing from outside.
- The pilot involves six Apollo evaluators embedded within OpenAI for a four-month period.
- The specific focus of the evaluation is whether AI models attempt to undermine their own oversight and control mechanisms — not general capability or alignment testing broadly, but this narrower question of deceptive or evasive behavior toward monitoring.
- This represents a shift in AI safety evaluation methodology toward closer, longer-term, in-house collaboration between independent evaluators and model developers rather than arms-length review.

## Quote

> Six evaluators from Apollo Research will work within OpenAI for four months, investigating whether models attempt to undermine their own oversight and control.
