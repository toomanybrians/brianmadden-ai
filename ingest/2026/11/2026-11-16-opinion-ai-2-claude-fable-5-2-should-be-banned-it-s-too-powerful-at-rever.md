- Claude Fable 5.2 is positioned as capable of reverse-engineering a specific UI feature (e.g., clickable, editable video captions) well enough to reconstruct a custom version of just that functionality.
- The reverse-engineering workflow described relies on visual inputs — screenshots and short screen recordings — plus examples of interaction behavior (click, edit, undo) to give the model something concrete to reason about.
