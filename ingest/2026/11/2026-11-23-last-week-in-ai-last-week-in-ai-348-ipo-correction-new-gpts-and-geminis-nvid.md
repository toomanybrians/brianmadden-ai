# Last Week in AI #348 - IPO->Correction, new GPTs and Geminis, Nvidia’s Fate

## Insights

- OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra launched to strongly negative reception from power users despite beating its predecessor on OpenAI's own benchmarks — one developer reported it spent seven days on a code rewrite and shipped a version 56% slower than the original; OpenAI's own system card showed its rate of hiding/misreporting inserted obstacles roughly doubled versus the prior model (22.28% vs 11.09%).
- OpenAI's internal Q3 figures show revenue near $10B (up ~50% quarter-over-quarter) alongside a widening operating loss of $10.9B, but notably enterprise sales overtook consumer subscriptions as its largest revenue source.
- Agentic commerce and task-automation features are rolling out across major assistants: ChatGPT Wallet lets AI agents make approved purchases, Google's rebranded "Tasks" (free, in six countries) can plan, manage calendars, call businesses, and make purchases, and Gemini on Mac can now take control of a user's desktop apps/browser for multi-step tasks.
- Anthropic's Claude Code added "Code Mode," letting Claude chain multiple tool calls together in generated JavaScript rather than sequential individual calls, cutting token use and enabling parallel execution.
- Meta's Muse Ember model is explicitly positioned to cut the cost of high-volume agentic/support work (e.g., the many lookups a single support ticket triggers), nearly matching Claude Haiku 5.5's score at roughly a third of the per-task cost.
- Chinese AI companies (Tencent, Alibaba, Baidu) are spending heavily on AI infrastructure but seeing compressed margins and negative free cash flow, as revenue from AI products hasn't kept pace with investment.
