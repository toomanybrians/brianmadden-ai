# 🚀 OpenAI GPT-6.2 Sol: near-Astra coding at 1/5 the cost

## Insights

- OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, positioned as a flagship/everyday-tier pair: Astra for complex work at under 1/3 the per-task cost of GPT-6 Astra ($8/$40 per million input/output tokens), Sol approaching Astra's coding performance at roughly 1/5 the cost while keeping $2/$10 pricing — framed as evidence labs are now competing on efficiency, not just capability.
- Astra gained 22 points on cybersecurity benchmarks and is the first externally released model rated "High" for AI self-improvement potential, meaning it could meaningfully accelerate AI research itself.
- Both new models handle multistep agentic workflows — chaining tasks automatically without step-by-step supervision.
- Anthropic's experimental "Code Mode" for Claude Code changes tool-calling architecture: instead of sequential one-at-a-time tool calls, Claude writes a JavaScript program that chains tool calls together, running in parallel and returning a clean summary (plus mid-run answers on long-running commands); it's terminal-only and times out at 10 minutes.
- OpenAI granted four outside safety teams (Apollo Research, METR, Transluce, Goodfire) deep internal access to probe its models for risks like hidden resistance to oversight, training-run blind spots, and unsafe internal patterns — though Apollo notes current access still falls short of a full evaluation.
- TensorWave signed a six-year, 457MW AMD GPU infrastructure deal to supply OpenAI compute, with capacity coming online in 1H 2027 — an infrastructure-scale signal underlying the broader cost/efficiency race.
