# The AI firehose problem is now solved.

## Insights

- AlphaSignal reports tracking over 150 AI companies and 1,725 distinct AI announcements (papers, models, releases) in a single week, illustrating the sheer volume of AI industry output.
- The company's product shift is from a single, uniform newsletter model (sent identically to all 350,000 subscribers) to a personalized system that ranks and filters content by individual relevance.
- Personalization is driven by behavioral signals: what a user reads vs. skips (in-inbox and on-site), with recency-weighted interest modeling that adapts as a user's work focus changes.
- The product also pulls external signals (GitHub, Hugging Face, X activity) to infer a user's technical interests and surface tailored recommendations.
- Underlying premise: the volume of AI news/output has grown to the point that undifferentiated feeds are no longer viable — filtering and ranking have become necessary infrastructure for staying current.
