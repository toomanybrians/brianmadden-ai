## Insights

- Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 nearly doubled the Remote Labor Index score of Opus 5.5 (41.3% vs 21.3%), a benchmark meant to measure performance on real remote/knowledge-work tasks.

- External testers (Vercel, Shopify, Datadog, Uber, Oracle, Snowflake, Cognition, etc.) describe the model as requiring less correction, exercising stronger engineering judgment on ambiguous tasks, and sometimes catching its own calculation/hallucination errors rather than confabulating.
