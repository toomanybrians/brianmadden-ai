# 10 Exits in Silicon Valley - Week of November 16 2026

## Insights

- Anthropic's Nasdaq IPO created roughly 2,800 employee millionaires, with 600 employees holding stakes valued at $100M+ each, and turned all seven cofounders into billionaires (each valued around $23B+).
- Anthropic's biggest outside shareholders are Amazon (14.6%, $219B) and Google (13.4%, $201B), followed by a long list of VC firms and individual investors (Lightspeed, ICONIQ, Menlo, Sequoia, Eric Schmidt, etc.), illustrating how concentrated AI-lab value creation has become among a small set of early backers.
- Applied Compute, an AI post-training/inference infrastructure startup founded by three ex-OpenAI researchers in 2025, was acquired by Palo Alto Networks for $4.5B less than 17 months after founding — after reaching $100M annualized revenue and raising $350M just three weeks prior.
- Weekly funding activity shows AI infrastructure as a distinct, active category: startups building agent infrastructure (Linq, $90M), agent governance/security (Metano), document extraction for agents (Unsiloed AI), and reserved inference capacity marketplaces (Touchmark) are all raising fresh capital.
- Other large AI-adjacent M&A in the same week: AMD acquired spatial-intelligence startup World Labs for $8.2B, and Workday acquired Honeydew, a semantic layer for AI business intelligence — signaling large incumbents actively buying AI capability rather than building it all in-house.
- The pace and scale of these AI-related exits (IPO, billion-dollar acquisitions, rapid founder-to-exit timelines) reflect an unusually compressed value-creation cycle concentrated in AI infrastructure and foundation-model-adjacent companies.
