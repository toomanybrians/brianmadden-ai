# Nov. 16 - AI frameworks help hackers | P2P2Shell used to target firewalls

## Insights

- Google warned that agentic AI frameworks are pushing automated hacking tools to an "inflection point," becoming the latest major authority to flag this shift.
- A sponsored survey found 91% of security practitioners plan to give AI systems more autonomy in 2027, but distinguishes between trusting AI for detection versus trusting it for full containment/response actions.
- Arctic Wolf's cited report claims agentic AI now resolves over 60% of security investigations without human intervention, based on 13 months of operation at scale.
- A White House task force stated that existing regulatory standards for cyber defense are outdated for the AI era and suggested companies may deserve liability relief for AI-fueled cyberattacks.
- Separately, attackers chained a PHP2Shell vulnerability to target unpatched Check Point firewalls, in some cases deploying destructive wiper malware alongside political messaging — illustrating that AI-driven offense and traditional exploit chains are both live threats simultaneously.
- Guardrail guidance (via sponsor content) emphasizes that before AI agents are granted system access, organizations need privilege controls, behavior monitoring, and continuous trust assessment.
