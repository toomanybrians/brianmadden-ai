# 🔮 The $22 billion question about AI self-improvement #610

## Insights

- UK consumer AI subscriptions have grown fast: 5% of adults now pay for AI tools (over $1.3B/year), up from 2.5% a year ago and 1% two years prior; adoption skews toward tech, finance, education, and PR professionals, concentrated in London.
- Claude's market share among UK AI subscribers has quadrupled in a year, possibly linked to an improved "harness" via Claude Cowork.
- AI shopping agents are being used for narrow, well-defined tasks — securing scarce/limited products (iPhone drops, winter sweaters, finding niche items like surge protectors or medicine pouches) — but not yet for simple, low-friction tasks like booking an Uber (only 4 of 320 million Halloween-week rides were agent-booked).
- Adding wind to solar and batteries lowers median energy costs significantly (e.g., $105 to $86/MWh broadly, $164 to $99/MWh in some regions), per modeling referenced from Ember.
- Renewables plus gas backup are already cost-competitive (under $100/MWh) with 83% of currently planned fossil fuel plants, according to the modeling cited.
- Scaling a renewable-based grid requires more than falling technology costs — it also needs improved cost of capital for renewables (currently worse than gas) and new financial instruments to handle renewable energy price volatility.
