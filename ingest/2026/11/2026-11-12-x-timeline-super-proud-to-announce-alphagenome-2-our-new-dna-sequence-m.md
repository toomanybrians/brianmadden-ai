# Super proud to announce AlphaGenome 2 - our new DNA sequence model! 🧬 It predict

## Insights

- Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome 2, a DNA sequence model that predicts how genetic variants affect specific cell types, aimed at understanding complex diseases at a cellular level.
- The model's key advance over its predecessor is improved cell-type specificity — distinguishing why a single DNA change might affect one tissue (e.g., heart) while sparing others (e.g., brain, immune cells) despite shared DNA.
- AlphaGenome 2 can generalize to a user's own single-cell RNA-seq data and impute unobserved data modalities, rather than being limited to pre-trained cell types.
- The model is paired with "AlphaGenome Atlas," a separate resource offering pre-computed variant-impact scores (AVI score) across roughly 9 billion variants genome-wide, accessible via API or a no-code website.
- Access is positioned as free for non-commercial/academic research use via API, with query rate limits that vary by demand and by whether users query the Atlas versus running live model predictions.
- The product ships with extensive documentation (tutorials, API reference, vignettes on specific gene loci) suggesting a mature, developer-facing research tool rather than a consumer product.
