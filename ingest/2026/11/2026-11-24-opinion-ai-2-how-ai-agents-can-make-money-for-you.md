# How AI Agents Can Make Money for You

## Insights

- Frames a shift in AI usage: from 2023-style "ask a question, get an answer" chatbot interaction to agents that take semi-autonomous action while a human still approves the final step.
- Argues the money-making opportunity isn't in flashy tech-company use cases but in overlooked, unglamorous small-business workflows (wedding venues, catering, trades).
- Illustrates the opportunity with concrete billing/reconciliation gaps: a caterer billed for 80 guests when 110 attended, a plumber's extra part never added to the invoice — situations where facts went unconnected and money was left uncollected.
- Proposes a minimal agent design pattern: connect two disconparate facts, surface proof, and prepare one actionable recommendation, leaving the final decision to the business owner.
- Suggests this pattern can be monetized two ways: building it for one's own business, or packaging it as a paid service sold to other business owners.

## Quote

> Each needs an agent that can connect two facts, show its proof, and prepare one useful action.
