# Agent swarms have the potential to drive yet another leap in output from AI. The

## Insights

- Agent swarms represent a new leap in AI-driven output, following earlier leaps from chat-based AI (quick turnaround responses).
- This shift is expected to create a clear divide between teams/organizations that adopt swarm-based agent workflows and those that don't.
- AI usage, even among people already considered highly AI-native, follows a power-law distribution — a small subset drives disproportionate output.
- A new category of worker is emerging — "swarm riders" — defined by their ability to consistently and productively direct large numbers of AI agents at once.
- This implies a parallel concept of "swarm-native companies," organizations structured around this new mode of AI-agent orchestration.

## Quote

> There seems to be a new class of people — call them "swarm riders."
