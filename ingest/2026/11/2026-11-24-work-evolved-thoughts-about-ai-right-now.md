# Thoughts about AI right now

## Insights

- Enterprise AI tools (e.g. high-tier Copilot) lag roughly a year behind frontier capabilities due to data privacy rules, guardrails, capped token budgets, and poor voice features — keeping usage at "assistance" level rather than true augmentation or agentic work.
- This frontier/internal gap creates compounding organizational risk: leadership sees high AI spend without ROI (partly because productivity gains are poorly measured), employees covertly use external tools or recording devices exposing companies to data/legal risk, and few organizations invest in foundational work (data structuring, process redesign) needed for future gains.
- Designing workflows for AI is framed as a distinct, learnable skill — most people prompt AI reactively rather than intentionally redesigning end-to-end workflows; those who practice this skill report less fear about job market disruption.
- AI has become financially systemic: market exposure to AI (via pensions, investments) means volatility (cited market drops, Anthropic's post-IPO value swings) now materially affects most people's financial futures, independent of whether one personally uses or endorses AI.
- Public opinion on AI is politically polarized by geography — majority of Americans surveyed believe AI will do more harm than good, contrasted with more optimistic sentiment in regions with more economic upside or institutional trust; real-world AI-linked infrastructure failures are cited as accelerating backlash.
- Invokes the "Bitter Lesson" (Sutton) — general compute/data-driven methods outperforming hand-crafted expertise — citing a claimed Riemann hypothesis breakthrough by a mathematician working with Claude as evidence that human-AI collaboration, not AI alone, currently produces exceptional results in verifiable domains.
- Offers concrete individual practices for building AI fluency: redesign one full workflow, protect weekly practice time, shift from "asking" to "delegating" full tasks, organize underlying data/documents, and find peer groups for collective sensemaking.

## Quote

> Humans who use AI will win out over those who don't.
