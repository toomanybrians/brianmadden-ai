# Salesforce Data: Record Cyber Week Expected to Deliver $75.8B in AI-Driven Sales

## Insights

- Salesforce projects AI/agents will influence $75.8B (21%) of global Cyber Week online sales, up from $67B in 2025 — a vendor-generated forecast based on proprietary modeling, not audited results.
- AI referral traffic to retail sites is up 104% YoY and is claimed to convert at 2x the rate of typical ecommerce traffic; 20% of holiday shopping traffic is projected to be nonhuman (bots/agents).
- Only 31% of consumers report having tried a personal AI shopping agent (e.g., Meta's Muse, OpenAI's Dots), and just 8% say their agent has made a purchase for them — adoption still early despite headline AI-sales figures.
- Trust/friction gap: 74% say they trust AI recommendations, but 73% are not willing to hand payment credentials to a personal agent — suggesting agentic commerce is still discovery/comparison-focused, not transaction-complete.
- Brands with deployed shopper agents (cited examples: SharkNinja, Hibbett Sports) report 8% higher order value, 23% bigger baskets, and claimed 7x faster sales growth than non-agent competitors — company-supplied comparative stats, not independently verified.
- One cited deployment detail: Hibbett's Salesforce-powered shop agent reportedly handles 90% of core shopper journeys, paired with Agentforce Voice for order status/returns with live-agent handoff.

## Quote

> "The brands ready to meet that demand with their own shopper agents will see the biggest returns this holiday season." — Caila Schwartz, Salesforce
