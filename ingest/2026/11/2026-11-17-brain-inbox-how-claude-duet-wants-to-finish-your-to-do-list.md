# ⚙️ How Claude Duet wants to finish your to-do list

## Insights

- Persistent agents (Claude Duet, OpenAI Dots, Meta Muse, GrokBot) represent a shift from reactive assistants to background agents that run continuously on cloud compute, monitor files/messages, and proactively surface work.
- Duet's distinguishing behavior is being task-oriented rather than informational: instead of just flagging a conflict or change (as Dots does), it drafts the actual next step (a document, an email reply) and offers a Start/Edit button.
- Anthropic frames Duet's core value as "judgment" — deciding what needs human input, what can wait, and when to check in — and says calibrating approval frequency is a deliberate design problem due to "approval fatigue."
- Anthropic reports that urgency is highly individual, making a generalized evaluation metric for "what's important" imperfect but still useful, and something being tuned via early-tester feedback.
- Every Duet-drafted message/file passes through a safety classifier for sensitive information before being surfaced, a built-in compliance/safety layer for an agent with broad access.
- The author anticipates enterprise systems-of-record vendors will offer their own persistent agents for secure/compliant internal data handling, with a further stage where personal and enterprise agents communicate directly with each other.

## Quote

> "Claude Duet's biggest differentiator is its judgment... It is meant to be a resourceful agent that decides what needs you, what can wait, and when to check with you first."
