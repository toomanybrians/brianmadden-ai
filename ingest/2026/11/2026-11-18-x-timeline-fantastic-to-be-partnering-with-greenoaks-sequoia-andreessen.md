# Fantastic to be partnering with Greenoaks, Sequoia, Andreessen Horowitz, and man

## Insights

- Isomorphic Labs raised $5 billion in a Series C round, co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, with Andreessen Horowitz and other new investors joining, plus continued major investment from Alphabet.
- Existing investors also participated, including Thrive Capital, MGX, GV, CapitalG, Temasek, and the UK Sovereign AI Fund — signaling broad continuity of institutional backing.
- The company frames its mission as using AI to redesign drug discovery "from first principles," aiming to cut the time and cost of finding new medicines.
- Isomorphic Labs is scaling a proprietary AI drug design engine (IsoDDE) and an AI-designed therapeutic candidate pipeline as the core use of the new funding.
- The announcement positions AI-driven biomedical R&D as a major investment category, with legacy VC and sovereign funds betting at large scale on AI replacing traditional drug design methods.
