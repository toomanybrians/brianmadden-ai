# "I believe frontier model developers should jointly endeavour to establish share

## Insights

- Sam Altman proposes that frontier AI model developers jointly establish shared norms for evaluating models and red-teaming safety cases before first deployment.
- This includes a call for shared transparency standards across developers, not just internal/proprietary safety processes.
- He further proposes "common limits" on model capabilities or deployment, though the specific basis for these limits is cut off/unspecified in the text.
- In a reply to his own post, he frames OpenAI's pretraining progress transparency as part of this effort, calling it "the most important thing OpenAI has published in awhile."
- He explicitly ties shared norms and limits to safety and control, suggesting industry-wide coordination (rather than unilateral action) as the preferred path.

## Quote

> We are providing a lot of transparency into our pretraining progress and hope we can agree as a field to common limits.
