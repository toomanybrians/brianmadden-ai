# When the fix makes the outage worse #microsoft #outage #tech #ai

## Insights

- Microsoft's email outage recovery effort backfired: redirecting traffic to healthy servers during the fix overloaded those servers too, extending the outage to nearly a full day.
- The incident illustrates a cascading-failure pattern where the remediation step itself becomes a new point of failure.
- Raises the question of how security/ops teams can act quickly during incident response without the response itself creating new damage.
- Frames this as newly urgent because defenders are now expected to react at "AI speed" against attackers who also move faster, compressing the time available for safe, careful fixes.

## Quote

> How fast can you safely act when your last fix just made things worse?
