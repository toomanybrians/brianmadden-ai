# Folks who are very close to AI news on X tend to extrapolate too much from each

## Insights

- Observers who closely follow AI news on X tend to over-extrapolate broader trends from any single new model release.
- Anthropic had a rough patch with Opus model releases before Opus 5.5 arrived; Google similarly had an extended weak period in the past, yet both recovered.
- A new model (6.2) being unimpressive is framed as a single data point, not strong evidence of a trend or plateau.
- Separately (via quoted post), model "personality" — a quality not captured by benchmarks — is cited as important; Opus models reportedly lost their distinctive "Claude-y" feel for a stretch (roughly versions 4.7 to 5), resembling a "watered-down" alternative model, before Opus 5.5 restored that familiar character.
- This personality drift is speculatively linked to the use of "teacher models" in training.
