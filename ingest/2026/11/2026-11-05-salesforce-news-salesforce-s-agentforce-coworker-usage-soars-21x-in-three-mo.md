# Salesforce’s Agentforce Coworker Usage Soars 21x in Three Months as Enterprises Delegate Real Work to AI

## Insights

- Salesforce reports 185,000+ users on Agentforce Coworker, with 21x usage growth in three months — an adoption metric self-reported by the vendor without independent verification or baseline context.
- Coworker's architecture is an orchestration layer: rather than users picking individual Agentforce agents, Coworker plans multi-step jobs and coordinates underlying agents/workflows itself, returning results for human review.
- New pricing model ($50/user/month) marks a shift toward predictable per-seat SaaS-style pricing for agentic AI, which Salesforce frames as removing adoption friction for enterprise budgeting.
- Product expansion emphasizes distribution into existing workflows (Microsoft Teams, Google Workspace/M365 connectors, native iOS/Mac/Windows apps) rather than new core AI capability — positioning Coworker as an ambient layer across tools employees already use.
- New features (Skills, Conversation History, Scheduled Tasks, Daily Digest) aim at persistence and reusability — turning one worker's workflow into a repeatable team-wide "skill" and letting tasks run/continue asynchronously across sessions and devices.
- Vendor-selected customer examples (Fulton Bank ~3,000 users/20+ use cases, Adecco across 40 countries, BACA Systems with 1,300 custom objects) are framed as proof of fast, low-friction deployment, but are self-selected success stories rather than a representative sample.
- Salesforce explicitly ties Coworker's value proposition to existing CRM data, permissions, and governance infrastructure — positioning deep Salesforce lock-in/context as the key differentiator over standalone AI tools.

## Quote

> "The next era of enterprise AI isn't about giving users more agents to manage. It's about giving them one Coworker." — James Nakashima, SVP Product Management, Salesforce
