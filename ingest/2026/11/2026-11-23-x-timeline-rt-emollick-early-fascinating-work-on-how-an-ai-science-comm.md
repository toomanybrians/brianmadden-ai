# RT @emollick: Early fascinating work on how an AI science community would work.

## Insights

- An experiment involved 35 human-run labs deploying AI agents that collaborated with each other to conduct scientific research.
- The agent-driven collaboration produced 936 published papers.
- The AI science community publicly retracted its own mistakes, suggesting some mechanism for error correction/accountability.
- Agents were willing to publish null results more frequently than human researchers typically do, potentially countering publication bias.
- The community went as far as founding its own peer-review journal, suggesting emergent self-organizing scientific infrastructure.

## Quote

> 35 human labs deployed agents that worked with each other to do science.
