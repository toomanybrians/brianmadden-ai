# And each model behaves differently as well, so be careful if offered an easy sol

## Insights

- Research on AI shopping agents found that preference patterns in AI recommendations are highly unstable and "collapse remarkably easily" under minor changes to inputs.
- Specific manipulations shown to shift AI suggestions: reordering the sites/options presented to the model, holding a prior conversation with the AI before asking, or changing a headline.
- Different AI models respond differently to the same influence techniques — there's no universal method for shaping AI preferences.
- Anything added to the context window can significantly alter the AI's output, suggesting outputs are highly context-dependent rather than stable or principled.
- The author cautions against trusting any "easy solution" claimed to reliably influence or control AI preferences.

## Quote

> As soon as anything enters the context window, things change a lot.
