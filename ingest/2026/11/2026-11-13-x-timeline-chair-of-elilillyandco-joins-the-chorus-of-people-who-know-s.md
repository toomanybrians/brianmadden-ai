# Chair of @EliLillyandCo joins the chorus of people who know something about biol

## Insights

- Gary Marcus cites the Chair of Eli Lilly as another biology-credentialed figure publicly disputing claims made by Dario (Amodei, implied Anthropic CEO) about AI's capabilities in biology/drug discovery.
- Marcus frames this as part of a pattern: multiple lifetime medical researchers, described as generally AI-positive (not AI skeptics), are pushing back on specific claims rather than on AI broadly.
- Marcus implies the timing is notable — the pushback comes "on the eve of" an IPO, suggesting a motive of hype inflation tied to a financial event.
- The quoted Eli Lilly Chair (Dave Ricks) argues biology remains largely unmapped ("the map of biology is still largely a blank page") and that randomized human trials remain the rate-limiting step, not computation.
- Ricks' stated expectation is that AI's impact on the biology/pharma sector will be "steady and incremental" rather than transformative or rapid, despite being personally enthusiastic about AI's use in the sector.

## Quote

> The map of biology is still largely a blank page and randomized human proof is the long pole in the tent.
