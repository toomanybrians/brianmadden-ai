# How to Claim Your Business in ChatGPT

## Insights

- OpenAI now lets businesses create a "Business Agent" inside ChatGPT — a separate, labeled chat where users can ask product-specific questions (e.g., licensing terms) directly to a business's own profile, with answers drawn from seller-supplied product details and terms.
- The agent speaks on behalf of the seller ("our licence" = the business), and the conversation is clearly distinguished from the user's original ChatGPT chat.
- Businesses get aggregate analytics (mentions, website clicks) from these interactions, but the actual conversation content — both the user's original chat and their chat with the agent — remains private from the business.
- This creates a new pre-purchase touchpoint: a customer can get a purchase-deciding answer (like licensing terms) through ChatGPT without ever visiting the seller's website.
- The piece frames claiming a business profile as newly consequential — the content and reliability of answers the agent gives may now influence buying decisions before a customer reaches the site.

## Quote

> When the agent says "our licence," our means the seller.
