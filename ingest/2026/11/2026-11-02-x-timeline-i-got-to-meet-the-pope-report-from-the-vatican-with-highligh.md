# I got to meet the Pope! Report from the Vatican, with highlights from @Pontifex’

## Insights

- Pope Leo XIV's encyclical ("Magnifica Humanitas") argues AI's trajectory isn't predetermined and that reliability/foreseeable control matters more than raw capability; it rejects legal personhood for AI, insists certain decisions (targeting, border control, use of force) must retain human oversight, and calls for international institutions with shared standards and accountability mechanisms rather than just more ethical principles.
- Marcus argues current AI (LLMs) excels at next-word prediction but is weak at reasoning, fact-fidelity, and instruction-following — systems will fabricate citations or use copyrighted material even when explicitly told not to, because they follow correlation rather than causation/semantics.
- He distinguishes "narrow" AI (GPS navigation, AlphaFold) — bounded, task-specific, low-risk — from "open-ended" agentic AI with broad internet/credential access, which he says is responsible for a sharp rise in incidents (cites Hugging Face breach, French refrigeration system attack, Japan cyberattacks) — allegedly ~10,000 incidents recently.
- Marcus calls for a mandatory or voluntary "product recall" of unsafe AI agents, comparing them to Ford Pintos, and argues AI companies know their agent-based products are unreliable but deploy them anyway because agents burn more tokens and generate more revenue amid unsustainable company finances.
- He frames the moment as a "red line" decision point: either regulate/pull unsafe open-ended agents from market now, or lose moral authority to ever regulate AI safety later.
- On AI consciousness, Marcus argues apparent self-referential statements (e.g., an AI claiming "my children go to school at X") reflect meaningless word correlations, not genuine experience — consciousness requires experience, which these systems lack.

> We are building machines that are actually sort of unequally skilled... not so skilled at reasoning, not so skilled at sticking to facts.
