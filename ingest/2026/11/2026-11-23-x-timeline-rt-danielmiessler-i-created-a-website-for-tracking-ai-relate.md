# RT @DanielMiessler: I created a website for tracking AI-related hacking incident

## Insights

- A new tracking site, hackingincidents.ai, logs 289 publicly reported cyberattacks (Jan 2023–Nov 2026) where AI was known or suspected to be used, graded by evidence strength (Confirmed, Strong, Suspected).
- Incident volume has grown sharply year over year: 5 in 2023, 27 in 2024, 80 in 2025, and 177 already by mid-November 2026, suggesting accelerating reported use of AI in attacks.
- In 2026 so far, AI reportedly did actual attack work (not just incidental use) in 79 of 177 incidents, up from 13 of 80 in 2025 and 0 of 27 in 2024.
- The ledger distinguishes how autonomously AI operated: assisting a human (197 cases), semi-autonomous (67), and fully running an attack on its own (25) — with the first fully autonomous AI-run attack dated to mid-2025.
- Only 119 of 289 incidents name the specific model used; named models in recent entries include DeepSeek and GLM, often used in coordinated multi-agent attack operations (e.g., "BOSSAI," "Cairn").
- The site explicitly caveats that it measures published reports, not actual incident counts, and that only 57 of 289 incidents have two or more independent sources confirming them.
