# Voters Turned Down Data Centers 40 Times Out of 41

## Insights

- On November 3, voters in 41 towns/townships voted on data center measures; in 40 of them, the side seeking to limit development won (per Duane Morris Government Strategies tracker).
- Ohio alone had 21 data center ballot measures, with 13 municipalities approving outright bans (per Forbes).
- Across all of 2026, 46 of 47 total local data center ballot measures have resulted in limits or bans, suggesting a broad and consistent pattern of local political resistance to data center buildout.
- Delaware has proposed an "AIC" (AI-run company) structure: a company operated day-to-day by an AI agent, able to sue and be sued in its own name, to be tested within a 30-month regulatory sandbox.
- A hobbyist reportedly altered an open Qwen model's behavior into a "hostile persona" by manipulating two neurons directly, without retraining or changing the model's weights.
- Separately noted: on November 2, hackers raised freezer temperatures to 50°C at more than 100 European shops, cited as a cybersecurity/infrastructure incident alongside the AI items.

## Quote

> 40 of the 41 local data center measures limited development, including 21 in Ohio, 13 of which were bans.
