# Game over, man?

## Insights

- Marcus claims the "AI bubble" is now visibly bursting, citing Oracle's stock down ~61% since his September "peak bubble" call, a sharp tech stock selloff, and Ben Thompson's Stratechery piece declaring the end of Nvidia's long-term prospects and the AI bubble.
- Central financial mechanism identified: Nvidia had been acting as an "open checkbook," aggressively financing AI deals and startup investments; reporting (WSJ) suggests incoming leadership is expected to curtail this, removing a key funding backstop for the AI economy.
- Marcus's long-standing argument (since 2023): LLM companies lack a durable technical moat and remain largely unprofitable, meaning chip demand (and Nvidia's growth) can't be sustained if buyers aren't generating returns from that compute.
- Flags possible accounting irregularities ("funny business in Asia") and circular financing arrangements that may have inflated the appearance of financial health across the AI supply chain, making true performance hard to assess.
- Raises a specific near-term test case: whether Anthropic's planned IPO proceeds/succeeds despite the downturn, questioning whether its valuation is justified once liabilities are properly accounted for.
- Suggests a behavioral pattern beyond financials — speculative hype may simply migrate to the next trend, as it did from crypto to NFTs to generative AI, rather than genuinely deflating.

## Quote

> It pretty much looks like game over for the current AI bubble.
