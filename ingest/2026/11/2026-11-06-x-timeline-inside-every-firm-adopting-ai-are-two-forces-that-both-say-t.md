# Inside every firm adopting AI are two forces that both say they are "working on

## Insights

- Frames AI adoption inside firms as a contest between two internal factions, both claiming the same "AI transformation" label but pursuing opposite goals.
- One faction is "AI-pilled" — treating AI as either an existential threat or a major opportunity requiring real organizational change.
- The other faction wants AI framed as a "regular technology" specifically so that existing structures, roles, and processes don't have to change.
- Suggests that surface-level consensus language ("we're working on AI transformation") can mask deep disagreement about whether transformation should actually happen.

## Quote

> ...a set of people who are AI-pilled...& a set who desperately want everyone to view AI as a regular technology so nothing has to change.
