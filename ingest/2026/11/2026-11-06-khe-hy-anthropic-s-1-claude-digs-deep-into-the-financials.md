# Anthropic S-1: Claude digs deep into the financials

## Insights

- Used Claude to build an interactive "artifact" for navigating a 400-page Anthropic S-1, prioritizing targeted drill-down into specific sections over a generic summary.
- Designed a three-tier source hierarchy for the analysis — official filings first, financial media (WSJ, FT, Bloomberg) second, blogs/newsletters third — and had Claude label sources by tier.
- Used Claude's inline "threads" (comment-style) feature to iteratively refine the artifact, including adding links back to exact source text in the original document.
- Extended the analysis with "read-across" verification: cross-checking Anthropic's circular compute/chip transactions (e.g., with Broadcom, SpaceX's Colossus deal) against how counterparties describe the same deals in their own filings.
- Connected a Readwise MCP integration to pull previously saved articles as additional tier-3 sources, noting the enterprise equivalent would be a connector like CapIQ or FactSet.
- Frames the exercise as a test case for distinguishing AI used as an "amplifier" of analytical judgment versus a "shortcut," and introduces the idea of becoming a "meat proxy" — a human who merely relays AI-generated findings for others who won't prompt the model themselves.
