# ⚙️Why AI has ended security by obscurity

## Insights

- AI-powered vulnerability discovery (demonstrated by OpenAI's find of long-known PHP flaws used across ~70% of websites) is eliminating "security by obscurity" — the assumption that obscure or old vulnerabilities, or small organizational size, provide practical protection.
- Vulnerabilities previously deemed "unexploitable" for over a decade became exploitable once AI changed the economics/capability of discovering and weaponizing them, per Palo Alto Networks' Rich Campagna.
- The "defender's window" (time to find/fix flaws before attackers do) is collapsing — CrowdStrike reports exploit time has shrunk to hours, prompting calls to cut patch windows from 30 days to 30 minutes.
- AI lowers the cost of attack, which paradoxically makes smaller, less-resourced organizations the most exposed: they're now targets by default (profit-motivated attackers no longer discriminate by size) but often lack awareness or resources to defend.
- The proposed defense is symmetrical: embedding AI into the software development lifecycle so defenders use the same discovery capabilities attackers would, finding and patching flaws pre-release to regain the advantage.
- Large, resourced enterprises in regulated/critical sectors (finance, healthcare, infrastructure) are more likely to already treat this risk seriously; the exposure gap is concentrated among smaller, less-prepared organizations.

## Quote

> We can no longer have this situation where weaknesses that are known to exist can be assumed to be non-exploitable. — Rich Campagna
