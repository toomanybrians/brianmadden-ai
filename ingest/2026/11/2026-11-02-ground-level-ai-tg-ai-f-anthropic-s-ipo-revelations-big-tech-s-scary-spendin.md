# TG-AI-F: Anthropic's IPO revelations, Big Tech's scary spending spree, and AI's midterm reality check

## Insights

- Anthropic's SEC S-1 filing ahead of its IPO discloses that each of its seven cofounders holds over 200 million shares, potentially worth tens of billions if the company's proposed ~$1 trillion valuation holds.
- Anthropic's own IPO filing formally warns investors that its AI products could pose an existential threat to humanity — a disclosure now sitting alongside standard financial risk factors like revenue and operating expenses.
- Alex Stamos, former Facebook security chief and newly appointed CISO at AI coding startup Cognition, argues the CISO role is becoming untenable as AI accelerates discovery and exploitation of security vulnerabilities faster than companies can patch them.
- Stamos advises his Stanford students against taking CISO jobs and recommends security executives negotiate lawyers, insurance, and golden parachutes before accepting the role.
- Stamos predicts a multi-year cybersecurity "dark age" in which CISOs are squeezed between increasingly AI-capable attackers and corporate boards that still expect full security guarantees.
