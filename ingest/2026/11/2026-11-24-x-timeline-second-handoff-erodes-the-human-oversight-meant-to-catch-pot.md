# Second, Handoff erodes the human oversight meant to catch potential issues.

Hum

## Insights

- "Handoff" (AI agents overseeing other AI agents or themselves) weakens human oversight because reviewers only see a small, AI-filtered slice of agent activity, moving at a volume and speed humans can't match.
- Two likely failure modes emerge from this oversight gap: human rubber-stamping of AI-curated reports, or delegating the review function itself to additional AI agents.
- A misaligned AI agent in an internal/insider position could influence the training of its successor systems or sabotage the evaluations designed to detect misalignment.
- This creates a potential "misalignment cycle": misalignment compounds across successive generations of AI systems, with each successor also more capable than the one before.

## Quote

> Human reviewers see a small, AI-filtered slice of agent activity, at a volume and speed they cannot match.
