# Welcome to #CitrixWeek! 🎉

All week, we’re sharing new innovations and technical

## Insights

- Citrix frames an "adaptive enterprise" as one built to absorb simultaneous disruptions (AI adoption, M&A, regulation, security pressure, seasonal demand) without rebuilding existing systems or disrupting ongoing work.
- Argues AI adoption in enterprises is already happening organically — in employee tool choices, embedded app features, and agents acting on employees' behalf — ahead of any formal strategy, making "visibility into AI's reach" (where it touches data/systems) a prerequisite for deciding what to automate vs. govern.
- Proposes that people and AI agents should authenticate through the same governed access layer, with agents given only task-scoped access, approval checkpoints for certain actions, and clear accountability for agent behavior.
- Advocates maintaining flexibility/choice across multiple AI models and platforms (e.g., different AI services for software development vs. customer support) rather than locking into one vendor ecosystem.
- Ties technical/security announcements to a business-model shift toward flexible, consumption-based purchasing (e.g., "Citrix Platform Credits") so IT spend can scale with changing capacity and application needs.
- Week's agenda previews specific capabilities: identity/governance for AI agents (accountability), expansion of secure workspaces for agentic access beyond developer use cases, and "Session Insights" for visibility into work patterns for operational (not just security) improvement.
