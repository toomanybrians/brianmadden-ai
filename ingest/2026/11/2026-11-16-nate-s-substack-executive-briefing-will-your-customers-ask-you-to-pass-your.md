# Executive Briefing: Will Your Customers Ask You to Pass Your AI Savings Through in Price, Like Infosys's Clients Did? How to Find the Price That Protects Your Earnings.

## Insights

- Ramp data shows AI token spending growth flattening (near-zero growth in August/September, 1.65% in October) while token usage volume keeps rising — enterprises are getting more AI output without paying proportionately more.
- Buyer behavior has shifted: enterprise teams no longer ask which model to use, they test cheaper models against their own requirements and adopt them if they pass, rather than defaulting to the most expensive/capable option.
- This "eval fluency" — the ability to define good work, compare model outputs, and quantify what a difference is worth — is becoming a direct source of purchasing power, shifting leverage from AI suppliers to buyers/procurement.
- Suppliers can retain an account while losing their pricing premium: a customer using five times the tokens can still end up with the same bill via cheaper models or negotiated discounts.
- The piece frames most businesses as sitting on both sides of this dynamic — they buy AI/intelligence as input, then sell a product/service where their own customers may start demanding similar AI-driven savings be passed through in price.
- A worked example shows AI-driven margin improvement (40% to 50%) can get fully offset by price cuts customers demand, leaving kept dollars per job unchanged — illustrating how savings get competed away.

## Quote

> Eval fluency is becoming a source of purchasing power.
