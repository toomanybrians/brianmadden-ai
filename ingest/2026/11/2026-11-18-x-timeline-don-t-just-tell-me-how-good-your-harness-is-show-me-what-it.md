# Don’t just tell me how good your harness is. Show me what it produces. 

- sites

## Insights

- Argues that AI "harnesses" (agent/tool frameworks) should be evaluated by their concrete output, not by claims about the harness's capability.
- Proposes a list of tangible outputs as the real evidence of value: sites, mobile apps, designs, apps, tools, products, services, and ultimately "outcomes."
- Frames evaluation around three practical questions: does it help people, does it make money, has it improved your life.
- Positions "outcomes" as the category that supersedes mere artifacts — the harness should be judged on results, not on the sophistication of the process.
