# This wouldn’t have sounded possible a year ago, but it’s becoming much more reas

## Insights

- Claims that recent progress in AI models and "harnesses" (the tooling/scaffolding around models) is now enabling roughly 10X output gains for some teams, a jump described as unbelievable even a year prior.
- This is producing sharply uneven output levels between teams within the same company, rather than uniform productivity gains across an organization.
- Secondhand reports from large firms across manufacturing and financial services describe small teams of 2-3 people using AI to match the output of "dozens" of traditional workers, in far less time.
- This disparity is reportedly triggering significant internal management conflict and political tension over what these productivity gaps mean organizationally — implying unresolved questions about roles, headcount, and credit.

## Quote

> We have scattered teams of 2-3 people using AI that are doing the work of dozens of folks in a fraction of the time.
