# All of this focus on individual releases also misses the broader point with much

## Insights

- The author argues that focusing on individual AI model releases misses the larger story: the pace of model development itself is accelerating continuously.
- This ongoing acceleration is framed as the more consequential trend to track, rather than any single model's capabilities or benchmarks.
- Different models may excel at different use cases, so having multiple models with varying strengths/weaknesses is presented as valuable rather than something to resolve into one "best" model.
- Model versions are expected to be short-lived — current models get tweaked and new versions (e.g., a future "Astra") arrive quickly, reinforcing the acceleration point.

## Quote

> rapid, continuing acceleration of model development is very obviously happening and is the thing to actually pay attention to
