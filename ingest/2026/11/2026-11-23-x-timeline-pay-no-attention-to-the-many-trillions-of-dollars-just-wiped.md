# Pay no attention to the many trillions of dollars just wiped out across the US,

## Insights

- Gary Marcus highlights a sharp market downturn, citing roughly $2.5 trillion in value wiped from Nvidia alone, alongside broader losses across US, Asian, and European markets.
- He notes that several high-profile ("monster") IPOs have moved underwater or close to it, framing this as evidence of AI-related market excess correcting.
- Marcus's tone is sarcastic, mocking those he sees as downplaying the scale of the losses as insignificant.
- A quoted reply (@Domi_ka19) pushes back, arguing the "frontier race" in AI is not slowing and citing Nvidia's ~100% yearly revenue growth and ~25% quarterly growth, plus "dacatener" (likely datacenter) revenue growth of 110%.
- The reply frames stock price drops as insufficient evidence of an AI slowdown, pointing instead to underlying revenue growth as the more meaningful signal.
