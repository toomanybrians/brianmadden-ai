# The FBI re-issued this FLASH this morning and changed one claim.

The Nov 13 ver

## Insights

- An FBI FLASH advisory on ARTEX — described as an open-source agentic pentest framework — has been used by attackers since at least August, according to the author's claim.
- The FBI revised a key factual claim in the advisory: the Nov 13 version said a ConnectWise-related campaign "enabled access to more than 2,250 downstream tenants," while the reissued version downgrades this to "resulted in attempted sign-ins" to those tenants.
- The correction implies a meaningful distinction between confirmed unauthorized access and mere login attempts — a distinction the author treats as significant enough to highlight publicly.
- Some intrusions tied to this campaign reportedly progressed from initial targeting to privileged access, per the author's reading of the advisory (detail cut off in the source text).
- The author frames the ARTEX framework and its use in active attacks as something they had anticipated or worried about previously.
