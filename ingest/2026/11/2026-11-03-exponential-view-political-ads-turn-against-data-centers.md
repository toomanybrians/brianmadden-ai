# 📈 Political ads turn against data centers

## Insights

- The US Census Bureau's business survey now includes a question on firms' use of "Super Intelligence," though its own examples (e.g., voice recognition) fall well short of the term's more ambitious connotations.
- Reported "Super Intelligence" usage among firms rose from 17.3% in late November 2025 to 24.6% in early October 2026, suggesting rapid growth in self-reported AI adoption (even if the definition is loosely applied).
- Political advertising in October was sharply negative toward data centers: only 3% of ads mentioning them were supportive, while 57% were opposed, pointing to a shift in public/political sentiment against data center buildout.
- Semiconductor components made up nearly half of South Korea's exports in October, underscoring the chip sector's outsized role in the country's trade economy amid the AI buildout.

## Quote

> Only 3% of political ads mentioning data centers in October supported them.
