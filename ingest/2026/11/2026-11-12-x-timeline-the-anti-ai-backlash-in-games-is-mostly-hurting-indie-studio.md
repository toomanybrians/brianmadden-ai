# The anti-AI backlash in games is mostly hurting indie studios.

Insisting on no

## Insights

- A backlash against AI use in game development is described as disproportionately harming indie studios rather than large publishers.
- The piece argues that demanding zero AI involvement in code is unworkable because AI will become an unavoidable part of coding generally.
- An alternative is proposed: rather than policing AI use, fans/awards should reward and center human artists and designers.
- A specific case is cited — an indie game ("Big Walk") disclosed using AI for under 0.1% of its code, triggering major controversy among fans, developers, and game award judges after being a 2026 Game of the Year contender.
- The incident is framed as a flashpoint for broader unresolved questions about where to draw the line on generative AI use in creative/game production.

## Quote

> Insisting on no AI code in a world where all coding will effectively involve AI is an impossible standard.
