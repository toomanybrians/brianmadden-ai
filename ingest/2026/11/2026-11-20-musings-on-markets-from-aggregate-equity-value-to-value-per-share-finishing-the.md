# From Aggregate Equity Value to Value per share: Finishing the Anthropic Journey

## Insights

- Anthropic's IPO prospectus (filed mid-November 2026) prices the offering at $74/share initially, later set at $83/share, implying an aggregate equity value of roughly $1.35 trillion after adjustments — down from an initial $1.475 trillion estimate.
- Post-election assumptions were revised to reflect a more difficult environment for AI infrastructure: expected sales-per-dollar-invested in data centers was cut from $3 to $2.75, and an assumed regulatory margin "drag" was raised from 5% to 6% (target margin cut to 26.5%), reflecting expectations that data center buildout will get more expensive and AI regulation more stringent under a new Congress.
- IPO cash proceeds assumption was revised down from a rumored $100 billion to about $60 billion based on the disclosed share issuance (801 million shares) and expected offering price, cutting projected post-IPO equity value by roughly $40 billion.
- Anthropic's capital structure includes multiple share classes, 1.026 billion unvested restricted shares (4-year vesting), 1.774 billion options (deep in-the-money, strike price $2.91 vs. ~$74-83 offer price), and nearly 934 million shares committed to a philanthropic donation/matching plan.
- Anthropic's governance is built around a single founder-held "Class T" share carrying 50.01% of voting rights in perpetuity — described as more concentrated than government "golden share" structures used in privatized utilities, raising a flagged risk that regulators could someday attempt to treat the company as a de facto utility and challenge that concentrated control following an AI-related incident.
- A $74 billion one-time, non-cash charge to Anthropic's 2026 earnings is attributed to the rising value of conversion options on convertible debt as the company's private-market pricing increased.
