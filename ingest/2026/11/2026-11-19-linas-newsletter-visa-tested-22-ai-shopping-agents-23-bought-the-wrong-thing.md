# Visa tested 22 AI Shopping Agents & 23% bought the wrong thing 🤖🛍️; Nubank’s Q3 2026 earnings: half the profit growth came from taxes, the 12% margin that’s really 10% & what NU stock is worth 🏦💜

## Insights

- Visa tested 22 AI shopping agents built on models from Google, Meta, OpenAI, Anthropic and eight other labs in its new Agentic Commerce Benchmark; across all runs, agents bought a product that didn't actually meet the shopper's needs in 23.1% of cases.
- The headline pass rate was 57.2%, but the piece flags a separate "consistency" figure as more important than that headline number for judging real-world reliability.
- Using a more expensive/higher-end model did not fix the failure rate — model cost and shopping-agent accuracy appear decoupled.
- In live deployments, a mis-targeted agent purchase still clears normally: payment authenticates, card tokenizes, transaction settles — nothing in the payment flow signals that the agent misunderstood the shopper.
- No settled industry answer yet on who bears liability/dispute costs when an agent buys the wrong item, and the benchmark is framed as an early signal of the dispute volume agentic payments will generate.
- Visa's positioning is read as an attempt to establish where it sits in the value chain once liability for agent purchase errors becomes a live commercial issue for agent builders, merchants, and issuers.

## Quote

> In 23.1% of all runs, the shopper ended up buying a product that didn't meet their needs.
