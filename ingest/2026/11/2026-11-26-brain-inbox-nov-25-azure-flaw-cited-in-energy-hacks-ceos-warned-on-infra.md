# Nov. 25 - Azure flaw cited in energy hacks | CEOs warned on infrastructure risk

## Insights

- Microsoft patched an Azure vulnerability linked to attacks on Norwegian energy companies, but is still urging affected customers to rotate credentials and secrets as a precaution.
- An infrastructure security trade group is advising CEOs to prioritize critical assets and rigorously test incident response/recovery plans, framing recent tech outages as a preview of more chaotic disruptions ahead.
- OpenAI and Cisco jointly released a framework allowing AI agents to autonomously and safely block exploitation of known vulnerabilities, giving organizations temporary protection while patches are tested.
- An AI-enabled attack campaign compromised at least 22 organizations by exploiting critical vulnerabilities in Check Point Security Management servers.
- Retailers are facing a rising threat of AI-driven cyberattacks heading into Black Friday, with many reportedly lacking adequate insurance coverage for such incidents.
