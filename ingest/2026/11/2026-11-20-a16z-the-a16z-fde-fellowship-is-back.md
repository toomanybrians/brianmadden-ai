# The a16z FDE Fellowship is back

## Insights

- The "Forward Deployed Engineer" (FDE) role, originating at Palantir, is being adapted by a new generation of AI companies (OpenAI, Anthropic, Harvey, Exa, Perplexity, Decagon, Cognition, Replit, SpaceX, Wonderful) with different products and customers.
- The piece frames FDE and "Applied AI" as converging/interchangeable titles, suggesting a distinct professional function is coalescing around deploying AI with enterprise customers.
- Underlying driver: as AI models grow more capable, customer expectations shift from generic software toward systems that understand specific business processes, integrate with proprietary data, and complete the actual job hired for — not just provide a tool.
- This is framed as expanding scope and difficulty ("the promise is becoming much bigger, and so is the work of making good on it"), implying deployment/customization work is growing as a discipline alongside model capability.
- The fellowship itself (8 weeks, biweekly dinners/events in SF/NYC, cohort-based) is a networking and talent-community structure a16z is building around this emerging role, rather than a training curriculum.

## Quote

> As AI models get more capable, customers increasingly expect software that fits the contours of their business.
