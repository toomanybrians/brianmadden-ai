# Anthropic built Claude’s Personal Finance feature to do less than ChatGPT’s 🤖📲; Monzo goes from £10B takeover talks to a £7.5B funding round in just 6 weeks 😳📉

## Insights

- Anthropic's new Claude Personal Finance feature launched roughly six months after OpenAI's ChatGPT Finances, with fewer capabilities by design rather than by lagging behind.
- Claude can read bank balances and transactions via Plaid, but cannot see investment accounts, retain memory of past finance conversations, let its agents take actions on accounts, or display ads.
- ChatGPT Finances is positioned as a free money app that monetizes through card applications and ad-funded offers, contrasting with Claude's more restricted, non-monetized approach.
- Anthropic's likely target audience for this feature is institutional — banks, advisors, and fintechs it sells to — rather than end consumers directly.
- The feature's limitations are framed as consistent with a broader pullback on AI-agent account access seen at JPMorgan, Schwab, and Citi.
- The piece flags one underlying signal to watch: whether Anthropic's more restrained, enterprise-oriented bet proves out compared to OpenAI's consumer-facing, ad-monetized approach.
