# It's rolling out to eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers with a community galler

## Insights

- Google is rolling out Genie 4 and an updated Genie 3 to Google AI Ultra subscribers inside "Project Genie," a tool for generating and exploring interactive virtual worlds; Google AI Pro members can only join a waitlist.
- Genie 4 adds mid-session prompt editing (changing weather, adding characters, redirecting action in real time) and native adaptive audio (ambient sound, character dialogue) synced to the generated world.
- Genie 3 updates extend session length from 60 seconds to up to 3 minutes, allow chaining multiple generated worlds together, support seeding worlds from 360-degree panorama images, and allow real-time natural-language "in-world events."
- A new "Project Genie agent" lets users build complex interactive worlds through conversational natural-language prompts, orchestrating Google's multimodal models to generate visuals and custom scores rather than requiring single static prompts; Gemini can also observe a session live and surface suggestions or trigger events.
- A community gallery lets users publish, explore, and remix worlds (both curated examples and community/creator collaborations, e.g. with a creative technologist and a filmmaker).
- Google states all generated video/audio uses SynthID watermarking and safety screening, and frames the models as still-evolving "frontier research" with open challenges like camera latency, physical interaction fidelity, real-time audio precision, and long-term scene permanence.

## Quote

> This update unlocks first-time access to Genie 4, new upgrades to Genie 3, the Project Genie agent... and a community gallery to discover, explore, and remix worlds.
