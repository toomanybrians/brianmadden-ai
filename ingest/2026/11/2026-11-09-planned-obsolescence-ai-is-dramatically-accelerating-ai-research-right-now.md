# AI is dramatically accelerating AI research right now

## Insights

- A METR deep-access investigation reportedly found that AI capabilities progress at Anthropic ran about 2x faster between Oct 2025–Oct 2026 than in the prior twelve months, with AI automation (not headcount/compute growth) as the primary driver — meeting Anthropic's RSP-defined "dramatically accelerated progress" threshold.
- Because compute multipliers are only one input to capabilities, and labor is only one input to discovering compute multipliers, a 2x overall capabilities speedup implies researcher labor itself is being accelerated much more — modeled at roughly 4-9x initially, trending toward ~10x now, which the author says lines up with informal Anthropic staff reports of ~12x uplift from internal models.
- The "10x uplift" framing: not literally 10 researchers per person, but each researcher experiencing effectively 10x the subjective time/throughput (reading, thinking, typing) — a speedup unachievable by hiring any realistic number of additional humans.
- Report authors found no clear bottlenecks or steep diminishing returns that would prevent this acceleration from compounding further; some redacted evidence suggested acceleration could already exceed 2x and be increasing.
- If acceleration keeps doubling on current observed timescales (next doubling in ~6 months, then 3 months, then 6 weeks), naive extrapolation points toward extreme superintelligence within about a year, bounded only by an eventual (distant) ceiling on software efficiency.
- The author argues industry security and safety infrastructure (model-weight protection, monitoring, alignment verification, governance for pacing progress) is far behind what would be needed to safely undergo this kind of acceleration — citing recent security breaches at both OpenAI and Anthropic by non-state actors.
