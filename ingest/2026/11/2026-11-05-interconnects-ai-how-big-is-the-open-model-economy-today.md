# How big is the open model economy today?

## Insights

- Measuring open-model adoption is inherently noisy today — reliable data comes only from imprecise platform metrics, startup marketing figures, and imperfect public benchmarks, requiring informed interpretation.
- The author uses token volume from open-model inference startups (Baseten, Fireworks, Together AI) plus OpenRouter's reported open-model share as proxies ("bellwethers") for the health of the open-model inference market, since their business success tracks open-model demand directly.
- Hyperscalers (Amazon Bedrock, Azure Model Garden) likely have significant open-model inference volume too, but don't publicly break out usage by model category, leaving a gap in the data.
- Public disclosures show near-exponential growth in open-model token volume among these startups, with an expected further uptick tied to GLM 5.2's impact on open agent capabilities.
- Fireworks reported serving 40T+ tokens/day to customized models (just one segment of its business), with that vertical growing over 2x since summer — total volume this fall is expected to exceed 100T tokens/day.
- Google's earnings-report token volume disclosures are used as a reference point to contextualize open-model inference scale against a major closed/hyperscale platform.

## Quote

> This shows near-exponential growth, before the uptick that would follow GLM 5.2's step change in open agents.
