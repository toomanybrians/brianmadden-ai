# dirty and possibly and illegal https://t.co/ng7vkir9SD

## Insights

- Nvidia routed $104 billion of revenue through its Singapore subsidiary in 2025, a 72% year-over-year increase, accounting for nearly half of the company's total global sales.
- The same year this Singapore revenue surged, Nvidia stopped disclosing Singapore as a separate reporting segment in its financial results.
- Gary Marcus characterizes this combination of a sharp revenue spike and the simultaneous loss of reporting transparency as suspicious, calling it "dirty and possibly illegal."
- The underlying reporting (credited to Owen Walker and Antoine Gara) raises questions about revenue recognition practices and corporate transparency at a major AI hardware supplier during a period of explosive AI-driven demand.
