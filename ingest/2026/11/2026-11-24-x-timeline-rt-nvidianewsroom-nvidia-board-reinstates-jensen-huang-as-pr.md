# RT @nvidianewsroom: NVIDIA Board reinstates Jensen Huang as President, CEO and B

## Insights

- NVIDIA's Board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as President, CEO, and Board Director effective immediately, following discussions the Board says resolved its concerns about his "candor and ability to lead."
- The reinstatement followed reports that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort; the Board says it consulted senior U.S. government officials and concluded his return poses no national security risk.
- An independent-director committee's investigation into the matter will continue to conclusion despite the reinstatement, and Huang has committed to cooperating with it.
- NVIDIA is creating new governance infrastructure in response: a National Security and Export Control Committee (already formed) and a new Chief Compliance Officer role reporting to the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee.
- Colette Kress, NVIDIA's CFO, had served as interim CEO during Huang's absence and will continue as CFO going forward.

## Quote

> "I am honored to return to NVIDIA and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world." — Jensen Huang
