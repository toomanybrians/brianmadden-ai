# I think often about the fact that almost everyone working in the West has lived

## Insights

- Most people working in the West today have spent their entire careers in a historically unusual period of socio-technical stability.
- Of the 270 job categories listed in the 1950 US census, only one (elevator operator) is now obsolete — evidence of how little the occupational structure has actually shifted over roughly 75 years.
- The author frames 2026 as structurally similar to 1957, contrasting it with 1969's relationship to 1900, which was marked by far greater technological and occupational disruption.
- The post implies this long stretch of stability may be ending, without specifying what would cause the change.

## Quote

> 2026 is similar to 1957 in a way that 1969 was not from 1900.
