# And that was something we started in the GPT-4 era, you can be even more ambitio

## Insights

- Argues that AI model costs have dropped dramatically since the GPT-4 era — GPT-4-level capability can now run on a smartphone — which should enable far more ambitious nonprofit/social-impact projects than were previously feasible.
- Observes a gap: despite this cost collapse, few major not-for-profit efforts are attempting large-scale initiatives to tackle hard problems using AI.
- Claims AI agents now let small teams "scale the equivalent" of previously expensive, labor-intensive work.
- Cites a concrete example: an open-source project using AI agents to help teachers build pedagogically sound educational simulations — work that previously required substantial effort and cost, now much cheaper to produce.

## Quote

> Yet I see very few major not-for-profit efforts that are thinking about making giant leaps to solve hard problems.
