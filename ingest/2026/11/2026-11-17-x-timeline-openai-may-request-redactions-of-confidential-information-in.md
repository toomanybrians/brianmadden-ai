# OpenAI may request redactions of confidential information, including evidence be

## Insights

- Apollo Research states that OpenAI may request redactions of confidential material from Apollo's reports, including evidence underlying its conclusions.
- Apollo asserts redactions will not be granted merely because a finding is unfavorable to OpenAI — implying some review/negotiation process over what gets cut.
- Apollo retains the ability to disclose how any redactions affected its conclusions and to flag this for readers assessing the reports.
- Apollo plans to publish two separate reports: one on training incentives, and one covering evaluations, internal deployment, and detectability.
- Each report will explicitly document methods, conclusions, and limitations — including specific questions Apollo was unable to assess.

## Quote

> OpenAI may request redactions of confidential information, including evidence behind our conclusions, but not simply because a finding is unfavorable.
