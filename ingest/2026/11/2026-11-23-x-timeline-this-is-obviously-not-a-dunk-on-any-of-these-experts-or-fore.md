# This is obviously not a dunk on any of these experts or forecasting groups, but

## Insights

- Shortly after an AI system solved the Navier-Stokes problem, forecasters were asked to predict when the Riemann Hypothesis would be solved, and their estimates remained far out: Metaculus predicted May 2035.
- LEAP Superforecasters gave only a 10% chance that another Millennium Prize problem would be solved in 2026.
- Terence Tao estimated a solution via "some combination of humans and AIs" around 2050.
- The poster frames this not as criticism of the forecasters/experts themselves, but as evidence that even top forecasting groups and domain experts are systematically underestimating the pace of AI capability gains.
- The implicit argument: recent AI progress (solving a major unsolved math problem) should have updated expert forecasts about future AI math achievements more than it did.

## Quote

> it is an indicator that even the best forecasters and experts are still underestimating AI abilities
