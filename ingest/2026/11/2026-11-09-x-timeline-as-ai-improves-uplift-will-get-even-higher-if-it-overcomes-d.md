# As AI improves, uplift will get even higher. If it overcomes diminishing returns

## Insights

- Cites a METR AI R&D report finding that AI is nearly doubling the rate of capabilities progress in AI research itself.
- Frames this as "uplift" equivalent to researchers experiencing time ~10x faster — a scale of acceleration described as unachievable even by hiring every qualified human in the world.
- Argues that as AI capabilities improve further, this uplift effect would compound and increase further.
- Notes that if diminishing returns and bottlenecks are overcome, naive extrapolation from current trends implies reaching ASI (artificial superintelligence) within about a year.
- States the report's authors found no clear bottlenecks or sharp diminishing returns in this self-improvement dynamic, while flagging high uncertainty in that finding.

## Quote

> The authors say they found no clear bottlenecks or sharp diminishing returns, though they are very uncertain.
