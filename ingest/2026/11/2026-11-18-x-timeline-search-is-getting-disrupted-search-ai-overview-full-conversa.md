# "Search is getting disrupted!"

Search → AI Overview → Full conversational AI →

## Insights

- Frames search's evolution as a four-stage progression: traditional Search → AI Overview → full conversational AI → Agent, suggesting each stage represents a distinct product/UX shift rather than incremental tweaks.
- Raises an open question about whether this progression makes "Search" more or less useful, sticky, and monetizable — implying the business model implications are unresolved, not just the technology.
- Cites Google's own framing (via quoted Sundar Pichai post) of a shift in product purpose: from "knowing things" to "doing things" to "saving you time and money" — a stated repositioning of search/AI products around task completion rather than information retrieval.
- Notes a concrete example of this shift: Gemini's "Tasks" feature is being expanded free of charge to hundreds of millions of people across six countries, powered by "Gemini 4 Hydrogen."
- Tasks is described as enabling delegation of "everyday life admin," with email-forwarding cited as one specific mechanism for handing off tasks to the AI.

## Quote

> Knowing things → doing things → finding ways to save you time and money
