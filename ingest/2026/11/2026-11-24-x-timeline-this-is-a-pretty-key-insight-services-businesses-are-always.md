# This is a pretty key insight. Services businesses are always held back by their

## Insights

- Services businesses have historically been constrained in scaling by their delivery capacity — specifically, the ability to produce outcomes for customers.
- That delivery capacity was a function of headcount: no matter how much demand existed, growth was capped by the finite supply of available talent.
- The argument (attributed to the quoted post) is that genuine bullishness on AI implies services businesses should be able to scale the way software businesses do — decoupling growth from headcount.
- This reframes AI's potential impact on the enterprise less as efficiency/automation and more as removing a structural growth constraint unique to services/labor-based business models.

## Quote

> Services businesses are always held back by their ability to deliver outcomes to customers.
