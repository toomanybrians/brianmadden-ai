# RT @3DVR3: Googleの世界モデル「Genie 4」が使えるようになったので、渋谷を歩いてみた

歩いている人まで破綻せずリアルタイムで生成されてて

## Insights

- A tester used Google's "Genie 4" world model to generate a real-time walkable simulation of Shibuya, noting that even pedestrians were rendered without visual breakdown or artifacting.
- Turning around within the generated scene caused disorientation, with the environment losing coherence relative to the original orientation ("where even is this?").
- The tester assessed the visual quality as roughly comparable to video-generation models from a few years prior, but saw meaningful future potential.
- Generation cost was cited at approximately $1 per 10 seconds, including real-world details like waiting at a crosswalk still consuming paid generation time.
