# Welcome to the B2A Economy

## Insights

- AI agents reportedly now make up the majority of web traffic, write more than half of new online articles, and generate most code pushed to production — framed as early-stage platform shift where adoption outpaces visible business models.
- Prior platform shifts (web, mobile) paired new capability with new distribution; AI has advanced capability but lagged on distribution, which the piece argues explains limited consumer adoption outside ChatGPT.
- Concrete traffic/usage data cited: Shopify saw AI-driven store traffic grow 8x YoY (Q1 '26) and AI-search orders grow ~13x; Human Security recorded agentic browser traffic growing 8x between August–October '26 (Muse ~60% share, OpenAI ~30%); AI-referred traffic reportedly converts better and bounces less than non-AI traffic.
- Infrastructure for agents to act on identity/payments is emerging (e.g., "Sign in with ChatGPT," OpenAI's new "Businesses in ChatGPT" feature, Claude's enterprise plugin connections), moving agents from discovery toward executing transactions, not just referring traffic.
- Coding agents are presented as an early case study of this shift: ~40% of GitHub PRs reportedly start as agent drafts (up from 17% six months prior), with large shares of new database launches, API/CLI traffic, and deployments now agent-driven (Supabase, Neon, Stripe, Vercel figures cited).
- Four beneficiary categories are proposed: agentic models/products themselves, entirely new products only possible because of agents, incumbents who optimize for agent discovery/execution (beyond current GEO practices), and agent infrastructure providers (payments, fraud/identity, communication tools under strain).

## Quote

> Calling it "AI commerce" undersells the opportunity... they will also reshape how traffic, tokens, and revenue are routed to digital products.
