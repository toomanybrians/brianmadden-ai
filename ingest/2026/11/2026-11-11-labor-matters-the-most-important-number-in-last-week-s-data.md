# The most important number in last week’s data

## Insights

- Nonfarm business output per hour is now 10% above the trajectory implied by the 2011–2019 productivity trend.
- Output is growing near 3% annually while hours worked are near flat — a gap persisting for roughly two years.
- The productivity level never reverted to the pre-pandemic trend, and the gap has widened further since 2023.
- Framed as evidence the economy can produce substantially more output without adding proportionate labor, suggesting a structural (not cyclical) shift in labor demand.
- Author argues this productivity data point matters more than the period's headline jobs number (165,000).

## Quote

> The economy does not need many more workers to produce this much more.
