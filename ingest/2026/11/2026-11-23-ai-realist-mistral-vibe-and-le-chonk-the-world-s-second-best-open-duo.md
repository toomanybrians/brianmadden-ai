# Mistral Vibe and Le Chonk: The World's Second-Best Open Duo

## Insights

- Mistral publicly claimed Mistral Vibe (coding agent) + Large 4 ("Le Chonk") is the "world's best open model-harness duo," but the only evidence offered was a collage of seven social media screenshots—no benchmark numbers, charts, or links.
- Cross-checking Artificial Analysis's Coding Agent Index v2.1 (the actual source for the cited benchmarks) shows Kimi K3.1 Pro leading open models by a wide margin (~8 points on the index, 25 points on Terminal-Bench) — contradicting the "highest among open models" claim.
- On Cognition's FrontierCode benchmark, Large 4's score has been static while four other open models (Inkling 2 Small, DeepSeek, GLM-5.3, Kimi K3.1 Pro) moved ahead or into a near-tie after a rescoring — Mistral's page still cites outdated comparison data.
- Mistral's language is inconsistent across its own releases: a 0.6-point deficit vs. GLM-5.3 was called "effectively tied," while a 0.4-point lead was called a "lead," and now a 0.5-point edge is called "actually better."
- Kimi K3.1 Pro arguably meets the same openness bar Mistral claims for itself (public weights, revenue-based licensing clause) yet is omitted from Mistral's comparisons, despite Artificial Analysis's Openness Index scoring Large 4 only slightly higher than Kimi (39 vs. 33) and below DeepSeek (44).
- The author argues Mistral had a legitimately strong, honest claim available (Vibe/Large 4 ties GLM-5.3 at roughly a quarter of the per-task cost) but chose overstated marketing language instead.

## Quote

> Is Vibe with Large 4 better than GLM-5.3 and DeepSeek? Against DeepSeek, yes... Against GLM-5.3, on paper, by half a point.
