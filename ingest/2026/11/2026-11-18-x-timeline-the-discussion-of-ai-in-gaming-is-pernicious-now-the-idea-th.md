# The discussion of AI in gaming is pernicious now. The idea that games are going

## Insights

- Argues that current discourse around AI in gaming has become "pernicious," specifically the narrative that game development should remain a field where humans write code entirely unassisted by AI.
- Calls this idea "obviously wrong," implying no field of software development will remain untouched by AI-assisted coding.
- Frames the resistance to AI coding tools in gaming as a missed opportunity: energy spent defending human-only coding could instead go toward shaping how AI is adopted in the industry.
- Suggests an alternative framing: AI usage in gaming could be guided toward approaches that empower workers rather than replace or diminish them.
- Positions worker empowerment and industry thriving as compatible goals if AI adoption is steered deliberately, rather than resisted outright.

## Quote

> The idea that games are going to be the only field where humans write code unassisted is obviously wrong.
