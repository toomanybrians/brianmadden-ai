# Agree with this. Scheduled local Codex sessions that email you or message you of

## Insights

- Scheduled, locally-run Codex sessions (which notify the user via email or message) are described as offering more control over important tasks than always-on/ambient AI assistants, because the user can adjust context and prompts before/during execution.
- A practitioner reports shifting back to running Codex locally specifically to retain the ability to know when and how to intervene in a task.
- A "Dot"-style assistant is still seen as useful for many tasks, but explicitly not a substitute for the control offered by local, scheduled AI sessions.
- The underlying argument: "magic" (fully automated, default-mode AI behavior) doesn't need to be the default; deliberate control is positioned as a valuable, sometimes preferable, design choice for personal AI assistants.

## Quote

> Control is nice, especially for a personal assistant.
