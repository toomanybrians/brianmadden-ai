# Nov. 20 - Huge Anthropic IPO puts enterprises on alert I Now public, Anthropic could see conflicts with tech giants

## Insights

- Anthropic's IPO was the largest in market history, pricing at $83 and closing up 17% at $97.17, valuing the company at roughly $1.76 trillion fully diluted; shares fell the following day amid a broader AI-related sell-off affecting Nvidia and AMD.
- Coverage frames the IPO as confirmation that Wall Street now treats generative AI as a mature asset class rather than speculative, but notes the raise doesn't resolve open questions about enterprise AI ROI.
- Anthropic's public debut is described as highlighting its structural dependence on Amazon and Google, who function simultaneously as its investors, suppliers, and competitors — a dynamic framed as a risk for enterprise customers relying on Anthropic.
- An IBM report cited finds ~75% of organizations now have a Chief AI Officer, up from ~25% a year prior, but only 18% of CAIOs actually control AI investment decisions — pointing to a gap between the role's spread and its real authority.
- Related coverage flags AI agents as a growing cybersecurity concern, both accelerating attacks and complicating recovery/rebuild efforts, and raises identity management as a key control for securing agentic AI systems.
- Other noted items: rising attention to "AI decision models" (small judgment models) as an emerging tooling category, and the importance of having AI vendor exit strategies before committing to a platform.

## Quote

> Capital is rushing toward AI faster than organizations can build structures to deploy it responsibly.
