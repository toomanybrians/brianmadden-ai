# The Morning Download: Nvidia Needs Three CEOs in One

## Insights

- Nvidia's abrupt CEO search lacks succession planning, unlike Apple's Jobs-to-Cook transition, which showed deliberate staged signals (COO title, special projects, back-office reporting lines) years in advance.
- A veteran tech executive argues Nvidia's next CEO must simultaneously be three people: a technologist engineers respect, a steward of the CUDA platform developers trust, and a statesperson who can engage Washington and Taipei.
- Candidates discussed: Lisa Su (AMD CEO, seen as closest fit but would leave a rival benefiting from Nvidia's turmoil), Amin Vahdat (built Google's TPUs at scale), and Ian Buck (Nvidia insider who helped build CUDA, seen as preserving platform continuity).
- The framing of Nvidia's next growth phase is shifting from selling chips to the same few cloud customers toward selling full "AI factories" to governments, carmakers, banks, and hospitals — a harder, more political sales motion.
- Interim CEO Colette Kress (CFO) reportedly clashed with Huang over strategy input and had pushed back internally on credit-guarantee deals, including one tied to an OpenAI data-center project.
- Context noted: Huang's departure is tied to a reported affair with a suspected Chinese intelligence source, prompting congressional investigations and scheduled testimony before Thanksgiving.
