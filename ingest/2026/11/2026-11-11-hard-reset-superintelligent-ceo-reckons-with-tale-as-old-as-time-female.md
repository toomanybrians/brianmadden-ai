# “Superintelligent” CEO Reckons With Tale As Old As Time: Female Spies

## Insights

- Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang had a years-long relationship with Alice Huang, alleged to be a Chinese spy connected to Megaspeed, the largest chip buyer in Southeast Asia, raising questions about compliance with 2025 U.S.-China semiconductor export restrictions.
- Short-seller Culper Research traced Megaspeed's balance sheet growth ($33M to $3B in liabilities/equity) to billions in refundable deposits funneled through subsidiaries ultimately tied to Alibaba, suggesting undisclosed China links despite Nvidia's assurances to the U.S. government.
- Separate reporting claims Alice's actual company enabled $7.5B in Nvidia chip sales to China and moved 240,000 banned chips stuck in the supply chain.
- The White House reportedly knew of the relationship since October 2025 (coinciding with a $25M Nvidia donation to a Trump ballroom project) but took no action, and Jensen still attended a China tech summit with Xi Jinping in May 2026.
- The piece argues corporate security/compliance infrastructure (surveillance, NDAs, cybersecurity training) is built around fear of low-level employee leaks, while the actual vulnerability was concentrated power and personal access at the CEO level — a blind spot in how AI/chip companies model espionage risk.
- Frames the episode as evidence that geopolitical AI-race anxieties (e.g., concern about underestimating China) can be undercut by basic human factors rather than technical or policy failures.
