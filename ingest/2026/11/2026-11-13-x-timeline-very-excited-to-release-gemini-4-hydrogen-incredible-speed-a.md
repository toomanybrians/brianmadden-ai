# Very excited to release Gemini 4 Hydrogen! Incredible speed and intelligence for

## Insights

- Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a faster, cheaper model positioned below frontier models Gemini 4 Carbon and Argon, aimed at everyday coding, agentic workflows, and general use rather than deep long-horizon reasoning.
- A new "Orchestrator" feature in Google Antigravity dynamically switches between models (e.g., Carbon for coordination, Hydrogen for execution) within a single session, cutting quota use ~24% and completing tasks ~19% faster in testing.
- Compared to the prior Gemini 3.8 Flash, Hydrogen claims 2.5x faster task completion, more than tripled Terminal-Bench scores, 5x fewer fabricated claims, and better recovery from failed tool calls or empty search results.
- The model is marketed across consumer, developer, and enterprise surfaces simultaneously (Search, Gemini app, AI Studio, Gemini for business, Antigravity, Sheets), suggesting a push toward embedding agentic AI into everyday knowledge-work tools rather than just specialized coding contexts.
- Safety claims include reduced "overreach" and "under-service" in agentic tasks, a 12x cut in misaligned actions/task shortcutting, and improved resistance to prompt injection — framed as necessary for broader, faster, everyday deployment.
- Pricing is flat across its full 1-million-token context window ($1.50/M input, $7.50/M output), and it's pitched as a parallel "worker/verifier" model for multi-agent coding pipelines.
