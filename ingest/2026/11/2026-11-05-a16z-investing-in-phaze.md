# Investing in Phaze

## Insights

- A key lesson from recent agent-productivity work: giving AI agents full desktops, browsers, and apps (like a human user would have) works better than building agent-specific APIs or abstraction layers — models perform better acting "more like humans, not less."
- This approach runs into practical friction because many applications are OS-specific (e.g., Windows-only) or have particular hardware requirements, pushing sophisticated users toward remote access solutions for agents.
- Phaze is positioned as a high-performance remote desktop product giving both humans and agents "local-like" experience on remote workstations, aimed specifically at interactive, latency-sensitive workloads (e.g., Blender, Unreal Studio).
- Phaze was architected from the ground up for agent compatibility, management, and administration, distinguishing it from remote desktop tools designed primarily for human users.
- The founders previously built Parsec, a prior leading remote-desktop solution for graphics-intensive use cases, later acquired by Unity — suggesting continuity of expertise in solving latency/performance problems for remote compute access.

## Quote

> The reality is that AI models want to act more like humans, not less.
