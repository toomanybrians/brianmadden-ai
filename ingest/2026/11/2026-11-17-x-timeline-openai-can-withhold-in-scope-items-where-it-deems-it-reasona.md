# OpenAI can withhold in-scope items where it deems it reasonably necessary for te

## Insights

- Apollo Research describes a disclosure arrangement with OpenAI: OpenAI may withhold "in-scope" items only for technical, security, or IP reasons — not merely because the item is unfavorable to OpenAI.
- OpenAI is contractually obligated to disclose the existence of any withheld items and provide written justification for each withholding.
- For a specific "training assessment," OpenAI has committed to giving Apollo access to code, SFT (supervised fine-tuning) data, RL rollouts (including reasoning traces and rewards), and intermediate model checkpoints that can be queried.
- Access terms for other, unspecified assessments beyond the training assessment have not yet been finalized and remain under negotiation.
- This points to an external, third-party audit/evaluation relationship between an AI safety research org (Apollo) and a frontier AI lab (OpenAI), with formal transparency mechanisms around model development artifacts.

## Quote

> OpenAI can withhold in-scope items where it deems it reasonably necessary for technical, security, or IP reasons, but not simply because they're unfavorable.
