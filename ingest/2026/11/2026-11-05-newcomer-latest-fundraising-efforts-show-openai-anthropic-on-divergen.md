# Latest Fundraising Efforts Show OpenAI & Anthropic on Divergent Paths

## Insights

- OpenAI and Anthropic's concurrent fundraising campaigns are giving investors a direct basis for comparison, and the two companies' trajectories are described as diverging sharply.
- Anthropic is said to be distancing itself from OpenAI, while OpenAI is characterized as seeking a "comeback story," implying its position has weakened relative to Anthropic's.
- An investor backing both companies describes "infinite demand" for Anthropic's shares among long-term investors such as mutual funds.
- Anthropic's planned IPO is reported to be on track despite industry headwinds, with one investor expecting it to happen before Thanksgiving.
- The piece links Anthropic's momentum partly to political positioning, noting the company has been more aligned with Democrats than most of the AI industry, which is seen as advantageous after Democrats' midterm win.
- Broader "headwinds facing the AI industry" and a tougher political climate are mentioned as context, though not detailed further.
