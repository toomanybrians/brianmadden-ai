# Now there might be things to verify beyond errors - points of view, opinions, ap

## Insights

- Claims that recent AI models now outperform human experts, including PhD specialists, at finding facts and checking claims — reversing an earlier state where human fact-checking of AI was necessary.
- Argues the common workplace concern about needing to verify AI output for factual errors is outdated and overstated given this shift in model capability.
- Suggests the human role of "checking AI work for mistakes" is no longer an accurate description of the job today.
- Distinguishes between verifying factual errors (increasingly AI's strength) and verifying subjective elements — points of view, opinions, approaches — which still may require human judgment.

## Quote

> The idea that the job of humans should be checking AI work for mistakes is likely not true today, and good riddance.
