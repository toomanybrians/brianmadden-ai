# VC Funding Keeps Flowing as Nvidia Whiplash & Market Gyrations Shadow the AI Boom

## Insights

- Private AI markets haven't yet repriced despite public market turmoil (Anthropic trading below IPO price, Nvidia volatility); VCs still writing big checks (Sierra at $30B, Cognition reportedly discussing $75B) because funds operate on multi-year cycles insulated from short-term public swings.
- Inference startups are flagged as a potential bellwether: neoclouds like CoreWeave are newly demanding up to 50% upfront payment for compute (versus previously zero), turning inference from a capital-light business into one requiring billions in advance capital — right as funding tightens.
- Historical precedent (2022) suggests private valuations don't reset until a downturn persists roughly nine months and investors start doubting their ability to raise the next fund — a shorter public selloff likely won't break private AI funding.
- Nvidia's prior "circular" investment strategy (funding open-source model makers like neolabs) is now in question with Jensen Huang's leadership turmoil, with one investor worried Nvidia's ecosystem investments could effectively go to zero.
- Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge used Claude to produce a machine proof of the Riemann hypothesis (~17 million lines), validated by experts, but it sparked disagreement: tech executives (Musk, Alexandr Wang) framed it as evidence of near-superintelligence, while academics (Terence Tao, Yann LeCun, Nestor Guillen) were skeptical, with Tao calling the inelegant formal proof "toxic waste."
- Broader open question raised: whether tightening money conditions will separate strong AI businesses from weak ones, and whether public market skepticism (post-Anthropic IPO) has closed the near-term window for other AI startups to go public.

## Quote

> This moment of reckoning was a long time coming and will likely serve to strengthen the industry as the weaker businesses die off.
