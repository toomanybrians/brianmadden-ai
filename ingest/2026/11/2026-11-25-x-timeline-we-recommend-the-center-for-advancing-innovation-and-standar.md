## Insights

- Apollo Research proposes creating a government-recognized body ("Center for Advancing Innovation and Standards for Super Intelligence," CAISSI) tasked with rapidly developing a transparency standard specifically for "Handoff."
- The recommended structure has three parts: a standards task force, mandatory company reporting against that standard, and independent verification by government and/or third-party assessors.
- The framing implies "Handoff" is a specific, named AI event/capability significant enough to warrant policy-level transparency standards.
- Current disclosure practices around Handoff are described as voluntary, inconsistent, and self-defined — companies report sporadically and using their own metrics.
- The core problem stated is a visibility gap: policymakers currently rely almost entirely on companies' own self-reported information about Handoff.
