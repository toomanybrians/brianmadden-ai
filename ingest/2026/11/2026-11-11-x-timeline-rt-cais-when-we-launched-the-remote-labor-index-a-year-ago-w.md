# RT @CAIS: When we launched the Remote Labor Index a year ago with @ScaleAILabs ,

## Insights

- The Remote Labor Index, launched by CAIS and Scale AI roughly a year prior, measures what share of real freelance projects AI can fully automate.
- At launch, the best-performing AI could fully automate only 2.5% of real freelance projects.
- A model called "Fable 5.2" now automates 41.3% of those same projects, per the tweet.
- The automation rate on this benchmark is reportedly doubling approximately every 3 months.
