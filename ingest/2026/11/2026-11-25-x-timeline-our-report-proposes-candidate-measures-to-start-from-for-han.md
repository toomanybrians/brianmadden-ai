# Our report proposes candidate measures to start from.
For Handoff of review:

-E

## Insights

- Proposes two candidate metrics for evaluating AI agent oversight ("Handoff") processes: end-to-end detection efficacy (share of seeded flaws slipping past both AI monitors and human reviewers) and time-to-review-and-patch (latency from agent activity to flag, to review, to mitigation).
- Recommends creating a body called the Center for Advancing Innovation and Standards for Super Intelligence (CAISSI) tasked with rapidly developing a "Handoff transparency standard."
- Governance model proposed is three-layered: companies report against the standard, while independent assessors from government and/or third parties verify compliance.
- The framing assumes AI agents' work requires active monitoring for seeded flaws, implying a review pipeline where AI and human reviewers both act as checks before mitigation.
