# How to Make Viral Videos With Claude (Full Course)

## Insights

- The course teaches an end-to-end workflow for building videos using Claude (for scripting/code generation) and Python (for animation frames), with FFmpeg compositing and phone-recorded narration.
- A clock animation (an "8" losing two lights to become either "9" or an ambiguous "3") is used as the flagship example of designing a hook: ambiguity mid-transformation, not the final answer, is what sustains viewer attention.
- The course argues editing judgment — knowing when to cut from an intimate two-person scene to a wide shot of a crowd — is as essential a skill as the technical rendering process itself.
- The entire pipeline is positioned as achievable at $0 in generation fees, using a free Claude chat (within usage limits) and a personal computer, rather than paid AI video generation tools.
- The course includes prompts, working code, and editing breakdowns, going from original idea through script to final cut as a complete worked example.
