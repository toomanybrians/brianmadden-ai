## Insights

- Claims GPT-4 alone would have driven large productivity impact eventually, but realizing those gains required substantial process redesign within organizations, which takes time.
- Argues current frontier models require much smaller process changes to generate real gains compared to GPT-4, implying faster organizational adoption.
- Cites a specific capability benchmark: GPT-4 could complete about 4 minutes of independent work with a 50% success rate, versus current models handling multiple weeks of independent work.
- Identifies January 2026 as a reasonable marker for when "agentic work" began in practice.
- Asserts that before this agentic threshold, large productivity impacts from AI were mostly limited to coding tasks specifically.
