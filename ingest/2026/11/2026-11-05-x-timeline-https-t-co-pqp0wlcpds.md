# https://t.co/pqP0WlcPDS

## Insights

- The tool ("Cortex Cloud") converts an Obsidian vault or folder of markdown files into an interactive 3D visualization, rendering notes as a navigable "cloud" using three.js with bloom effects and a live force-directed layout.
- The indexer parses wikilinks, frontmatter links, tags, and aliases, and is positioned as fast at scale — claimed to process 8,000 notes in about 1.4 seconds.
- It's released as MIT-licensed open source, runs entirely locally ("on your machine"), and ships with a sample vault of 303 invented notes for an out-of-box demo.
- Distribution mechanism is notable: access is gated behind a social action (following the author on X) rather than payment or email signup, framed self-deprecatingly as "costs 1 follow."
- Includes developer-oriented tooling — a test suite (parser, indexer, layout-model tests) and CLI commands for installation, running a dev server, and indexing a custom vault.
