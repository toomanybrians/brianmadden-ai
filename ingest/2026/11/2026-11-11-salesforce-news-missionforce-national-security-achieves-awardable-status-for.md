# Missionforce National Security Achieves ‘Awardable’ Status for Department of War Recruiting Capabilities in the CDAO’s Tradewinds Solutions Marketplace

## Insights

- Salesforce's Missionforce National Security platform gained "Awardable" status on the Department of War's Tradewinds Solutions Marketplace, giving defense agencies direct procurement access for recruiting technology without a separate competitive bid.
- The stated problem: ~20,000 vacant DOW positions (concentrated in cyber/technical fields) and a leadership goal to cut hiring timelines from months down to 25 days.
- Technical stack disclosed: built on Agentforce 360, Public Sector Solutions, Data 360, Marketing Cloud, and Tableau Next, running at Government Cloud Impact Level 5 (IL5), and designed to integrate with legacy DOW systems rather than replace them.
- Named capabilities: AI-generated recruiting campaigns from plain-language prompts (Marketing Cloud); automated verification of ASVAB scores, role eligibility, and clearance status with anomaly flagging; and Tableau-based attrition-risk prediction linking engagement, performance, and promotion data to enable proactive retention outreach.
- Salesforce frames this as "agentic" automation with "critical human oversight over all agentic actions" retained — positioning human-in-the-loop governance as a selling point for sensitive government use cases.
- Contracting runs through Computable Insights LLC, described as a wholly owned Salesforce subsidiary, as the vehicle for Tradewinds marketplace listing.

## Quote

> "National Security comes down to the people behind the mission, and we're enabling defense leaders to recruit at the speed that mission demands." — Kendall Collins, CEO of Missionforce
