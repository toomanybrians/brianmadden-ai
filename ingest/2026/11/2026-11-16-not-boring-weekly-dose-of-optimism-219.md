# Weekly Dose of Optimism #219

## Insights

- Google DeepMind's AlphaGenome 2 is an AI model that predicts what specific DNA variants do in particular cell types/conditions (not just tissue-wide averages), trained on 107M+ individual cell profiles; it roughly doubled accuracy on a causal-variant benchmark (16% → 30%) versus the prior version — illustrating rapid generational improvement in domain-specific scientific AI models.
- Path Robotics' "Obsidian" model gives welding robots the ability to perceive each joint in real time and plan/adjust custom welds on the fly, rather than repeating a fixed programmed path — a case of AI-driven adaptive robotics displacing a skilled-labor bottleneck (expert welders are scarce and highly paid, e.g. cited $1M/year earners).
- Path Robotics raised a $300M Series E at a $2.45B valuation, with revenue up 17x in 18 months and major industrial customers (Lockheed Martin, Saronic, HII/Navy shipbuilding) — signals strong enterprise appetite for AI-robotics automation in physical/manufacturing labor, not just office knowledge work.
- Path also has a quadruped robot ("Rove," built on a Boston Dynamics platform) performing welding tasks, with 50 preorders headed to shipyards/construction sites — an example of AI-enabled mobile robots being adopted for field/industrial work.
- SpaceX is reportedly building large-scale vertical integration (satellites, chips via "Terafab," solar manufacturing) to support "Starmind" — AI data centers planned for deployment in space — an extreme case of enterprise infrastructure strategy built around anticipated AI compute demand.
