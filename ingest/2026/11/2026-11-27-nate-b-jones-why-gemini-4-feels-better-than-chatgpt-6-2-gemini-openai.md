# Why Gemini 4 feels better than ChatGPT 6.2 #gemini #openai

## Insights

- OpenAI's ChatGPT 6.2 Astra (released Nov 17) drew widespread negative reaction, with many calling it unusable.
- Google's Gemini 4 Neon, released the next day and reportedly not even Google's largest model, received strong positive reception — described as optimistic and enjoyable to write with.
- The author argues both reactions are valid simultaneously, suggesting current evaluation methods for AI models are breaking down.
- Comparing ChatGPT 6.2 Astra, Gemini 4 Neon, and Claude Fable 5.2 reveals the major labs are no longer pursuing a single shared definition of "intelligence" — they're diverging in what kind of model they're building.
- Frames this divergence as a bigger story than weekly leaderboard rankings between labs.
