**Suggested Substack title:** The 7-stage roadmap for human-AI collaboration in the workplace
**Suggested subtitle:** From "prompt and paste" to AI-orchestrated work — a practical framework for where your workers actually are with AI, and where they're headed next.

I spend most of my time at Citrix exploring, discussing, and planning for AI's impact on knowledge work. But I keep running into the same problem: there's no shared understanding of what "AI adoption" even means, which makes every conversation about it harder than it needs to be.

So I built a practical framework — seven stages of progression for how human workers actually use and collaborate with AI at work. It starts where everyone starts: simple "prompt and paste" tasks in 2023. It ends somewhere most companies haven't gotten close to yet: AI orchestrating large-scale workflows across people, agents, and systems, with humans setting strategy and stepping in as an escalation point rather than doing the work themselves.

In between are five more stages — and where your workers actually sit on that path (not where you assume they sit) determines whether their opinion about AI is even worth listening to yet.

The specific timing will vary by company, department, and individual worker. What matters isn't the exact labels — it's the trajectory.
